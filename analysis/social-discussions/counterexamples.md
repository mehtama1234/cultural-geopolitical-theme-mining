# Counterexamples and disconfirming signals

The social layer must keep evidence that pushes against the first interpretation.

## AI and work control

- LinkedIn relays distinguish shallow productivity gains from employment and
  job-quality outcomes; this pushes against a simple “AI creates productivity,
  therefore workers win” story.
- Reddit replies include both historical substitution skepticism and accounts
  of real occupational hollowing-out; neither frame is treated as settled.

## Platform attention and exit

- Selective retention of LinkedIn for work, or Reddit/YouTube after leaving
  other platforms, pushes against “quit” meaning total non-use.
- The non-use research relay describes reversibility and social pressure, while
  personal accounts describe breaks as easy and beneficial; both are retained.

## AI companionship and trust

- Users describe genuine support and emotional rehearsal, pushing against a
  harm-only interpretation.
- The Anthropic research relay says emotional-companion use was rare in its
  Claude data, pushing against claims that all AI use is companionship.
- Privacy/control and continuity concerns push against the idea that feeling
  understood is sufficient for safe or durable support.

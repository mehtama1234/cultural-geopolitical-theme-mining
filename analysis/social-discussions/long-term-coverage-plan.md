# Long-term social coverage plan

This is the operating plan for adding public discussion to the existing
14-theme research atlas. The current queue is focused on USA industrial and
consumer questions. The social lane is an evidence layer over the atlas; it
is not a replacement for the research records and it is not limited to the
three-topic pilot.

## Current scope

Prioritize discussion that helps explain US production, firm strategy,
automation, investment, competition, supply chains, labor capacity, prices,
credit, insurance, product or service quality, consumer dependence, recourse,
switching, and household adaptation. Use adjacent themes only when they
explain one of those industrial or consumer mechanisms. Do not treat general
political, cultural, or international discussion as in scope without a direct
US industrial or consumer anchor.

## Coverage rule

Work through the themes in bounded waves. For each wave, select one open arrow
or research question, then search YouTube, Reddit, LinkedIn, and X using the
same date window and a documented query rule. Record usable items even when a
platform produces no usable result. A theme is not complete because it has
four links; it is complete for a wave only when the discussion has been
compared with the relevant research, counter-signals, and limits.

Each wave should preserve:

- the atlas theme and subtheme;
- the research record or source-led map being augmented;
- the end-to-end stage or open arrow under review;
- platform, URL, author/account, date, and retrieval date;
- item type and discussion signal;
- counterexamples, missing platforms, and ranking/access limits; and
- the next research or acquisition test.

## Delivery sequence

1. **Wave 1 — pilot calibration:** retain the existing 20 records for AI/work,
   platform exit, and AI companionship; document what the records do and do
   not show.
2. **Wave 2 — thin and high-value arrows:** choose the next four themes using
   the atlas coverage audit, prioritizing consumer recourse, time/care, public
   systems, and cultural meaning where open middle stages are visible.
3. **Wave 3 — full thematic rotation:** add at least one bounded social wave to
   every remaining theme, preserving platform gaps instead of filling them
   with weaker material. The first rotation is now complete; later waves
   should revisit each theme with different groups, places, and open arrows.
4. **Wave 4 — cross-theme synthesis:** compare recurring language and
   counter-language across themes, while keeping each claim attached to its
   original source, unit, date, and research anchor.

The independent-trend-discovery lane remains separate. It may produce new
candidate themes, but it must not be used to make the atlas appear socially
complete before the 14-theme coverage queue has been worked.

# Social augmentation acquisition log — atlas batch 0086

Date: 2026-10-20  
Window: 2025-10-17 through 2026-10-17  
Scope: ten reopened U.S. time, care, leave, transport, and household-
production records in `atlas-batch-0086.json`.

## Research question

How do care, travel, leave, paperwork, and household production become hidden
prices for U.S. households and employers? Keep separate:

`service availability → usable access → time burden → work substitution →
income/career effect → household recovery`

Earlier gaps are retained as history. A public discussion is promoted only when
it identifies the atlas source or a distinctive source-linked lived or policy
finding. A service being available does not prove that a person can use it at
the needed time, place, price, or quality.

## Selected rows

- Parent care, EITC incentives, multigenerational living, and working-parent
  boundaries
- Leave access and unmet need
- Airport transit and telework/travel planning
- Eldercare time and provider work
- Household production and disability-related care/work constraints

## Search boundary and limits

Search YouTube, Reddit, LinkedIn, and X for exact source names, distinctive
findings, named programs, and lived consequences within the stated window.
Record platform gaps rather than filling them with generic parenting or
caregiving advice. Public discussion can show time pressure, substitution,
work conflict, or service friction; it cannot by itself estimate population
burden, causal effects, or household welfare.

This is public-web discovery, not a platform-native Creator/API export. Search
can omit comments, deleted posts, private groups, and non-indexed content. The
batch is not completion of the time/care theme or the broader 14-theme atlas.

## Next test

Compare lived time costs with the atlas's population, universe, and method
boundaries. Prioritize counterexamples where formal access exists but the
person still cannot use the service without unpaid substitution or work loss.

## Research question

When a U.S. consumer is harmed, can the person identify the problem, reach a
responsible institution, obtain correction or compensation, and recover the
lost money, safety, time, or trust? The pass will keep these stages separate:

`exposure → visibility → complaint → response → remedy → recovery → exit`

Earlier documented gaps are retained as history. A result is promoted only
when it identifies the atlas source or a distinctive, source-linked public
discussion. General consumer anger is not evidence of an institutional
outcome.

- Cross-source consumer recovery and remedy-visibility rows
- CPSC recall surveillance and recall-progress rows
- Federal Reserve household fraud-recovery row

## Search boundary and limits

Search YouTube, Reddit, LinkedIn, and X for exact source names, distinctive
findings, named cases, and consumer-facing consequences within the stated
window. Record platform gaps rather than filling them with unrelated advice or
news. Public discussion can show lived experience, interpretation, or policy
reaction; it cannot by itself prove recovery, safety correction, agency
effectiveness, or population-wide consumer welfare.

This is public-web discovery, not a platform-native Creator/API export. Search
can omit comments, deleted posts, private groups, and non-indexed content.
The batch is not completion of the consumer-recourse theme or the broader
14-theme atlas.

## Current findings

Nine selected rows are augmented:

- Bilt transition, BrightSpeed refund distribution, CFPB complaint routes,
  Navient compensation, cross-source consumer recovery, consumer-visibility,
  CPSC surveillance and recall progress, and Federal Reserve household fraud
  recovery.
- The CPSC surveillance row adds a public evidence-preservation concern: some
  recall instructions can require destruction or disposal before a consumer
  understands how proof and compensation will work.
- The fraud row adds the largest-scale loss/recovery tension: aggregate
  recovered dollars can coexist with unknown victim-level recovery, timing, and
  administrative burden.

One selected row is a documented access gap after bounded searches:

- `us-cfpb-ombudsman-redress-process-friction-2025`

The CPSC surveillance row has usable LinkedIn discussion and is augmented; its
remaining platform gaps are retained in the queue rather than treated as
missing evidence.

Two adjacent CFPB complaint-data rows also received directly relevant
discussion during the search: `us-cfpb-narrative-filter-api-boundary-2026` and
`us-cfpb-product-response-routes-2024-2025`. They are recorded as supplemental
links, not silently counted as selected batch rows.

The main learning is that visibility, institutional response, redress
eligibility, payment issuance, and actual recovery remain separate stages. A
high response rate or a named refund program does not prove that the affected
person is restored.

## Next test

Compare any usable discussions with the atlas stage boundary. The highest-value
finding will be a case where visible routing exists but recovery, correction,
or exit remains uncertain, or a counterexample where a named remedy clearly
reaches the affected person.

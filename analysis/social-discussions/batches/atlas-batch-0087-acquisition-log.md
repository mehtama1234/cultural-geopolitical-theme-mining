## Research question

How do issuer scale, geographic availability, credit-tier pricing, promotional
terms, fees, and rewards become practically meaningful to borrowers? Keep the
offer surface separate from approval, assigned APR, balance carrying, payment,
remedy, and household outcome.

## Retained public discussion

- Reddit: a borrower discussion interprets a 24.99% approved APR through credit
  history, balance-carrying, rewards, and paying in full.
- LinkedIn: a financial-education relay distinguishes 0% introductory APR
  from deferred-interest promotional financing and emphasizes disclosure.

YouTube and X searches were completed as documented access gaps. Public social
discussion is contextual evidence, not a representative borrower panel.

## Next test

APR, balance, payment, fee, complaint or remedy, and later switching or exit.

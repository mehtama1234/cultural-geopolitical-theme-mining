# Social discussion wave 002 queue

This is the first execution queue after the three-topic calibration wave. It
adds public discussion to four existing atlas areas selected because the
current atlas identifies thin or open middle stages there. It is a queue for
acquisition, not a claim that the discussion has already been collected.

## Common acquisition rule

Use the same bounded window for all four topics: **2025-10-17 through
2026-10-17**. Search each topic on YouTube, Reddit, LinkedIn, and X with
platform-specific queries built from the research question and ordinary user
language. Retain up to six strong items per platform when available, with at
least one counter-signal. If a platform cannot be sampled or produces only
secondary summaries, record the gap instead of silently substituting another
source.

Every retained item must link to an existing research record, source-led map,
or open arrow and must say whether it illustrates, challenges, reframes, or
leaves the research finding open.

## Queue

### 1. Consumer power and recourse

- **Atlas entry:** `analysis/US-BROAD-CONSUMER-SOURCE-LED-SUBTHEME-MAP_V1.md`
- **Research question:** When people face a fee, error, denial, complaint, or
  unsafe product, do they describe a usable remedy, a costly escalation, or
  practical inability to switch?
- **Search language:** billing error, customer service loop, chargeback,
  complaint, refund, appeal, account lock, data correction, cancel, switch.
- **Open arrow:** formal complaint or remedy -> received remedy -> trust,
  continued use, or practical exit.
- **Required counter-signal:** examples where a complaint was resolved or a
  competing provider was genuinely usable.

### 2. Time, care, and social reproduction

- **Atlas entry:** `analysis/US-BROAD-TIME-HIDDEN-PRICE-SOURCE-LED-SUBTHEME-MAP_V1.md`
  and `analysis/US-BROAD-CARE-SOURCE-LED-SUBTHEME-MAP_V1.md`
- **Research question:** How do people describe waiting, unpaid care,
  transport, scheduling, paperwork, and the trade between paid work and care?
- **Search language:** caregiver schedule, waiting room, medical appointment
  time, childcare work, eldercare, unpaid care, commuting, paperwork burden.
- **Open arrow:** time or care burden -> adaptation or work change -> health,
  income, social participation, or household security.
- **Required counter-signal:** accounts of formal support, shared care, or a
  service that actually returned time.

### 3. Public systems and policy feedback

- **Atlas entry:** `analysis/US-BROAD-PUBLIC-SYSTEMS-SOURCE-LED-SUBTHEME-MAP_V1.md`
- **Research question:** Do people experience benefits, eligibility rules,
  enforcement, and public services as protection, administrative friction, or
  a source of distrust and political judgment?
- **Search language:** benefits application, recertification, denied claim,
  SNAP, Medicaid, housing voucher, disaster aid, public service paperwork,
  eligibility cliff.
- **Open arrow:** rule or eligibility -> take-up or interruption -> material
  result -> interpretation, trust, or collective demand.
- **Required counter-signal:** cases where navigation, an agency, or a local
  organization made the route usable.

### 4. Trust, identity, and cultural meaning

- **Atlas entry:** `analysis/US-BROAD-CULTURAL-POLITICAL-SOURCE-LED-SUBTHEME-MAP_V1.md`
- **Research question:** How do people turn material or institutional
  experiences into belonging, humiliation, blame, fairness, identity, or
  political action?
- **Search language:** dignity, respect, humiliation, belonging, fairness,
  institutional trust, community identity, who gets heard, organizing,
  political action.
- **Open arrow:** lived condition or institutional encounter -> meaning or
  identity -> trust, blame, organization, or action.
- **Required counter-signal:** accounts that reject the proposed meaning or
  describe trust, cooperation, or non-political interpretation.

## Wave completion gate

Do not publish a wave finding until the acquisition log records the query,
date, platform result, and access boundary; the normalized records pass the
social validator; each topic has a research bridge and counterexample; and the
finding states what the discussion cannot establish about prevalence,
causality, or population change.

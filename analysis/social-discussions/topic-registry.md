## Research-to-discussion bridge

The bridge must keep these units separate: research respondent, worker,
household, platform user, creator, post, video, comment, and audience reaction.
A public discussion item can illustrate or challenge a mechanism; it does not
turn unlike units into one longitudinal observation.

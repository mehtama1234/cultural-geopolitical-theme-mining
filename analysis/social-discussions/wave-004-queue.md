# Social discussion wave 004 — second rotation

**Window:** 2025-10-17 through 2026-10-17  
**Retrieved:** 2026-10-17  
**Scope:** one new public discussion trace for each of the 14 existing social
topics, with a deliberately mixed set of lived examples, counter-signals,
organizational claims, and policy discussion.

This is an execution wave, not a claim that the 14 topics are socially
complete. Search ranking, public indexing, moderation, deletion, language,
login barriers, and platform-specific visibility remain material limits.

## What was added

The wave adds 14 normalized records in `records/wave-004-*.json`. It widens
the corpus from 60 to 74 records and gives every topic a second dated pass.
The records are linked to existing atlas maps or research projects; they are
not treated as survey observations or as evidence of population prevalence.

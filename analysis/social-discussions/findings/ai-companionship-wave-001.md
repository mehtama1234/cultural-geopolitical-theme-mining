# AI companionship and trust: first discussion wave

The first sample contains three competing frames:

- **Support:** users describe availability, non-judgment, emotional rehearsal,
  and continuity as valuable when human support is unavailable.
- **Substitution and control:** research relays and user discussions raise
  disclosure, dependency, memory, product changes, and exit rights.
- **Governance:** LinkedIn and X discussions reframe companion use as an issue
  of age limits, privacy, disclosure, and institutional responsibility.

This is a strong discussion complement because the public material supplies
meaning, lived trade-offs, and control concerns that ordinary usage statistics
cannot show. It still cannot establish therapeutic benefit, harm, prevalence,
or durable replacement of human relationships.

# AI and work control: first discussion wave

**Status:** pilot, not a prevalence estimate  
**Window:** 2025-10-17 through 2026-10-17  
**Items:** seven public items: two YouTube, two Reddit, two LinkedIn, and one
X platform-summary item.

## What the discussion adds

The first sample shows three different public frames rather than one public
opinion:

1. **Career-survival advice.** YouTube items frame AI as a reason to choose
   durable skills, personal visibility, or a new career path. This makes
   perceived alternatives visible, but it is advice and speculation, not an
   employment estimate.
2. **Threat and disagreement.** The Reddit discussion contains disagreement
   between a historical “technology creates replacement work” frame and
   accounts of real occupational hollowing-out. The disagreement is useful for
   studying trust and perceived room to maneuver; it does not resolve the labor
   market question.
3. **Research relay with subgroup boundaries.** LinkedIn posts relay research
   that distinguishes productivity from employment, younger workers from older
   workers, and complement use from automation use. The public relay preserves
   more nuance than a simple “AI will take jobs” frame, but it remains a
   selected professional audience.

## Research bridge

The discussion items connect to the atlas question: **does AI change practical
worker alternatives and control, or only the measured speed of a task?** The
public material adds perceived threat, career adaptation, and interpretation of
subgroup differences. It does not supply a same-worker panel linking AI use to
hours, earnings, autonomy, wellbeing, or later exit.

## What is still missing

- a directly inspectable X post-and-reply sample rather than an X-generated
  trend summary;
- comments and audience reactions sampled with a declared rule rather than
  search snippets;
- US-specific worker/lived-experience material separated from global career
  advice;
- comparison with the research records already in `analysis/projects/ai-work-control/`;
- the same four-platform pass for platform exit and AI companionship/trust.

The current result is therefore a validated acquisition start, not a finished
cross-platform finding.

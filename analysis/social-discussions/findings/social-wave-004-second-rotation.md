# Social wave 004 — second rotation readout

Window: 2025-10-17 through 2026-10-17. Retrieved 2026-10-17. Fourteen new
public discussion traces were normalized, one for each existing social topic.

## What the wave adds

Across the second pass, public discussion repeatedly describes a conversion
chain rather than a single topic: a price, rule, platform, workplace system,
care duty, or geopolitical shock becomes a time cost, a loss of room, a
switching problem, a meaning claim, or a demand for collective action. Several
items also show the reverse direction: a workaround, successful switch,
supportive employer, organizing route, or substitute platform can return some
room.

The AI-companionship and platform-exit items make substitution especially
visible. The AI-work and infrastructure items connect technical deployment to
control, bargaining, power, and supply constraints. The household, housing,
care, and public-system items show how formal systems become lived through
arrears, paperwork, waiting, care coordination, and tenure choices.

## What it does not prove

This is a discovery and interpretation layer, not a population sample. The
records do not establish prevalence, causal effects, legal liability, or the
truth of claims repeated by creators and commenters. Reddit is overrepresented
in this execution batch because it returned accessible, date-bounded indexed
threads; this is itself a platform-composition result, not a reason to infer
that Reddit is more representative.

## Next test

The next wave must deliberately pair each of these 14 records with a second
platform or a counter-signal, then compare the repeated mechanism with the
corresponding research record. A dedicated YouTube, LinkedIn, and X pass is
still required, and the independent-trend queue should only be updated after
those cross-platform comparisons are logged.

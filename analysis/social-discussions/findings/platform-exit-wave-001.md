# Platform attention and exit: first discussion wave

The first sample makes exit look selective and reversible. Research relays and
personal accounts describe people cutting back, deleting apps, or leaving some
platforms while retaining LinkedIn, Reddit, YouTube, or professional ties.
Threads also show that “quitting social media” is contested: users disagree
about whether particular platforms count and whether substitution is success.

The discussion complements the atlas's non-use and collective-exit research by
making the mechanism visible: status, work, information, and relationships can
make non-use costly. It does not estimate how many people exit or whether a
break improves wellbeing.

X did not produce a directly inspectable, current user-discussion sample for
this topic in the first pass; that gap remains explicit.

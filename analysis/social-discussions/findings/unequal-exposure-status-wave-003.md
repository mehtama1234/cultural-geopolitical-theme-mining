# Unequal exposure and status — social wave 003

Window: 2025-10-17 through 2026-10-17

Records: four public items: one LinkedIn post, one Reddit thread, one YouTube
access explainer, and one X trending summary.

## What the discussion adds

The items repeatedly separate formal access from usable access. The LinkedIn
post describes an event that asked about access but still left a deaf attendee
to fight for microphones, hearing-loop support, and captionable speech. The
Reddit thread shows a worker trying to convert a disability and safety need
into a workplace accommodation while management and HR remain hard to reach.
The YouTube item moves the same distinction into healthcare: a service can
exist while equipment, communication, or provider assumptions make it unusable.
The X summary adds a counter-signal: inclusion and employment success stories
can coexist with concerns about wages, safety, individual variation, and
hostility.

The bounded interpretation is that unequal exposure is often produced at the
interface between a person and an institution: request, recognition, response,
correction, and durable participation. These items do not establish prevalence,
causality, or later recovery.

## Bridge to the atlas

The social items attach to the open route:

status or functional difference
  -> exposure, institutional response, accommodation, or reporting burden
  -> usable access, work/care participation, safety, dignity, or recovery

They add lived language about extra labor, legibility, being believed, and the
gap between a rule and an encounter. They do not replace the atlas's survey,
administrative, court, health, labor, or quasi-experimental evidence.

## Counter-signal and next test

Some discussion shows institutions and peers offering practical support, and
some employment stories emphasize capability and inclusion. The next test
should compare these claims with accommodation requests and receipt, facility
and equipment availability, complaint/appeal stages, work retention, care
continuity, and subgroup differences. The X item is retained as a non-US
platform summary, not silently generalized to the United States.

# Local economic participation: money can enter a place without local control

**Checked:** 2026-09-22  
**Status:** bounded cross-source synthesis; not a pooled estimate

## The subtheme in simple words

Money, contracts, credit, investment, jobs, and visitor spending can all be
visible in a place without showing whether local people and local firms got a
usable share of the opportunity.

The key distinction is:

```text
money or capacity enters a place
  -> local firm or resident can reach the opportunity
  -> terms, payment, ownership, and control
  -> work, income, survival, service, and household effect
  -> durable local security or changed power
```

The atlas currently has enough aggregate evidence to state this as a recurring
subtheme. It does not yet have a common local-participation rate, and the
sources below do not describe the same places or firms.

## Four different routes

### 1. Credit route: the firm must first pass a gate

The [Federal Reserve small-business credit finding](projects/us-fed-small-business-credit-route/findings/us-fed-small-business-credit-route-001.md)
shows that lender choice and reported firm risk shape the path from application
to partial or full approval. The survey does not show funding use, repayment,
survival, jobs, or owner-household effects.

### 2. Recovery-contract route: public money may not be locally tracked

The [GAO local-vendor finding](projects/us-gao-disaster-local-vendor-use/findings/us-gao-disaster-local-vendor-use-001.md)
shows that FEMA and the Army Corps had local-vendor preferences, but records
and monitoring were weak. In the selected FEMA records, about 3% of $2.9
billion in obligations was identified as local-vendor work. That is not a
national rate and does not show local jobs, payment, business survival, or
repair.

### 3. Investment route: projects can be counted before community change

The [Opportunity Zone finding](projects/us-opportunity-zones-community-outcomes/findings/us-opportunity-zones-community-outcomes-001.md)
shows investment and completed projects without strong tracking of jobs,
housing distribution, displacement, or durable community outcomes. Capital
entry is not the same as shared local improvement.

### 4. Place-economy route: a region can specialize without equal access

The [outdoor-recreation finding](projects/us-bea-outdoor-recreation-economy/findings/us-bea-outdoor-recreation-economy-001.md)
shows a large and geographically uneven recreation economy. Value added and
employment identify economic activity, not whether residents can afford,
reach, own, or benefit from the cultural resource.

## What the sources jointly support

They support a careful interpretation:

> Local economic participation is a conversion problem. Investment, credit,
> contracts, and regional production are inputs or intermediate stages; local
> receipt, workable terms, control, durability, and shared security are later
> stages that must be measured separately.

This is not a claim that outside firms, visitors, lenders, or investors are
necessarily harmful. They may provide the only fast capacity, capital, or
service available. The missing data matter because without them we cannot tell
necessary outside capacity from missed local opportunity, unequal terms, or
benefits captured elsewhere.

## What remains open

- Which named local firms or residents applied, qualified, received, and used
  the opportunity?
- What were the price, credit, contract, wage, ownership, and payment terms?
- Did the money produce jobs, survival, repair, affordable service, or household
  security?
- Who could refuse, switch, bargain, or leave?
- Did the change alter local trust, belonging, political action, or power?

## Stopping rule

Do not add another national investment, value-added, award, or application
total to this subtheme unless it adds a new unit or later stage. Reopen the
lane for a firm-, resident-, contract-, property-, or place-linked source that
shows receipt, terms, use, distribution, durability, exit, or local power.

Until then, the safe conclusion is that economic presence and local control run
on different clocks.

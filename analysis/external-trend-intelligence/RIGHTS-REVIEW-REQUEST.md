# Rights review request

This is the human handoff for clearing source-family rights and publisher
dependence. It is not a license grant. Until a row is signed, the family stays
blocked for public redistribution, trend promotion, and company use.

## Required review for every family

For each family, record the exact terms or license URL, upstream dataset and
publisher identity, permitted storage, transformation and redistribution
scope, retention/deletion obligations, and any shared-host or restatement
dependence. Keep the source-specific evidence artifact or locator with the
decision.

## Release rule

Do not mark a family `review_ready` from a URL, a source count, or a local
terms file alone. A rights owner must confirm the exact artifact and scope.
Publisher dependence must be reviewed separately from permission. If either is
uncertain, retain the hold and record the next evidence request.

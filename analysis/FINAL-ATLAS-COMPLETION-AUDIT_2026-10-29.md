## Final bounded thesis

> Systems increasingly earn legitimacy at the point where a dependent person,
> household, community, institution or partner can use, contest, correct,
> substitute, sustain and attribute them—not at the point where capacity is
> announced.

The recurring cross-domain mechanism is:

```text
formal capacity
  -> situated encounter
  -> hidden effort or burden
  -> correction, substitution, permission or response
  -> material protection or exposure
  -> attribution and cultural meaning
  -> trust, cooperation, refusal, switching, exit or leverage
```

The evidence does not support a single trend rate, universal legitimacy score or
causal master trend. It supports a portfolio of bounded mechanisms:

- formal remedy is not durable recovery;
- administrative correction is not usable protection;
- public-AI readiness is not claimant remedy;
- infrastructure governance is not realized household incidence;
- local non-consent may redistribute burden rather than stop capability; and
- persistent alliance response is not deterrence without attributable external
  behavior change.

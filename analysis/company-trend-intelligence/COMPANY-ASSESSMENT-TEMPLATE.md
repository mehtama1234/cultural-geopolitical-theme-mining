## Decision pathway

For each material exposure, document:

```text
trend signal
  -> company exposure
  -> affected customer, worker, supplier, community, or institution
  -> cost, risk, opportunity, or loss of alternatives
  -> available response and reversibility
  -> leading indicator and trigger
  -> decision, owner, budget, and review date
```

### Customer and market

- Which customer segments are exposed first?
- What changes in affordability, demand, trust, access, or switching?
- Which customer outcome should be measured rather than inferred?

### Workforce and organization

- Which tasks, skills, schedules, controls, or bargaining relationships change?
- Who receives training, voice, protection, or the gain?
- What measures distinguish efficiency from higher intensity or worse job quality?

### Product and service

- What feature, language, accessibility, reliability, or remedy requirement
  changes?
- What user task must be completed to call the intervention successful?
- What is the rollback or correction route?

### Finance and operations

- What changes in price, margin, credit, insurance, capital, supplier, energy,
  data, or recovery exposure?
- Who pays when the system fails or the forecast is wrong?
- What redundancy or practical alternative exists?

### Institutions, reputation, and geopolitics

- Which permits, standards, regulators, communities, or public institutions
  determine the company’s operating room?
- Which identities, values, or local authorities affect legitimacy?
- Which sanctions, alliances, dependencies, or state actions can alter supply,
  data, infrastructure, or market access?

# Company trend-intelligence theme coverage

This matrix is the scope control for the company-facing layer. It prevents the
seven current records from being mistaken for complete coverage of the
fourteen-theme research programme.

`Deep` means the current portfolio has a named record with direct evidence and
a business translation. `Lens` means the theme is materially present in one or
more records but does not yet have its own decision-grade record. `Open` means
the next company-facing record should establish the evidence and exposure.

## Current coverage decision

The portfolio has seven deep themes and seven lens-level themes. The health,
disability, aging, and care theme now has a qualified record, while the
remaining lens-level themes should not be treated as absent; they
are present as mechanisms or affected-group questions, but they need a named
decision, direct evidence, and a next test before promotion.

The next record queue should therefore prioritize:

1. time, care, and social reproduction as a company operating constraint;
2. firm, market, ownership, and concentration power; and
3. deeper company-specific access, accommodation, and care outcome tests.

## Promotion rule

A theme moves from `Lens` or `Open` to `Deep` only when its record has:

- a direct or measured observation;
- a mechanism and affected-group distribution;
- counterevidence or a competing explanation;
- a named company exposure and decision owner;
- now / next / longer-term horizons;
- leading indicators with cadence and trigger; and
- a smallest next test that could change the conclusion.

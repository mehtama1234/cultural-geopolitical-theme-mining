# Company trend-intelligence index

This is the working entry point for the company-facing layer of the cultural,
social, and geopolitical research atlas.

For the cross-domain interpretation, read the [portfolio view](PORTFOLIO.md).

For a browser-based reader, open the [company trend board](board.html) from a
local HTTP server.

For management use, read the [decision playbook](DECISION-PLAYBOOK.md).

For an executive one-page view, read the [executive readout](EXECUTIVE-READOUT.md).

## What this layer does

It turns source-backed research into decision-relevant trend records. A record
must show the evidence, mechanism, distribution, uncertainty, company exposure,
leading indicators, and next test. It does not turn a weak signal into a
forecast or a business recommendation without showing the missing link.

## Next record queue

The next records should cover different parts of the 14-theme program and
different company decisions:

1. **Infrastructure capacity and local incidence:** whether announced energy,
   data, transport, or broadband capacity becomes reliable service and how
   costs and dependency are distributed.
2. **Cultural and language technology:** whether digitization and AI create
   real access, repeated use, community control, and market-localization value.
3. **Geopolitical dependence:** how supply, technology, sanctions, or alliance
   changes affect operating continuity, strategic options, and state leverage.

Each new record should use the same schema and should be promoted only after a
counter-signal and an independent evidence check.

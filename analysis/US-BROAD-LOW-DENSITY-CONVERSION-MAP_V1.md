# Low-density theme conversion map v1

**Checked:** 2026-09-21  
**Purpose:** identify the next missing lived or operating conversion in the
three least-dense areas of the 14-theme atlas.  
**Status:** cross-theme planning artifact; not a pooled estimate.

The three low-density themes are different in substance, but they share a
structural problem: formal capacity is easier to observe than what happens
when a person, community, or another state actually depends on it.

| Theme | What is already visible | Missing conversion | Minimum next evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Platforms, data, and attention | Use, advice, social meaning, privacy requests, safety controls, complaint routes, attempted goodbye, and some remedy rules; Google now adds request-status notification and optional ongoing filtering for similar explicit results | User action → platform response → recurrence, durable wellbeing/control, switching, or exit | Same account/user episode with action date, response, repeat exposure, human or product alternative, continued use reason, trust, and verified exit or non-use; for search removal, add actual de-indexing and recurrence |
| Infrastructure, technology, and dependency | Construction, revenue, tariffs, cost allocation, grid operation, data-center growth, procurement, and modeled savings | Named project/facility operation → experienced service or burden → replaceability and local acceptance | Identifier-bearing project or account, metered/operating output, actual payment or service change, affected place, contestation/compensation, alternative, and later durability |
| Geopolitical and state consequences | Contracts, deliveries, acceptance, exercises, readiness, alliance posture, local-security integration, public judgments, and incident response | Accepted capability or state action → changed external behavior, sustained cooperation, public consent, or leverage | Dated event-country-party ledger with capability/operation, attributed response, refusal/concession/substitution/deterrence, maintenance or reversal, and before/after public judgment where relevant |

## Shared conversion rule

```text
formal capacity or right
  -> dependent actor encounters the system
  -> actual use, cost, burden, or risk
  -> alternative, contestation, or refusal
  -> durable result or failure
  -> meaning, trust, switching, exit, cooperation, or leverage
```

The arrow means different things in each lane:

- For a platform, “encounter” may be a user attempting deletion, correction,
  safety reporting, or a goodbye.
- For infrastructure, it may be a household receiving a bill, a facility
  drawing power, a resident facing land or service effects, or a utility
  operating the asset.
- For geopolitics, it may be an ally, adversary, foreign public, or domestic
  constituency responding to an accepted capability or state action.

The common rule is therefore methodological, not substantive: do not call a
system powerful merely because it exists. Identify who encounters it, what
changes for that actor, what alternatives exist, and whether the result lasts.

## Current evidence anchors

### Platforms

The [AI-companion exit-boundary audit](projects/us-digital-habits-attention/ai-companion-exit-boundary-audit-v1.md)
observes continuation tactics after farewell signals but not account deletion,
replacement, or durable wellbeing. The [GM/OnStar data-control record](records/us-ftc-gm-onstar-data-control-order-2026.json)
adds formal access, deletion, and opt-out rights without proving account-level
use or verified removal. GM's implementation statement adds an exit boundary:
model-year controls vary, valued services may be affected, and safety or
battery exceptions can leave limited collection possible after unenrollment.
These are complementary control stages, not one user outcome.

### Infrastructure

The [infrastructure-bargain synthesis](US-BROAD-INFRASTRUCTURE-BARGAIN-SYNTHESIS_V1.md)
shows that capacity, cost allocation, voice, and public value move on different
clocks. The Virginia large-load route is the most useful governance comparator,
while the [PJM large-load operating record](records/us-pjm-large-load-disconnection-response-2026.json)
adds a real operating disruption and technical response. Neither route closes
facility-level causation, household incidence, service quality, replaceability,
or resident acceptance.

The [Champlain Hudson Power Express operating record](records/us-nyserda-champlain-hudson-power-express-operation-2026.json)
adds a cross-border operating stage: Quebec-to-New-York electricity delivery is
reported after commercial operation begins. The [NYPA Smart Path Connect record](records/us-nypa-smart-path-connect-realization-2026.json)
adds a domestic realization-and-allocation stage: a named project is energized
and a formal facilities-charge route exists, while retail pass-through,
household bills, reliability, and host acceptance remain open. Neither route
identifies a household bill, durable local benefit, or external leverage.

### Geopolitics

The [capability-legitimacy clock synthesis](projects/ai-work-control/capability-legitimacy-clock-synthesis-v1.md)
keeps procurement, realization, domestic meaning, external reception,
cooperation, and leverage separate. The Polish F-35 ledger reaches arrival,
acceptance, training, and aircraft-count stages; readiness, sustainment,
mission use, deterrence, and changed external behavior remain open.

## Promotion rule

Promote a new low-density finding only when it adds at least one of the missing
conversion fields above. A new use-rate, announcement, contract, approval,
poll, or modeled benefit belongs in the coverage map unless it also identifies
the dependent actor and a later observed consequence. If the actor or later
consequence is unavailable, record the boundary and rotate to another lane.

This map complements the [lane maturity and next action](US-BROAD-LANE-MATURITY-AND-NEXT-ACTION_V1.md)
and the [strongest end-to-end chains](US-BROAD-STRONGEST-END-TO-END-CHAINS_V1.md).

## Current rotation decision

The next pass stays broad but changes the unit of proof in each lane:

1. **Platform:** follow a real control attempt—deletion, correction, safety
   report, or refusal—through the platform response, any repeat exposure, and
   continued use, switching, or non-use.
2. **Infrastructure:** follow one named project, facility, or account through
   operating output, payment or service incidence, local burden or benefit,
   and later reliability, replaceability, or acceptance.
3. **Geopolitics:** follow one accepted capability or state action through an
   attributed response by an ally, adversary, partner, or domestic constituency
   and then through cooperation, refusal, reversal, or leverage.

The current local evidence does not yet qualify any of these as a complete
same-unit episode. The public records remain useful because they show where the
conversion boundary is; they should not be promoted into stronger causal or
end-to-end claims. The next acquisition priority is therefore authorized
respondent-level data, especially the UAS route, while the public rotation
continues to build bounded operating and response comparators.

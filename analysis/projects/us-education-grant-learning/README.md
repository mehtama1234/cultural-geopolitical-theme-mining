# Project: emergency education funding and institutional learning

## Question

When a large emergency grant program ends, does the oversight system retain
reliable data, consistent guidance, and lessons for the next crisis?

## Decision rule

Keep funding, compliance, data quality, technical assistance, documented
learning, and student or school outcomes separate. A rule intended to protect
high-poverty schools is not the same as proof that the right schools were
protected or that the lesson will persist.

## Current bounded finding

The [GAO finding](findings/us-gao-k12-moequity-grant-learning-2026-001.md)
shows that Education oversaw Maintenance of Equity requirements during COVID
relief implementation, but incomplete data, undocumented technical-assistance
procedures, and missing lesson-sharing processes limited what the agency could
verify and carry forward.

The [school-finance macro-output record](../../records/us-nber-school-finance-reforms-macro-output-2026.json)
adds the long-run conversion test: state school-finance reforms predict little
change for 10–15 years, then earnings per worker rise about 4.7% above the
counterfactual after 30 years, with GDP per capita more than 5% higher. These
are aggregate results; the next test is distribution across students, places,
workers, household resources, and school quality.

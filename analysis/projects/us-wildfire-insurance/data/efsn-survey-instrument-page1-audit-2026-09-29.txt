The publicly accessible first page of the English questionnaire is titled
“L.A. Fire Insurance Survey - English Language Edition.” It presents three
required fields:

1. Address of the affected home.
2. January 2025 fire event: Eaton Fire or Palisades Fire.
3. Tenure: renter/tenant or owner by self or with family members.

The invitation page states approximately 15 minutes and one response per
household, but the accessible instrument page does not expose a probability
sample, recruitment denominator, response count, weighting plan, or results.
The first page collects an exact address, which is consistent with the separate
data-handling policy's validation and recoding provisions; it does not authorize
the atlas to submit a response or obtain raw records. No survey was submitted.

Boundary: this is instrument and access evidence, not respondent data,
representativeness, claim adequacy, payment timing, recovery, or household
mobility.

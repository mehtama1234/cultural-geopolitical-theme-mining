# California wildfire insurance payment and complaint-recovery recheck

**Checked:** 2026-10-20  
**Status:** aggregate claims payment and complaint intervention are reported; household recovery remains open

## What changed

California’s Department of Insurance wildfire tracker reports 41,727 claims
filed for the Eaton and Palisades fires, 40,482 claims partially paid, and
$25.2 billion paid to date. The Department’s current market update separately
reports more than $338 million recovered through direct intervention in
consumer complaints, including $52 million for smoke-damage claims.

```text
wildfire damage and displacement
  -> insurance claim and public complaint route
  -> aggregate claims partially paid / complaint recovery reported
  -> [open] named-household payment receipt and adequacy
  -> [open] lender release, repair, occupancy, and safe return
  -> [open] durable insurance access, trust, action, and exit
```

This closes an aggregate payment and public-recourse stage. It does not turn
claims paid into completed recovery: the tracker mixes home, business,
living-expense, auto, and other claims, and neither source publishes a
household panel linking payment amount and timing to lender release, rebuild,
occupancy, or return.

## Next decisive evidence

Seek a lawful aggregate of unresolved or closed claims, complaint outcomes,
payment timing and adequacy, lender-held proceeds, rebuild completion, or
occupancy. Preserve the distinction between insurer payment, complaint
intervention, and a household’s actual return.

partially paid, dollars paid, complaint-intervention recovery, and regulatory
response. They do not support individual receipt, adequacy, lender release,
rebuild completion, occupancy, stable housing, trust, or exit.

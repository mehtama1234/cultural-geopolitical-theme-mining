# Project: Security-cooperation workforce and durable partner capacity

## Question

Does the U.S. workforce that manages security cooperation have the people,
skills, information, and continuity needed to sustain partner commitments?

## Decision rule

Keep legal provisions, position counts, filled staffing, certification,
training, monitoring, equipment, partner capability, and later strategic effect
separate. A workforce reform is not the same as a working partner capability.

## Current bounded finding

The [GAO workforce finding](findings/us-gao-security-cooperation-workforce-2026-001.md)
shows that DOD had completed many formal workforce provisions while still
planning work through FY2028 and reporting staffing and training-capacity gaps.

The route is:

```text
U.S. security objective
  -> workforce structure and training
  -> planning, transfer, monitoring, and partner contact
  -> [open] partner capability and sustained use
  -> [open] trust, alliance behavior, and strategic effect
```

The public report does not follow a named workforce or partner from staffing
through an operational result.

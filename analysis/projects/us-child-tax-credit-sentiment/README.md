# Project: US Child Tax Credit and economic sentiment

## Question

Can a change in public help alter how a family sees its own finances and the country's economy, beyond the dollars received?

## Short first pass

Separate benefit amount, benefit loss, current income, financial stress, personal economic feeling, national economic feeling, political attitude, and voting.

## Possible connection

Public policy can change the story people tell about their economic position, not only their bank balance. That feeling may shape later support for programs or candidates.

## Decision rule

Move on after one policy exposure measure, one household sentiment measure, and one observed action. Do not treat a change in sentiment as proof of a changed vote or lasting financial gain.

# Project: US family support as a hidden safety net

## Question

When a household is hit by a loss, care need or education bill, how much of the shock is moved to parents, adult children or unpaid family time?

## Short first pass

Use panel data on transfers after adverse events, then compare financial help with informal care and public support. Keep money received, debt held, work time and care time separate.

## Matched evidence pass

- [Family help can catch one fall by moving it to another household](../../findings/us-family-support-hidden-safety-net-matched-evidence-001.md)
- [Reader-friendly HTML](../../../site/us-family-support-hidden-safety-net-matched-evidence-001.html)

## Possible connection

Family support can protect a household from a missed payment or lost care. It can also move the burden to a parent or child who has less money, less time or a weaker work position.

## Decision rule

Move on after one source shows how support changes after a shock, one source shows the work or care cost, and one next dataset can follow both sides of the family. Do not call family help free or assume it reaches every household.

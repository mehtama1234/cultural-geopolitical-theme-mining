# Project: Religious community, volunteering, and trust

## Question

Can a strong community institution create volunteering and trust, and who gets
the same access to that social resource?

## Decision rule

Keep religious affiliation, participation, assigned service, volunteering,
trust, belonging, burden, exclusion, and political action separate. A high group
average is not proof of equal experience or a causal community effect.

## Current bounded finding

The [Pew Latter-day Saint finding](findings/us-pew-latter-day-saints-communal-trust-volunteering-2026-001.md)
reports high volunteering and generalized trust among current Latter-day Saint
respondents compared with all U.S. adults, while education and regional gaps
remain visible inside the group.

The route is:

```text
religious identity and local institution
  -> role, contact, and service opportunity
  -> volunteering and social trust
  -> [open] equal belonging, burden, political action, and durable support
```

The source adds a community-institution layer without proving that the
institution caused the reported outcomes.

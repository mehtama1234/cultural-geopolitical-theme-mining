# Project: Cultural AI, creator agency, and audience legitimacy

## Question

When AI enters cultural production and discovery, does it expand participation
and creator agency—or increase output while weakening attribution, entry routes,
trust, and local cultural meaning?

## Short end-to-end goal

Follow cultural production from tool access and creator practice through
disclosure, attribution, audience trust, income or participation, community
meaning, and durable cultural value.

```text
tool access and cultural practice
  -> creation, curation, or recommendation
  -> disclosure, attribution, representation, and audience verification
  -> creator livelihood, participation, belonging, or trust
  -> cultural value, local voice, and durable agency
```

This is a separate depth lane in the ongoing 14-theme program. It joins
cultural participation, consumer trust, platform power, AI governance, and
unequal access without treating content volume as cultural welfare.

## Current bridge

The [creator agency and audience legitimacy bridge](creator-agency-audience-legitimacy-bridge-v1.md)
compares New Zealand participation, UK creative-business adoption, Gartner
consumer trust, and UNESCO creator practice and safety evidence. It finds that
audience access, active creation, AI use, disclosure, and trust are separate
conversion stages.

## Next test

The next qualifying record is a creator or cultural organization episode with
tool use, workflow, attribution/disclosure, audience response, compensation or
participation, and a later trust, livelihood, or community outcome.

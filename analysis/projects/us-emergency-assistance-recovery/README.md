# Project: Emergency assistance and household recovery

## Question

When emergency cash, rent, utility, or food assistance reaches a household,
does it prevent the immediate crisis, create room for work and health recovery,
and remain protective after the payment ends?

## Short end-to-end goal

Follow one assistance episode from need and eligibility through receipt, rent,
food, utility, work, health, debt, housing stability, and later security.

```text
shock or shortfall
  -> application, selection, and assistance
  -> immediate payment or hardship relief
  -> work, health, food, housing, and household adaptation
  -> continuity, recovery, or renewed insecurity
  -> trust, political response, and program redesign
```

This is a separate depth lane in the ongoing 14-theme program. It keeps
assistance receipt, immediate relief, and durable recovery as different stages.

## Current bridge

The [assistance-to-recovery bridge](assistance-receipt-household-recovery-bridge-v1.md)
compares NBER emergency rent and financial-assistance studies, SIPP person-month
records, SHED household adaptation, and bounded public discussion. It finds
that assistance can improve an immediate constraint without guaranteeing
housing stability, food security, health, or durable financial room.

## Next test

The next qualifying record is a protected same-household episode with need,
application, receipt, amount, timing, landlord/provider response, next-period
security, work/health effects, and later renewal or recovery.

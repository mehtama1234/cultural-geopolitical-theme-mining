# Source search: federal-building accessibility complaint visibility

**Search date:** 2026-09-23  
**Geography:** United States federal office buildings  
**Status:** bounded source retained

## Working question

Can a formal accessibility complaint route provide practical accountability if
the people encountering barriers do not know how to use it?

| Source | What it contributes | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| [GAO-26-108575: Federal Real Property—GSA Should Publicize Accessibility Complaint Process for Its Office Buildings](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-26-108575) | Access Board complaint counts, six site visits, and stakeholder evidence about awareness of the Architectural Barriers Act complaint route | Does not estimate barrier prevalence or establish that a complaint was corrected or that later access improved |

The source is useful because it separates a formal enforcement route from the
person's ability to discover and use that route. The next acquisition should
preserve building, barrier, filing, response, correction, and repeat-access
fields rather than treating complaint volume as a prevalence rate.

# Project: family routines, enrichment, and school engagement

## Question

When families face material pressure, which parts of children's daily social
and cultural life remain steady, and which opportunities are more unequal?

## Bounded answer

The Census Bureau's 2023 SIPP child-well-being brief reports that reading and
family dinners were broadly stable from 2014 to 2023, while outings dropped
sharply during the pandemic and had not fully returned to the 2019 level by
2023. Organized activities were more stratified: only 1.2% of children in
families below the poverty line participated in sports, lessons, and clubs,
compared with 8.8% in families at 200% of poverty or higher.

This suggests that family routines and organized opportunity are different
social resources. It does not prove that poverty caused participation or school
engagement differences.

# Project: US trust in the Federal Reserve

## Question

When does an economic message become a test of who people think is on their side?

## Short first pass

Separate personal prices, party identity, perceived Fed alignment, trust, message receipt, inflation belief and later household or political action.

## Possible connection

An institution can give the same message to everyone while people hear different messengers. Trust may decide whether a household treats the message as useful information or as another political signal.

## Decision rule

Move on after one experiment measures trust and belief updates, one source measures actual household responses, and one next dataset can follow communication into spending, saving or public action. Do not call belief change behavior change.

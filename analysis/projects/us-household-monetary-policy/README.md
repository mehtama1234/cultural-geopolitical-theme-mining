# Project: US households and the meaning of an interest-rate change

## Question

When the Federal Reserve changes rates, what do households think will happen to their own spending, borrowing and savings?

## Short first pass

Start with the NBER survey of more than 25,000 US households. Keep the rate change, the household's belief about inflation and borrowing, and the actual spending response separate.

## Possible connection

Policy can work through what people think it will do, not only through the loan rate they face. If households read a rate increase as a sign of higher future prices, they may cut spending for a different reason than standard models assume.

## Decision rule

Check results by income, debt, home ownership and asset holdings. Move on unless the evidence shows which households hear the policy message, change behavior and bear the cost.

# Project: US remote work and the value of coming together

## Question

When fully remote workers meet in person once a month, does the gain come from the office day itself or from the communication and feedback that continue afterward?

## Short first pass

Use the NBER randomized trial of 248 customer-service workers as the starting point. Keep output, communication, manager feedback, retention, commute cost, housing choice and worker preference separate.

## Possible connection

A small amount of planned in-person contact may build relationships that improve remote work later. That could affect productivity and staying, while also shifting travel time and office cost back to workers or firms.

## Decision rule

Start with the trial's measured changes and one serious counterpoint from fully remote work. Move on unless the data show which workers benefit and who carries the time and travel cost.

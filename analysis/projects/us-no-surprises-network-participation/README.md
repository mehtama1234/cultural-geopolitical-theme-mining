# Project: No Surprises protections and provider networks

## Question

Did the No Surprises Act change provider participation in insurance networks,
and did that translate into lower or more predictable patient costs?

## Decision rule

Keep legal protection, network participation, negotiated payment, patient
billing, affordability, and care access separate. A change in claims or
payments is not automatically a patient-outcome or causal estimate.

## Current bounded finding

The [GAO finding](findings/us-gao-no-surprises-network-participation-2026-001.md)
shows that in-network claim shares rose after the law for three of four selected
specialties, while payment trends largely continued patterns that began before
the law.

The [protection-to-patient-incidence bridge](protection-network-premium-patient-incidence-bridge-v1.md)
connects that claims result to NBER evidence on arbitration, provider networks,
and premiums while preserving the still-open patient and household outcomes.

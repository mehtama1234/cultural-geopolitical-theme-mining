# Project: US bank customers and the cost of staying put

## Question

When people leave their money in the same bank as rates change, how much is convenience worth—and who receives the value of that inaction?

## Short first pass

Start with the NBER study of “sleepy deposits” and the HBS summary. Keep the depositor's lost interest, the bank's funding benefit, switching effort and financial stability separate.

## Possible connection

The same habit that costs a customer some interest can give a bank cheaper, steadier funding. That makes a private act of not switching part of a wider system: competition, bank survival and the household's cash buffer.

## Decision rule

Check the result by account type, balance, age, bank quality and rate cycle. Move on unless the evidence shows the customer's net gain from switching and the effect of wider switching on bank risk.

# Project: US rideshare price search

## Question

When two apps offer the same trip at different prices, why do many customers not check both?

## Short first pass

Separate price gap, time to compare, app opening, booking choice, convenience, service quality and the money left after the ride.

## Possible connection

A digital market can look competitive while customers still see only one price. The platform's place in the phone and the effort of checking another app can matter as much as the posted fare.

## Decision rule

Move on after one matched-trip price test, one observed search measure, and one result on customer cost or platform competition. Do not call a price gap a customer loss unless the person could reasonably switch.

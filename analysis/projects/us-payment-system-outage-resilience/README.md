# Project: US payment-system outage resilience

## Question

What can a household still buy when the payment network goes down?

## Short first pass

Separate the outage, payment failure, cash access, completed purchase, essential need and later recovery. Compare cash, cards, offline-capable tools and network restoration.

## Possible connection

Payment is part of basic infrastructure. A household may have money but still be unable to use it when electricity, communications or a merchant terminal fails.

## Decision rule

Move on after one source measures payment changes in outages, one source identifies essential spending or service access, and one next dataset can follow households through the disruption. Do not treat a shift to cash as a preference without checking availability.

# Long-term goal: Harness and RL environments

## Objective

Build a reproducible, evidence-backed account of how AI harnesses and
reinforcement-learning environments shape agent capability, reliability,
safety, cost, and adoption. Cover the six months from 2026-04-17 through
2026-10-17 first, then maintain the project as a continuing research stream.

## Questions

1. What does the harness control: context, memory, tools, permissions, state,
   verification, recovery, observability, or human intervention?
2. What does the environment control: observations, transitions, rewards,
   authority, failures, costs, and terminal conditions?
3. Are improvements due to the base model, policy learning, harness evolution,
   environment design, evaluator choice, or hidden benchmark assumptions?
4. Do gains transfer to held-out tasks and models without unacceptable safety,
   latency, cost, or governance regressions?
5. Do LinkedIn adoption narratives and shared papers match the primary evidence?

## Required coverage

- coding-agent and long-horizon harnesses;
- evaluation, benchmark, red-team, and safety environments;
- tool/API/MCP harnesses;
- robotics and simulation environments;
- RL training, online RL, and self-evolving agents;
- context, memory, trajectory, and observability systems;
- open-source and commercial platforms;
- the existing Harness Design Atlas and RL-environment survey;
- LinkedIn and shared-paper discussion captured through the ScrapeCreators
  pathway when credentials are available, with public-source fallbacks clearly
  labeled.

## Pipeline contract

Every candidate must have a stable ID, date, facet, canonical URL, source
type, method/environment description, evidence strength, limits, and adoption
signal. Social records go through the `research-signals` normalization,
deduplication, analysis, verification, connection, and publication stages.
Primary claims require source resolution. No social post is treated as proof.

## Deliverables

1. A dated six-month ledger and deduplicated inventory.
2. Ten-facet coverage with explicit gaps.
3. Deep evidence matrices for prioritized papers and artifacts.
4. A comparison with the existing Harness Design Atlas/RL survey.
5. Matched evaluation or rerun plans where artifacts permit them.
6. Cross-facet synthesis connecting technical design to social adoption,
   organizational authority, labor, safety, and public consequences.
7. A final completion audit that distinguishes verified, source-reported,
   unresolved, and inaccessible claims.

## Current state

The initial six-month package exists in the `research-signals` run folder and
has passed the existing social-analysis stage. It is not final: the dedicated
ScrapeCreators capture, repository checks, source resolution, and final
coverage reconciliation remain explicit work items in that package's status and
gaps files.

## Definition of done

The goal is complete only when every facet has a dated inventory, every
priority claim has a primary-source resolution or an explicit access gap, the
survey comparison is reviewed, social and paper records are deduplicated, the
pipeline validators pass, and the final report preserves counterevidence,
non-transfer results, costs, safety boundaries, and reproducibility limits.

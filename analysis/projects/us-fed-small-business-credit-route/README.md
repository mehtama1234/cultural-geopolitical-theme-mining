# US small-business credit route

This project examines how small employer firms move from wanting credit to
choosing a lender and receiving some or all of the requested amount. It treats
credit as a route shaped by lender type, firm risk, requested amount, and
provider terms—not as one uniform market.

The source is a Federal Reserve summary of the 2023 Small Business Credit
Survey. It describes applicant firms and lender outcomes. It does not show
later investment, employment, survival, household income, or community benefit.

# Project: US hometown business and the loss of local ownership

## Question

When people who might start a business leave home more often, what happens to local ownership, jobs, services and a place's sense of control?

## Short first pass

Start with the HBS account of Census research on where self-employed people stay or move. Keep birthplace, industry, capital needs, migration, business survival and local belonging separate.

## Possible connection

The decline of hometown entrepreneurship may mean that local business is less tied to local networks and more tied to mobile capital, skills and opportunity. A place can still have businesses while losing the people who once had a long stake in it.

## Decision rule

Check which industries and groups drove the change, and whether movers create stronger firms elsewhere or leave weaker local services behind. Move on unless the evidence shows what changed for residents, workers and customers.

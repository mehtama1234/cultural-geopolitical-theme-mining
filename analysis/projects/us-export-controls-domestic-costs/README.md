# Project: export controls and domestic firm spillovers

## Question

Can export controls achieve a targeted geopolitical restriction while imposing
costs on firms' other products, destinations, and workers?

## Decision rule

Keep national-security purpose, targeted exports, indirect exports, destinations,
payroll, jobs, prices, firm adaptation, and diplomatic outcomes separate. A
domestic firm cost is not by itself proof that the control failed strategically.

## Current bounded finding

The [Census CES finding](findings/us-census-export-controls-domestic-costs-firm-spillovers-2010-2024-001.md)
reports that the share of exports subject to controls doubled from 2010 to 2024;
the 2014 Russia restriction reduced targeted exports and also affected other
exports, destinations, and average payroll.

# Project: Regional hospital capacity and usable care

This project tracks the difference between hospital-system capacity and local
access. A Census CES working paper reports an era of declining hospital counts
alongside increasing hospital-industry employment and more employees per
establishment. It also reports important regional and rurality differences,
with rural places—especially in the South—facing greater access challenges.

The working theme is not simply “hospitals declined, so care worsened.”
Consolidation can bring specialization or efficiency, while a larger
establishment can still be farther away or offer a different mix of services.
The project keeps establishment count, employment, service availability,
travel, prices, quality, patient outcomes, and household care burden separate.

The decisive next artifact is a protected place-service-patient ledger joining
hospital opening or closure, service lines, staffing, travel time, insurance,
prices, emergency and routine care, quality, patient outcomes, and later place
change. The current working-paper abstract does not close that chain.

# Project: US older consumers are not one market

## Question

What do firms miss when they treat people over 65 as one customer group instead of separating health, mobility, income, independence and social influence?

## Short first pass

Start with the HBS account of Elie Ofek's work on older customers, then compare it with NBER evidence on spending and health risk. Keep market size, buying power, need, loyalty and vulnerability separate.

## Possible connection

An older customer can have money, need help, want independence, and influence a younger family member at the same time. A market that sees only age may either sell too little or sell the wrong kind of protection.

## Decision rule

Check differences by health, wealth, living arrangement, age and product. Move on unless the evidence shows whether better targeting improves access and dignity or mainly extracts more from a loyal customer.

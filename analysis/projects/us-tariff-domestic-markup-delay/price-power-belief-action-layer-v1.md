# Price, market power, belief, and action layer v1

**Checked:** 2026-09-12  
**Status:** cross-source firm/consumer/political synthesis; not a complete tariff-to-vote estimate

## Why this layer exists

A price change is simultaneously a household experience, a firm decision, a
trade or monetary-policy consequence, and a political story. The program needs
to keep those levels connected without collapsing them into “inflation” or
“consumer sentiment.”

```text
trade, monetary, fiscal, or firm decision
  -> import cost, financing cost, input cost, competition, or markup changes
  -> shelf price, wage, quality, availability, or payment burden changes
  -> households substitute, delay, borrow, save less, or work more
  -> people name a cause and judge the responsible actor
  -> firms, agencies, and voters respond; power and future dependence shift
```

The chain is a research map. The current sources establish different portions
of it, not the entire sequence for the same person, firm, or product.

## Source layers

| Layer | Unit | Current contribution | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tariff pass-through | Product/import exposure and consumer price | The 2025 tariff study estimates partial consumer-price pass-through, with a direct component and a delayed indirect component through imported inputs and higher domestic markups | Does not identify a household basket, job effect, firm entry/exit, or political response |
| SHED price adaptation | US adult respondent | Reports substitution, reduced use, saving cuts, borrowing, delayed purchases, extra work, and emergency capacity | Does not identify the exact price, firm, tariff, or later recovery for each response |
| Inflation-cause beliefs | US survey respondent | People name multiple causes and differ by party and news source; policy preferences do not always match the stated cause | Does not link the respondent’s actual basket, information path, and later vote or spending |
| Monetary-policy communication | US household respondent in randomized information studies | Messages about rates, inflation, or the Federal Reserve can change expectations and reported spending or portfolio choices | Expectations and reported choices are not administrative financial records, and borrower/saver exposure differs |
| Real wages and elections | County/election outcome | Local purchasing power provides a separate population-level route into election results | County exposure cannot reveal an individual’s attribution, source, or vote reason |
| Firm, trade, and infrastructure records | Firm, sector, product, country, or infrastructure project | Shows who controls supply, inputs, ownership, capacity, and replaceability | Does not show equal household experience or automatic geopolitical leverage |

## What the combined evidence suggests

### 1. Prices have a path, not one cause

The opening tariff evidence distinguishes direct import pass-through from
delayed effects through domestic inputs and markups. The monetary-policy
records distinguish the policy rate from the message households receive and
from their different balance-sheet exposures. The consumer layers distinguish
posted price from effective cost, including time, quality, credit, and
protection.

The safe societal claim is therefore not “policy X raised prices by Y” in the
abstract. It is: a policy or firm decision can travel through multiple cost and
competition channels, with different timing and incidence.

### 2. Households adapt, but adaptation is not welfare

SHED and related household layers show multiple ways of preserving immediate
functioning: buy cheaper, use less, delay, borrow, reduce saving, or add work.
Those responses can protect food, housing, health, or current consumption while
reducing future room, quality, time, or resilience. A stable headline purchase
can hide a transferred cost.

### 3. Political meaning is produced rather than read directly from the price

Inflation-belief and ANES layers show that people interpret economic conditions
through party identity, news source, prior trust, and beliefs about government,
firms, or the Federal Reserve. A person can identify a firm as the cause and
still demand government action; distrust a messenger and accept part of its
information; or experience a real loss but have too little alternative room to
change behavior.

### 4. Market power is a distributional question

The important firm question is not merely whether a price rose. It is who could
pass through a cost, delay a response, change a markup, substitute an input,
restrict access, or make the customer absorb uncertainty. The geopolitical
question is not merely whether a country depends on imports. It is who owns the
capacity, who can switch providers, and who bears the domestic adjustment.

## Keep the outcomes separate

| Outcome | Do not substitute it for |
|---|---|
| Tariff or rate change | Consumer price or household burden |
| Import price | Domestic input cost or domestic markup |
| Shelf price | Effective cost after quality, time, credit, and availability |
| Household adaptation | Improved welfare or resilience |
| Stated cause | Proven economic cause |
| Policy preference | Completed policy or political action |
| County election result | Individual vote motive |
| Firm margin or output | Household security or public legitimacy |
| Domestic capacity | Local control or geopolitical independence |

## Evidence status by arrow

| Arrow | Status | Current safe conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Policy/firm decision → direct and indirect price paths | Compared/reported | Tariff and monetary evidence support multiple channels and timing; firm records are needed for mechanism detail. |
| Price → household adaptation | Reported/compared | People substitute, reduce use, delay, borrow, save less, or work more under pressure. |
| Household adaptation → future room | Inferred/open | The direction is plausible and appears in separate credit, food, time, and savings layers, but is not one completed longitudinal estimate. |
| Price → cause belief | Open/partly measured | Beliefs and causes are measured separately from actual household exposure. |
| Cause belief/source/identity → policy or political action | Compared/open | Survey and county evidence provide distinct links; the same-person sequence remains open. |
| Firm/trade dependence → state leverage | Open | Capacity, ownership, replaceability, and alliance response must be observed before claiming geopolitical power. |

## Next bounded empirical comparison

Choose one tariff-exposed product group and one defined time window. Align:

1. tariff and import exposure;
2. importer and domestic input prices;
3. firm prices, quantities, margins, entry, exit, and sourcing;
4. a consumer basket and substitution or quality change;
5. household resources, debt, food/housing/health tradeoffs, and recovery;
6. cause belief, source environment, trust, policy preference, and political
   action where the same respondent is observed;
7. foreign retaliation, supplier switching, domestic capacity, and later policy
   response.

Use a less-exposed product or place as a counterexample. Keep borrowers and
savers, import-dependent and domestic firms, and high- and low-resource
households distinct. A valid individual or firm link requires a shared unit;
otherwise report a layered cross-source result.

## What would change the interpretation

The working picture would weaken if delayed domestic effects were absent across
exposed products; if household adaptations usually increased later room after
accounting for quality and time; if cause beliefs tracked measured exposure
without identity or source differences; if political action did not vary with
attribution and alternatives; or if dependence did not alter switching cost,
capacity, or state bargaining power.

## Relationship to the broad program

This layer expands the map across household room, consumer power, work,
inequality, trust, public systems, political judgment, firm/sector power,
infrastructure, and geopolitical consequences. It makes the central question
explicit: when a cost enters the system, which actor has the ability to pass it
on, which group absorbs it, what meaning is attached to it, and what action
follows?

# Project: US tariff price paths and delayed domestic markups

## Question

When a tariff raises prices, how much comes from the taxed import and how much arrives later through US producers and weaker competition?

## Short first pass

Separate tariff rate, importer price, imported good price, domestic input cost, domestic markup, supply-chain delay and household price.

## Possible connection

The consumer effect of a trade policy may last longer than the first shelf-price jump. Domestic firms can face higher inputs and less pressure from imports at the same time.

## Decision rule

Move on after one direct price measure, one delayed domestic response, and one household basket measure. Do not call the first price change the full cost of a tariff.

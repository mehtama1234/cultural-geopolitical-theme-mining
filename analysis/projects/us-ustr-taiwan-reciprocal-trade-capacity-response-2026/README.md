# Project: Taiwan reciprocal trade and capacity response

## Question

When U.S. trade and technology pressure meets a strategically important
partner, does the response become a usable domestic-capacity and procurement
route, or remain an announced agreement?

## Bounded result

The [machine-readable record](../../records/us-ustr-taiwan-reciprocal-trade-capacity-response-2026.json)
adds an external-response stage to the prior Taiwan semiconductor capacity
record. USTR reports tariff and non-tariff concessions, planned Taiwan-linked
U.S. industrial capacity, and $84.8 billion in named planned purchases for
2025–2029. These are commitments and plans, not delivered goods, production,
replaceability, or leverage.

## Decisive next evidence

Match the agreement to legislative completion, product-level contracts,
payments, deliveries, fab output, supplier switching, and any subsequent
Taiwan or U.S. policy change. Keep local energy, water, labor, ownership, and
consumer-price effects separate from strategic-capacity claims.

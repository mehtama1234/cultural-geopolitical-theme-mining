# Project: US platform-owned products and customer choice

## Question

When a platform sells its own products beside outside sellers, does its control over placement help customers or steer them unfairly?

## Short first pass

Separate platform-owned product, outside alternative, search placement, product removal, switching, price, product fit and customer welfare.

## Possible connection

A platform can be both the place where customers search and a seller inside that search. That can create a conflict, but the platform's own product may also fit a real customer need better than the alternatives.

## Decision rule

Move on after one placement or availability change, one customer choice result, and one welfare or price measure. Do not call prominent placement harmful without testing what customers choose when it disappears.

# Project: cross-agency performance goals and durable federal coordination

This project examines whether shared federal goals become durable operating
coordination or remain a temporary framework around agency boundaries.

GAO reports that participants across three groups saw cross-agency priority
goals help define ownership, create energy around shared priorities, and open
opportunities for agencies to work together. The same participants reported
inconsistent progress tools, uneven leadership engagement, and limited staff or
funding to sustain implementation.

The safe theme is not that cross-agency goals fail. It is that a shared goal
can create coordination capacity before agencies have stable ownership,
resources, transition plans, and comparable evidence of progress.

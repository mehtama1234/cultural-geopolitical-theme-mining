# Claims ledger: US vehicle repair as a household shock

**Check date:** 2026-09-11  
**Scope:** United States; repair bills, payment timing, transport, work, care, and household access

| ID | Claim | Evidence type | What supports it | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C-001 | An unexpected repair can be hard to meet when cash is thin. | NBER household-finance evidence | NBER uses a car repair as an example of a sudden expense and shows that benefit timing affects bill payment for low-income and older households. | It does not follow the repair through work or travel. |
| C-002 | Transportation is a large household cost. | BLS consumer expenditures | BLS reports $13,318 in average transportation spending in 2024, with other vehicle expenses at $4,206 and vehicle insurance rising 12.3%. | Averages hide repair timing, income, and alternatives. |
| C-003 | Transport access has economic value. | Place and transport research | NBER and HBS connect commuting and transport quality to access to jobs and services. | They do not show that one repair causes a specific missed job or visit. |
| C-004 | Paying for a repair can preserve access while using the same buffer needed for other bills. | Mechanism comparison | C-001 through C-003 connect sudden bills, transport cost, and access value. | Same-household evidence on the tradeoff is still missing. |
| C-005 | Vehicle repair is a timing-and-access question, not yet a proven job-loss cause. | Our inference | The sources support the cash problem and the value of mobility but not the full household chain. | Repair amount, alternatives, missed trips, and later outcomes remain open. |

## Mechanism under test

```text
unexpected repair
  -> cash is not enough before payday
  -> borrow, delay another payment, ask family, or stop a trip
  -> work, care, food, and service access change
  -> later debt, missed income, or a move may follow
```

## Next test

Follow the same household around a repair: pay date, cash, credit, repair choice, vehicle use, work hours, care visits, food trips, and later payments. Compare households with and without a usable transit alternative.

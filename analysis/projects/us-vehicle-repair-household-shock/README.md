# Project: US vehicle repair as a household shock

## Question

When a car needs an unexpected repair, does the household pay with cash, delay another bill, borrow, or lose access to work and care?

## Short first pass

Use household survey data on emergency savings and bill timing, then add transport data on car dependence and commute alternatives. Keep the repair amount, the missed trip, the lost work time and the later debt as separate measures.

## Possible connection

Vehicle repair sits between transportation and household credit. A repair can protect access to work or care, but the payment can also use the same small cash buffer that would have covered food, medicine or a bill.

## Decision rule

Move on after one source shows the cash-timing problem, one source shows the value of transport access, and one next dataset can follow the same household. Do not turn a reference to car repair into a claim that repairs cause job loss.

# Project: US emergency assistance and the work path

## Question

Does preventing a housing break protect work, or does work recover for another reason?

## Short first pass

Separate housing continuity, employment, earnings and the type of job. Follow the household after help ends instead of treating a later paycheck as proof that the housing crisis was solved.

## Possible connection

Housing is not only a place to live. A short-term payment may protect the time and stability needed to keep a job, especially for a very low-earning household.

## Decision rule

Move on after one study links housing-crisis aid to tax-record work outcomes, one source measures housing stability, and one next dataset can follow the same family through both. Do not infer job quality or lasting security from earnings alone.

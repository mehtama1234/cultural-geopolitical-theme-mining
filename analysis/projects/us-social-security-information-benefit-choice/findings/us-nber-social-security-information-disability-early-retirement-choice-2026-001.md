# Finding: a personalized public statement changes the benefit route people pursue

**Status:** bounded Social Security information and application-choice finding · **Checked:** 2026-09-23

## The bounded finding

NBER Working Paper 35748 examines people ages 62 to full retirement age who
have a work-limiting health condition or low work ability. It finds that an
initial Social Security statement containing personalized estimates of
Disability Insurance and Old-Age Insurance benefits increases the probability
of applying for both programs.

The increase in Disability Insurance applications is split between DI-only and
joint DI/OAI applications, and the response is concentrated among people
further from full retirement age.

The administrative-choice insight is:

> A public information interface can change which formal route a person
> pursues. But making an option legible is an access-stage effect, not yet a
> benefit, health, income, work-exit, or security outcome.

## Limits and next test

- The exact abstract denominator and statement-delivery process are not
  disclosed here.
- Application is not approval, receipt, adequacy, or continuity.
- Increased applications may increase administrative burden if awards do not
  rise.
- The study does not establish changes in health, work, household finances,
  trust, or later political action.

The next decisive artifact is an applicant–statement–claim ledger joining
statement receipt, estimated benefits, application route, adjudication,
appeal, award, payment timing, work exit, health, and household security.

## Source

# Project: assets on paper versus usable household buffer

## Question

What does household wealth actually consist of, and does common asset
ownership mean that households have a large, usable buffer when a shock hits?

## Bounded answer

The Census Bureau reports median household wealth of $204,900 in 2024, but the
10th percentile was zero or less and the 90th percentile was $1.81 million.
Financial-institution assets were common (95.7% of households) but had a
median value of $10,100; vehicles were also common (82.5%) with a $12,900
median value. Home equity and retirement accounts made up most aggregate
wealth.

This shows unequal balance sheets and different kinds of assets. It does not
show how quickly a household could sell or borrow, what penalties it would
face, or whether it could actually withstand a particular shock.

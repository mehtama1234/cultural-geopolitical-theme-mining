# Finding 001: A formal reentry route can exist while timing and capacity delay usable return

**Status:** bounded federal reentry and institutional-capacity finding · **Checked:** 2026-09-22

## The bounded finding

GAO's 2026 audit found that the Federal Bureau of Prisons could not readily
identify when people became eligible for transfer to a residential reentry
center (RRC) or home confinement. In a review of 29,934 people, BOP had not
applied all earned time toward placement for 21,190. GAO also found that BOP
did not know the full scale of the capacity shortage, while it contracted with
roughly 150 RRCs across the country.

The administrative problem reached the provider side too. BOP made about
65,000 late contractor payments from fiscal year 2022 through March 2025,
including $12.5 million in interest penalties. RRCs were paid late about 70%
of the time in fiscal years 2023–2024; one provider said late payment made it
harder to pay staff and could make providers reluctant to bid for new work.

The practical route is:

```text
earned time and formal eligibility
  -> eligibility date and available RRC/home-confinement space
  -> timely transfer
  -> employment, long-term housing, treatment, and community reconnection
  -> [open] durable reentry, later justice contact, trust, and inclusion
```

The broader social insight is that “release support exists” is not one thing.
Data systems, available space, provider solvency, and transfer timing decide
whether a formal opportunity becomes a usable bridge back into community life.

## Limits and next test

- The 21,190-of-29,934 review is an audit sample, not a complete count of all
  people who were delayed.
- BOP could not provide the full scale because eligibility dates were not
  readily available.
- Delays had multiple explanations, including insufficient capacity and court
  orders; the audit does not assign one cause to each person.
- RRC residence or home confinement does not establish employment, housing,
  treatment completion, community acceptance, recidivism, or durable safety.
- BOP's reported lower return-to-prison likelihood for RRC residents is not a
  causal comparison in this audit.

The next decisive evidence would link a person or named RRC to eligibility date,
transfer timing, services actually received, housing and work after release,
and later justice contact or stability.

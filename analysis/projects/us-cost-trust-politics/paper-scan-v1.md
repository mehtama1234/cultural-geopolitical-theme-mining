# Cost, trust, and political response: paper scan v1

**Checked:** 2026-09-12  
**Question:** When does a household cost become a judgment about the country, a party, or an institution?

## The deeper pattern

Households may first respond to costs without changing their view of themselves. They buy less, save less, borrow, or work more. They may then judge the national economy more harshly than their own household situation. That gap is important, but it is not proof of a political cause.

```text
price, pay, or benefit change
  -> lost choice or household adjustment
  -> personal economic judgment
  -> story about who is responsible
  -> trust, blame, demand, turnout, or vote
```

The missing middle is the story of responsibility. Two families can face the same price rise and blame a company, a president, a party, a foreign country, or no one. Party identity, local conditions, news, and personal experience may all shape the answer.

## The key separation

Do not merge these four measures:

1. **Material change:** the price, pay, bill, or benefit.
2. **Behavior:** what the household bought, delayed, borrowed, or stopped doing.
3. **Judgment:** how the person rates their own and the country’s economy.
4. **Political action:** trust, blame, demand, turnout, or vote.

A survey can show all four at population level while still failing to show the path for one person. A county study can connect real wages to vote shares without showing the person’s reasoning. The strong claim must name which link is actually measured.

## Next bounded test

Use the Federal Reserve household tables to compare price actions, personal financial status, national economic views, trust, party identity, and income. Then read the NBER real-wage paper’s county design and compare its measure of buying power with local housing, transport, health, and food costs.

In the future household calendar, record the bill or price change, the adjustment, the household’s explanation of responsibility, and the later judgment separately. Ask again after the pressure ends. A one-time angry answer is not the same as a durable change in trust or political action.

## What would change the working picture

The working idea would weaken if household price actions did not relate to personal or national economic judgments after accounting for party identity and local conditions; if real-wage changes did not predict political outcomes in other periods; or if material pressure changed behavior but not blame, trust, turnout, or policy demand.

## Reading rule

When a source says voters reacted to the economy, ask: which cost changed, what did people give up, what did they think caused it, and what did they actually do?

# The national price story fragments into different household baskets

**Status:** provisional cross-source finding · **Checked:** 2026-09-13

## The bounded finding

A national inflation rate can conceal meaningful differences in the baskets,
earnings positions, and spending room that people use to interpret the economy.
The New York Fed's December 2025 Economic Heterogeneity Indicators estimate
different inflation gaps across demographic and regional consumption baskets,
show unequal earnings ratios among employed workers, and report divergent real
retail-consumption directions by income. The Federal Reserve SHED and NBER Fed
trust studies then supply separate evidence about household adaptation and
institutional interpretation.

Together, these sources support a layered claim: material exposure and room
shape the context in which a public economic message is heard. They do not
prove that a group inflation estimate caused a specific person's spending,
trust, blame, or vote.

## Source separation

| Layer | Direct evidence | Unit and limit |
|---|---|---|
| New York Fed EHI | December 2025 demographic/regional inflation gaps; earnings ratios; income-group retail-consumption directions | Constructed group indicators using CEX/CPI, BLS-based labor measures, and Numerator receipts; not household-specific causal estimates |
| Federal Reserve SHED 2025 | Reported price adaptations, financial margin, hardship, and economic judgments | Separate US adult survey; multiple actions and no single identified price event |
| NBER W33071 | Perceived Fed alignment associated with trust, information demand, and weighting of Fed communications | Online survey experiment; no realized spending, borrowing, or voting outcome |

## What the EHI layer shows

The EHI release estimates that Hispanic households were 0.39 percentage points
below national inflation in December 2025, while the Northeast was 0.57 points
above and the South 0.51 points below. It reports that Black workers earned
78.7% of white workers' earnings and that workers without a college degree
earned 57.4% of the earnings of workers with a degree. Since 2023, its receipt
panel shows real consumption increasing for high-income households, remaining
flat for middle-income households, and decreasing for low-income households by
November 2025.

These figures should not be read as a single deprivation ranking. Inflation
gaps are constructed from spending weights and assigned prices; earnings ratios
condition on employment; spending directions can reflect income, wealth,
credit, composition, and product mix. The counterexample is important: a group
with a lower estimated inflation rate can still have less cash, lower earnings,
or less ability to absorb a shock.

## The end-to-end route under test

```text
group basket + earnings + wealth + debt + place
  -> experienced cost and available room
  -> substitution, delay, borrowing, saving, or continued purchase
  -> interpretation of prices, policy, and institutional responsibility
  -> trust, blame, political demand, or action
```

The EHI directly informs the first segment and the aggregate spending
direction. SHED informs the adaptation menu. NBER W33071 informs the
messenger/trust filter. The links among the three source units remain open.

## Next test

Use a household panel or linked administrative/transaction design around a
dated rate or price event. Measure the actual basket and price paid, income and
wealth, debt and deposit exposure, source receipt, trust, expectations, actual
spending, and later political judgment. Include households whose spending did
not change and groups for whom lower inflation did not imply greater security.

**Evidence status:** convergent distributional context across official/derived
indicators and separate survey experiments; no same-household material-to-
meaning-to-action chain is claimed.

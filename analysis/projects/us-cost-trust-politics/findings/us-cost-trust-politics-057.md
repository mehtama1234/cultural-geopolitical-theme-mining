# Finding 057: Belonging can function as civic infrastructure

**Status:** bounded youth civic-capacity finding · **Checked:** 2026-09-21

## The bounded finding

CIRCLE's 2026 Political Homes research reports that young people with access
to a political home showed roughly 20 percentage-point higher civic action and
civic self-efficacy than peers without that access. The political-home frame
includes more than shared ideology: safe third spaces, belonging, civic
information, food, financial support, and transportation.

## Why it matters for the broader atlas

This adds a practical support route between social belonging and political
action. Participation is not only a matter of motivation or opinion. It can
depend on whether a person has a place to belong, trusted people, information,
transport, time, and enough material support to take part.

```text
material or social constraint
  -> accessible group, place, or political home
  -> belonging, support, and civic self-efficacy
  -> action, organizing, voting, or withdrawal
  -> durable identity, institutional response, and wellbeing [open]
```

The deeper point is that civic capacity is partly infrastructural. A group can
create room for action, but it can also create obligations, emotional labor,
conflict, or dependence. Belonging is therefore a resource and a possible
cost, not a single positive attitude.

## Evidence boundary

The comparison is observational. It does not prove that a political home
caused action, that every participant received the listed support, or that
the effect persists into validated turnout, policy influence, or wellbeing.
The public summary does not provide all subgroup denominators or a same-person
dated path from material hardship to group entry and later action.

## Next test

Follow a young person or group episode from a dated material or institutional
barrier through entry, support received, time and cost, civic action, and later
continued participation or withdrawal. Retain non-entry and failed-support
routes in the same denominator.

# Project: wildfire damage, income loss, and community composition

## Question

How does direct home destruction affect individual economic trajectories and who
returns to the community afterward?

## Decision rule

Keep wildfire exposure, property damage, income, employment, rebuilding,
insurance, aid, occupancy, return, displacement, and community composition
separate. A wealthy average affected area does not mean every damaged household
is protected.

## Current bounded finding

The [Census CES finding](findings/us-census-wildfire-damage-income-community-composition-2026-001.md)
reports three years of lower income after home destruction, cumulative losses of
26% of pre-fire annual income, and slow, composition-changing reoccupancy.

**Captured:** 2026-09-23

## Extracted claims

- CalPrivacy reports that more than 325,000 consumers had signed up for DROP
  and submitted deletion requests by July 10, 2026; the route covered more than
  600 active data brokers.
- A consumer can submit one verifiable request. DROP hashes the submitted
  information; CalPrivacy says raw information is not stored in DROP or shared
  with brokers through the mechanism.
- Beginning August 1, 2026, brokers must access the mechanism at least once
  every 45 days and process matched deletion requests within the stated
  operating cycle, subject to exemptions.
- The public status vocabulary includes record deleted, record exempted, record
  opted out of sale, record not found, and pending. Status updates can take up
  to 90 days to appear.
- CalPrivacy says optional identifiers such as a mobile advertising ID,
  connected-TV ID, or VIN can improve matching, and that the request can be
  submitted in under ten minutes.

## Boundary preserved

The public pages do not provide a consumer-level matched-broker denominator,
status distribution, verified post-deletion scan, residual-data rate,
recurrence rate, or trust/behavior outcome. A centralized route and status
vocabulary show administrative reach and observability; they do not prove
durable deletion or equal practical control.

# Project: USMCA labor response and cross-border remedy

## Question

What happens when a cross-border labor complaint becomes a trade-enforcement
case rather than remaining a public allegation?

## Decision rule

Keep complaint, government review, company remediation, worker receipt, union
access, monitoring, tariff leverage, and durable workplace change separate. An
official resolution is a real institutional outcome, but it is not automatically
proof of lasting worker power or sector-wide compliance.

## Current bounded finding

The [USMCA finding](findings/usmca-rapid-response-labor-remedy-001.md) shows a
dated case in which Mexico and a facility responded to a U.S.-initiated labor
review with reinstatement, backpay, union access, policy changes, training, and
monitoring, after which the U.S. resumed tariff liquidation. Long-term receipt,
enforcement, and worker power remain open.

# Project: US income volatility and the room to spend

## Question

When pay is less predictable, does the household cut spending, borrow more, or keep spending and accept a later squeeze?

## Short first pass

Measure pay level and pay risk separately. Track spending volatility, liquid savings, credit access, housing and durable purchases around a move into more uncertain work.

## Possible connection

Two workers can earn the same amount over a year and still have different lives if one cannot predict next month's pay. Uncertainty can change what a household is willing or able to buy, borrow or plan.

## Decision rule

Move on after one source observes income risk and spending change, one source measures credit or housing access, and one next dataset can follow the same worker after a job change. Do not treat a high annual income as proof of financial room.

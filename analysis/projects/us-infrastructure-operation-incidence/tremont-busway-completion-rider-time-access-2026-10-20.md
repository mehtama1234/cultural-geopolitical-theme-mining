# Tremont busway: completed access intervention, open rider outcome

**Checked:** 2026-10-20  
**Record:** [Tremont busway time-and-access record](../../records/us-nyc-tremont-busway-time-access-2026.json)

## What the official completion notice shows

NYC announced completion and activation of the Bronx's Tremont Avenue busway,
serving the Bx36 corridor and a reported 39,000 daily riders. Buses had
historically traveled as slowly as 5 mph. The busway operates seven days a week
from 6 a.m. to 8 p.m.; the announcement also identifies a transit-dependent
community in which 72% of households do not own a private car.

This is a completed implementation and operating-access stage, not yet a
measured rider-outcome stage. The public notice does not publish post-activation
travel times, reliability, missed connections, safety, accessibility, or
adjacent-street burden.

## End-to-end boundary

```text
slow and unreliable transit in a transit-dependent corridor
  -> design, construction, and busway activation
  -> scheduled bus-only operation serving 39,000 daily riders
  -> [open] realized time savings, reliability, safety, accessibility, incidence, legitimacy, continuation
```

The daily rider figure is a service-population denominator, not evidence that
each rider experienced a faster or more reliable trip. A later pass should seek
post-activation operating data or rider-level evidence rather than treat the
completion announcement as realized welfare.

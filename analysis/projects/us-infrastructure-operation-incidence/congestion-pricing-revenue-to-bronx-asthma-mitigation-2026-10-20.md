# Congestion pricing: revenue reaches a targeted health-mitigation allocation

**Checked:** 2026-10-20  
**Record:** [NYC congestion-pricing implementation record](../../records/us-nyc-congestion-pricing-implementation-2026.json)

## What this adds

The NYC Mayor's Office reported that the MTA allocated $20 million from its
congestion-pricing mitigation program to improve childhood-asthma outcomes in
the Bronx. The broader mitigation program had allocated $100 million to
neighborhoods disproportionately burdened by pollution, climate impacts, and
poor health outcomes.

This moves the congestion-pricing episode beyond toll collection, traffic,
transit, and air-quality monitoring into a named distributional response. It is
still a funding allocation, not evidence of service uptake, exposure reduction,
fewer emergency visits, medication access, or improved child health.

## End-to-end boundary

```text
traffic and pollution exposure
  -> congestion pricing and dedicated revenue
  -> targeted Bronx health-mitigation allocation
  -> [open] provider delivery, household uptake, exposure change, asthma outcomes, trust, legitimacy
```

The historical decline in citywide childhood asthma emergency visits provides
context but cannot be attributed to this new allocation. The public announcement
does not publish a recipient denominator or a child-level outcome series.

# Project: Infrastructure operation, incidence, and public legitimacy

## Question

When a major infrastructure project or technology load becomes operational,
who receives the benefit, who carries the cost or risk, and can the affected
public see and contest that exchange?

## Short end-to-end goal

Follow one named project, facility, or operating incident from approval or
contract through operation, reliability, local burden or benefit, replacement,
public response, and durable legitimacy.

```text
approval, contract, or load growth
  -> operation, dispatch, or disruption
  -> bill, service, land, water, labor, or environmental incidence
  -> mitigation, alternative, replacement, or contestation
  -> public attribution and durable acceptance
```

This is a separate depth lane in the ongoing 14-theme program. It joins the
infrastructure, firm-power, household-room, public-systems, and geopolitical
themes without treating capacity or investment as lived benefit.

## Current bridge

The [operation-to-incidence bridge](operation-incidence-public-legitimacy-bridge-v1.md)
compares named cross-border power, large computational loads, Virginia cost
allocation, and New York congestion pricing. It finds that operation and
governance response are visible earlier than household incidence, replacement,
or durable acceptance.

The [infrastructure operation-to-incidence acquisition gate](infrastructure-operation-incidence-acquisition-gate-2026-10-19.md)

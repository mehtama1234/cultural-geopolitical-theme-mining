# Poland Starlink-station sabotage investigation follow-up

**Checked:** 2026-10-20  
**Status:** named infrastructure incident and multinational law-enforcement response; service, attribution, and deterrence outcomes remain bounded

## Observed route

Poland's Ministry of Interior and Administration reports that two Turkish
nationals suspected of intentionally setting fire to infrastructure associated
with a Starlink ground station in Wola Krobowska, Mazowieckie, on September 23,
2026, were arrested in Germany through multinational operational activity.

The ministry says Polish Police, the Internal Security Agency, and prosecutors
prepared the evidence, issued wanted notices and European Arrest Warrants, and
coordinated information with international partners. The account describes the
station as strategically important satellite-communications infrastructure and
the suspected act as sabotage; the minister attributes it to Russian services,
which remains an official allegation rather than an independently adjudicated
finding in this record.

## Stage boundary

| Stage | What is public | Still open |
|---|---|---|
| Incident | Named station-related infrastructure, location, date, and intentional-fire allegation | Damage, outage duration, service users, financial loss, and technical repair |
| Investigation | Polish evidence preparation, warrants, and Police/ABW/prosecutorial work | Evidentiary record, court findings, attribution, and chain of command |
| Cross-border response | Suspects arrested in Germany through multinational cooperation | Extradition, prosecution, conviction, and final judicial outcome |
| Security effect | Official warning and claimed operational success | Deterrence, recurrence, public confidence, satellite-service continuity, and civilian/firm impact |

This is an official incident-and-response account, not proof of final guilt,
Russian state responsibility, restored service, or deterrence.

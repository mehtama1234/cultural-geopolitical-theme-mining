# Project: Consumer AI platform choice, trust, and privacy control

## Question

How do people choose among consumer AI assistants, and does privacy concern
translate into protective action when task fit, price, and platform dependence
also matter?

## Short finding route

Keep primary-platform choice, task specialization, multihoming, trust,
privacy knowledge, protective tools, and hypothetical willingness to pay as
separate stages. This is a weighted cross-sectional survey and choice
experiment, not platform telemetry, a longitudinal switching panel, or a
causal privacy-behavior estimate.

See the [bounded finding](findings/us-ai-assistant-platform-choice-trust-privacy-2026-001.md).

The current [AI advice, verification, agency, and human fallback bridge](ai-advice-verification-agency-fallback-bridge-v1.md) compares shopping, financial-guidance, platform-choice, and relational-authority evidence while keeping actual decision outcomes, correction, and exit open.

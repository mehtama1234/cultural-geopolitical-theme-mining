# Project: Social-media non-use and practical exit

## Question

When people try to limit or stop using a major platform, can they actually
exit, and what do they trade away or carry forward when non-use is incomplete?

## Short finding route

Keep platform utility, social connectivity pressure, user tactics, account
status, reported friction, reactivation or substitution, and political meaning
as separate stages. This is comparative qualitative evidence from young Korean
Instagram users, not a U.S. prevalence estimate or a platform-wide deletion
audit.

See the [bounded finding](findings/us-sage-social-media-nonuse-exit-2026-001.md).

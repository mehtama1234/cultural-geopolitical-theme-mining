# Project: US school safety, fear, and participation

## Question

When schools become safer on broad measures, do all students experience the
school as usable and safe enough to attend classes and activities?

## Decision rule

Keep victimization, bullying, fear, avoidance, attendance, school response,
mental-health support, belonging, and later learning as separate stages. A lower
crime rate does not prove that every student feels safe or participates fully.

## Current bounded finding

The [school-safety finding](findings/us-school-safety-001.md) adds a student
experience layer to the broad safety atlas: in 2021–22, 3.8% of students ages
12–18 reported fear of attack or harm at school and 3.6% reported avoiding
school activities or classes because of fear. The report also shows a lower
long-run victimization rate, so aggregate improvement and unequal lived safety
can coexist.

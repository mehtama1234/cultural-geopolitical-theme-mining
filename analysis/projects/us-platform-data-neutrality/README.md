# Project: US platform data neutrality

## Question

When a platform controls the data that sellers need, does an equal-access rule help customers or weaken the data they rely on?

## Short first pass

Separate data access, price for access, affiliated and outside sellers, platform investment in data, competition and customer welfare.

## Matched evidence pass

- [A data rule can open the market and weaken the data supply](../../findings/us-platform-data-neutrality-matched-evidence-001.md)
- [Reader-friendly HTML](../../../site/us-platform-data-neutrality-matched-evidence-001.html)

## Possible connection

A platform can be both the market gate and the maker of the information used in the market. A rule that opens the gate may also change the quality or supply of that information.

## Decision rule

Move on after one platform data rule, one seller or price result, and one customer-welfare measure. Do not call equal access good or bad without checking both competition and data supply.

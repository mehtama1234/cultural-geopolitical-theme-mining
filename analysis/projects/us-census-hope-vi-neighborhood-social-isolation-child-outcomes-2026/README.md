# Project: HOPE VI, neighborhood connection, and intergenerational outcomes

## Question

Can public-housing redevelopment change children's opportunity by changing
neighborhood social interaction, even when adult residents' earnings do not
rise?

## Short finding route

Keep redevelopment investment, neighborhood poverty, adult resident earnings,
child exposure, social interaction, long-run child outcomes, displacement, and
resident control as separate stages. This is a Census CES working-paper result
using linked administrative data, not proof that every original resident
benefited or that redevelopment is universally safe or equitable.

See the [bounded finding](findings/us-census-hope-vi-neighborhood-social-isolation-child-outcomes-2026-001.md).

# Project: HUD HEARS housing-assistance exit types and outcomes

This project adds a bounded regional comparator to the national HUD–LEHD labor
trajectory study. It preserves the distinction between positive, neutral, and
negative assistance exits and follows linked people for one year across housing,
health, behavioral-health, homelessness-service, and wage records.

The finding is useful because an aggregate “exit” can conceal opposite lived
outcomes: a move to stable unsubsidized housing and an eviction are both exits,
but they do not represent the same transition.

The source is the official [HUD HEARS final report](https://www.huduser.gov/portal/portal/sites/default/files/pdf/HUD-HEARS-Study.pdf).
It covers Seattle Housing Authority and King County Housing Authority, not the
United States as a whole.

# Project: tribal water infrastructure and the maintenance gap

## Question

When federal programs build water infrastructure for Tribes, does the system
also make the homes eligible, the funding routes usable, and the ongoing
operation affordable?

## Decision rule

Keep construction, property eligibility, application burden, operation and
maintenance, reliable service, and health outcomes separate. A completed tank
or pipe is not proof of durable safe water.

## Current bounded finding

The [GAO finding](findings/us-gao-tribal-water-infrastructure-maintenance-2026-001.md)
shows that statutory boundaries and multiple agency processes can leave some
homes outside a project's funding route, while Tribes remain responsible for
operating systems without routine federal O&M support.

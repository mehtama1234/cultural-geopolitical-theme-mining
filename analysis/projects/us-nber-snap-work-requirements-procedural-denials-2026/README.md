# Project: SNAP work requirements and procedural denials

## Question

Do SNAP work requirements reduce participation through administrative burden,
and can the denial route be separated from ordinary disenrollment?

## Short finding route

Keep policy eligibility, notice, paperwork, procedural denial, appeal, benefit
restoration, food security, work, and later trust/action as separate stages.
This NBER study supplies an administrative denial mechanism, not a complete
same-claimant remedy or recovery episode.

See the [bounded finding](findings/us-nber-snap-work-requirements-procedural-denials-2026-001.md).

The [procedural-denial remedy and recovery bridge](procedural-denial-remedy-recovery-bridge-v1.md)
extends the finding to the still-open appeal, receipt, food-security, work,
and household-recovery stages.

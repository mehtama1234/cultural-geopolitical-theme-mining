## Official source

- Jason B. Cook, Elizabeth Cox, and Chloe N. East, “SNAP Work Requirements,
  Administrative Burden and Procedural Denials,” NBER Working Paper 34698,
  January 2026, [official NBER page](https://www.nber.org/papers/w34698),
  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3386/w34698).

## Extracted claims

- The paper studies procedural denials in SNAP and aims to isolate
  administrative burden from other sources of disenrollment.
- It studies work requirements for Able-Bodied Adults Without Dependents and
  uses age-based exemptions applying to people aged 50 and older.
- Administrative data and difference-in-differences models using waiver periods
  show that work requirements significantly increase procedural and overall
  denials.
- The authors interpret the results as evidence that work requirements deter
  participation through imposed administrative burdens.

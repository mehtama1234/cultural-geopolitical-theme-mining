# Finding 001: A formal work requirement can become a procedural denial route

**Status:** bounded administrative SNAP-denial and participation finding ·
**Checked:** 2026-09-23

## The bounded finding

NBER Working Paper 34698 uses SNAP administrative data and a difference-in-
differences design that exploits age-based exemptions for Able-Bodied Adults
Without Dependents aged 50 and older, together with periods when work-
requirement waivers apply.

The study reports that work requirements significantly increase procedural and
overall denials. The authors interpret the result as direct evidence that
administrative burden can deter SNAP participation, rather than treating every
disenrollment as a change in need or eligibility.

The safe interpretation is:

> Access can be lost through the procedure for proving eligibility, before the
> record shows whether a person could appeal, restore benefits, replace food,
> or recover from the interruption.

## Evidence chain

```text
work requirement and exemption rule
  -> paperwork, documentation, timing, and administrative burden
  -> procedural or overall denial
  -> participation deterrence or benefit interruption
  -> [open] notice, appeal, restoration, food security, health, work,
     later trust, and political action
```

## What it adds to the broader atlas

Existing SNAP records show participation, work, hardship, and administrative
context. This study adds a policy-linked denial mechanism designed to separate
procedural denials from ordinary disenrollment. It advances the public-system
route toward remedy while preserving the fact that denial is not yet a complete
same-claimant service episode.

## What the source does not show

- It is not a national denial rate in the public abstract.
- It does not identify each claimant's notice, paperwork effort, reason for
  denial, appeal, or benefit restoration.
- It does not measure food security, health, work quality, debt, or household
  recovery after denial.
- It does not establish that every denial reflects administrative burden rather
  than a valid eligibility or documentation failure.
- It does not observe trust, political action, switching, or exit from other
  service routes.

## Next decisive test

Link a claimant or case identifier from work-requirement notice through
documentation, denial reason, appeal, restored or continued benefit, food and
health outcomes, work and time cost, alternative support, and later trust or
political action. Preserve exempt and waived groups, procedural versus
substantive denials, non-filers, and successful appeals.

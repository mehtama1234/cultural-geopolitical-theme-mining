# Project: California fast-food wage floor and uneven firm adjustment

## Question

When a wage floor rises sharply, does the adjustment appear as better worker
pay, fewer jobs, higher prices, lower turnover, slower firm entry, or small-firm
exit—and which actors bear each part?

## Decision rule

Keep wages, employment, separations, entry, exit, prices, quality, and worker
household outcomes separate. Aggregate employment is not the same as the
experience of a worker or a small chain.

## Current bounded finding

The [establishment-level finding](findings/us-nber-california-fast-food-wage-small-chain-entry-exit-2026-001.md)
reports negative effects concentrated among small chains, including slower
entry and increased exit, while overall employment effects were modest so far.

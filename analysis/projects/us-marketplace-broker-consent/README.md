# Project: health-marketplace broker consent and consumer control

## Question

When consumers use an insurance agent or broker, can they tell who changed
their plan and did they actually authorize the change?

## Decision rule

Keep broker licensing, consumer consent, identity verification, plan change,
consumer notice, complaint, coverage loss, and correction separate. A licensed
intermediary is not automatically a verified consumer-controlled transaction.

## Current bounded finding

The [GAO finding](findings/us-gao-marketplace-broker-consent-2026-001.md)
shows that federal Marketplace controls can verify broker registration while
still failing to reliably verify consent or notify consumers of every action.
Selected state Marketplaces provide a counterexample with stronger controls.

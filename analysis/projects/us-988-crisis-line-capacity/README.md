# Project: 988 crisis-line capacity and downstream care

## Question

When a universal crisis number makes it easier to reach a responder, does the
larger care and social-support system turn that contact into safety and health?

## Decision rule

Keep call access, answer quality, risk assessment, referral, dispatch,
treatment, social support, mortality, and trust separate. A connected call is
not the same as completed care or recovery.

## Current bounded finding

The [988 finding](findings/us-nber-988-crisis-line-capacity-mortality-2026-001.md)
shows that implementation improved in-state crisis-line operations in states
with larger initial gaps, but did not produce a detectable short-run population
change in suicide mortality or broader mental health.

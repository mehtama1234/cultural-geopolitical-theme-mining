# Project: American Indian heterogeneity and human capital

## Question

What disappears when very different American Indian communities are treated as
one statistical group?

## Decision rule

Keep tribal and community identity, geography, historical policy, resources,
self-governance, economic development, education, health, mobility, and
intergenerational outcomes separate. Disaggregation is necessary, but it is
not itself a remedy.

## Current bounded finding

The [heterogeneity finding](findings/us-nber-american-indian-heterogeneity-human-capital-2026-001.md)
summarizes evidence that outcomes differ substantially across American Indian
populations and that historical policy and measurement choices shape what the
data can show.

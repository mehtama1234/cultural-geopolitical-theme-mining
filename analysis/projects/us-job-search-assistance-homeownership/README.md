# Project: US job-search assistance, work, and housing security

## Question

Can an early job-search intervention improve long-run work and housing
security, and for whom does it fail or backfire?

## Short finding route

Keep the offer, attendance, counseling, compliance, job search, employment,
earnings, UI, disability, tax, and homeownership stages separate. A positive
average outcome can coexist with negative effects for people whose constraints
or health make the intervention poorly matched.

## Decision rule

Do not call job-search assistance a durable recovery mechanism unless the
analysis preserves treatment heterogeneity, job quality, benefit transitions,
housing adequacy, health, and participant meaning or exit. Administrative
earnings and homeownership are valuable outcomes but are not a complete welfare
measure.

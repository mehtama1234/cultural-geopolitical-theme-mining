# Project: US student debt and delayed life choices

## Question

When education debt follows a person into work, housing and family life, is the main effect the size of the balance, the monthly payment, the risk of falling behind, or the choices the debt rules make harder?

## Short first pass

Use the Federal Reserve for borrower-reported payment trouble, the New York Fed for balances and delinquency, and NBER for life-choice mechanisms. Keep an outstanding balance separate from a payment problem. A debt can be large and manageable, or small and badly timed.

## Possible connection

Student debt is a long-running version of the credit bridge. It can finance education and future earning power, but repayment can meet housing, family, work and public-policy choices at a later life stage.

## Decision rule

Move on after one current balance measure, one subgroup payment comparison, one life-choice study and a counterpoint about education returns or repayment protection.

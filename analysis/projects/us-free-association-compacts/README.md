# Project: Free Association compacts and strategic partnership

## Question

Can a U.S. security-and-assistance compact become durable local capacity, or
can strategic access remain stronger and more visible than implementation,
service quality, and partner control?

## Decision rule

Keep formal access, money, project implementation, local labor and revenue,
service outcomes, public acceptance, and strategic effect separate. A renewed
compact is not automatically durable partnership or self-sufficiency.

## Current bounded finding

The [GAO compact finding](findings/us-gao-free-association-compacts-implementation-oversight-2026-001.md)
shows that the amended U.S. compacts provide long-term assistance and military
access while project delivery, reporting, staffing, and oversight remain
delayed or incomplete.

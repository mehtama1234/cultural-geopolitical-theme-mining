# Project: US local prices and the geography of consumption

## Question

How much does a family's real standard of living depend on where it lives, even when its income looks the same on paper?

## Short first pass

Separate nominal income, local prices, product mix, real consumption, moving choice, and the services or jobs available in the place.

## Possible connection

A national income number can hide a local customer reality. If low-income households face wider price differences by place, the same paycheck may leave very different room for food, housing, transport and care.

## Decision rule

Move on after one local price index, one observed consumption measure, and one place-level outcome. Do not call a city cheap or expensive without naming the household group and product basket.

# Project: floods, firm reallocation, and public recovery spending

## Question

After a major flood, does public spending help firms rebuild and reallocate
productive capacity, and what happens to workers and places during that process?

## Decision rule

Keep plant exit, machinery transfer, entrant productivity, survivor rebuilding,
financing, employment, household recovery, and local service continuity
separate. Productive reallocation is not automatically shared recovery.

## Current bounded finding

The [Census CES finding](findings/us-census-flood-disaster-creative-destruction-government-spending-2026-001.md)
reports that less productive plants were more likely to exit after large floods,
while entrants acquired used machinery and survivors upgraded; federal disaster
spending helped finance young and small firms in the study framework.

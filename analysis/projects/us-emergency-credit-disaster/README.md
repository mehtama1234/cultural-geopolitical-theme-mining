# Project: US emergency credit after a disaster

## Question

When does borrowing protect a household, and when does it only move the loss forward?

## Short first pass

Separate the disaster loss, loan approval, interest and repayment, bankruptcy, preserved purchase and later financial room. Compare emergency credit with insurance, cash and direct aid.

## Possible connection

Credit can be a rescue tool when it arrives before bills compound. The same product can be a burden when the rate, amount or repayment date leaves no room after the shock.

## Decision rule

Move on after one study identifies a causal credit effect after a disaster, one source measures the payment or housing shock, and one next dataset can show loan terms, preserved needs and repayment. Do not treat every new loan as either help or harm.

# Project: enforcement climate, reporting, and public safety

## Question

Can a public-safety policy change what people are willing to report, so that
official crime records hide a change in actual victimization?

## Decision rule

Keep enforcement exposure, fear, reporting, police records, victimization,
community support, remedy, trust, and later civic action separate. A flat
reported-crime count is not automatically a flat safety outcome.

## Current bounded finding

The [Census CES finding](findings/us-census-immigration-enforcement-victim-reporting-public-safety-2026-001.md)
reports that Secure Communities reduced reporting by Hispanic victims and
increased offending against Hispanics while reported crime stayed unchanged.
The result is a mechanism finding, not a universal judgment on all immigration
enforcement or all immigrant communities.

# Project: Champlain Hudson Power Express and usable energy service

## Question

When a cross-border transmission project reaches commercial operation, what
has actually been converted for households, workers, and the host communities?

## Decision rule

Keep construction, commercial operation, delivered energy, projected system
benefits, customer bills, reliability, local burden, and public acceptance
separate. A working transmission line is real infrastructure, but it is not
automatically cheaper or fairer service for every customer.

## Current bounded finding

The [CHPE finding](findings/us-nyserda-champlain-hudson-power-express-operation-2026-001.md)
follows a named 339-mile project from Quebec hydropower through New York's
Zone J grid. New York reports commercial operation beginning in May 2026 and
expects 10.4 terawatt-hours per year, but the public record does not yet show
customer-level bill changes, actual monthly delivery, reliability outcomes, or
durable host-community acceptance.

The route is:

```text
cross-border agreement and state procurement
  -> buried transmission construction
  -> commercial operation
  -> electricity delivered into a high-demand zone
  -> [open] customer bill, reliability, and emissions experience
  -> [open] durable acceptance, affordability, and replaceability
```

This is a genuine infrastructure-and-dependence realization case. It is not a
household benefit estimate or proof of lasting geopolitical leverage.

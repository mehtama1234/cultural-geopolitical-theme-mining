# Project: US fiscal news and household choice

## Question

When does a future public bill change today's household choice?

## Short first pass

Separate current debt, future debt, household expectation, inflation belief, spending, saving, borrowing and political judgment.

## Possible connection

People may change how they use money before taxes or benefits change if they expect the public balance to create a future bill. The message can matter even when today's paycheck is unchanged.

## Decision rule

Move on after one US experiment measures fiscal news and household beliefs, one source measures real spending or debt, and one next dataset follows the same household through policy realization. Do not treat a forecast as a received cost.

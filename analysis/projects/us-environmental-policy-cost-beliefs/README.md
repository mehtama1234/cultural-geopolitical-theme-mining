# Project: US environmental policy cost beliefs

## Question

When people think one climate or energy policy will raise their bills, how does that belief shape the policy they support?

## Short first pass

Separate the policy tool, the actual cost, the expected cost, the household bill, the environmental result, and the political choice.

## Possible connection

A policy can lose support because of who people think will pay, even when the actual cost and the environmental result tell a different story. The consumer bill becomes part of the design of public action.

## Decision rule

Move on after one belief measure, one cost measure, and one environmental or political result. Do not treat stated cost beliefs as actual household burden.

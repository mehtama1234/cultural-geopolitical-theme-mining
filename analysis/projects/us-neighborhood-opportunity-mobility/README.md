# Project: neighborhood opportunity and mobility

## Question

How do housing markets and neighborhood institutions turn a family's address
into different long-term opportunities?

## Decision rule

Keep neighborhood exposure, search, voucher or mobility assistance, actual
move, school and social experience, cost, continuity, and later outcomes
separate. A high-opportunity address on paper is not automatically a usable or
durable move.

## Current bounded finding

The [neighborhood-effects finding](findings/us-nber-neighborhood-effects-missing-markets-opportunity-2026-001.md)
summarizes evidence that childhood neighborhoods affect later outcomes and
that credit, search, social, and behavioral barriers can limit access to
opportunity. It complements, but does not replace, household-level voucher
evidence.

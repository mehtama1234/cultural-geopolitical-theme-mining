# Project: US inflation and the price people feel

## Question

When prices rise, what part of the experience changes first: the amount people can buy, their sense of fairness, or their trust in the economy?

## Short first pass

Start with the NBER surveys on why Americans dislike inflation and the HBS work on price changes and cheaper goods. Keep measured prices, lost buying power, personal stories and political views as separate pieces.

## Possible connection

People may judge inflation through the goods they buy often and the wages they think are not keeping up. That can make a broad price measure feel like a direct loss of room, even when the national rate is falling.

## Decision rule

Look for US evidence that joins prices, wages, purchases and views of firms or government. Move on unless the sources show whether the response comes from actual loss, expected future loss, or how the price change is explained.

# Project: US transit service realization and usable mobility

## Question

What does operating transit service show about the gap between national
infrastructure output and usable household mobility?

## Decision rule

Keep agency reporting, fleet, workers, vehicle miles, passenger trips,
passenger miles, route coverage, reliability, fare, accessibility, and a
household's completed trip separate. Service supplied is not automatically
service reachable or useful.

## Current bounded finding

The [FTA finding](findings/us-transit-service-realization-001.md) shows a large
operating transit system with billions of passenger trips while local access,
reliability, affordability, and household outcomes remain unmeasured.

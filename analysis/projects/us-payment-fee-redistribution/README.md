# Project: US payment fees and who pays

## Question

When card rewards are funded by fees charged to merchants, who carries the cost: cash users, debit users, other shoppers, or the merchant?

## Short first pass

Track payment method, merchant fee, price, reward and household income separately. Compare where cash, debit and premium-card users shop rather than assuming everyone faces the same price.

## Possible connection

The payment method can change the price a household effectively pays even when the shelf price is the same. A financial product can therefore redistribute money through ordinary shopping.

## Decision rule

Move on after one source measures the fee and payment mix, one source estimates who receives rewards, and one next dataset can test prices and merchant response by place and income. Do not call every card reward unfair or every merchant pass-through complete.

# US international defense arrangements

This project examines the institutional middle between a U.S. defense goal and
an actual international cooperative capability. It focuses on the review,
approval, coordination, and oversight of acquisition-related arrangements with
foreign partners.

The source is a GAO review of Department of Defense data and processes for
fiscal years 2021–2025. It shows administrative capacity and friction, not the
military effectiveness, partner consent, deterrence, or strategic outcome of
any particular arrangement.

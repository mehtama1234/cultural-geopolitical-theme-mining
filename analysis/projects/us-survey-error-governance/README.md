# Survey error and public-data governance

This project studies how survey data become public evidence: coverage,
nonresponse, imputation, respondent reporting, administrative benchmarks, and
the difference between net bias and absolute error.

The first bounded finding uses NBER Working Paper 35680. It applies a Total
Survey Error framework to eighteen CPS ASEC income and program-receipt
variables linked to administrative records over more than two decades. It
reports that respondent measurement error, especially failing to report receipt,
often dominates nonresponse and that absolute error can rise even when net bias
looks stable.

## Next test

The decisive extension is a source-by-year audit joining survey question,
response, contact and paradata, imputation, administrative definition, match
quality, correction, subgroup error, published estimate, and downstream policy
use.

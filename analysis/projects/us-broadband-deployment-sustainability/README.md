# Project: broadband deployment, targeting, and sustainability

## Question

Can federal broadband money reach the places that need it and keep networks
operating after construction?

## Decision rule

Keep provider-reported availability, map challenges, awards, construction,
completion, affordability, subscription, and long-term financial sustainability
separate. A planned or funded network is not yet durable household access.

## Current bounded finding

The [GAO finding](findings/us-gao-broadband-deployment-targeting-sustainability-2026-001.md)
shows that federal programs were distributing funds, but mapping challenges,
limited completion data, long timelines, and unresolved sustainability oversight
left the route from targeting to lasting service open.

The [dig-once coordination finding](findings/us-gao-broadband-dig-once-highway-coordination-2026-001.md)
adds the physical-infrastructure layer: state coordinators, provider
registration, highway-project notification, and cross-agency planning can be
present without evidence yet of completed fiber, affordable service, or
household use. It should be read as a separate mechanism, not merged into a
broadband coverage rate.

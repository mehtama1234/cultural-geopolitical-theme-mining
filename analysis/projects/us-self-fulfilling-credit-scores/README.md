# Project: US self-fulfilling credit scores

## Question

When does a credit score stop describing a problem and start making the problem harder to escape?

## Short first pass

Separate the score change, the lender's decision, the borrower's payment, default, and later access to housing or other services.

## Possible connection

A score can be treated as a neutral record while changing the price or availability of credit. For people already carrying a damaged record, that new cost may make the forecast come true.

## Decision rule

Move on after one causal score change, one observed lender response, and one later household outcome. Do not generalize a result for prior derogatory records to every borrower.

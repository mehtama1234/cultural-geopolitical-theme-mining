# Project: Mortgage lock-in and constrained housing mobility

## Question

When higher rates make a household's existing mortgage unusually valuable,
does the household stay, move down the housing ladder, or face a wider housing
and rental burden?

## Bounded result

The [machine-readable record](../../records/us-nber-mortgage-lockin-housing-mobility-2022-2024.json)
adds an observed/model-linked “stay” pathway. The NBER study reports a 25%
mobility reduction associated with mortgage lock-in, disproportionate reduction
in moves down the housing ladder, and modeled increases in aggregate house
prices and rents relative to a no-lock-in counterfactual.

This is a mobility constraint, not proof that staying was harmful for every
borrower or that a move would have improved household security.

## Decisive next evidence

Link the same borrower to mortgage rate, desired move, listing or sale,
replacement rent/mortgage, repair and insurance costs, household composition,
care/work needs, destination, and later security or exit.

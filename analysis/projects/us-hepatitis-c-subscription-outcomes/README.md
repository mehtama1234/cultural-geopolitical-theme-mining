# Project: hepatitis-C subscription model and realized outcomes

This project follows a public-payer financing design through treatment access,
health outcomes, spending, and fiscal return. It keeps program-level effects
separate from individual receipt, cure completion, and household experience.

The first bounded finding uses NBER Working Paper 35583 on Louisiana's 2019
Medicaid subscription model. The study reports tripled treatment volume, a 23%
decline in new diagnoses, $37 million in incremental antiviral spending, and
an estimated $266 million in five-year savings.

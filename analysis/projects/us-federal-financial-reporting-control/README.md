# Project: Federal financial reporting and usable public knowledge

## Question

When the U.S. government publishes consolidated financial statements, what
does the oversight record show about whether the underlying information is
reliable enough for public and institutional decisions?

## Decision rule

Keep reported totals, auditability, internal controls, corrective action, and
public trust separate. A correction or recommendation is not proof that the
underlying reporting system is already reliable, and a control weakness is not
proof that every published number is wrong.

## Current bounded finding

The [GAO finding](findings/us-federal-financial-reporting-control-001.md)
shows that the federal government can publish a consolidated financial report
while material weaknesses and open recommendations still limit how confidently
the public can use it.

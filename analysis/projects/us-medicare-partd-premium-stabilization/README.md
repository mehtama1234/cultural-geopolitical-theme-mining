# Project: Medicare Part D premium stabilization and enrollment

## Question

When the government subsidizes drug-plan premiums to prevent a price shock,
does it preserve medication access, and who carries the financial risk?

## Decision rule

Keep projected premiums, actual premiums, enrollment, medication access,
subsidy cost, insurer risk, and causal evaluation separate. A stable average
premium is not proof of stable medication use or long-term affordability.

## Current bounded finding

The [GAO finding](findings/us-gao-medicare-partd-premium-stabilization-2026-001.md)
shows that a 2025 demonstration reduced the immediate premium shock and
enrollment stayed broadly stable, while Medicare took on financial risk and the
causal effect remains under evaluation.

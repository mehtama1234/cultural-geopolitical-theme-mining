# Source search: employment-discrimination recourse and workplace change

**Search date:** 2026-09-22  
**Geography:** United States employment-discrimination enforcement  
**Status:** bounded pass complete; enforcement output is not the same as durable workplace protection

## Working question

What did the EEOC's FY 2025 enforcement route receive, resolve, and return to
workers?

## Opening source

| ID | Source | What it tells us | Status | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| US-EEOC-FY2025-APR | [EEOC FY 2025 Agency Performance Report](https://www.eeoc.gov/sites/default/files/2026-04/EEOC_APR_FY2025_APP_FY2027_508.pdf) | Reports inquiries, new charges, pending inventory, charge resolutions, monetary relief, equitable relief, and systemic investigations. | Official EEOC report | It does not follow each charging party through receipt, continued employment, workplace conditions, retaliation, or later security. |

## First pattern

```text
workplace discrimination concern
  -> inquiry or charge
  -> investigation, mediation, conciliation, or litigation
  -> monetary/equitable relief and compliance
  -> workplace change, safety, job continuity, trust, or exit [open]
```

In FY 2025, EEOC field offices received 269,755 inquiries and 88,201 new
charges. The agency resolved 90,743 charges, ended the year with 49,807
pending charges, and secured more than $528 million in pre-litigation monetary
relief for individuals. It reported targeted equitable relief in 99% of
conciliation agreements. Its systemic program resolved 444 investigations and
obtained more than $55 million for 1,823 workers.

## Counterpoint to keep visible

Charge resolutions and monetary relief are agency-level outputs. They do not
show how many workers received money, whether an employer changed behavior,
whether retaliation occurred, or whether the worker stayed, moved, or became
safer. A large resolution count can coexist with a large pending inventory.

## Decision rule

Use EEOC data for demand, administrative throughput, and reported relief. Do
not convert relief totals or favorable-resolution shares into a same-worker
recovery, prevention, or durable workplace-trust estimate.

# Project: tip reporting, hidden income, and workplace incentives

## Question

What can tax and revenue records reveal about missing tip income, and what do
they still fail to show about workers, employers, and households?

## Decision rule

Keep reported tips, actual tips, tax thresholds, employer records, missing income,
worker pay, employer conduct, and household security separate. A reporting gap
does not identify who caused it or who benefited.

## Current bounded finding

The [Census CES finding](findings/us-census-tip-reporting-bunching-missing-income-2005-2018-001.md)
reports that threshold bunching accounted for only about 1.5% of estimated
missing tips, despite more than $4 billion in estimated unreported tips annually
under the paper's assumptions.

# Project: defense installation maintenance, readiness, and quality of life

This project follows the often-hidden middle of state capacity: appropriated
maintenance need → workforce and information systems → facility condition →
mission readiness and the daily quality of life of people who use the
installation. It keeps a backlog, a funding rate, a risk statement, and a
realized mission or household outcome as separate stages.

The first bounded finding uses GAO-26-107255. DOD estimated a $285 billion
deferred-maintenance requirement in fiscal year 2025 and funded about 80% of
identified needs against a 90% goal. GAO found that DOD had not fully
determined the resulting mission and quality-of-life risks, and that work-order
data were not reliable enough for department-wide effectiveness assessment.

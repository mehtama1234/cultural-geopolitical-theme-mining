# Project: school attendance and usable participation

## Question

How widely does chronic absence interrupt ordinary access to school, and what
does the public data actually explain about it?

## Decision rule

Keep chronic absence, its reported reasons, school response, learning, safety,
belonging, and later outcomes separate. A high absence rate is a participation
signal, not a diagnosis of why students were absent or what happened afterward.

## Current bounded finding

The [federal attendance finding](findings/us-ed-chronic-absenteeism-2022-2023-001.md)
shows that 27.8% of students nationally were chronically absent in 2022–23,
with large state differences. The file does not follow the same student through
cause, support, learning, or return.

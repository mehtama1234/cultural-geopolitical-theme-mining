# Source search: school facility disruption

**Search date:** 2026-09-22  
**Geography:** United States public school districts  
**Status:** bounded pass complete; facility disruption and estimated lost days
are reported, while student recovery and long-term outcomes are not linked

## Working question

Can an official national survey show school buildings functioning as social
infrastructure, rather than treating facilities as a background capital issue?

## Opening source

| ID | Source | What it tells us | Status | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| US-GAO-SCHOOL-FACILITIES-2026 | [GAO-26-107970, K-12 Education: Facility Issues Led About One in Five Districts to Cancel School in 2024–25](https://files.gao.gov/reports/GAO-26-107970/index.html) | National district survey estimates cancellations, students and learning days affected, chronic facility problems, and district financing/maintenance challenges. | Official oversight report | It does not follow individual students, measure actual learning loss, or establish which repairs changed outcomes. |

## First pattern

```text
school facility condition
  -> plumbing, temperature, air, or other building problem
  -> closure or disrupted instruction
  -> lost learning time and changed family/work arrangements [estimated]
  -> student learning, health, trust, and later opportunity [open]
```

GAO estimates that 19% of districts canceled school for a facility issue in
2024–25, affecting about 2 million students and 3.5 million days of learning.
Nearly half of districts had chronic facility issues in at least one school,
and 55% considered chronic facility problems a serious or very serious
challenge to meeting facility needs.

## Counterpoint to keep visible

The report measures district-reported disruption, not a national test-score
effect. A closure can protect students from unsafe heat, plumbing, air, or
other conditions, and a district may provide make-up instruction or other
support. The closure count alone cannot rank the educational or health result.

## Decision rule

Use this source for the infrastructure-to-time-and-access boundary. Do not
turn estimated lost days into a causal estimate of learning loss, unequal harm,
family income loss, or later political meaning without linked student or
household evidence.

# Project: school facilities and interrupted learning

## Question

What happens when the physical condition of public schools interrupts the
ordinary route from attendance to learning?

## Decision rule

Keep a canceled school day, a facility problem, lost learning time, student
experience, and later educational outcomes separate. Infrastructure disruption
is a real social consequence, but it is not automatically a learning or
wellbeing effect for every student.

## Current bounded finding

The [GAO finding](findings/us-gao-school-facility-disruption-001.md) reports
that 19% of school districts canceled school for a facility issue in 2024–25,
affecting an estimated 2 million students and 3.5 million days of learning.
Nearly half of districts had chronic facility problems, while later student
outcomes and the distribution of disruption remain open.

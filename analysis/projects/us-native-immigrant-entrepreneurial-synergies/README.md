# Project: native–immigrant entrepreneurial synergies

This project tests whether the social composition of founding teams changes how
immigration translates into firm formation, employment, financing, product
markets, and exit. It keeps startup performance distinct from worker welfare
or a general claim about immigrant entrepreneurship.

The first bounded finding uses NBER Working Paper 33804. Mixed
native–immigrant founding teams are reported to outperform native-only and
immigrant-only teams on employment three years after founding, funding access,
funding-round size, and exit success. The paper points to labor-pool,
venture-capital, and product-market channels and reports an instrumental-
variables check based on native shares in university-degree programs.

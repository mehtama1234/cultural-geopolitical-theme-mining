# Project: Child-tax-credit reach and dollar receipt

This project separates four stages that are often collapsed: eligibility,
claiming, receipt, and household relief. A Census CES working paper links the
2022 CPS ASEC to IRS administrative data for tax year 2021. It reports that
91% of eligible taxpayers participated in the CTC/ACTC program, while dollar
participation was 85%, during the temporary American Rescue Plan expansion.

The safe theme is not “the credit reached 91%” or “15% received nothing.” The
two measures have different denominators and meanings. High taxpayer
participation can coexist with unequal dollar amounts, timing problems,
eligibility complexity, or insufficient household relief.

The decisive next artifact is a protected eligible-taxpayer-to-household ledger
joining eligibility, filing, claim, payment timing and amount, child expenses,
debt, food and housing room, later income, and wellbeing. The current linked
estimate does not close that household-outcome chain.

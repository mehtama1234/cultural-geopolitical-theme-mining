# Project: bankruptcy venue, firm survival, and public consequences

## Question

Can the legal venue chosen during corporate distress change whether a firm
closes and whether jobs remain, and who receives the benefit?

## Decision rule

Keep venue choice, court procedure, liquidation, creditor recovery,
establishment survival, employment, worker wellbeing, consumer outcomes, and
community effects separate. A firm surviving bankruptcy is not automatically a
fair or durable recovery for every stakeholder.

## Current bounded finding

The [Census CES finding](findings/us-census-bankruptcy-forum-shopping-delaware-employment-1990-2021-001.md)
reports a local causal effect for firms whose venue choice responds to distance
from Delaware: fewer liquidations and closures, shorter cases, greater creditor
recovery, and higher post-bankruptcy employment. The finding does not cover all
courts or prove equal stakeholder benefit.

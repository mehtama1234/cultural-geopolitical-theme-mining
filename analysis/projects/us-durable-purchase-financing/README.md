# Project: US durable purchases and borrowed room

## Question

When a temporary income payment arrives, does it help a household replace a needed vehicle or appliance, or does financing turn the payment into a longer obligation?

## Short first pass

Track the payment, the purchase, the loan, the monthly bill and the household's liquid buffer. Separate a useful replacement from a purchase that mainly brings future payment pressure.

## Possible connection

Temporary cash can move quickly into durable goods, especially vehicles. The lender then helps decide who can make the purchase and how much of the future household budget is committed.

## Decision rule

Move on after one source measures a temporary income shock and durable purchase together, one source shows the financing share, and one next dataset can follow repayment and later household room. Do not call all financed purchases harmful.

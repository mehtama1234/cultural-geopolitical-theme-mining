# Project: housing assistance, work, and household room

## Question

When a household enters or leaves federal housing assistance, what happens to work and earnings—and does that change reflect the housing support, changing need, or the kind of exit that occurred?

## Short end-to-end goal

Follow a person or household through housing assistance entry or exit, rent and location, time and care constraints, work, earnings, job quality, and later housing security.

```text
housing need and program access
  -> voucher, public housing, or project-based assistance
  -> rent burden, stability, place, time, care, and job-search conditions
  -> employment and earnings trajectory
  -> entry, continued assistance, positive exit, negative exit, or loss of support
  -> [open] housing quality, household security, dignity, and later civic meaning
```

The first bounded finding shows a reversal around entry and flattening around exit, but the study calls the associations suggestive because selection and pre-trends remain. The next test should identify the type of exit and join assistance with rent, location, care, health, job quality, benefits, and later housing outcomes.

## Writing rule

Say whether the record shows assistance, work, earnings, or housing stability. Do not turn an earnings trend into proof of a successful exit or a causal benefit.

# Project: Native housing, homelessness, and federal program fit

## Question

What happens when federal housing programs exist but their definitions,
funding, and application routes do not fit the ways housing insecurity appears
in Native communities?

## Decision rule

Keep program availability, funding adequacy, housing construction, tribal
control, and actual housing security separate. Do not treat “doubling up” as
either ordinary cultural support or proof of homelessness without observing
the conditions and stability of the arrangement.

## Current bounded finding

The [GAO finding](findings/us-gao-native-housing-homelessness-001.md) identifies
30 federal programs that can support Native housing, while nearly all Tribes
and Tribal Designated Housing Entities contacted said funding did not keep pace
with construction, rehabilitation, or maintenance needs. It also shows that
doubling up can be widespread but poorly aligned with federal homelessness
definitions.

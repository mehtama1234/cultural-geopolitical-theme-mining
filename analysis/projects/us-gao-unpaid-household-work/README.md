# Project: unpaid household work and the hidden economy of care

## Question

How much time and economic value sits in cooking, cleaning, caregiving, and
other household work that ordinary economic totals do not show clearly?

## Decision rule

Treat the GAO numbers as an estimate of time and replacement value, not as a
measure of wellbeing, fairness, exhaustion, or household security. Value is not
the same as pay, and time spent is not the same as freely chosen time.

## Current bounded finding

The [GAO finding](findings/us-gao-unpaid-household-work-001.md) estimates that
unpaid household work was worth at least $5.6–$6.0 trillion in 2024 and took
almost four hours a day for most people, with women overrepresented among those
doing the work. The estimate makes hidden social capacity visible, while the
distribution of burden and its later effects remain open.

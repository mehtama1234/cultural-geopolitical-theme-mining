# Project: US eviction as a household cascade

## Question

When an eviction order arrives, what happens next to housing, work, health, spending and credit—and which problems began before the court case?

## Short first pass

Separate the rent problem, the court order, the move or homelessness, and later outcomes. Compare tenants with similar cases but different judge assignments where possible.

## Possible connection

An eviction can turn a housing problem into a work, health and credit problem. The order may also be the visible point in a longer fall that began with lost earnings or financial distress.

## Decision rule

Move on after one source identifies a causal eviction effect, one source measures pre-court distress, and one next dataset can test legal help or rent support. Do not treat every eviction filing as an eviction order or every later problem as caused by the court event.

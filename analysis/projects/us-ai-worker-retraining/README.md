# AI-exposed workers and retraining outcomes

This project tests whether the adaptive capacity identified in occupation-level AI exposure measures becomes a usable route in observed workforce-program outcomes. The initial NBER source follows U.S. Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act training spells from 2012–2024 and compares trainees with matched people who received job-search assistance only.

## Initial finding

- [Finding 001: Training can improve earnings for AI-exposed workers, mainly through moving to less exposed work](findings/us-nber-ai-worker-retraining-returns-2026-001.md)

## Next test

The decisive next layer is access and durability: who was able to enter and finish training, what time and care costs they carried, whether the new job was stable and acceptable, and whether earnings gains persisted.

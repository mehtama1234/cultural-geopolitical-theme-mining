# FTC Consumer Sentinel 2025 extension v1

The [FTC testimony to the Joint Economic Committee on the rising scam
economy](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/ftc-testimony-jec-hearing-on-the-rising-scam-economy.pdf)
extends the administrative fraud frame beyond the 2024 Data Book.

For 2025, the testimony reports approximately 3 million consumer fraud
reports and $15.9 billion in reported losses. More than one million reports
concerned imposter fraud, with more than $3.5 billion in reported losses;
investment scams generated more than $7.9 billion. These are administrative
reports, not a population incidence rate or verified loss census.

The extension is useful because it separates scam-category composition from
the household survey's exposure and recovery measures. It also gives the
program a later comparison point for the FTC's 2024 reported-loss frame.
However, the reporting frame can move with awareness, reporting access,
category definitions, contributor coverage, and high-value cases. It cannot
establish that the same people measured by the Federal Reserve lost money,
received a remedy, changed financial behavior, or lost trust.

The next test remains a linked case or panel path:

```text
reported scam -> payment/account exposure -> loss -> dispute or recovery
  -> time and household tradeoff -> later trust, use, or exit
```

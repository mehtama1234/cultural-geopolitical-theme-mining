# Project: US housing-court legal help

## Question

Can legal help keep a rent dispute from becoming an eviction, and what problems remain after the court case?

## Short first pass

Separate legal representation, possession judgment, money judgment, eviction warrant and actual eviction. Then follow the household into credit, work, health and later housing.

## Possible connection

Legal help is a voice and access lever in the eviction chain. It may stop the visible court event even when the rent problem remains.

## Decision rule

Move on after one study measures a legal-help effect, one source measures the later eviction cascade, and one next dataset can follow the same tenant beyond court. Do not call court representation a complete housing solution.

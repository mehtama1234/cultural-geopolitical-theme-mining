# Project: US news selection and inflation belief

## Question

How does the news people receive shape the economic future they expect?

## Short first pass

Separate the price event, the story selected, the household's exposure, the inflation expectation and later spending, saving or political judgment.

## Possible connection

News is not a neutral list of all price changes. High inflation is more likely to be reported, and households may react more strongly to that news than to lower-price news.

## Decision rule

Move on after one study measures selected news and expectations, one source measures household price experience, and one next dataset can follow media exposure into action. Do not turn expectation into realized inflation or political behavior.

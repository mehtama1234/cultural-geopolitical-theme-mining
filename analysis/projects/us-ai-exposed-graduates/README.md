# Project: AI-exposed graduates and the first job

This project tracks the gap between completing education and converting that
credential into a usable first job. The initial Census working paper reports
that the most AI-exposed decile of college majors saw a five-percentage-point
fall in initial employment and a 13% fall in full-quarter initial earnings
after ChatGPT became available. About half of the earnings decline came from
lower pay within sectors and the rest from movement toward lower-paying
sectors.

The safe theme is not “AI permanently replaces graduates.” It is that a
credential can remain in place while the first labor-market step becomes less
available, less paid, or redirected into a different sector. The project must
keep major exposure, occupation, employer demand, later recovery, household
room, and worker control separate.

The decisive next artifact is a protected graduate-to-work ledger joining
major and exposure, graduation, applications, offers, employer and sector,
pay, job switching, location, debt, household support, later employment, and
career progression. The current paper does not provide that same-person
household and life-course bridge.

# Project: NATO resilience requirements and civilian military readiness

## Question

When an alliance defines resilience as a military requirement, which civilian
systems become part of readiness, and what remains unknown about implementation
and public burden?

## Decision rule

Keep the official requirement separate from national compliance, spending,
service performance, public consent, and crisis outcomes. A policy can widen the
responsibility of civilian systems without proving that those systems are ready.

## Current bounded finding

The [NATO finding](findings/us-nato-resilience-baseline-requirements-001.md)
shows that the 2026 baseline requirements connect whole-of-government and
whole-of-society planning, civilian infrastructure, and Host Nation Support to
military readiness. Implementation, cost allocation, and civilian experience
remain open.

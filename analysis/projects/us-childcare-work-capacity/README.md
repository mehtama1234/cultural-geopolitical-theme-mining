# Project: Childcare access, work capacity, and household stability

## Question

When childcare is costly, unavailable, or unreliable, how does a household
adapt its care and work, and what happens later to earnings, housing, health,
family time, and stability?

## Working chain

`care need -> price, availability, quality, and schedule fit -> paid, unpaid,
multiple, or no-care arrangement -> work hours, leave, job search, or exit ->
earnings and household room -> child, parent, and longer-term outcomes`

## Relationship to existing research

Existing care work measures cover eldercare, SNAP transitions, and childcare
time loss. This is a separate depth lane for the childcare-specific conversion
from inadequate care to work and household outcomes, while keeping paid care,
unpaid care, employer flexibility, and policy access distinct.

## Next test

Follow the same household through a dated care disruption, search and cost,
care arrangement, work-time change, pay or job change, child and parent
outcome, and later stability. Retain households with stable work, informal
care, and no reported loss as counterexamples.

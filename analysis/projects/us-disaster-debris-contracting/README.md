# Project: disaster debris contracting, information, and recovery visibility

This project tracks how advance contracts and public information shape the
usable middle between disaster damage and community recovery.

GAO found that only six of 16 selected governments had advance debris-removal
contracts. It also found different public-information timelines after the Los
Angeles wildfires and Hurricane Helene, confusion about FEMA roles, and fraud-
risk controls that did not fully follow leading practices.

The safe theme is that preparedness, field capacity, public visibility, and
household recovery are separate stages. A contract can speed response without
proving equitable cleanup, and a public map can improve contestability without
proving that a household is safe or recovered.

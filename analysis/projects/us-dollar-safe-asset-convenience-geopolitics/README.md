# Project: dollar safe-asset convenience and domestic/global power

This project follows a macro-financial infrastructure mechanism: safe-asset
design and market depth → domestic liquidity convenience and global relative
appeal → investor, central-bank, and repo behavior → fiscal, monetary, and
geopolitical consequences. It keeps market convenience distinct from household
financial wellbeing and state coercive power.

The first bounded finding uses NBER Working Paper 35742. Dollar safe-asset
convenience was about unchanged domestically in 2025 relative to 2019 and is
expected to persist over the next decade, while the relative richness of U.S.
dollar safe assets—especially Treasurys—versus other currencies fell
considerably. The paper identifies dollar repo as the highest-convenience
dollar safe asset.

# Project: disaster contracts and local economic recovery

## Question

When federal agencies buy disaster-response goods and services, does the
spending reach firms in the affected place, and can the government tell?

## Decision rule

Keep local-vendor preference, contract award, contract performance, local
economic recovery, and household recovery separate. A low recorded local share
may reflect weak local capacity, bad coding, advance contracts, or missed
opportunities; it is not by itself a failure rate.

## Current bounded finding

The [GAO finding](findings/us-gao-disaster-local-vendor-use-001.md) reports
that FEMA and the Army Corps had policies favoring local vendors but did not
monitor the data well enough to know how often they used them or helped restart
local economies. In GAO's selected disasters, FEMA's data identified about 3%
of $2.9 billion in obligations as local-vendor contracts.

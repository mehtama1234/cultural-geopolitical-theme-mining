## Result

The official Fair Work Commission search located a post-merits stay order in
the Waheed matter. This advances the remedy record beyond the earlier merits
decision, but it does not close lived implementation.

The 21 May 2026 merits decision found the deactivation unfair, ordered
reactivation within seven days, and ordered lost remuneration in principle
with the amount to be determined. A 16 June 2026 consent order stayed those
orders pending appeal. The stay was conditioned on Rasier Pacific paying
**$259.38 per week** into Waheed's nominated bank account from 28 May 2026
until the appeal decision or further order, and not seeking repayment of those
interim payments.

That is an observed **payment obligation and legal implementation condition**,
not a payment receipt. The public search returned no later final quantum,
appeal determination, bank-side receipt, actual account restoration, or
continued-work record.

## Stage distinction

```text
unfair deactivation
  -> reactivation and lost-remuneration orders
  -> appeal stay
  -> conditional interim payment obligation
  -> [open] payment receipt, actual access, final quantum
  -> [open] continued work, durable protection, or switching
```

The case therefore advances from **formal remedy ordered** to **conditional
interim payment obligation observed**, but remains open at verified receipt,
practical restoration, and recovery. It must not be coded as paid remedy or
practical exit.

## Why this matters

The order demonstrates that legal implementation can have its own protective
stage while the merits remedy is stayed: a worker may have a tribunal-ordered
interim income floor without a public record showing that the payment arrived
or that the original work route became usable again. This is a useful
counterexample to collapsing order, payment obligation, payment receipt, and
recovery into one endpoint.

The record still does not identify an alternative-work choice, household
outcome, trust change, non-retaliation, or later switching/non-use. It is a
deeper remedy-stage comparator, not a qualifying end-to-end episode.

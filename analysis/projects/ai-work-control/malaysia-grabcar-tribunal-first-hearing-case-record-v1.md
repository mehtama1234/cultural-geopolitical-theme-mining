# Malaysia GrabCar Tribunal first-hearing case record v1

**Status:** hearing independently corroborated by public reports; primary case
file and outcome open  
**Checked:** 2026-09-15

## Why this record matters

This is the first case-level proceeding currently visible in the Malaysia Act
872 packet. It crosses an important threshold: the Tribunal is no longer only a
legal design or appointed institution; a named platform and a concrete dispute
have reportedly reached a hearing. The record still does not establish what the
claimant requested, what legal provisions were applied, or what the Tribunal
ultimately ordered.

The hearing is evidence of institutional use, not evidence of a successful
worker remedy. “Human hearing” must not be silently converted into “effective
human review”: the current reports do not show the Tribunal's reasoning,
fact-finding result, or changed platform consequence.

## Interpretation

The case creates a new observable transition in the Malaysia sequence:

```text
Act 872 in force
  -> Tribunal and Council established
  -> first GrabCar proceeding reported
  -> evidence and witness examination [reported as pending]
  -> award / correction / payment / prevention [open]
```

It also broadens the decision surface beyond deactivation. A platform's trip
products, incentives, and booking rules can become a contractual dispute even
when the public record does not yet identify a specific automated model or
score. Future coding should first identify the rule or function at issue and
only then determine whether the dispute belongs in the algorithmic-management
subset.

## Decisive next acquisition

Obtain the Tribunal register entry, case number, hearing notice, filed claim,
next-hearing notice, transcript/minutes, settlement, or award. The minimum
upgrade to a primary case record is a document that identifies the case and
the relief sought. The minimum upgrade to a verified remedy outcome is an
award or official record showing the decision, reasoning, payment, access,
correction, or other changed consequence.

# Finding 119: An alliance response can be visible before deterrence is proven

**Status:** bounded Poland/NATO incident-to-response finding · **Checked:** 2026-09-20

## The bounded finding

On 10 September 2025, Poland reported repeated Russian-drone violations of
its airspace and requested NATO Article 4 consultations. NATO reported that
Polish F-16s, Dutch F-35s, Italian AWACS, NATO tanker support, and German
Patriots participated in the response, and that the North Atlantic Council
expressed solidarity with Poland.

Polish records then describe a further response: NATO announced Eastern Sentry
to strengthen air defense on the eastern flank, Poland summoned Russia's
chargé d'affaires and issued a protest, and a Polish advisory body addressed
disinformation resilience.

```text
airspace violation
  -> interception and national defense procedures
  -> Article 4 consultation and allied participation
  -> Eastern Sentry / eastern-flank reinforcement
  -> diplomatic and information response
  -> [open] deterrence, Russian adaptation, public consent, or durable security
```

**Why it matters:** this adds an observed incident-to-alliance-response stage
to the geopolitical atlas. It is different from a procurement contract,
delivery milestone, or public opinion measure. A state can demonstrate
procedural and allied response before it demonstrates durable deterrence.

**Do not overread:** the public record does not show that a particular weapon
system caused the response, that Eastern Sentry was sustained at a measured
level, that Russia changed behavior, or that Polish and allied publics found
the response legitimate. Official statements are not an independent incident,
damage, civilian-safety, or public-belief audit.

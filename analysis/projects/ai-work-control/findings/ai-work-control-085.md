# Finding 085: Domestic capacity does not become autonomy at the announcement stage

**Status:** provisional infrastructure/geopolitical realization synthesis · **Checked:** 2026-09-17

## The bounded finding

The retained US infrastructure, semiconductor, critical-mineral, and defense
records show a recurring distinction: money, facilities, and agreements can
advance before the country has a qualified output, a replaceable supplier, or
an observable external response. Domestic capacity can therefore rise while
dependence remains high. The relevant unit is not “strategic capacity” in the
abstract, but a dated application with inputs, ownership, qualified output,
delivery, alternatives, and a demonstrated ability to switch or bargain.

```text
foreign input or strategic exposure
  -> policy, investment, award, or procurement commitment
  -> facility, workforce, research, and production milestone
  -> qualified output, shipment, acceptance, and operational availability
  -> substitute supplier, inventory, portability, or recovery loop
  -> local incidence and public legitimacy
  -> state bargaining room or changed external behavior
```

## Evidence comparison

| System | Observed stage | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Critical minerals | USGS reports high import reliance for selected minerals; GAO identifies recycling and substitution possibilities and their technical bottlenecks | A specific cutoff, shortage, firm redesign, output loss, or successful supplier switch |
| CHIPS incentives/R&D | Commerce had awarded 49 projects across 24 companies and disbursed $13.1B by the cited 2026 vintage; 24 milestones were completed and 125 remained in progress | Operating yield, customer delivery, workforce retention, reduced import exposure, or strategic autonomy |
| Data centers/grid | National load estimates, county capacity/revenue, large-load governance, and observed load-drop events are separately recorded | Facility-level public benefit, household incidence, reliable compute output, ownership control, or replaceability |
| JASSM/LRASM procurement | Awards, subawards, recipient identity, facility capability context, production support, testing, and a Polish 2026–2030 delivery plan are visible as separate stages | Specific accepted quantity, shipment, fielding, inventory, substitute qualification, or partner/adversary response |

The units are deliberately not pooled: mineral/application, program project,
data-center or grid event, defense award/order, facility, and country are
different. The comparison is a realization rule, not an autonomy index.

## Four clocks that must remain separate

### 1. Commitment clock

Appropriation, award, agreement, possible-sale authorization, or a construction
announcement establishes intent and a governance relationship. It does not
establish production. The CHIPS award and JASSM agreement records are useful
precisely because they identify the commitment without promoting it to output.

### 2. Capacity clock

Construction, workforce funding, facility certification, production support,
and flight-science testing show that complements are being assembled. They can
be meaningful intermediate capability, but they do not tell us whether a named
customer received a qualified product at the required volume and date.

### 3. Operating clock

Energization, qualified yield, shipment, acceptance, fielding, inventory, and
service reliability are closer to realized capability. Even here, a single
delivery or operating event does not show maintenance, surge capacity,
portability, or replacement under stress.

### 4. Leverage clock

Leverage requires a relational outcome: a supplier accepts a condition, a
partner changes behavior, a state refuses or switches, or an adversary adjusts
because continued operation, exit, or retaliation is credible. No reviewed
record establishes that final arrow for these cases.

## The critical-mineral complication

Domestic manufacturing can remain dependent on foreign processing, purity,
technology, or recycling feedstock. Import reliance is an exposure measure;
the strategic question is whether the exact battery, semiconductor, defense,
or grid application has a qualified substitute at the required performance,
price, volume, and time. Recycling is not an operating alternative until
collection, sorting, processing yield, permitting, and demand for recovered
material work together at scale.

This gives the atlas a useful counterexample to “more domestic production means
more autonomy.” Production value can increase while processing concentration
and supplier dependence remain. Conversely, import reliance can fall while
ownership, software, or specialized equipment remains externally controlled.

## The governance and distribution layer

Public money and capacity have local consequences before strategic benefits are
proven. Facilities can require power, water, land, housing, skills, and public
infrastructure. Semiconductor incentives can create jobs or training while
leaving workforce retention, child-care access, environmental burden, and
customer supply continuity unmeasured. Data-center growth can generate county
revenue while households face uncertain rate allocation and reliability risk.

These are not arguments against investment. They define the required bridge:
who pays, who benefits, who controls the system, and who can refuse or replace
it. Local incidence and geopolitical leverage must be reported as separate
outcomes unless the same project, place, and time record supports the join.

## Counterexamples and limits

- A high import-reliance number does not prove a cutoff, coercion, or realized
  economic harm.
- A completed milestone or disbursement is not qualified output or customer
  delivery.
- A facility certificate or broad capability description is not assignment of a
  specific defense work package.
- A planned delivery window is not proof of delay, shipment, acceptance, or
  operational availability.
- A successful test can coexist with no fielding, sustainment, or inventory
  change.
- Domestic ownership may improve control, but ownership alone does not prove
  substitute supply, portability, or bargaining power.
- The absence of a public realization record is not proof that realization did
  not occur; it is a bounded non-observation.

## Next decisive test

Choose one material/application pair and one named project or procurement
route. Build a compact ledger containing:

```text
input disruption or policy change
  -> contract, shipment, price, purity, inventory, and exposure
  -> redesign, substitute qualification, recycling, or production change
  -> accepted output and customer delivery
  -> worker/local incidence and ownership/control rights
  -> supplier switch, continuity during stress, or external response
```

The minimum promotion event is an identifier-bearing production, shipment,
acceptance, or customer-use record. The minimum leverage event is an observed
switch, refusal, concession, regulatory response, alliance action, or changed
external behavior tied to that capability. Until those events exist, the safe
claim is staged capacity and dependence—not autonomy or leverage.

# Finding 066: Defense announcements, awards, tests, and production plans are different capability stages

**Status:** provisional state-capacity realization ledger · **Checked:** 2026-09-14

## The bounded finding

The defense evidence now supports a staged realization ladder, not one
measure of geopolitical power:

| Stage | What the current records show | What remains unverified |
|---|---|---|
| Strategic intent and procurement | Poland announced a roughly $735 million JASSM-ER agreement with a stated future delivery window; the official record later gave a different 2028–2030 schedule | Actual delivery, inventory, acceptance, and operational use |
| Contract and supplier routing | A bounded USAspending extract identifies a $1.14 billion JASSM/LRASM parent-award subaward surface with 51 named recipients | Delivered components, production quantities, facility output, and replaceability |
| Production capacity | A program announcement describes production-capacity investment and a 225,000-square-foot facility | Actual output, delivery rate, workforce incidence, and resilience |
| Integration and test | Official or public records describe LRASM integration flight tests and a two-missile loaded flight-test event | Acceptance, readiness, deployment, combat effectiveness, or deterrence |
| Regional industrial plan | Poland, the United States, Estonia, and Latvia announced Barracuda co-production, with Polish assembly followed by planned production and later competence-building | Commissioning, units produced, local-content share, financing allocation, and supplier independence |
| Financing and partnership | A reported $4 billion Foreign Military Financing agreement and reciprocal-access arrangements broaden the institutional relationship | Program-specific allocation, changed bargaining terms, alliance cohesion, or external behavior |

The defensible synthesis is: **official defense records show a widening chain
from intent and procurement toward production and institutional follow-up, but
they do not yet establish realized capability or geopolitical leverage.**

## Why the stages must remain separate

A procurement agreement can be strategically meaningful while still being a
future delivery promise. A subaward map can reveal concentration and component
diversity without showing whether the components reached a functioning
system. A flight test can establish an integration milestone without proving
accepted inventory or operational readiness. A production plan can name local
partners and intended technology transfer without proving that local learning
or output has occurred.

The distinction is especially important for state-capacity claims. Capability
requires more than resources and announcements:

```text
budget / financing
  -> contract and supplier route
  -> production and integration
  -> acceptance and deployable inventory
  -> operational use and sustainment
  -> observed state choice, external response, or leverage
```

The current evidence is strongest through the middle stages and remains open
at acceptance, deployment, switching, and consequence.

## Source roles and boundaries

| Source family | Role in this pass | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| DoD and DSCA | official contract, security-cooperation, and financing context | stated policy, notification, or agreement is not delivery or readiness |
| Polish government and national-security institutions | partner-side procurement, industrial, and competence-building claims | official plan is not commissioned production or realized local capability |
| USAspending | award and recipient routing | award value is not output, performance, or operational effect |
| DVIDS | public operational/test context | imagery and captions rarely establish quantity, acceptance, or strategic result |
| contractor disclosure | production and integration claims | management or program statements need independent realization checks |

## Counterexamples kept visible

- The JASSM-ER record contains different official delivery schedules; a stated
  window is not a stable delivery fact.
- A large financing agreement is broader than the Barracuda program and cannot
  be assigned to it without an allocation record.
- A named recipient can appear across multiple facilities under one parent,
  so recipient count is not supplier independence.
- A production target of “thousands” is a reported plan, not an observed
  production count.
- Reciprocal access and competence-building language can signal institutional
  direction without demonstrating changed bargaining power or external
  behavior.

## Next decisive test

The next state-capacity pass should add milestone evidence with stable program
and facility identifiers: award action, production start, unit count, delivery,
acceptance, inventory, deployment, sustainment, local-content share, workforce
and supplier data, and any observed external or alliance response. The ledger
must preserve planned, reported, corroborated, and verified statuses separately.

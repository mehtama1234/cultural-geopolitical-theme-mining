# Finding 104: Infrastructure growth creates public voice before it creates public agreement

**Status:** bounded infrastructure public-response finding · **Checked:** 2026-09-20

## The bounded finding

In the Virginia Golden-Mars transmission case, the State Corporation
Commission reported more than **600 registered public speakers** in December
2025 and hundreds of pages of written comments. Residents raised undergrounding,
home-value, landscape, appearance, and possible health concerns. The Commission
first conditionally selected Route 4 as the path it found to have the smallest
community impact, found undergrounding infeasible, and adopted agency
mitigation recommendations. Route 4 required Loudoun County School Board
consent; after the School Board did not consent, the SCC approved Route 3A on
June 29, 2026.

This adds a real public-response stage to the infrastructure chain. It does
not show that residents agreed, that comments caused the route decision, or
that the project produced a measured property, health, bill, or legitimacy
outcome.

## The chain

```text
large-load growth and transmission need
  -> locally concentrated property, landscape, and health concerns
  -> hearings and written public participation
  -> conditional route selection and mitigation
  -> land-control constraint and final Route 3A substitution [observed at institutional level]
  -> construction, compensation, resident acceptance, trust, or action [open]
```

The larger insight is that infrastructure legitimacy is not the same thing as
infrastructure capacity. A state can create a formal voice channel and make a
route decision—or change the route because of an institutional land-control
constraint—while leaving the lived distribution of costs and the durability of
public acceptance unresolved.

## Limits and next field

- Speaker and comment counts are not representative public opinion.
- The SCC summary does not identify which comments changed the decision.
- The case is a transmission comparator; it does not prove that data centers
  alone caused the project or each resident concern.
- The next decisive artifact is a construction/property/compensation or
  post-decision public-response record tied to the affected place or resident.

# USMCA Alimentos Grole labor-remedy follow-up

**Checked:** 2026-10-19  
**Status:** official facility-level resolution; partial same-case remedy surface

## Observed route

The U.S. Trade Representative announced the successful resolution of a
USMCA Rapid Response Labor Mechanism matter at Alimentos Grole in Ciudad
Obregón, Sonora, Mexico. The petition was submitted by current and former
workers affiliated with SITIES after alleged retaliation and formation of a
company-aligned internal labor committee. The U.S. request for review was
submitted on August 28, 2025; Mexico concluded that a denial of rights had
occurred on October 10, 2025; and the resolution was announced October 19,
2026.

The facility-level remediation reported by USTR includes:

- nine unlawfully dismissed workers reinstated with full backpay and benefits;
- two workers paid full severance based on their preference;
- dissolution of the company-aligned internal labor committee;
- a union-access protocol for SITIES;
- HR/labor-relations restructuring, neutrality rules, zero-tolerance policy,
  training, worker notices, and an anonymous complaint mechanism; and
- Mexican monitoring, worker-rights training, and reporting channels.

The United States resumed liquidation of unliquidated entries from the
facility after concluding that the situation had been remediated.

## Stage boundary

| Stage | What is public | What remains open |
|---|---|---|
| Dated condition and actor | Named facility, petition, alleged retaliatory dismissals, U.S./Mexico review | Individual worker identifiers and full initiating chronology |
| Effort and action | Worker/union petition and intergovernmental review | Individual search, money/time burden, or rejected alternatives |
| Protected outcome | Reinstatement, backpay/benefits, severance, union access, complaint route | Independent worker receipt, adequacy, actual use, and household effect |
| Institutional response | Facility remediation, Mexican monitoring, U.S. trade response | Durable enforcement, recurrence, non-retaliation, and bargaining power |
| Meaning, recovery, exit | Collective worker voice and formal remedy are reported | Direct worker trust/meaning, later work, switching, persistence, and exit |

This is a facility-level official resolution, not a complete same-worker
recovery episode. “Full backpay and benefits” is retained as an official
reported remedy, not independently verified receipt.

## Source question

What does AI require to create useful capability in low- and middle-income countries, and how might concentration of the AI value chain change power within and across countries?

## Report frame

The World Bank organizes the report around three linked paths:

1. **Adopt:** use existing tools;
2. **Adapt:** fit systems to local language, data, institutions, and needs;
3. **Advance:** build frontier models and the infrastructure that powers them.

The report says adopting and adapting are more feasible for most developing countries than building frontier systems. It also warns that importing a tool is not enough if local complements are weak or the deployment context does not match the development context.

## Four practical foundations

The World Bank's AI-readiness work groups the foundations into four “Cs”:

- **Connectivity:** reliable internet and power;
- **Compute:** data centers and secure cloud;
- **Context:** good data that fit the local setting;
- **Competency:** people with the skills to use, adapt, and govern systems.

The report also adds the institutions, capital, procurement, interoperability, and enforcement needed to turn those foundations into durable choice.

## What this source establishes

- Frontier AI production is concentrated, while adopting existing tools is more feasible than building the frontier for most developing countries.
- Local fit and complementary capacity are part of whether AI produces value.
- Public procurement and government use can influence local capability, not only service delivery.
- AI can change power within countries as well as between countries.

## What it does not establish

- It does not rank countries by AI sovereignty.
- It does not prove that provider concentration causes coercion.
- It does not show that every imported system fails to fit local needs.
- It does not provide a causal estimate of AI's effect on national growth, jobs, or political stability.
- It does not substitute for a country case with contracts, outcomes, switching costs, and worker or citizen evidence.

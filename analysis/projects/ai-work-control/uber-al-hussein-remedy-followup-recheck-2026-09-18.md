# Al Hussein remedy follow-up recheck v1

**Status:** bounded public-document recheck; payment quantum and implementation remain open  
**Checked:** 2026-09-18

## Result

The Fair Work Commission decision in Al Hussein v. Rasier Pacific found the
deactivation unfair and ordered reactivation. It directed the parties to
confer on lost-pay quantum and report the outcome or status by 7 November 2025.
The concurrent PR792947 order required reactivation and deemed work during the
interruption, but did not quantify the lost-pay amount.

A targeted search of the Commission's public decisions and orders route found
no later public quantified lost-pay order, payment record, actual account
restoration record, or post-order work/switching record in the checked route.
This is a bounded non-observation, not proof that a private agreement, payment,
or restoration did not occur.

## Current chain

```text
deactivation
  -> adjudicated unfairness
  -> reactivation plus deemed-work order
  -> lost-pay conference/status direction
  -> [open] quantified order, receipt, actual access, and later choice
```

This case is a useful counterpoint to Mansoor: formal access restoration can be
ordered while the financial remedy remains unresolved in the public record.
The safe coding is **reactivation observed; lost-pay quantum and lived
implementation open**.

## Next decisive test

The smallest useful upgrade is a later Commission direction/order or a lawful
worker-side record showing the conference outcome, payment receipt, actual
account status, and later work or non-use. Do not infer a payment from the
reactivation order or the passage of the reporting deadline.

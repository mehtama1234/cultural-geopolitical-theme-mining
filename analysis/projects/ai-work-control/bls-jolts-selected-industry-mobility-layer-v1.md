# BLS JOLTS selected-industry mobility layer v1

This is the conditioned companion to the [national JOLTS mobility layer](bls-jolts-national-mobility-layer-v1.md).
It preserves annual means for 2022 and 2025 in manufacturing, professional
and business services, education and health services, and leisure and
hospitality.

The comparison demonstrates sector heterogeneity. For example, in 2025 the
annual mean quits rate was 1.39% in manufacturing, 2.34% in professional and
business services, 1.93% in education and health services, and 3.93% in
leisure and hospitality. The openings, hires, layoffs, and total-separation
rates are retained in the machine-readable record rather than reduced to a
single mobility score.

These are seasonally adjusted establishment rates. Their denominators differ
by measure, and the data do not follow workers or reveal why a transition
occurred. Sector differences therefore identify where the next worker-level
test should look; they do not establish that one sector offers more autonomy,
better jobs, or stronger bargaining power.

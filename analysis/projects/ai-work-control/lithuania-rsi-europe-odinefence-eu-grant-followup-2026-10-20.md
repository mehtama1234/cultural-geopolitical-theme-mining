# Lithuania defense-industrial grant and capacity follow-up

**Checked:** 2026-10-20  
**Status:** official funding and planned-capacity milestone; production and operational effects remain open

## Observed route

The Lithuanian Ministry of National Defence reports that a project prepared by
Lithuanian defense company RSI Europe with Estonian partner Odin Defence was
selected for funding under the European Defence Industry Programme's
“Energetic Components” call. The ministry reports **€29.5 million** in EU
funding, one of eight selected projects from 83 applications, within a total
of up to €166.4 million for the selected projects.

The ministry says RSI Europe plans to expand in Panevėžys district. It reports
that the planned investment is intended to create new jobs, attract qualified
specialists, support regional economic development, strengthen national
defense-industrial potential, and improve supply-chain security and production
capacity for Lithuania and allies.

## Stage boundary

| Stage | What is public | Still open |
|---|---|---|
| Funding and selection | Named firms, named EU call, selection among 83 applications, and €29.5m reported EU funding | Grant agreement conditions, disbursement, co-financing, milestones, and procurement |
| Industrial capacity | Planned Panevėžys expansion and stated jobs/specialist/production aims | Facility construction, workforce, equipment, output, unit cost, and delivery |
| Security effect | Ministry frames the project as supply-chain and defense-capacity strengthening | Accepted systems, availability, readiness, operational use, deterrence, and civilian protection |
| Regional conversion | Planned contribution to regional economic development | Actual jobs, wages, local suppliers, spillovers, and persistence |

This is an official funding-selection and planned-capacity milestone, not proof
of a functioning plant, delivered energetic components, accepted military
capability, or realized regional benefit.

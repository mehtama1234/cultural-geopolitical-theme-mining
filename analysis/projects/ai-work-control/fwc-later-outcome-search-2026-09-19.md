# Fair Work later-outcome search: alternative employment is not recovery

**Checked:** 2026-10-19
**Status:** official-route acquisition memo; no payment-receipt claim

## Search question

Can a public employment-remedy decision be followed far enough to show the
worker's alternative, the material trade-off, compensation, payment, and later
work or exit in one named episode?

## What the official route supplies

The Fair Work Commission search produced several useful cases, but none of the
checked public documents closes the full episode:

| Case | Observed middle | Still missing |
|---|---|---|
| **Pavitt, [2025] FWC 1448** | The decision identifies an offered alternative location, longer commute, the worker's employment context, and a reduced six-week redundancy payment of $10,032 | Actual payment receipt, whether the alternative was accepted, later job duration, household effect, trust, or exit |
| **Adcon/Smith, [2024] FWC 2775** | The decision records dismissal, financial stress and family pressure, no discussion of alternative employment, unpaid notice/redundancy entitlements, and a compensation remedy | Payment receipt, later employment, household recovery, recurrence, and whether the replacement opportunity materialized |
| **Johnson, FWC Bulletin May 2024** | The summary records alternative work within two weeks, a non-local location, longer local-search time, and a compensation reduction | Payment receipt, job durability, reason for staying/leaving, household outcome, and later action |
| **FWC Bulletin September 2025** | A gaming-company tester was classified as an employee, dismissal was found unfair, and reinstatement, continuity, and about US$9,450 in backpay were ordered | Whether reinstatement occurred, whether backpay was received, later work quality, recurrence, bargaining, or exit |
| **Mudiyanselage, [2025] FWC 1570** | The Commission's decision and September 2025 bulletin record persistent underpayment, FWO involvement, a forced resignation, new employment, a documented $4,000 partial payment, and a $27,424.88 compensation order; the worker declined reinstatement because of broken trust and emotional stress | No later public payment/compliance record, final receipt, residual wage or superannuation recovery, new-job durability, household outcome, or later trust/action record was located in the official route checked |

These cases strengthen the alternative-work and trade-off stages. They do not
qualify as practical-exit episodes because a decision or compensation amount
is not the same as money received, restored work, or a freely usable
alternative.

The September 2025 reinstatement example adds a sharper distinction: even an
order for reinstatement, continuity, and backpay is still an institutional
remedy stage until a later record shows compliance and actual return. It is
stronger than a compensation calculation, but it is not the same as a verified
post-order outcome.

Mudiyanselage is the closest retained case to a lived end-to-end route because
it directly records forced exit, a constrained alternative job, partial
payment, a formal compensation order, and worker-linked broken trust. The
official decision, order, 2025 case bulletin, and the Commission's current
decision/bulletin route were rechecked on 2026-10-19 for the case name,
U2025/2081, and PR788459. No later public compliance, final-payment, or
employment follow-up for this case was located. This is a bounded
non-observation, not proof that the order was unpaid or that recovery did not
occur.

## Bounded finding

Employment remedies can make the worker's option set more visible than consumer
records often do: the public decision may show another job, lower pay, travel,
locality, or a failed search. But the remedy record still commonly ends at an
order or calculation. The missing handoff is:

```text
dismissal or job loss
  -> alternative job/search and pay/time trade-off
  -> adjudicated compensation or reinstatement order
  -> payment receipt and accepted work
  -> durable employment, household security, trust, bargaining, or exit
```

The safe interpretation is therefore **alternative employment and formal
remedy visibility**, not recovered livelihood or practical exit.

## Next acquisition rule

Prioritize a named case with a later compliance, enforcement, payroll, or
reinstatement record. The minimum promotion fields are:

1. the original job-loss or control event and date;
2. identified alternative work or a documented failed search;
3. pay, hours, commute, status, and household trade-off;
4. the remedy order and payment/reinstatement receipt;
5. a later work or non-work observation; and
6. recurrence, trust, collective action, switching, or exit where public.

Do not use the amount ordered, the existence of a payslip, or the presence of
alternative employment as a proxy for payment, recovery, or free choice.

# Fair Work Commission route recheck: Westrip and Meadley

**Checked:** 2026-09-28  
**Purpose:** compare the two closest retained public employment-remedy cases
against the broad end-to-end promotion gate.

## Result

Neither case closes the required route from a dated employment shock through
alternatives, effort or trade-off, institutional response, verified remedy,
and later recovery, meaning, action, or exit.

| Case | Strongest observed stages | Decisive missing stage |
|---|---|---|
| **Westrip v Madinah Wheels & Tyre Pty Ltd [2023] FWC 2632** | Dismissal; five-week transition; alternative employment; interim income loss; non-return; $3,744 compensation order | Worker-level payment receipt and later job/household recovery |
| **Meadley v Sort Worx Pty Ltd [2013] FCA 1012** | Unfair-dismissal order; employer noncompliance; worker enforcement; officially reported compensation payment; failed reinstatement; further court remedy | Alternative employment or non-use before enforcement, and later work/household recovery |

## Evidence check

The primary Westrip decision orders the employer to pay **$3,744 gross less
tax** within 21 days. It records that the worker obtained another job and did
not seek reinstatement, but it does not record payment execution or receipt.
The current public search surfaced the decision and secondary reproductions,
not a payment instrument, compliance filing, or later worker follow-up.

The Fair Work Commission's Meadley case summary reports that the company paid
the compensation but did not reinstate the worker, after which the worker
sought court enforcement. It does not report alternative employment,
substitute earnings, later work, household effects, or durable trust/exit.

## Interpretation boundary

These cases should remain complementary comparators, not a pooled or combined
episode. Westrip supplies the alternative-employment and non-return side but
only an ordered remedy. Meadley supplies reported remedy implementation but
not the alternative route. Combining them would manufacture a same-worker
chain across different people and years.

## Next decisive test

Search for a public compliance or payment follow-up tied to Westrip. If none
exists, retain Westrip as an alternative-to-remedy comparator and move to a
different source only if it exposes, under one stable episode key:

```text
alternative or non-use
  -> effort / money / time trade-off
  -> institutional response
  -> verified remedy receipt or defined outcome follow-up
  -> later recovery, trust, action, recurrence, or exit
```

No new end-to-end finding is promoted from this recheck.

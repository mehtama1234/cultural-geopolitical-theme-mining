# BLS JOLTS and union industry context layer v1

This bridge places two 2025 BLS views beside one another for four sectors:
JOLTS establishment mobility and CPS formal union membership. The sectors are
manufacturing, professional and business services, education and health
services, and leisure and hospitality.

The descriptive contrast is useful: the 2025 annual mean quits rate ranged
from 1.39% in manufacturing to 3.93% in leisure and hospitality, while the CPS
union-membership rate ranged from 2.1% in professional and business services
to 8.2% in education and health services. The measures refer to different
populations and sampling frames, so the contrast identifies conditioning
questions rather than a union effect.

The safe interpretation is:

```text
sector labor-market context + formal representation context
  -> identify worker-level comparison cells
  -> test schedule, pay, control, grievance, household, and exit outcomes
```

The bridge does not establish that representation lowers quits, that quits
measure worker power, or that sector differences cause household or political
effects. BLS also warns that the 2025 CPS annual estimates exclude October and
are not strictly comparable with earlier annual averages. The next study must
use compatible worker/workplace units, dates, and a defined exposure or event.

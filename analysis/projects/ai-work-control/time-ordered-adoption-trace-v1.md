# Time-ordered adoption trace v1

**Status:** working comparison; incomplete by design  
**Checked:** 2026-09-11  
**Project:** AI, work, and control

## Purpose

This trace follows a change through time instead of treating adoption as the outcome:

```text
intention -> investment or rule -> system function -> exposure -> measured result -> distribution -> dependence
```

An empty cell is evidence of a missing link, not permission to fill the gap with a story.

## Current cases

## What can be compared now

1. Intention is often measured before worker exposure.
2. Capital and production-process changes can be visible before a clean labor result.
3. The strongest firm results still do not identify the workplace function of the AI system.
4. Worker evidence identifies functions better than most firm-level productivity studies.
5. Infrastructure evidence explains a possible constraint on adoption, but not who captures the value.

## Required fields for the next version

- exact date of intention, purchase, deployment, and first worker exposure;
- named software, model, data, cloud, chip, power, and labor provider;
- system function and decision right changed;
- worker group, contract, location, and outside options;
- output, time, pay, promotion, discipline, health, and exit measures;
- appeal, override, audit, and bargaining path;
- ownership and cross-border dependency;
- counterfactual: what would likely have happened without the change.

## Interpretation rule

Do not call the chain complete when a source only reports intention, adoption, a scenario, or an industry average. A finding can move outward to social or geopolitical meaning only after the missing links are named and the inference is labeled.

# France: Platform Work Directive implementation record

**Checked:** 2026-09-15  
**Country:** France  
**Directive:** Directive (EU) 2024/2831  
**Current status:** official coordination record; later national measure not identified in this pass

## Evidence

An official written question in the French Senate asked the responsible
ministry about the timetable for transposing Directive (EU) 2024/2831. The
ministerial answer published on 13 February 2025 confirms the 2 December 2026
transposition deadline and states that the European Commission convened the
first transposition working group in December 2024, with experts from each
Member State and social-partner representatives. It says that discussions were
expected to continue through 2025.

The reviewed official record establishes French participation in the EU-level
coordination process. In this pass it does not establish a French draft bill,
adopted transposition measure, effective date, competent national authority for
the new rights, or an exercised remedy. The absence of a later record in this
search is not evidence that no French preparation or measure exists.

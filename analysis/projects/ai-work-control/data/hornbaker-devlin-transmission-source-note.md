# Hornbaker–Devlin transmission project source note

**Checked:** 2026-09-15  
**Source:** [Dominion Energy Hornbaker–Devlin project page](https://www.dominionenergy.com/about/delivering-energy/electric-projects/power-line-projects/hornbaker-devlin)

## Observation

Dominion describes a proposed double-circuit 230 kV transmission line and
switching station intended to support data-center growth and increased
reliability in the Prince William County Innovation Park and southern Data
Center Opportunity Zone Overlay District. The project page states that the
proposal was submitted to the Virginia State Corporation Commission in March
2025.

## Boundary

This is a proposed infrastructure and utility-planning event, not evidence of
construction completion, energization, facility-specific load, customer
acceptance, cost recovery, reliability improvement, or household benefit. The
page does not identify which named data-center customer caused or received the
project, nor does it link the project to either reported large-load drop event.

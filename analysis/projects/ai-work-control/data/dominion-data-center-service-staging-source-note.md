# Dominion data-center service-staging source note

**Checked:** 2026-09-15  
**Source:** [Dominion Energy Virginia Data Center Requests](https://www.dominionenergy.com/virginia/large-business-services/data-center-requests)

## Observation

Dominion publishes a site-specific service-planning process for new data
centers. The page says service is staged around expected load and that a site
under 50 MW may often be served from existing distribution infrastructure,
while a site over 50 MW is likely to require a transmission extension or new
substation. It also asks for a site-specific power plan and load profile.

## Why it matters

This is an explicit institutional mechanism connecting customer scale to the
kind of grid infrastructure that may be required. It makes the transmission
and substation pathway legible before a public case can be joined to a named
facility or account.

## Boundary

The page is general service guidance. It does not identify a customer, confirm
a threshold crossing, establish an interconnection approval, prove that a
particular project caused Hornbaker–Devlin or another line proposal, or show a
bill, payment, cost recovery, reliability outcome, or household effect.

# US LRASM sustainment architecture source note

**Checked:** 2026-09-18

## Observed institutional stage

The Department of the Navy FY2025 Operation and Maintenance budget describes
the Weapons Maintenance activity as covering operational, intermediate, depot,
engineering, logistics, hardware, software, and reliability support. Its
Air-Launched Missile Rework Program lists LRASM among the systems supported.
The Navy's FY2025 LRASM budget materials separately describe production support
that includes production engineering and the stand-up of training and
sustainment programs for the C-3 transition.

**Official sources:** [Department of the Navy FY2025 O&M budget](https://www.secnav.navy.mil/fmc/fmb/Documents/25pres/OMN_Book.pdf); [Navy FY2025 LRASM budget justification](https://www.secnav.navy.mil/fmc/fmb/Documents/25pres/WPN_Book.pdf)

## Promotion boundary

These documents establish a budgeted/institutional sustainment architecture,
not a dated maintenance action, readiness rate, serviceable inventory,
repair turnaround, or deployed-unit outcome. They also do not prove that the
C-3 transition was completed. The source therefore advances the sustainment-
governance stage while leaving operational performance open.

```text
fielding -> sustainment architecture -> maintained inventory/readiness -> use
 observed          observed                     open                 open
```

# NIK Poland defense-budget 2022 source note

Source: [Najwyższa Izba Kontroli, execution of the 2022 state budget, part 29 — national defence](https://bip.nik.gov.pl/plik/id,27864.pdf)

Retrieval: 2026-09-14. The official PDF was read through the indexed official
PDF text. The direct host returned an anti-bot interstitial to a command-line
retrieval, so this note preserves the exact page/line locations used in the
finding rather than presenting the blocked response as the source artifact.

## JASSM-specific passage

Page 19, lines 967–985 of the indexed PDF text says NIK reviewed nine DD Form
645 settlement forms covering Q4 2021 and Q1–Q3 2022, totaling PLN 210,543.7
thousand. For four contracts, the Armament Inspectorate lacked source
documents confirming the subject and value of deliveries or services totaling
PLN 1,942.0 thousand. For one settlement concerning JASSM-ER missile
production costs, the agency used a “Program management review” presentation
that showed contract milestones (dates) and described the production process,
but contained no numerical value connected to the settlement. NIK says the
result was lack of knowledge about the actual realization and effects of the
contracts and unreliable recording of nearly PLN 2 million in advances.

The footnote identifies the relevant line as “Pociski JASSM–ER – koszty
produkcji, abonament” (“JASSM-ER missiles — production costs, subscription”).

## General sampled-contract passage

Page 19, lines 952–966 says NIK examined eight advance-funded contracts from
2014–2021 totaling PLN 597,763.9 thousand that had not been settled. The
report gives examples of delayed deliveries; one separate contract had been
amended three times, changing delivery dates and equipment quantity, and after
the third amendment only half of the planned equipment—32 units—had been
delivered by the end of 2022. The report does not identify that sampled
contract as JASSM in the cited passage, so this is retained only as a general
Polish defense-procurement control comparator.

## Boundary

This note supports an audit/control finding about evidence quality and advance
settlement for a JASSM-ER production-cost line. It does not establish the
number of JASSM-ER missiles produced, accepted, delivered, or fielded. The
separate 32-unit example is not assigned to JASSM. Neither passage proves that
Poland's 2024 JASSM-ER order was delayed or undelivered.

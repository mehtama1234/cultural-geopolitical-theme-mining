# Virginia high-load customer terms source note

**Checked:** 2026-09-15  
**Source:** [Virginia SCC Joint High Load Proposal and Recommended Stipulation](https://www.scc.virginia.gov/docketsearch/DOCS/87_%4001%21.PDF), Case PUR-2025-00058, filed August 29, 2025

## Observation

The public filing identifies Amazon Data Services, CloudHQ, the Data Center
Coalition, Google, Iron Mountain Data Centers, Microsoft, and STACK
Infrastructure among the supporting parties. It proposes high-load terms for
customers beginning service on or after January 1, 2026 with contract capacity
of at least 50 MW. The filing also describes aggregation rules for accounts
under common ownership and for contiguous property sharing a substation.

## Boundary

This is a public filing and recommended stipulation, not a facility-level load
record or proof that every named organization accepted or received the terms.
It does not identify which customer participated in the 2024/2025 fault
events, establish actual load, billed cost, or cost recovery, or show household
incidence. The aggregation rule is a design feature to verify in final orders
and account-level records, not evidence of realized ownership or usage.

# NIK 2024 FMS realization source note

Source: [Najwyższa Izba Kontroli, execution of the 2024 state budget / Armament Agency audit](https://www.nik.gov.pl/kontrole/wyniki-kontroli-nik/pobierz,kon~p_25_001_202502181310531739880653~id1~01,typ,kj.pdf)

Retrieval: 2026-09-14. The official PDF was read through indexed official PDF
text. The note preserves the exact page/line locations used in the finding and
does not treat the audit's sample as a complete Polish procurement ledger.

## FMS schedule and delivery passages

Page 12, lines 603–605 states that the FMS construction provides only an
estimated delivery schedule: “Items to be Supplied (costs and months for
delivery are estimates).”

Page 13, lines 616–621 describes the PL-D-SAI agreement: four F-35 aircraft
were delivered in December 2024 and January 2025; the estimated contractual
schedule had placed delivery in Q3 2024; and the advance had not been settled at
year-end because of delivery dates and FMS accounting cycles. Footnote 53 says
the settlement forms covering those deliveries arrived only in 2025.

Page 13, lines 655–658 says that, based on US DD Form 645 settlement documents,
the Armament Agency settled PLN 1,534,148.4 thousand of advances in 2024; the
forms covered Q4 2023 and the first three quarters of 2024.

## Boundary

This source establishes an FMS timing/accounting mechanism and a concrete F-35
example. It does not identify JASSM-ER, its delivery quantity, its acceptance,
or its 2024-order status. The F-35 example is a comparator for observability,
not a proxy for JASSM realization.

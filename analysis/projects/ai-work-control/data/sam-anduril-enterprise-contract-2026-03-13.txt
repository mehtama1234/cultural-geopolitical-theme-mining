Normalized evidence note:

The public SAM.gov award notice identifies Anduril Industries, Inc. as the
awardee of a single-award Enterprise Contract issued by the Department of the
Army. The notice reports a ten-year structure (five-year base plus five-year
option period), a firm-fixed-price indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity
vehicle, and a maximum contract value/ceiling of $20,000,000,000. The notice
describes the vehicle as available for ordering by the Department of War and
other authorized federal agencies for Anduril commercial software, hardware,
data and compute infrastructure, and related technical support offerings.

Interpretation boundary:

This is an executed federal contract vehicle, not a Barracuda-specific order.
The notice does not identify SLB-500M, a Barracuda quantity, a task order,
obligation, delivery, acceptance, production output, or Polish demand. The
$20B figure is a ceiling across the vehicle; it cannot be used as Barracuda
funding without a product-specific order and obligation record. A separate
USAspending keyword search for exact “SLB-500M” returned no award match on
2026-09-18, so the product-specific federal execution arrow remains open.

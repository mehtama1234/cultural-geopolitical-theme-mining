Normalized evidence note:

Anduril reports that the U.S. Department of War and its Office of the
Undersecretary of War for Research and Engineering signed a framework
agreement for the surface-launched Barracuda-500M (SLB-500M) under the
Ground-Launched Low-Cost Containerized Munition program. The release reports
procurement and delivery of a minimum of 3,000 all-up SLB-500M systems to the
Army's Program Acquisition Executive FIRES across three years, a minimum rate
of 1,000 all-up rounds per year, and a first delivery tranche in the first
half of 2027. It also reports more than 60 associated containerized launchers
starting in 2027.

The release separately reports that Anduril's dedicated 115,000-plus-square-
foot Southern California production facility opened earlier in 2026 and had
begun producing Barracuda variants, and that production is intended eventually
to shift to the Arsenal-1 facility in Ohio. It reports private and Defense
Production Act investment in the solid-rocket-motor industrial base and a
company projection of high-single-digit-thousands Barracuda-500 production
capacity by the end of 2026.

Interpretation boundary:

This is a contractor-reported framework agreement and production-capacity
claim. It is direct evidence of a stated U.S. demand route and reported
facility activity, but not an independently verified federal contract file,
completed delivery, acceptance, operational inventory, or Polish demand. The
U.S. order cannot be assigned to the Polish assembly/production plan without a
separate allocation, license, or execution record.

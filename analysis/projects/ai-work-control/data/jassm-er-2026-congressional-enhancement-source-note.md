# JASSM-ER 2026 congressional enhancement source note

**Checked:** 2026-09-15  
**Source:** [Congressional Record, July 23, 2026](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CREC-2026-07-23/pdf/CREC-2026-07-23-pt1-PgS4259-2.pdf)

## Observation

The transmittal identifies report number `RSAT 0F–26`, delivered to Congress
on July 22, 2026, as an enhancement or upgrade in the sensitivity of
technology or capability described in the original Section 36(b)(1)
certification `24–33` of March 12, 2024. The prospective purchaser is the
Government of Poland and the implementing agency is the Air Force. The
description repeats the possible-sale ceiling of up to 821 AGM-158B-2 JASSM
with Extended Range All-Up-Rounds and related support; the cited estimated
total value is $1.77 billion.

## Boundary

This is an authorization or notification-stage update. It does not identify a
signed modification to Poland's May 2024 order, a changed quantity, shipment,
acceptance, fielding, training, inventory, operational use, or external
response. It therefore updates the procurement-stage ledger without closing
the customer-realization or leverage arrows.

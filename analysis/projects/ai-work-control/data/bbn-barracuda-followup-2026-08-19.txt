Page title: Spotkanie Szefa BBN z przedstawicielami Anduril Industries

The head of BBN, Bartosz Grodecki, met Anduril Industries co-founder and CEO
Brian Schimpf and vice-president for Europe Brian Moran. The page describes
the Barracuda-500M (SLB-500M) program as one of the main topics and says the
meeting concerned building the competencies in Poland needed to implement it.
It states that assembly followed by production in Poland would mean access to
long-range weapons and development of national technological and production
backing. The page characterizes the model as cooperation with a strategic ally
combining modern weapons for the Polish armed forces with a stronger Polish
defense industry.

Acquisition boundary: normalized excerpt captured from the official page for
the program record; it establishes a dated institutional follow-up and stated
competence-building objective, not plant commissioning, output, delivery,
workforce, control rights, or operational capability.

Normalized evidence note:

The official FY 2027 FAMM00 budget justification identifies the Family of
Affordable Mass Missile as an Air Force missile-procurement line. Its resource
summary reports a FY 2026 spend-plan amount of $335 million in mandatory funds
for a quantity of 1,000 and a FY 2027 request of $355 million total for a
quantity of 1,000, consisting of $55 million in discretionary funds and $300
million in mandatory funds. The justification says the request continues
FAMM procurement and seeks seven-year multiyear-procurement authorization to
stabilize the industrial base. It describes FAMM-P as a palletized baseline
configuration and FAMM-L as a later lugged variant.

The FY 2026 Air Force Missile Procurement Exhibit P-1 separately lists FAMM
as line 10 with a reported quantity of 3,010 and reported cost of $656.333
million in the exhibit's budget columns. The two documents are retained as
budget-document observations with their own column and fiscal-year context;
the figures are not summed or treated as contract obligations.

Interpretation boundary:

This is more specific than a generic low-cost-missile funding category because
it names an Air Force procurement line, quantity, and budget request. It still
does not identify a contractor task order, obligation, selected vendor,
production output, delivery, acceptance, or Polish demand. The documents do
not establish that FAMM-P is Barracuda SLB-500M, so these figures cannot be
assigned to Anduril or Barracuda without a program-specific award record.

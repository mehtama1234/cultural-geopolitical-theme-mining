Normalized evidence note:

The official notice states that the government is executing a targeted
acquisition approach for the Family of Affordable Mass Missiles–Palletized,
Beyond Adversary's Reach (FAMM-P BAR) effort. It describes a three-month
closed risk-reduction effort intended to finalize design feasibility and
inform a baseline System Requirements Document. Participation is limited to
vendors down-selected from an April 17, 2026 request for information and a
May 6, 2026 industry day; the solicitation package is distributed directly
through DoD SAFE because it contains Controlled Unclassified Information.

The notice says the government is evaluating whether to pursue a separate,
overarching development-prototype effort after risk reduction. That possible
follow-on would require approvals and funding and would be competitively
awarded if released. Participation in the current risk-reduction spiral is
not a prerequisite for any future competition.

Interpretation boundary:

This is an official acquisition-strategy and risk-reduction stage, not a
public production award, obligation, delivery, or acceptance record. The
notice does not name Anduril, Barracuda SLB-500M, a selected vendor, a funded
prototype, a production quantity, or Polish demand. The FAMM-P category-level
funding plan and this acquisition route therefore cannot be used to attribute
the reported 1,000 FAMM-Palletized systems to Barracuda.

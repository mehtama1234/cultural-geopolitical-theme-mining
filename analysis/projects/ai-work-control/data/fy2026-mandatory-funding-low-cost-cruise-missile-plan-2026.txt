Normalized evidence note:

The plan identifies $1.5 billion for development, procurement, and integration
of low-cost cruise missiles. It states that the funding lines support continued
development and maturation of Affordable Mass Munitions, including procurement
of 1,000 FAMM-Palletized systems, 1,000 Low-Cost Containerized Missile–Ground
Launched (LCCM-GL) systems, and JSMs. The plan describes these efforts as
modernizing acquisition and revitalizing the defense industrial base, including
allies and partners, while supporting testing, procurement, production rates,
and surge capacity.

Interpretation boundary:

This is official financing and program-category evidence, not a Barracuda-
specific award or allocation. It does not identify Anduril, SLB-500M, a
recipient, contract line, obligation, production site, delivery, acceptance,
or Polish demand. The 1,000 LCCM-GL quantity cannot be assigned to Barracuda
without a program-specific federal record.

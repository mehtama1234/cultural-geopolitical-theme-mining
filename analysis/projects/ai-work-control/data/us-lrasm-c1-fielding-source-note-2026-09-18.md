# US LRASM C-1 fielding source note

**Checked:** 2026-09-18

## Observed event

The US Marine Corps **2026 Marine Aviation Plan** states that the AGM-158C
LRASM C-1 variant was operationally fielded on the F/A-18E/F in FY2024. The
same passage says integration on the F-35B and F-35C continues, while the
upgraded C-3 variant begins its production cut-in in FY2026.

**Official source:** [2026 Marine Aviation Plan, Aviation Weapons and Spectrum
Dominance](https://media.defense.gov/2026/Feb/10/2003873872/-1/-1/0/260210-USMC-2026-AVIATION-PLAN.PDF)

## Promotion boundary

This is direct official evidence of operational fielding for the **C-1
variant on the F/A-18E/F**, not for LRASM C-3, F-35 integration, or Poland's
JASSM-ER order. It establishes fielding, not a quantity, inventory level,
maintenance performance, combat employment, replacement time, or an observed
external response.

```text
integration/testing -> operational fielding -> combat use / sustainment / response
       observed              observed                 open
```

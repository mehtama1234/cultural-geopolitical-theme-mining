# US LRASM C-1 fielding source note

**Checked:** 2026-09-18

## Observed event

The US Marine Corps **2026 Marine Aviation Plan** states that the AGM-158C
LRASM C-1 variant was operationally fielded on the F/A-18E/F in FY2024. This
is later fielding language, not the first operational milestone: an earlier
Navy budget describes C-1 Early Operational Capability on the F/A-18E/F in
Q1 FY2020. The 2026 plan separately says integration on the F-35B and F-35C
continues, while the upgraded C-3 variant begins its production cut-in in
FY2026.

**Official source:** [2026 Marine Aviation Plan, Aviation Weapons and Spectrum
Dominance](https://media.defense.gov/2026/Feb/10/2003873872/-1/-1/0/260210-USMC-2026-AVIATION-PLAN.PDF)

## Promotion boundary

This is direct official evidence of operational fielding for the **C-1
variant on the F/A-18E/F**, not for LRASM C-3, F-35 integration, or Poland's
JASSM-ER order. It establishes fielding, while the earlier EOC record shows
that fielding and initial operational availability are different labels. No
source here establishes a quantity, inventory level, maintenance performance,
combat employment, replacement time, or an observed external response.

```text
integration/testing -> operational fielding -> combat use / sustainment / response
       observed              observed                 open
```

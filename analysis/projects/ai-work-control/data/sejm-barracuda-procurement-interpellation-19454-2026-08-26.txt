Normalized evidence note:

The interpellation concerns orders by the Polish Armed Forces that would
condition investment in Polish defense industry. It identifies the July 2026
PGZ/WZL nr 2/Anduril cooperation agreement for assembly and later production
of Barracuda-500M/SLB-500M and the public possibility of producing several
thousand systems. It then states that public information had not presented a
contract by the Ministry of National Defence or the Armament Agency to
purchase the weapon for the Polish Armed Forces.

The first question asks whether the Ministry or Armament Agency has concluded,
is negotiating, or plans to conclude a purchase agreement, and if so the
expected signing date and order volume. Additional questions ask about force
requirements, prior purchase commitments, economic justification, and which
state-supported defense investments already have executive delivery contracts.

Interpretation boundary:

This is an official parliamentary oversight and public-information checkpoint.
It does not establish that no contract, negotiation, classified arrangement,
production, or delivery exists. It does establish that the public industrial
announcements did not, as of the interpellation, themselves disclose a Polish
Armed Forces purchase contract, and that procurement demand is an explicit
realization gate for the production plan.

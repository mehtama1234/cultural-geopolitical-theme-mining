# Virginia direct-assignment policy source note

**Checked:** 2026-09-15  
**Source:** [Virginia SCC facts and data-center initiatives page](https://www.scc.virginia.gov/about-the-scc/scc-facts/)

## Observation

The SCC reports that on August 25, 2026 it established docket PUR-2026-00131
for Dominion's proposed line-extension-policy amendments. The page describes a
2026 Rider T1 framework requiring large-load customers to fund direct-connect
transmission facilities that would not otherwise be built for general system
needs. It also reports an amended allocation illustration in which the
projected increase for a typical residential customer using 1,000 kWh falls
from $2.90 to $0.94 per month, a 67.5% reduction relative to the previous
methodology.

## Boundary

This is an observed regulatory decision and modeled incidence illustration. It
is not an observed household bill, payment, service outcome, or proof that a
specific data center paid a specific direct-connect cost. The policy’s future
implementation, account-level allocation, enforcement, and public response
remain open.

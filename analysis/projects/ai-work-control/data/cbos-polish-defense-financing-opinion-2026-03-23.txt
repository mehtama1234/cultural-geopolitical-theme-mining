Source: CBOS Flash 11/2026, “Polacy o źródłach finansowania zakupu uzbrojenia”
Publisher: Centrum Badania Opinii Społecznej (CBOS)
Source URL: https://www.cbos.pl/PL/publikacje/flashe_tekst.php?nr=11%2F2026
Publication date: 2026-03-30
Fieldwork: 2026-03-23 through 2026-03-25
Sample: N=1000 adult residents of Poland; CATI 80% and CAWI 20%

Normalized observations from the published CBOS flash:
- 51% support Poland's participation in the EU SAFE low-interest loan for military equipment; 36% oppose; 7% had not heard of the proposal.
- Within those groups, 36% strongly support and 27% categorically oppose the SAFE participation proposal.
- SAFE support is 84% among respondents on the left and 59% among respondents in the political center; 65% of respondents on the right oppose it.
- 46% support financing armaments from NBP profit on gold operations; 37% oppose; roughly 10% had not heard of the proposal.
- NBP-gold-profit financing is supported by 65% on the right; opposition is 52% on the left and 50% in the center.
- 17% support both financing routes; 35% support SAFE only; 29% support NBP-gold-profit financing only; 8% oppose both.

Interpretation boundary: this is a cross-sectional measure of Polish public judgment about defense-financing routes and their political meaning. It is not evidence about Barracuda-500M specifically, a Polish production order, assembly, delivery, public reception of the local-production agreement, trust change, political action, or geopolitical leverage. Support is a reported opinion, not observed behavior.

Uncertainty: subgroup base sizes and sampling errors are not reproduced here; ideology and potential-electorate groupings are survey classifications; the findings may reflect broader EU, sovereignty, fiscal, or party-political views rather than weapon-specific preferences.

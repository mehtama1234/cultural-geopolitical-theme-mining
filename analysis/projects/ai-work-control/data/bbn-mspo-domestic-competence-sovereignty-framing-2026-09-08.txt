Normalized evidence note:

At the opening of MSPO 2026, the Chief of Poland's National Security Bureau
said that acquiring systems is as important as building durable domestic
industrial and technological competencies. The statement described domestic
technological capabilities as a foundation of strategic resilience and the
state's ability to act independently in a crisis, and framed them as a form of
state sovereignty. The event also emphasized allied and partner participation
and solidarity in security cooperation.

Interpretation boundary:

This is an official institutional framing and public-visibility event. It
shows how domestic competence and sovereignty are being articulated in the
security discourse around MSPO, but it is not a measure of resident or voter
reception, trust, production, commissioning, local-content share, delivery,
operational capability, or geopolitical leverage. It is not Barracuda-specific
and cannot substitute for a plant, order, output, or acceptance record.

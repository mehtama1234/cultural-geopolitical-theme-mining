Normalized observations:
- Lithuania's Defence Resource Agency signed a contract with Veho Lietuva, representing Mercedes-Benz Group AG/Daimler AG, for military off-road vehicles and trucks for the Lithuanian Armed Forces.
- The reported contract value is approximately EUR 1 billion; the project is partly financed with EU SAFE funds.
- Delivery and payment are scheduled for 2026–2032. Payments are staged: the 2027 payment is not expected to exceed EUR 100 million, while 2026 payments are planned at approximately EUR 3 million and may change by year-end depending on produced vehicles.
- The stated outcome is strengthened Lithuanian logistics and development of the First National Infantry Division's operational capabilities.

Boundary: this is a foreign SAFE implementation and payment-schedule comparator, not evidence of Piorun procurement, delivered equipment, acceptance, operational use, or a Polish domestic-industry outcome. The contract has a delivery schedule and planned payments, but the cited source does not report completed deliveries or accepted vehicles.

Uncertainty: the page does not identify the exact SAFE tranche, full contract annexes, vehicle quantities, delivery lots, acceptance records, or the share of production and value retained in Lithuania. “Partly financed” is not treated as a quantified SAFE share.

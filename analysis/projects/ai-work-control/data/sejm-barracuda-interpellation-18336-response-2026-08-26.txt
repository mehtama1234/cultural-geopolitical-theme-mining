Normalized evidence note:

The response states that the document signed on 2026-07-06 is a cooperation
agreement corresponding to the current development stage, ongoing analyses,
and the parties' current level of work. It says the pace of implementation and
Polish involvement are correlated with the schedule of the United States
administration and progress on orders placed by the US government.

The response states that detailed production, licensing, and technology-
transfer obligations depend on confirmation of demand for the product by the
Polish Armed Forces. It says that, if this condition is met, negotiations on a
licence agreement could be completed within several weeks, subject to the
parties' continued agreement and cooperation.

The response also states that the cooperation agreement provides for a defined
production volume whose realization is directly tied to an order from, among
others, the Polish Armed Forces. Joint working groups had already begun work
on technical, organizational, and production solutions; their work was to
prepare documentation and arrangements for technology transfer, production
organization, supply-chain security, and other requirements for production and
planned delivery volumes.

The first phase is described as assembly and servicing by PGZ companies,
including WZL-2. Gradual Polish and European localization of components is
described as a later phase after the necessary technology base, production
process, and first-phase implementation are established. The response also
states that detailed answers concerning technical/control terms and defense
planning documents are protected by trade-secret or classified-information
rules.

Interpretation boundary:

This is direct official evidence of an implementation-stage cooperation
agreement, a demand-contingent production/licensing path, active working
groups, and a staged assembly-to-localization plan. It is not evidence that a
Polish Armed Forces purchase order has been placed, that licensing or transfer
agreements have been signed, that a plant is commissioned, or that any unit has
been produced, accepted, delivered, or fielded.

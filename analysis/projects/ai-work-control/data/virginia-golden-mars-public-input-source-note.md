# Virginia Golden-Mars public-input comparator source note

**Checked:** 2026-09-20
**Sources:** [Virginia SCC facts page, Golden-Mars Transmission Case Decision](https://www.scc.virginia.gov/about-the-scc/scc-facts/); [Dominion Northern Virginia project page](https://www.dominionenergy.com/nova)

## Observation

The SCC reports that the Golden-Mars transmission project received two public
hearings in Loudoun County in September 2025, more than 600 registered public
speakers in December 2025, and hundreds of pages of written comments. The
page summarizes resident concerns about placing portions of the lines
underground, potential home-value loss, landscape and appearance effects, and
possible health effects of power lines.

## Later route-stage update

Dominion's current Northern Virginia project page reports that Route 4 required
Loudoun County School Board consent, that the School Board did not consent, and
that the SCC approved Route 3A on June 29, 2026. This is a route-substitution
and institutional land-control stage, not evidence of construction,
compensation, property effect, reliability benefit, or resident acceptance.

## Boundary

This is a public-participation comparator for transmission infrastructure. It
does not measure data-center attribution, representative public opinion,
household bills, health effects, or the outcome of the Prince William cases.
The concern categories are reported institutional summaries, not a coded
survey or causal estimate.

Normalized observations:
- A March 27, 2025 executive contract covered 111 Borsuk infantry fighting vehicles, with delivery planned for 2025–2029.
- The Polish Ministry of National Defence reports that the first 15 serial Borsuks were handed over to the Polish Armed Forces in December 2025. This is product-level delivery evidence for the 111-vehicle contract, not a SAFE-funded delivery observation.
- The June 8, 2026 SAFE program page separately lists 146 Borsuks in the scope of contracts signed May 28–30, 2026. This is a SAFE contract-scope observation, not evidence that those 146 vehicles—or any subset—were delivered or accepted.

Boundary: same product family does not mean same contract, funding source, delivery cohort, or acceptance record. The 15 delivered vehicles must not be subtracted from or pooled with the 146 SAFE-listed vehicles. The comparison advances the product-level realization baseline while leaving SAFE-specific delivery, acceptance, production pace, local-content share, and operational use open.

Uncertainty: the cited pages do not provide a vehicle-by-vehicle contract identifier linking the December 2025 handover to the later SAFE contract, nor a SAFE delivery schedule or acceptance report for the 146-vehicle scope.

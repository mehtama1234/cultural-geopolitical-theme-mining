# Germany: Platform Work Directive implementation record

**Checked:** 2026-09-15  
**Country:** Germany  
**Directive:** Directive (EU) 2024/2831  
**Current status:** official preparation record; no final transposition measure identified in the reviewed sources

## Evidence

The Federal Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs (BMAS) states in the
Federal Government's answer of 14 April 2026 (Bundestag Drucksache 21/5454)
that it was preparing a draft bill (`Referentenentwurf`) to transpose the
directive into German law. The answer gives 2 December 2026 as the deadline.
It also says that the BMAS was examining all possible measures concerning
subcontractors under Article 3, including a possible direct-employment
requirement.

The Federal Government's answer of 22 April 2026 (Bundestag Drucksache
21/5577) says that the BMAS is the lead ministry, is in continuing exchange
with other Member States and the Commission expert group, and is still working
through the implementation of Article 6. It states that concrete plans for the
required algorithmic-management training could not yet be given. The same
answer says that expanding the directive beyond platform work was not planned
at that point.

The BMAS policy page describes the same stage as preparation of a
`Referentenentwurf` informed by an earlier stakeholder dialogue. It explains
the intended national implementation, but it is not itself a final law or an
individual remedy record.

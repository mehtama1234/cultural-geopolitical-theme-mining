# Rehman order follow-up recheck v2

**Status:** current official-route recheck; receipt and durable recovery remain
unobserved  
**Checked:** 2026-09-21

## Result

A fresh search of the Fair Work Commission's official decision and bulletin
routes found no later public record that verifies payment of the A$7,096.96
gross lost-remuneration order, enforcement, post-order earnings, continued
platform access, or durable non-retaliation. The April 2026 bulletin still
reports the March 2026 reactivation and lost-remuneration order, but it is an
order-level outcome, not worker-confirmed receipt or recovery.

The episode therefore remains:

```text
background-check deactivation
  -> voluntary access restoration
  -> formal reactivation order
  -> A$7,096.96 gross lost-remuneration order
  -> [open] receipt, net recovery, continued work, durability, trust, exit
```

## What changed from the earlier check

The current search adds a later official bulletin surface to the earlier
primary-decision and primary-order review. It does not add a new same-worker
outcome. The absence of a later public record is a bounded public-record
non-observation, not evidence that payment or continued work did not occur
privately.

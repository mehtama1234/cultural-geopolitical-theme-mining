# Lithuania AN/TPS-77 MRR radar deployment follow-up

**Checked:** 2026-10-20  
**Status:** delivered and being integrated into air-surveillance capacity; operational effect remains open

## Observed route

The Lithuanian Ministry of National Defence reports that mobile medium-range
AN/TPS-77 MRR radars manufactured by Lockheed Martin have been delivered to
Lithuania and are being integrated by the Lithuanian Armed Forces. The ministry
says the radars can detect airborne objects in time and provide flexibility in
airspace surveillance.

The ministry further reports that the radars will provide real-time data to
NATO's integrated air and missile defence system, contribute to the common
Alliance air picture, and improve interoperability between Lithuania and its
allies. The minister said additional contracts would be signed later in 2026
to strengthen recognition and neutralization of air threats.

## Stage boundary

| Stage | What is public | Still open |
|---|---|---|
| Procurement and delivery | Named AN/TPS-77 MRR system, U.S. manufacturer, and delivery to Lithuania | Contract value, quantity, acceptance documents, sustainment, and lifecycle cost |
| Integration | Lithuanian Armed Forces are reported to be deploying the radars and linking data to NATO's integrated system | Network availability, training, staffing, uptime, latency, and interoperability testing |
| Operational capability | Claimed improvement in detection, common air picture, and allied interaction | Actual detections, response time, aircraft/drone outcomes, readiness, and civilian protection |
| Follow-on procurement | Minister reports additional contracts planned in 2026 | Signed contracts, delivery, deployment, and marginal capability change |

This is stronger than a procurement intention because delivery and integration
are reported, but it is not evidence of operational effectiveness, deterrence,
or reduced civilian risk.

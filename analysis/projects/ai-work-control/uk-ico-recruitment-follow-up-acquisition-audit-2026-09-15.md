# UK ICO recruitment follow-up acquisition audit

**Checked:** 2026-09-15

## Question

Has the ICO publicly documented a post-review enforcement action, follow-up
audit, or candidate-level outcome showing that an automated recruitment
decision was explained, reviewed, changed, or compensated?

## Official search boundary

The search covered the ICO action and decision-notice areas for automated
recruitment, AI recruitment enforcement, and jobseeker human-review complaints.
The relevant official materials located were:

- the ICO's 2024 audit overview of AI sourcing, screening, and selection tools;
- the accompanying AI in Recruitment Outcomes Report;
- the ICO's 2026 public explanation of jobseekers' rights and expectations; and
- general ICO guidance on automated decision-making, Article 22, and meaningful
  human intervention.

## Result

**No public candidate-level or organization-specific outcome was located.** The
available record supports regulator monitoring, recommendations, and expected
contestability. It does not show a named candidate's decision being changed,
an account or application being restored, compensation being paid, or
anti-retaliation being tested.

This is a bounded non-observation, not a claim that no such event exists. ICO
publication practice, privacy constraints, or a later source may reveal an
outcome outside this packet.

## Coding consequence

The linked UK record remains:

```text
regulator review: observed
organizational commitment: observed_commitment
candidate notice / human review: observed_rule_or_expectation
candidate correction / compensation / anti-retaliation: not_observed
```

Do not promote the record to an individual-remedy or enforcement-outcome row
without a primary artifact identifying the affected decision or an aggregate
official outcome with a defined denominator and time window.

# Philippines DOLE platform-governance record v1

**Status:** official state dialogue and complaint-capacity action observed;
exercised algorithmic remedy open  
**Checked:** 2026-09-15

## Why this record matters

This record adds the state side of the Philippines platform-work comparison.
Fairwork shows what platforms could evidence in 2022; the Department of Labor
and Employment (DOLE) records what the government began doing in 2026:
convening platforms and stakeholders, naming algorithmic-management and remedy
issues in the policy agenda, requesting platform position papers, and directing
the creation of a dedicated complaint channel.

These are meaningful institutional actions, but they are not the same as an
enacted platform-work remedy, a completed inspection, or a corrected worker
decision.


The August inspection episode should not be merged with an algorithmic-remedy
case. It shows that DOLE can direct an inspection and protect signatories from
dismissal in a safety dispute; it does not show that a platform algorithm was
reviewed or changed.

## Comparative interpretation

The Philippines now has three distinct evidence layers:

```text
platform audit        -> formal appeals at 2/9 assessed platforms; outcomes open
state dialogue        -> algorithmic-management/remedy agenda and complaint unit announced
worker remedy         -> no public corrected automated decision yet
```

This is a useful contrast with Malaysia. Malaysia has an enacted Act and a
Tribunal hearing; the Philippines has an official policy dialogue and planned
complaint capacity without a comparable enacted gig-worker Tribunal in this
packet. Neither comparison should be reduced to “strong” or “weak” regulation:
the relevant variables are legal status, institutional authority, worker access,
case visibility, and verified outcome.

## Decisive next acquisition

Acquire the platform position papers, the formal ILO Convention No. 193

## Boundary

This is an official institutional-action record. It does not establish that
DOLE's dialogue changed policy, that the complaint unit resolved a case, or
that any platform algorithm was corrected.

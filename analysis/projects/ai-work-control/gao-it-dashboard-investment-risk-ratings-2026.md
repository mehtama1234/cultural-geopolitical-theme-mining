# Federal IT risk ratings can understate project exposure while delivery remains open

**Checked:** 2026-10-20  
**Stage:** investment risk rating → oversight gap; project delivery and public consequences remain open

GAO's October 7, 2026 IT Dashboard review adds a federal digital-governance route.
The federal government spends more than $100 billion annually on IT and cyber
investments. In a selected sample of 53 major investments across 12 agencies,
GAO's assessments matched CIO ratings 27 times, identified more risk 24 times,
and identified less risk two times. Twenty-one CIO ratings were not updated in
a timely manner under the agencies' own processes, and two agencies used rating
processes longer than quarterly despite OMB guidance.

The institutional route is unsettled: OMB announced steps to sunset the public
Dashboard and replace it with a streamlined system but gave no release
timeframe. GAO issued 17 open recommendations to nine agencies to improve the
quality and frequency of ratings. The finding matters because stale or
understated risk can delay oversight of expensive modernization work, but the
report does not show that a reviewed investment failed or that a member of the
public experienced a service, access, or cybersecurity harm.

The evidence boundary is therefore material. The 53-investment sample is not a
census, rating disagreement is not project failure, and the public record does
not establish realized cost or schedule outcomes, service reliability,
cybersecurity harm, public access, user experience, or completed corrective
action.

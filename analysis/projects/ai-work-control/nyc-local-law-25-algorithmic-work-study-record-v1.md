# NYC Local Law 25: an enacted study mandate for algorithmic work impacts

**Status:** bounded institutional-response record · **Checked:** 2026-09-18

New York City Local Law 25 of 2026 requires the city technology department to
study how algorithmic tools and automated employment decision tools affect city
employee hiring, regular work functions, and employee feedback. It also
requires a report to the mayor and City Council speaker within three months of
the study's completion.

This is a genuine movement in the chain:

```text
algorithmic-work governance concern
  -> enacted study mandate
  -> required attention to hiring, work functions, and employee feedback
  -> [open] completed study/report
  -> [open] named tool, implementation, worker decision, appeal, correction,
             workload/pay change, trust, or exit
```

The law is not evidence that a particular city employee encountered an
algorithmic decision, that a tool changed work, or that a worker received a
remedy. The current official public search located the enacted law and the
existing DCWP AEDT rule/complaint route, but not a completed study report or a
named worker outcome.

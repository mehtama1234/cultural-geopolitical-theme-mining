# DOD responsible-AI use reaches initial oversight while governance capacity remains open

**Checked:** 2026-10-20  
**Stage:** responsible-AI principles → selected-use-case oversight and workforce recommendations; operational and strategic conversion remains open

GAO's October 14, 2026 review adds a defense-AI governance route beneath
geopolitical capability, technology dependence, and institutional trust. DOD
established AI Ethical Principles and six Responsible AI tenets. Most of the 10
surveyed components reported at least initial steps to implement the tenets and
establish oversight in selected use cases.

The capacity gap is part of the finding. The Responsible AI Working Council did
not meet from late 2024 until August 2025. Four components reported challenges
that would have benefited from collaboration, and six of 10 said shared
resources and best practices would help. As of February 2026, only 3 of 10
authorized Responsible AI Division positions were filled. GAO issued two new
open recommendations: charter the council with defined roles and meeting
frequency, and address the division's workforce needs.

The evidence boundary is material. The selected survey does not establish
consistent DOD-wide implementation, model accuracy, human review, operator
workload, mission deployment, battlefield effect, adversary response,
deterrence, safety incidents, or public legitimacy. A governance framework and
initial oversight steps are institutional capacity signals, not demonstrated
strategic effect.

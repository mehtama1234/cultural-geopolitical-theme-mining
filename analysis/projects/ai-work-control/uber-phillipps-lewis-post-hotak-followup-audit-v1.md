## Search performed

The search checked the Fair Work Commission decision/order surface and targeted
queries for `UDE2025/94`, the worker’s name, and the primary decision number

  Bench decision; and
- the Commission’s public [decision and order search](https://www.fwc.gov.au/hearings-decisions/find-decisions-and-orders),

## Result

No later public final decision, lost-pay order, payment record, or durable
access record for `UDE2025/94` was located in this search. This is a
non-observation bounded by the public search surface and date checked. It does
not establish that the matter was abandoned, settled, resolved privately, or
never relisted.

```text
regular-work eligibility       observed adjudicated
deactivation status             not resolved in located public record
merits                          not observed
lost-pay quantum                not observed
payment receipt                 not observed
durable access                  not observed
```

The worker’s access had resumed on 2 June 2025 before the eligibility decision,
but resumed access is not equivalent to a formal merits remedy or recovery of
the claimed earnings. The correct current coding is therefore:

```text
eligibility objection dismissed
→ proceedings stayed / relisting direction
→ later public outcome not located
```

## Why this matters for the atlas

This search closes an acquisition question without closing the underlying
case. It prevents three common errors: treating the positive eligibility
ruling as a merits victory, treating resumed access as payment, and treating a
missing follow-up document as proof of non-payment or private settlement.

The finding also makes implementation visibility measurable. The public record
can make the worker’s eligibility and the procedural stay highly legible while
leaving the final access, earnings, and remedy state opaque. That opacity is a
property of the evidence route, not an outcome estimate.

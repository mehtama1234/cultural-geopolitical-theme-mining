# Baltic drone exposure to capability, procurement, and industrial resilience

**Checked:** 2026-10-19  
**Status:** bounded geopolitical capability map; no claim of deterrence or civilian protection

Three official-source surfaces describe different stages of a Baltic and
European defence route.

| Stage | Evidence | Observed | Still open |
|---|---|---|---|
| Operational exposure and response | Lithuanian NATO air-policing accounts | A reported drone interception, later repeated air-policing alerts, and a related civil-aviation disruption; Lithuania and Italy framed the response as alliance unity | Independent attribution and intent, readiness burden, cost, deterrence, civilian-risk reduction, public consent, adversary adaptation |
| Allied procurement | Polish/Lithuanian/Norwegian Piorun records | SAFE/common-procurement framework, first executive contract, named allied demand, production-capacity claims, and a pre-SAFE Norwegian live-fire/fielding comparator | Country quantities, payment, delivery, acceptance, availability, sustained production, operational effect, and SAFE-attributable capability |
| Industrial resilience | Lithuania–Ukraine letter of intent | Planned Lithuanian joint venture for UAV and electronic-warfare production, with a stated 2027 start target and policy rationale around shorter supply chains and independence | Binding contract, investment, facility, workforce, production, delivered systems, cost competitiveness, accepted capability, and reduced dependence |

## Bounded synthesis

The route is not “drone incident → deterrence.” It is exposure → operational
response → political interpretation → procurement or industrial option →
delivery and readiness → civilian protection or strategic effect. The official
records make the first four stages visible but do not establish that an
interception changed adversary behavior, that a procurement framework became
usable inventory, or that a letter of intent became a functioning factory.

The Piorun records also require strict scope separation: a pre-SAFE Norwegian
fielding event is operational evidence for that earlier bilateral route, not
proof that the 2026 SAFE framework delivered systems. Likewise, the Lithuania–
Ukraine industrial plan is related to, but not evidence of, Piorun delivery.

## Next bounded test

The next useful evidence is one named procurement or industrial facility with a
contract, lot-level delivery or acceptance, training/availability, and a later
operational or civilian-protection outcome. Until then, retain procurement,
production, and response as separate capability stages.

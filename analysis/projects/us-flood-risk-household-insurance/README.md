# Project: household flood risk, insurance, and housing

## Question

Who is actually exposed to flood risk, and how do ownership, building type,
and insurance change the picture beyond income alone?

## Decision rule

Keep hazard exposure, location, ownership, insurance, protection, claims,
repair, migration, displacement, and durable housing security separate. A
household in a floodplain is not one uniform experience.

## Current bounded finding

The [Census CES finding](findings/us-census-flood-risk-household-exposure-2026-001.md)
reports nonlinear household exposure patterns and identifies low-income,
uninsured homeowners as a vulnerable subgroup. It does not measure a complete
flood-to-recovery episode.

# Project: US cash aid and household structure

## Question

When a family receives regular cash, does it mainly pay old bills, reduce crowding, or change who can live independently?

## Short first pass

Track the payment schedule, rent or mortgage arrears, people in the home and moves. Keep a change in living arrangement separate from a claim about family well-being.

## Possible connection

Cash aid can change housing because it gives a household room to catch up, move, or leave a financially necessary arrangement. That makes household structure partly a money and policy outcome.

## Decision rule

Move on after one source measures cash and housing together, one source tests the change against a comparison group, and one next dataset can follow stability and child outcomes. Do not assume that a smaller household is always better or that every cash payment changes housing.

# Project: Trust, health-care seeking, and care avoidance

## Question

How does trust in different health-care entities enter the choice to seek,
delay, or avoid care, and which trust relationships are replaceable?

## Short finding route

Keep trust, beliefs about usefulness, expected disutility, cost and denial
expectations, access, care-seeking, treatment receipt, health, and later trust
as separate stages. This NBER study supplies a national survey and structural
mechanism layer, not a causal trust-shock or realized-health panel.

See the [bounded finding](findings/us-nber-trust-health-care-seeking-behavior-2026-001.md).

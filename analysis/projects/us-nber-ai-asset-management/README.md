# Project: AI asset management, performance decay, and market structure

This project tracks how AI enters financial firms, how adoption is measured,
whether early performance advantages persist, and how strategy similarity or
return comovement changes. The first bounded record uses NBER Working Paper
35273, combining regulatory disclosures, labor-market data, strategy
descriptions, and hedge-fund performance.

The project keeps AI mention, direct investment use, adoption, performance,
risk exposure, comovement, investor outcomes, and systemic risk separate.
Early alpha is not treated as durable capability, and lower comovement is not
treated as proof of lower financial-system risk.

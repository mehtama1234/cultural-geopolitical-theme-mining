# Project: Digital workplace surveillance, worker effects, and federal oversight

## Question

How can employer digital surveillance improve safety or operations while also
changing worker health, evaluation, employment risk, and practical control—and
what federal routes exist to oversee those effects?

## Short finding route

Keep tool deployment, worker exposure, implementation transparency, productivity
targets, evaluation, appeal, discipline, health, exit, and household security
as separate stages. GAO supplies a method-screened review and institutional
oversight layer, not a worker-level causal panel.

See the [bounded finding](findings/us-gao-digital-surveillance-workers-federal-oversight-2025-001.md).

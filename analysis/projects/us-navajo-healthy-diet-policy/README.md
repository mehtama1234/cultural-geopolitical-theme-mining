# Project: Navajo healthy-diet fiscal policy and place-specific implementation

## Question

Can a tribal government change diet through prices when retailers, product
availability, household budgets, and nearby markets shape what people can
actually buy?

## Decision rule

Keep tax design, retailer implementation, prices, purchases, cross-border
shopping, cultural acceptability, health, and community sovereignty separate.
A null health estimate is not proof that the policy had no intermediate effect.

## Current bounded finding

The [Healthy Diné Nation finding](findings/us-nber-navajo-healthy-diet-fiscal-policy-health-null-2026-001.md)
shows that a 7–8% healthy/unhealthy food price wedge produced no detectable
improvement in the studied maternal and newborn outcomes, with implementation
and cross-border shopping left as possible explanations.

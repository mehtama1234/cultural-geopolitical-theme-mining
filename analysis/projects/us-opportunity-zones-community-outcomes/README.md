# Project: Opportunity Zone investment and community outcomes

## Question

When tax incentives attract investment into distressed places, what can the
public actually see about jobs, housing, businesses, and community benefit?

## Decision rule

Keep tax advantage, fund assets, project completion, local investment, jobs,
housing, displacement, and durable community improvement separate. Investment
activity is not automatically public value.

## Current bounded finding

The [GAO finding](findings/us-opportunity-zones-community-outcomes-001.md)
shows that Opportunity Zone investment and completed projects can be real while
specific community outcomes remain poorly tracked and unevenly understood.

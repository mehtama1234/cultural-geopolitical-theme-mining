# Project: US rent-guarantee insurance

## Question

Who can buy protection before a rent shock, and who is left with the loss?

## Short first pass

Separate the income or health shock, the rent payment, the insurance claim, the landlord's response and later housing or credit outcomes. Compare private coverage with public protection.

## Possible connection

Rent insurance shifts the question from emergency aid after a problem to risk sharing before it. But the people most likely to need it may be the hardest to insure at a price they can pay.

## Decision rule

Move on after one source models the product, one source measures actual prevention aid, and one next dataset can observe premiums, claims, housing outcomes and credit. Do not describe a modeled benefit as a proven market result.

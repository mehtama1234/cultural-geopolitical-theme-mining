# Claims ledger: US rent-guarantee insurance

**Check date:** 2026-09-11  
**Scope:** United States; rent shocks, insurance, deposits, eviction, homelessness, and public risk sharing

| ID | Claim | Evidence type | What supports it | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C-001 | Insurance that pays rent after an income or health shock can improve modeled household welfare. | Quantitative equilibrium model | NBER 32582 finds higher welfare, smaller security deposits, and less homelessness under rent-guarantee insurance. | This is a model, not a broad US rollout. |
| C-002 | Unrestricted coverage is not financially viable. | Model with insurer behavior | The model finds moral hazard and adverse selection make unrestricted private or public coverage unworkable. | Actual premiums, claims, and behavior may differ. |
| C-003 | Private and public coverage would target different renters. | Model comparison | Private insurers must target better-off renters to break even; public insurers focus on households most at risk of homelessness. | It does not identify a tested eligibility rule or budget. |
| C-004 | Cash aid and legal help solve different parts of the rent problem. | Observed policy evidence | NBER rental-aid research finds more payment and less eviction fear; legal representation reduces court judgments and evictions. | These interventions were not directly compared with insurance. |
| C-005 | Rent insurance is a risk-sharing design, not a measured market result. | Our inference | C-001 through C-004 connect pre-shock coverage, rent payment, landlord risk, court, and housing. | Take-up, exclusion, landlord response, and later credit remain open. |

## Mechanism under test

```text
income or health shock
  -> rent cannot be paid
  -> insurance claim, public aid, legal help, or no protection
  -> landlord receives payment or starts a case
  -> housing, credit, and household path
```

## Next test

Find actual US products or pilots and follow premiums, exclusions, claims, landlord choices, tenant housing, credit, and public cost. Compare coverage with direct rent aid and legal help.

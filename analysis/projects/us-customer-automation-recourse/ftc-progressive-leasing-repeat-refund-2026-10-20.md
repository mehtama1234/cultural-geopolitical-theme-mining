# Progressive Leasing: large repeat refund, unresolved household conversion

**Checked:** 2026-10-20  
**Record:** [US FTC Progressive Leasing repeat refund](../../records/us-ftc-progressive-leasing-repeat-refund-2025.json)

## What the official route shows

The FTC reports that the first June 2021 distribution produced more than
$143 million in refunds. In September 2025 it announced a second round of
1,221,146 checks totaling more than $27 million for people who cashed the first
check and paid Progressive Leasing at least $616.62. New checks were subject to
a 90-day cashing window.

The enforcement context concerns rent-to-own plans marketed as “no interest” or
“same as cash,” where consumers could pay more than the sticker price and often
about twice that price after completing scheduled payments. The second-round
count is therefore a reachability measure inside a previously reached subgroup,
not evidence that all harmed households recovered or that pricing exposure was
corrected.

## End-to-end boundary

```text
rent-to-own price and payment exposure
  -> FTC enforcement and first refund
  -> first-check cashing + payment threshold
  -> second-check dispatch
  -> [open] second receipt/cashing, contract correction, adequacy, household recovery, trust, exit
```

The public page does not expose second-check cashing, individual amounts,
remaining payment burden, product retention or substitution, or household-level
recovery. This episode advances the recipient-conditioned remedy lane while
preserving the distinction between administrative reach and lived recovery.

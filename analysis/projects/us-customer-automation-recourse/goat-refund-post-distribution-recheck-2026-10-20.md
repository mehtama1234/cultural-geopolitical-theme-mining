# GOAT refund post-distribution recheck

**Checked:** 2026-10-20  
**Status:** distribution remains publicly documented; recipient cashing or redemption remains unavailable

## Recheck result

The FTC's current GOAT refund page still reports that 6,205 eligible customers
are being sent more than $100,000 in October 2026. Most recipients receive a
mailed check and some receive PayPal. The page instructs check recipients to
cash within 90 days and PayPal recipients to redeem within 30 days.

The accompanying FTC press release repeats the distribution count, prior
company refunds of more than $736,000 in 2025, and the payment windows. The
active refund-program index lists GOAT as an October 2026 program and supplies
the administrator telephone number. Neither official page publishes a
recipient-level delivery, check-cashing, PayPal-redemption, returned-payment,
reissue, amount, account-correction, product-resolution, repeat-use, switching,
trust, or household-recovery field.

## Route boundary

```text
shipping/refund control failure
  -> company direct refunds in 2025
  -> FTC checks or PayPal payments sent in October 2026
  -> [open] delivery and cashing/redemption
  -> [open] adequacy, residual loss, and product/account correction
  -> [open] repeat use, switching, trust, or practical exit
```

The recheck closes the repeated public-page search for this distribution at an
access boundary. A future update should only promote the route if the FTC,
administrator, court filing, or another lawful source supplies a new aggregate
or recipient-conditioned endpoint such as cashed checks, redeemed PayPal
payments, reissues, or account correction. The reported mailing of payments is
not treated as recipient receipt.

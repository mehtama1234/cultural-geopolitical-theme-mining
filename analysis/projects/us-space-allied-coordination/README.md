# Project: US space operations and allied coordination

## Question

When the United States says it is working with allies in space, what does the
operational machinery show about whether that cooperation is usable?

## Decision rule

Keep alliance commitment, cooperation mechanisms, staffing, coordination,
information sharing, interoperability, operational effect, public consent, and
strategic success separate. A plan or agreement is not proof that cooperation
works in practice.

## Current bounded finding

The [GAO finding](findings/us-space-allied-coordination-001.md) shows that the
United States has real mechanisms for allied space cooperation, while staffing
gaps, overlapping responsibilities, difficult points of contact, and missing
implementation milestones can still limit usable coordination.

# Project: FEMA assistance, housing reach, and disaster recovery access

## Question

Can FEMA Individual Assistance convert disaster exposure into temporary
housing, rental, and repair support when survivors face communication barriers,
constrained housing, and changing federal/state delivery roles?

## Short finding route

Keep disaster damage, eligibility, notice, application, award, payment, repair,
safe occupancy, temporary housing, move/stay, and durable recovery as separate
stages. This GAO review supplies assistance scale and implementation barriers,
not a survivor-level recovery panel.

See the [bounded finding](findings/us-gao-fema-assistance-housing-reach-2026-001.md).

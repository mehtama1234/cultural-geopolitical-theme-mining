# Official-source extract: FEMA assistance and housing reach

**Captured:** 2026-09-23  
**Purpose:** preserve the smallest source-grounded extract used by the
machine-readable record. This is not a substitute for the linked GAO report.

## Official source

- U.S. Government Accountability Office, “Disaster Assistance High-Risk Series:
  FEMA Assistance for Disaster Survivors,” GAO-26-108154, April 22, 2026,
  [official landing page](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-26-108154),
  [report PDF](https://www.gao.gov/assets/gao-26-108154.pdf).

## Extracted claims

- FEMA provided more than $3 billion to 1.2 million individuals and
  households affected by Hurricanes Helene and Milton, the 2025 Los Angeles
  wildfires, and the 2025 Texas floods, according to agency data.
- Individual Assistance can cover necessary expenses and serious needs not
  covered by insurance or low-interest loans, including temporary lodging,
  rental housing, and home repairs.
- GAO reports long helpline waits and inability to reach a representative for
  most survivors in the reviewed Helene/Milton and Texas-flood evidence.
- Some survivors reportedly interpreted requests for more information as denial
  letters; FEMA revised letters to use plainer language and clearer instructions.
- Survivors and officials faced post-disaster housing constraints, including
  destroyed pre-existing housing resources and setup requirements that delayed
  direct housing in selected cases.
- FEMA shifted toward state/local centers and discontinued door-to-door
  canvassing, raising access concerns for older, disabled, rural, and
  phone/internet-limited survivors.

## Boundary preserved

Aggregate assistance and selected institutional evidence do not establish
individual eligibility, payment receipt, repair completion, safe return,
move/stay, durable housing security, or later trust and political action.

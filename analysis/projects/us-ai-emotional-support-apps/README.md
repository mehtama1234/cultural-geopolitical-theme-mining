# Project: US AI emotional support and the private care gap

## Question

When people use an AI app for comfort or mental support, does it help them feel less alone—or keep a serious need inside a product built to hold attention?

## Short first pass

Start with the HBS reports on AI companions and a separate prevention app. Keep short-term relief, emotional attachment, crisis safety, clinical care and business incentives separate.

## Possible connection

The same personal reply that makes an app feel supportive can make it hard to leave or question. This links loneliness and care to attention, product design and the limits of private consumer services.

## Decision rule

Check which users benefit, which risks appear, and whether the app is meant for prevention, wellness or treatment. Move on unless the evidence shows outcomes over time and a clear route to human help when needed.

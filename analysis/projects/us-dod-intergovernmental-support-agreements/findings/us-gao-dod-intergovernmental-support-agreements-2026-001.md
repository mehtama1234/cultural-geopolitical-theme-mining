# Finding 001: Installation service agreements expand without verified savings

**Status:** bounded public-partner infrastructure and labor finding · **Checked:** 2026-09-23

## The bounded finding

GAO reports that DOD intergovernmental support agreements with state, local,
and Tribal governments increased from 45 in 2018 to 316 in 2025. The agreements
cover services such as utilities, road maintenance, and waste management.

The expansion is not the same as realized fiscal capacity. GAO found that some
agreements lacked cost estimates or cost-benefit analyses, and the military
services lacked procedures to verify actual costs and savings. In nine of 21
single-installation agreements reviewed, public partners used private
contractors. For five selected positions, public-partner wages ranged from 19%
below to 50% above comparable Service Contract Act wage determinations.

```text
installation need
  -> intergovernmental agreement and public-partner delivery
  -> contractor, wage, cost, and service arrangement
  -> verified savings and reliable installation support
  -> readiness, local capacity, worker security, and public legitimacy
```

## What this adds to the atlas

This adds a concrete public-partner and cost-verification stage to the
infrastructure/state-capacity lane. A rapidly growing agreement portfolio can
be real institutional coordination while its savings, labor incidence, and
service results remain only partly observable.

## Limits and next test

- The agreement and wage samples are selected and not nationally representative.
- Agreement counts do not establish service quality, savings, readiness, or
  local public benefit.
- Wage comparisons are not a full compensation or worker-power measure.
- Public partner and contractor arrangements may produce genuine benefits even
  when measurement and verification are weak.

The next decisive test is a named agreement followed through planned cost,
actual cost, service delivery, worker pay and benefits, maintenance, user
experience, renewal, and installation readiness.

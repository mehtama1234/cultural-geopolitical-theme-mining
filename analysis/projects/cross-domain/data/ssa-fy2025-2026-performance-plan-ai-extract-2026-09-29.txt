Evidence extract retained from the official SSA FY 2025-2026 Annual Performance
Plan. The plan says SSA is using artificial intelligence to enhance Interactive
Voice Response tools and promote a chatbot; it projects approximately four
million automated calls in each of FY 2025 and FY 2026. It describes IMAGEN as
transforming evidence in electronic disability folders into machine-readable
text with enhanced search and intelligent analysis. The plan reports a FY 2026
target of 220 days for average initial disability-claim processing time and a
97 percent State Disability Determination Services decisional-accuracy target.

This is a source extract, not a claimant-level dataset. It preserves agency
plans and targets separately from realized performance, individual AI
exposure, explanation, appeal, correction, payment, health, work, or household
outcomes.

# Home is becoming resilience infrastructure

**Source:** Ingka Group/IKEA, [Life at Home Report 2026](https://www.ingka.com/newsroom/new-ikea-life-at-home-report-2026-reveals-a-world-in-flux-but-home-remains-the-foundation-for-resilience/)  
**Method:** YouGov online survey of 31,689 adults in 31 countries, supplemented by 24 home ethnographies and 36 digital immersions in India, Spain, and the United States; related People & Planet consumer research covered 30 countries.

## Finding

As climate, economic, health, and technological uncertainty enters everyday life, home is becoming a resilience infrastructure: a place where people seek security, comfort, control, and future optimism. But there is a persistent gap between what people need from home and what they experience.

```text
external uncertainty
  -> home becomes a security, comfort, and control infrastructure
  -> lived gap between desired and experienced home conditions
  -> repair, organization, reuse, and sustainable routines become agency practices
  -> affordability and health benefits determine whether climate action is reachable
```

## Theme and subtheme map

| Program theme | Subtheme | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| Housing, place, and mobility | Home as resilience infrastructure | The household is used to absorb external uncertainty and restore control |
| Household room and consumption | Home-condition option gap | Security, comfort, and control are desired more often than experienced |
| Trust, identity, and meaning | Domestic agency | Everyday routines and space organization become ways to regain future confidence |
| Care, health, and reproduction | Health and comfort conversion | Health is both a concern and a motivator for sustainable household change |
| Unequal exposure and status | Unequal ability to secure home | Cost, knowledge, confidence, and online-panel composition limit generalization about who can close the gap |
| Infrastructure, technology, and dependency | Household climate interface | Climate action becomes practical through energy, repair, reuse, storage, and resource routines |

## Boundary

This is retailer-commissioned mixed-method consumer research, with most quantitative fieldwork conducted in 2025. It does not establish causality, observed emissions reduction, housing affordability, or that household interventions close the security/comfort/control gap.

## Next test

Connect household security, comfort, and control to housing quality, energy cost, health, repair and reuse behavior, climate outcomes, and income-stratified access to practical agency.

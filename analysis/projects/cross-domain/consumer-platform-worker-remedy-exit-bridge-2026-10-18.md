# Consumer, platform, and worker power: remedy is not the same as exit

**Status:** third active depth artifact in the ongoing program
**Checked:** 2026-10-18
**Scope:** consumer recourse, platform control, worker contestability, and practical exit

## Research question

When a customer or worker encounters a harmful rule, error, denial, fee, or
automated decision, can they understand it, reach a human, correct it, receive
an adequate remedy, and still switch or leave?

This route treats remedy and exit as separate outcomes. A formal complaint path
may produce a response without restoring money, access, time, safety, trust, or
an alternative relationship.

## Evidence layers

| Layer | Unit and period | What it shows | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Federal Reserve SHED fraud/recovery | US adults, 2024 | 21% experienced some financial fraud; among adults with non-credit-card fraud, 63% lost money, 32% recovered only some or none, and about 30% spent at least ten hours dealing with recovery | No case-level firm liability, verified remedy, or later switching |
| CFPB complaint records | Product complaints and company responses | Consumer problems and response routes become visible at scale | Complaint counts and response labels are not verified compensation or restored access |
| Commonwealth Fund coverage denials | Privately insured working-age adults, 2025 | Denials can delay care, increase cost, worsen health, and create debt; appeals can change outcomes but may take weeks or months | Does not follow the same person from denial to adequate care and later trust |
| FTC redress records | Named enforcement or redress programs | Formal orders can create refund, reissue, deletion, or correction routes | Public aggregate distributions do not show receipt, residual loss, switching, or trust |
| Platform-control records | Accounts, privacy requests, and removal/control routes | Formal rights can make data, deletion, opt-out, or removal routes more visible and contestable | Validity, completion, recurrence, service loss, safety, and practical exit remain open |
| SHED work/AI-control layer | US adults/employees, 2025 | AI use differs by reported control over task completion (30% high control versus 20% lower control) and education (10% high-school-or-less versus 43% graduate degree) | No same-worker employer policy, task change, pay, monitoring, remedy, or later job outcome |
| Selected worker remedy cases | Named cases and public decisions | Review, reactivation, compensation, alternative work, and non-return can be observed at separate stages | Worker-confirmed receipt, durable security, household outcome, and free alternative work are sparse |

The cross-source pattern is: **formal visibility and contestability are
improving faster than verified recovery and practical exit.** This is a
mechanism claim about measurement stages, not a consumer-power index.

## What public discussion adds

| Source | Discussion signal | What it contributes |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn customer complaint post | A customer describes using public tagging and executive visibility to chase a refund when ordinary support did not work | Public reputation becomes a substitute escalation route; outcome is not verified |
| Reddit Amazon thread | A delayed refund and difficulty reaching a human are framed as a reason to stop using the platform, while replies describe workarounds to reach an agent | Human access, refund uncertainty, and exit are connected in lived language; not a prevalence estimate |
| YouTube Music community post | A refund requested soon after a failed service was denied, with no visible human review or reply | A formal policy can exist without a usable appeal path; single case, locked thread |
| X bank-support reply | A public support account says charges were reversed and finance charges adjusted | Case-specific response is visible, but the original complaint and final outcome are not fully exposed |
| LinkedIn software-renewal account | A 68% renewal increase, costly migration, and later retention offer show both switching power and switching cost | A large enough shock can make exit worthwhile, but one successful switch is not market evidence |
| Reddit platform-worker discussion | A proposed earnings floor and dispute route are debated as both protection and a possible new platform control | Formal remedy can coexist with concern about acceptance and deactivation leverage |

These records add public language about human reach, dependence, humiliation,
retention, and refusal. They do not turn selected posts, votes, or replies into
population behavior.

## Finding that survives the boundaries

> Practical power is the ability to convert a formal right into a timely,
> adequate remedy while retaining a usable alternative. A complaint, appeal,
> refund offer, reactivation order, privacy request, or compensation award may
> improve visibility without restoring the lost money, access, time, health,
> safety, bargaining position, or trust. Exit is only meaningful when a person
> can leave without an unmeasured and unacceptable penalty.

This connects consumer power, platforms, work control, firm power, unequal
exposure, household room, and cultural meaning without collapsing them into a
single autonomy score.

## Counterexamples and falsification tests

The interpretation must be narrowed if stronger evidence shows that:

- complaint or appeal responses reliably produce verified and adequate recovery;
- a formal privacy or removal request reliably completes, prevents recurrence,
  and does not remove a necessary service;
- customers with high switching costs still receive equal remedy and practical
  alternatives;
- worker compensation or reactivation is consistently received and followed
  by durable, comparable work and household stability;
- automated support routes produce equal outcomes for people with different
  literacy, disability, language, time, or cash-buffer conditions; or
- continued use after a failure reflects satisfaction rather than dependence,
  lack of alternatives, or the cost of leaving.

The strongest counterexample already visible is the public bank-support reply:
a case may receive a stated correction. That prevents the analysis from
equating all public support routes with failure, while still leaving adequacy
and durability unverified.

## Next measurable test

Build a same-account or same-worker remedy ledger:

```text
exposure or adverse decision
  -> notice and understanding
  -> first contact, effort, wait, and failed attempts
  -> review, correction, appeal, or remedy decision
  -> verified receipt and adequacy
  -> residual loss, continued use, switching, non-use, or exit
  -> later trust, safety, household security, bargaining, or collective action
```

For customers, compare automated versus human routes and high- versus
low-alternative products. For workers, compare offered, reachable, and usable
alternative work; record pay, travel, schedule, safety, receipt, and later
movement. Preserve non-reporters and people who stayed because leaving was too
costly as explicit comparison cells.

# Election legitimacy is conditional on an information environment voters can authenticate

**Source:** Catawba College Center for North Carolina Politics & Public Service, [Catawba College–YouGov National Survey: The Ballots & Bots](https://www.catawba.edu/news/all-news/2026/yougov-38/)  
**Method:** Online YouGov survey of 1,200 U.S. adults, fielded September 4–9, 2026, weighted to the national population; margin of error ±3.6 percentage points.

## Finding

Americans can remain committed to secure and fair elections while losing confidence that the information surrounding elections is authentic, correctable, and locally explainable. AI-generated deception therefore creates a legitimacy problem even when it does not persuade anyone to believe one specific false claim.

```text
commitment to fair elections
  -> synthetic or suspected-synthetic political content
  -> uncertainty about authenticity and correction speed
  -> weaker confidence in election information and administration
  -> demand for labels, removal, penalties, and trusted local verification
```

## Theme and subtheme map

| Program theme | Subtheme | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| Political judgment and action | Conditional electoral legitimacy | Support for fair elections is stronger than confidence in the current system |
| Trust, identity, and meaning | Authenticity under uncertainty | Voters worry they cannot tell what is real or whether corrections will arrive in time |
| Platforms, data, and attention | Synthetic political distribution | Social and video platforms are reported as major exposure routes, while trust in social and AI channels is low |
| Public systems and feedback | Responsibility and local verification | Respondents support disclosure and platform accountability while trusting election offices and local officials more than AI tools |

## Boundary

This is a cross-sectional, self-reported survey. It does not verify synthetic-media exposure, measure detection accuracy, establish persuasion or post-election behavior, or test whether labeling/removal policies improve trust. The linked crosstabs and AAPOR transparency materials were access-gated during review.

## Next test

Track whether local election-office transparency, provenance labels, rapid correction, and platform enforcement preserve acceptance of results when voters encounter suspected synthetic political content.

# A necessary institution under a delivery test: global expectations for the next UN Secretary-General

**Checked:** 2026-09-28  
**Source:** United Nations Foundation and GeoPoll, *A Mandate for Renewal*  
**Record:** [machine-readable source record](../../records/comparative-un-foundation-geopoll-un-secretary-general-mandate-2026.json)

## Core finding

The poll adds a leadership-transition layer to the multilateralism atlas.
Global publics still see the United Nations as necessary, but many believe it
is struggling to deliver results. They want the next leader to face outward—
ending wars, managing crises, and speaking on rights—while the selection race
itself remains largely invisible.

```text
global turbulence and future fear
  -> continued demand for an international system
  -> frustration with delivery and institutional habit
  -> demand for visible crisis-facing leadership
  -> representation and public visibility test
  -> restored or weakened multilateral legitimacy
```

The gender and North-South differences add a representation dimension: who
leads and who feels the institution can deliver are part of legitimacy, not
separate from it.

## Boundary

GeoPoll surveyed 5,000 people across 20 countries, with 250 per country,
between September 1 and 16, 2026, using mobile web and computer-assisted
telephone interviewing. The release notes digital-access limitations and does
not provide within-country subgroup tables. This is public-opinion evidence,
not a forecast of the selection, a causal leadership study, or an evaluation
of UN performance.

# INSEE September 2026: precautionary saving can coexist with weak household room

## Finding

France’s September 2026 monthly consumer-confidence survey shows a divergence between confidence, saving sentiment and discretionary room. Household confidence remained at 86, well below its long-term average of 100. The savings-climate index fell four points to 121, but remained well above its long-term average of 100.

Major-purchase intentions remained weak at a balance of -37, far below the long-term average of -16. Judgments of past and future personal finances were also below their long-term averages. At the same time, saving intentions remained high at 44, compared with a long-term average of 19.

The cultural-consumer mechanism is precautionary room: a household can report strong saving opportunity or intention while still postponing major purchases and feeling that future living standards are poor. “Saving” therefore cannot be read as comfortable abundance without knowing whose balance sheet, liquidity and unmet consumption needs are represented.

## Cost and expectation signals

The balance of households saying prices had risen over the prior year rose seven points to 33. The future-living-standard balance was -60, compared with a long-term average of -29. Unemployment fears eased slightly to 55, but remained elevated. These indicators show how price salience and future insecurity can sustain restraint even when one risk signal improves.

## Limits

The survey interviews approximately 2,000 households by telephone during the first three weeks of each month. Its balances are differences between positive and negative answers; they are not percentages, realized spending, saving, debt or income. Synthetic indexes summarize correlated opinions and cannot identify distribution across households.

## Program placement

This source extends `household_room_consumption`, `time_hidden_price`, `trust_identity_meaning`, `unequal_exposure_status`, `public_systems_feedback` and `housing_place_mobility`. It connects consumer sentiment to precautionary saving and major-purchase deferral while keeping confidence, perceived room and realized consumption separate.

The next tests are whether high saving intentions reflect actual liquid saving, debt reduction, postponed cultural and durable consumption, or unequal household protection—and whether price expectations predict later substitution, borrowing, social continuity or political attribution.

# Conditional legitimacy measurement design v1

**Checked:** 2026-09-28  
**Status:** active research design; not a completed causal model

This design operationalizes the cross-episode synthesis [Conditional public capacity: when formal provision becomes lived legitimacy](conditional-public-capacity-legitimacy-2026-09-28.md). It exists to keep future research cumulative. Each pass should identify the unit, observe the route, preserve the denominator, and stop at the strongest stage the evidence actually supports.

## The five tests

| Test | Minimum observable question | Stronger same-unit evidence | Cultural/political endpoint | Common false shortcut |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reachability | Could the person, household, rider, student, or policyholder enter the route? | Eligibility or exposure linked to an attempted application, trip, meal, claim, or appointment | Whether formal inclusion is experienced as belonging or exclusion | Treating eligibility, funding, or a published program as use |
| Usability | Did the route deliver something usable at an acceptable cost, time, quality, and dignity? | Actual receipt/consumption/travel/repair linked to price, delay, quality, and substitution | Whether the system is interpreted as practical support rather than symbolic provision | Treating a benefit amount, enrollment, traffic average, or payment as welfare |
| Continuity | Could the route be repeated through renewal, disruption, payment, or later need? | Longitudinal observation of retention, re-entry, repeat use, or interruption | Whether trust becomes durable, conditional, or protective non-use | Treating one successful encounter as security |
| Correction | Could the person contest denial, delay, poor quality, displacement, or inadequate payment? | Complaint/appeal/assistance record linked to response time, triage/referral, investigation, correction, remedy adequacy, and restored capability | Whether institutions are seen as answerable and worth re-approaching | Treating a complaint channel, referral, enforcement action, or policy reform as completed remedy |
| Attribution | Could people identify who imposed the cost, delivered the benefit, and could repair failure? | Same-unit exposure and later belief/action, with timing and competing explanations | Trust, blame, civic action, switching, mobility, exit, or political coalition | Inferring legitimacy from aggregate outcomes or stated attitudes alone |

## Episode-specific implementation

### Medi-Cal

- **Unit:** applicant/member or household, ideally linked across eligibility, renewal, plan/provider, encounter, and outcome records.
- **Reachability:** application completion, enrollment date, language, county, immigration-status category where legally available.
- **Usability:** appointment, prescription, treatment completion, provider acceptance, travel/time and out-of-pocket cost.
- **Continuity:** renewal, lapse, re-entry, premium payment, and care interruption.
- **Correction:** county fair hearing, Health Consumer Alliance or other assistance, plan grievance, resolution, and retained coverage.
- **Attribution:** whether the person attributes access or loss to state policy, county administration, provider, immigration risk, or household resources.

  The current DHCS advisory report adds a distinct voice surface: member recommendations are voted on, posted with agency responses, and linked to at least one adopted communication change. The next test is independent verification of implementation and whether advisory influence changes access, continuity, or trust beyond the participating members.

  DHCS Ombudsman reports add a separate route-performance layer—contact volume, wait, abandonment, language, referrals, and recorded results. These fields can measure reachability and administrative friction, but not by themselves whether a member received adequate care or regained trust.

### NYC congestion pricing

- **Unit:** trip, vehicle owner, transit rider, business, or route-linked neighborhood; do not merge these into one household story without a join.
- **Reachability:** zone entry, toll account, exemption/discount application, transit alternative, and route availability.
- **Usability:** toll, fare, travel time, reliability, accessibility, delivery cost, and emergency-response time.
- **Continuity:** repeated trips, mode switching, route displacement, repeated toll payment, and capital-project completion.
- **Correction:** toll dispute, exemption appeal, public hearing, project feedback, and response.
- **Attribution:** whether users credit pricing, transit investment, congestion change, fuel prices, or broader post-pandemic travel shifts.

### NYC universal school meals

- **Unit:** student-school-day or household-school-year; meal counts alone are not unique students.
- **Reachability:** eligibility, service schedule, cafeteria access, queue, disability accommodation, and awareness.
- **Usability:** meal picked up and consumed, menu acceptance, dietary fit, time saved, food quality, and household grocery substitution.
- **Continuity:** repeat participation, attendance-day coverage, summer transition, and sustained menu/service quality.
- **Correction:** student/family feedback, menu change, accommodation response, food-safety complaint, and school-level remedy.
- **Attribution:** whether students and families interpret the program as ordinary shared care, stigmatized assistance, school quality, or household relief.

### California wildfire insurance / FAIR Plan

- **Unit:** property-policy-claim trajectory, linked to household and mortgage where permitted.
- **Reachability:** quote, policy offer, renewal/non-renewal, FAIR Plan enrollment, mitigation documentation, and lender requirement.
- **Usability:** premium, deductible, coverage limit, claim acceptance, advance payment, repair, temporary housing, and replacement cost.
- **Continuity:** policy retention, reinsurance/market transition, claim supplements, mortgage continuity, and later mobility.
- **Correction:** claim dispute, complaint, investigation, legal assistance, supplemental payment, enforcement, and verified adequacy. The current episode now has a documented regulator complaint/intervention route, but not a household-level adequacy denominator.
- **Attribution:** whether households blame insurer, FAIR Plan, regulator, wildfire risk, mitigation requirements, or climate policy—and whether that attribution changes residence or political action.

## Evidence rules for future passes

1. Keep policy, person, household, trip, student, property, claim, firm, and place as distinct units unless a documented key joins them.
2. Record the first observed encounter and the later endpoint separately; do not convert a cross-sectional association into a trajectory.
3. Treat aggregate money as capacity or throughput until a common denominator establishes receipt, adequacy, and timing.
4. Treat reported satisfaction, trust, or support as meaning evidence, not proof of use, remedy, or recovery.
5. Preserve negative findings and non-use: a route can be formally available, technically functional, and practically avoided.
6. Every new episode should identify the strongest observed stage and name the next missing arrow before it is promoted into a cross-domain synthesis.
7. Report correction using the ladder `route -> reach -> triage/referral -> investigation/action -> correction -> restored capability -> later trust/action`; never collapse these stages into “remedy.”

## Source routing

- **Same-unit household and individual depth:** PSID, UAS, SIPP, MEPS, SHED/HTOPS, and authorized administrative extracts.
- **Public-system event depth:** DHCS enrollment/renewal and encounter routes; MTA toll, route, capital, and exemption records; NYCPS meal participation and school-quality records; California DOI policy, claim, complaint, and FAIR Plan records.
- **Meaning and attribution:** linked or repeated surveys, school surveys, complaint narratives, public-hearing records, and local political behavior, kept separate from administrative outcome measures.
- **Remedy and recovery:** event-level claim or complaint ledgers with dates, denominator, action taken, payment/correction, and later status.

The design is successful when it narrows the open middle with compatible units. It is not successful merely because every box has a source name.

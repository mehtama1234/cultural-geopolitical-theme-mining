# Disability-inclusive employment moves beyond placement toward accommodation, retention, progression, and digital access

## Finding

An ILO account of the September 2026 Disability Employment Package mutual-learning seminar reframes employment services for persons with disabilities as a pathway rather than a placement event. The pathway includes preparation, matching, reasonable accommodation, skills development, retention, progression, accessible workplaces, and employer-side support.

The distinct finding is that digital employment inclusion has a frontier gap. Digital tools are concentrated on access, job search, training, and matching, while there is much less evidence on their use after hiring—to support accommodation, sustained employment, workplace participation, and career progression.

## Cross-domain interpretation

`disability and labour-market barrier -> person-centred public employment service -> matching and accommodation -> employer adaptation and assistive technology -> retention, progression, and workplace agency`

This shifts the unit of success from “placed” to “able to remain and advance.” It also makes inclusion an ecosystem problem: public employment services, employers, disability organizations, health and social services, training providers, and technology actors must coordinate. A matching algorithm cannot solve inaccessible onboarding, rigid hours, poor workplace design, or absent accommodation.

The technology signal is double-edged. AI-supported skills matching, smart glasses, digital work instructions, captioning, and assistive tools may open options, but inaccessible interfaces, biased data, surveillance, and employer overreliance can reproduce exclusion. Disability inclusion therefore becomes a test of whether digital systems change the conditions of work, not only the speed of recruitment.

## Cultural, social, consumer, and political significance

- Employment dignity is increasingly tied to retention, progression, autonomy, and equal opportunity—not simply entry into a job.
- Public employment services become ongoing institutions of workplace accommodation and worker agency.
- Accessibility is a design and infrastructure issue affecting everyone, not a niche compliance add-on.
- Employers are treated as active sites of inclusion, with duties around recruitment, adaptation, work organization, and retention.
- Digital inclusion claims should be tested after hiring: who can use the system, remain in work, advance, and obtain remedy?

## Boundary and next test

The ILO source reports practice exchange and emerging examples, not comparable outcomes. It does not establish employment gains, wage equality, algorithmic fairness, retention, or universal accessibility. The next test is a named public employment service or employer pathway followed from intake and matching through accommodation, workplace experience, retention, progression, and remedy.

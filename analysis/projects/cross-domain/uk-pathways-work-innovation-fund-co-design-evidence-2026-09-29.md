# Employment support is becoming an evidence-and-co-design portfolio

**Checked:** 2026-09-29  
**Source:** UK Department for Work and Pensions, *Pathways to Work Innovation Fund applicant bid pack*  
**Record:** [machine-readable source record](../../records/comparative-uk-pathways-work-innovation-fund-co-design-evidence-2026.json)

## Core finding

The new fund treats employment support for disabled people and people with
long-term health conditions as an “ideas factory”: up to £60 million over two
years will support experiments designed to move people toward, into, through,
and onward in work while producing evidence about what works and for whom.

The distinct trend is institutional learning as a service. Lived experience,
accessibility, user research, co-design, partnership formation, milestones, and
future scale are being built into the funding architecture rather than added
after delivery.

## Cultural/social interpretation

The fund turns disabled people from recipients of a fixed programme into sources
of institutional knowledge. That can change the social meaning of employment
support: from a gatekeeping encounter to a negotiated service whose design is
supposed to be answerable to lived experience.

But the power question remains open. Co-design can be participatory in form yet
weak in decision rights; partnerships can broaden access yet concentrate funds in
organisations already able to navigate public procurement. The fund’s success
therefore depends not only on employment outcomes, but on who gets to define the
problem, what evidence counts, and whether unsuccessful experiments produce
learning rather than stigma or withdrawal.

## Open middle

```text
health/disability employment barrier
  -> lived-experience insight and co-design
  -> experimental joined-up support
  -> participant outcomes and implementation learning
  -> evaluation, adoption, replication, or abandonment
  -> trust, agency, and durable employment-support capacity
```

This is a prospective funding architecture. It does not show that funded
projects have begun, that participants control design, or that the eventual
portfolio will reduce the disability employment gap.

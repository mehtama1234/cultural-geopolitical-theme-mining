# Public industry legitimacy weakens where firms are linked to household cost and visible risk

## Finding

Gallup’s 2026 industry ratings show sharp declines in public favorability toward the computer industry, electric and gas utilities, farming and agriculture, and oil and gas. The computer industry fell 11 points to 48%, utilities to 33%, farming to 54% and oil and gas to 30%. Only farming and restaurants receive positive ratings from a majority of Americans across the 25 sectors tracked.

## Cross-domain mechanism

`visible household cost or technological disruption -> attribution to sector -> public legitimacy decline -> political and consumer pressure`

The pattern links infrastructure, firms and household room. Energy, food and utilities are judged amid inflation and financial hardship; technology is judged amid AI, data-center and job-disruption concerns. The sector is not evaluated only by output—it becomes a visible carrier of the risks and costs households experience.

## Boundaries

The article reports Gallup’s annual poll conducted August 3–24, 2026; the public summary does not state the respondent count. Ratings are broad public perceptions, not measures of actual firm behavior, prices, service quality, consumer welfare or causal attribution. Partisan differences are descriptive.

# Social Security becomes household capacity through information, support, and mobility

**Checked:** 2026-09-29  
**Status:** bounded cross-source synthesis; no pooled estimate

## Finding

Social Security is not only a distant fiscal account. Across three different
evidence surfaces, it appears as an information interface, an intergenerational
support system, and a public promise whose future credibility can shape how
households organize work, care, mobility, and risk.

```text
public benefit rule or fiscal deadline
  -> information and expected support
  -> application, family transfer, work, care, or mobility choice
  -> household security and intergenerational opportunity
  -> trust, attribution, and political legitimacy
```

The sources do not form one contemporary household panel. Their value is a
careful comparison of compatible stages:

| Evidence surface | What it adds | What remains open |
|---|---|---|
| CBO 2026 long-term projection | A conditional current-law projection of widening financing gaps and OASI trust-fund exhaustion in fiscal year 2032 | Reform, payment level, household interpretation, saving, retirement timing, and political response |
| NBER working paper 35748 | Personalized DI and OAI amounts in an initial Social Security statement increased applications among older adults with work-limiting health or low work ability | Awards, receipt, adequacy, administrative burden, health, work exit, and household security |
| NBER working paper 35456 | Historical parental Social Security coverage is estimated to have changed sons’ later migration, earnings, and neighborhood trajectories, with different results for daughters | Contemporary transferability, every caregiving exchange, destination choice, and the mechanism’s present size |

## Cross-domain interpretation

The NBER information result supplies a direct choice mechanism: a formal
benefit becomes actionable when people can see personalized amounts. But an
application is not an award or usable income. The historical parent-child
result supplies a longer intergenerational mechanism: public support can alter
whether children must remain near family, can move toward better labor
markets, and later accumulate earnings or neighborhood opportunity. That
mechanism is estimated from an earlier program and cannot be treated as a
current national effect.

The CBO projection supplies the institutional-legitimacy layer. A visible
2032 date can make a long-range financing question legible as a present social
contract—who pays, who expects protection, and who has to revise plans—without
specifying what policy will follow exhaustion.

## Cultural, social, and consumer significance

- Benefit information is a form of practical infrastructure. A statement can
  change an application decision even before it changes eligibility or receipt.
- Retirement and disability policy can function as family infrastructure. When
  public support is more reliable, adult children may have more room to move,
  work, or substitute formal care for proximity-based support.
- The same promise is distributed unevenly. Health, wealth, job control,
  housing, gendered care obligations, and the replaceability of family support
  change who can convert a benefit into mobility or security.
- A fiscal deadline can become a trust signal, but the direction is not
  predetermined. It may prompt reform demand, private planning, delayed
  retirement, family transfers, or withdrawal of confidence; those responses
  require separate observation.

## Evidence boundary

These sources must not be pooled into a single causal estimate. CBO is a
conditional projection; NBER 35748 studies an information-to-application
response; NBER 35456 estimates historical intergenerational effects. None
establishes contemporary benefit adequacy, award or payment receipt, actual
care substitution, current migration, or political behavior.

## Next decisive test

The strongest next pass is a contemporary, age- and income-stratified route
that joins:

```text
personalized benefit information or policy change
  -> application and award
  -> payment receipt and adequacy
  -> work, retirement, care, or family-transfer decision
  -> mobility, health, household room, and later trust/action
```

It should preserve health limitation, wealth, gender, family structure,
housing, job control, geography, and whether formal support replaced or merely
rearranged unpaid care. Until that route exists, the atlas can claim a
stronger information-to-application mechanism and a historically estimated
intergenerational pathway, but not a current household-security outcome.

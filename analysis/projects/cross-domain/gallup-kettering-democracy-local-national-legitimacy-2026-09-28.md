# Democratic commitment, local trust, and national legitimacy

**Checked:** 2026-09-28  
**Source:** Gallup and Charles F. Kettering Foundation, *Democracy for All: Frustration Rises, Commitment Endures*  
**Record:** [machine-readable source record](../../records/us-gallup-kettering-democracy-for-all-2026-local-national-legitimacy-frustration.json)

## Core finding

The report describes a democratic contradiction rather than a simple collapse:
Americans retain commitment to democracy while judging national performance,
rule-of-law strength, equal treatment, and political accountability poorly.
Democracy is evaluated more positively in people’s own cities and towns than at
the national level.

The resulting subtheme is **scale-dependent legitimacy**. Local leaders,
neighbors, and direct experience may provide a reservoir of democratic meaning,
but the public summary does not prove that local trust causes national
resilience or produces policy influence.

## Open middle

```text
local trust and direct experience
  -> participation, voice, and responsiveness
  -> policy influence and equal treatment
  -> national legitimacy and durable democratic commitment
```

The report does not establish actual participation, local policy influence,
causal trust-building, or democratic recovery. Those remain the next tests.

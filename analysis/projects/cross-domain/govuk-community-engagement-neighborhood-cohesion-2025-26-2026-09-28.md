# Local cohesion is broad but uneven

**Source:** UK Department for Culture, Media and Sport / Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government, [Community and Engagement Survey 2025/26](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/community-and-engagement-survey-202526-neighbourhood/community-and-engagement-survey-202526-neighbourhood-and-community-report)  
**Method:** Official survey of people aged 16+ in England, with over 175,000 responses in 2025/26; the community pathway was asked October 2025–March 2026 and includes time-series, subgroup, and local-authority estimates.

## Finding

Local cohesion is neither absent nor evenly available. Everyday neighbor contact and willingness to pull together remain widespread, but reported trust is much lower, local trajectories are often judged negatively, and belonging varies sharply by age, disability, deprivation, and place.

```text
everyday contact / shared place
  -> belonging and willingness to pull together
  -> trust does not automatically follow
  -> local decline and unequal access weaken the conversion
  -> place-specific intervention must be tested against actual participation and repair
```

## Theme and subtheme map

| Program theme | Subtheme | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| Housing, place, and mobility | Place-specific cohesion | National averages conceal large local-authority differences |
| Trust, identity, and meaning | Contact-to-trust gap | Chatting and willingness to collaborate are broader than reported neighborhood trust |
| Public systems and feedback | Local trajectory legitimacy | People can perceive intergroup coexistence while judging the area as getting worse |
| Unequal exposure and status | Unequal belonging | Younger, disabled, and more deprived populations report lower belonging or cohesion measures |
| Care, health, and reproduction | Social infrastructure access | Local belonging and mutual aid are potential supports, but actual help and outcomes remain unmeasured |

## Boundary

This is official, self-reported survey evidence. It does not identify causal local interventions, actual aid, service quality, or durable trust. Local-authority comparisons are descriptive and should respect the report’s rounding and confidence-interval cautions.

## Next test

Follow a named local intervention—community space, transport, housing, safety, cultural, or participation program—from funding and access through repeated participation, cross-group contact, actual assistance, institutional response, and durable belonging.

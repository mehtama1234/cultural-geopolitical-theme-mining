# Project: US arms transfers and geopolitical dependence

## Question

What do actual international arms-transfer flows show about US influence and
allied dependence?

## Decision rule

Keep arms deliveries, orders, production, training, maintenance, operational
use, political alignment, consent, and strategic leverage separate. A transfer
flow is not automatically a military outcome or a durable alliance choice.

## Current bounded finding

The [SIPRI finding](findings/us-arms-transfer-dependence-001.md) shows the US
as the dominant supplier of major arms and Europe as a rapidly growing importer,
while operational dependence and political alignment remain open.

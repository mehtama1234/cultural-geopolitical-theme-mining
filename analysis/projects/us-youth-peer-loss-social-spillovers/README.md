# Project: youth peer loss and social spillovers

## Question

When a young person dies by self-harm, how does the event move through peers,
schools, care systems, family life, and later behavior?

## Decision rule

Keep exposure, distress, diagnosis, care-seeking, offending, school continuity,
family support, and later safety separate. A diagnosis or visit is not a
complete account of grief, contagion, or recovery.

## Current bounded finding

The [peer-loss finding](findings/us-nber-peer-self-harm-death-surviving-youth-outcomes-2026-001.md)
shows that exposure to a peer's self-harm death is followed by more self-harm
diagnoses and mental-health visits in linked South Carolina data, while some
students without prior diagnoses show increased felony offending rather than
care-seeking.

# Claims ledger: US medical debt relief and what it changes

**Check date:** 2026-09-11  
**Scope:** United States; medical bills, collections, credit, care use, health, and household money

| ID | Claim | Evidence type | What supports it | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C-001 | Removing medical debt can improve some credit access, but the effect depends on whether the debt would have been reported. | Two randomized experiments | NBER Working Paper 32315 finds modest credit-access improvement where credit reporting was part of the counterfactual, and no credit-report effect where it was not. | The study concerns downstream debt sold or about to be sold to collectors. |
| C-002 | Relief can reduce payments on existing medical bills. | Randomized outcome | The NBER study finds a moderate, statistically significant reduction in payments. | It does not erase the illness, original bill, or future care need. |
| C-003 | Debt relief alone did not improve average surveyed health, care use, or financial wellness. | Randomized outcome | The NBER study finds no average effect on mental or physical health, health-care use, or financial wellness. | Average results can hide effects for particular groups or debt types. |
| C-004 | Unclear prices and costly billing create the setting in which debt is formed. | HBS analysis | HBS describes wide price differences, weak ability to compare value, and substantial staff time spent on billing and insurance work. | Administrative cost is not the same as the patient's bill or outcome. |
| C-005 | Medical debt may be a record of an earlier health and money shock rather than the whole cause of later hardship. | Our inference | C-001 through C-004 separate illness, bill, collections record, credit, care, and health. | More evidence is needed on the original bill and later household path. |

## Mechanism under test

```text
illness or care need
  -> price, insurance, and billing process
  -> medical debt or collection record
  -> payment, credit, care, health, or trust change
  -> relief removes one link but not necessarily the illness or bill system
```

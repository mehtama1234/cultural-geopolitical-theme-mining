# Project: US medical debt relief and what it changes

## Question

When medical debt is removed, which part of life changes: credit access, later payments, health, care use, or none of these right away?

## Short first pass

Start with randomized debt relief, then separate the debt record from the illness, the bill and later care. Compare credit, collections, payments, health and care use rather than treating debt as one outcome.

## Possible connection

Medical debt may mark a prior health and money shock without causing every later problem attributed to it. Relief can improve a credit path while leaving the illness, care need or household strain in place.

## Decision rule

Move on after one source provides causal relief evidence, one source explains the billing burden, and one next dataset can follow health, care and money together. Do not use a correlation between debt and poor health as proof that debt relief will improve health.

# Project: Material pressure and political conversion

## Question

When does a material problem become attribution, political contact, voting,
protest, organized action, or exit rather than private adaptation, distrust, or
non-use?

## Working chain

`material exposure or public-system change -> access, information, and time ->
attribution and efficacy -> contact, vote, protest, organization, non-use, or
exit -> institutional response -> later trust, security, and repeated action`

## Current bridge

- [Material pressure, attribution, and political action bridge](material-pressure-attribution-action-bridge-v1.md)

## Next test

Build one same-person or same-place episode with dated exposure, notice and
access, time and information costs, attribution, efficacy, action, institutional
response, and later trust or repeated action. Keep causal estimates separate
from cross-sectional screens and public discussion.

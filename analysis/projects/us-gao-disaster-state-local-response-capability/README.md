# Project: state and local disaster-response capability

## Question

Can federal disaster support be converted into usable response capacity when
state and local capabilities vary and the federal role is changing?

## Short finding route

Keep preparedness capability, federal support, role transition, survivor reach,
repair, and durable recovery separate. The GAO review establishes selected
jurisdiction variation and intergovernmental dependence, not a survivor-level
outcome or national capability rate.

See the [bounded finding](findings/us-gao-disaster-state-local-response-capability-2025-001.md).

# Project: federal shared-services adoption

## Question

When government centralizes common services, does that create usable capacity
or merely move the coordination problem to a new marketplace?

## Decision rule

Keep centralization, adoption, legal and technical fit, integration, leadership,
performance measurement, savings, service quality, and mission outcomes
separate. A shared platform is not automatically a shared improvement.

## Current bounded finding

The [shared-services finding](findings/us-gao-federal-shared-services-adoption-leadership-data-2026-001.md)
shows varied federal adoption, reported benefits and challenges, unfilled key
leadership roles, and incomplete performance data. Realized government-wide
savings and service quality remain open.

# Project: Worker safety voice, retaliation, and public protection

## Question

When workers report a workplace or environmental hazard, can they do so safely,
obtain a remedy, return to safe work, and protect other workers and the public?

## Working chain

`hazard or unsafe work -> worker notice and voice -> retaliation or protection
-> investigation and remedy -> payment, safe return, and employer correction ->
recurrence, community protection, trust, and continued voice or exit`

## Relationship to existing research

The worker-remedy project studies receipt, income recovery, and practical exit.
This is a separate safety-voice lane: the key outcome is whether speaking up
becomes safer and changes conditions, not merely whether money or access was
ordered.

## Next test

Follow one protected complaint through exposure, reporting effort, retaliation
risk, investigation, remedy receipt, actual return, measured safety correction,
recurrence, and later worker or community response. Retain silent or non-reporting
workers where an authorized denominator exists.

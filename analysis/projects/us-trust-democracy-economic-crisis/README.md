# Trust, economic crisis, and democratic stability

This project tests whether generalized interpersonal trust is related to how
democracies absorb economic crises. It keeps country, county, and individual
evidence separate and does not turn trust into a universal cause.

## Finding

See [Finding 001](findings/us-trust-democracy-economic-crisis-001.md).

## Source

- [NBER Working Paper 24187](https://www.nber.org/papers/w24187)

The [participation, social support, trust, and political voice conversion
bridge](participation-support-trust-voice-conversion-bridge-v1.md) adds a
comparative layer across Korea, England, Canada, Scotland, South Africa, CDC
indicators, and OECD participation pilots. It keeps activity, support, trust,
perceived voice, and institutional response separate and identifies policy
timing and feedback as the missing middle stage.

# Project: parental homeownership, housing quality, and adult outcomes

## Question

Does growing up in an owned home improve later adult income and homeownership,
or do family and neighborhood differences explain the association?

## Decision rule

Keep parental tenure, housing quality, family resources, neighborhood, adult
income, adult ownership, and causal effect separate. A positive association is
not automatically an intergenerational housing benefit.

## Current bounded finding

The [Census CES finding](findings/us-census-parental-homeownership-housing-quality-later-outcomes-2026-001.md)
reports positive raw associations—about $2,000 higher adult income and a
6-point ownership difference—but no detected causal effect in its unobservable
and sibling-comparison tests.

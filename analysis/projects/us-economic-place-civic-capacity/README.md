# Project: Economic place conditions and civic capacity

## Question

How do local economic conditions and a major economic shock relate to whether
people have room to volunteer and help sustain community life?

## Decision rule

Keep volunteering, informal help, belonging, political action, and institutional
trust separate. A place-level association is not a causal story about one person
and volunteering is not the whole of civic capacity.

## Current bounded finding

The [Census working-paper finding](findings/us-economic-place-civic-capacity-001.md)
shows that local disadvantage and inequality were associated with lower
volunteering, the Great Recession dampened volunteering, and the earlier rural
volunteering advantage disappeared after the recession. It does not show why a
particular person stopped volunteering or whether another form of support
replaced it.

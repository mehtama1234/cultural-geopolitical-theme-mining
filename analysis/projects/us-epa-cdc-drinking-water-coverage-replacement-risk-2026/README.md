# Project: Drinking-water coverage, replacement need, and household responsibility

## Question

How does national drinking-water infrastructure capacity translate into
household protection when responsibility is shared across public systems,
states, and private well owners?

## Short finding route

Keep infrastructure need, lead-service-line inventory, public-water coverage,
private-well exposure potential, household responsibility, replacement, notice,
and health as separate stages. This is a national capacity and governance
finding, not a household exposure or illness estimate.

See the [bounded finding](findings/us-epa-cdc-drinking-water-coverage-replacement-risk-2026-001.md).

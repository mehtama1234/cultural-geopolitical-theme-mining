# Project: US transmission facilitation and realized infrastructure

## Question

When the federal government selects a transmission project and offers capacity
support, what is known about whether the line is actually built and energized?

## Decision rule

Keep selection, conditional award, facilitation agreement, contract, permitting,
construction, energization, transmission service, cost recovery, and household
benefit separate. Federal support or a planned megawatt figure is not proof of
operating infrastructure.

## Current bounded finding

The [DOE finding](findings/us-transmission-facilitation-realization-001.md)
shows that the Transmission Facilitation Program can make federal capacity
support visible before construction and service are visible; the current DOE
project page also records later termination or withdrawal notices for several
selected projects.

# Source search: US transmission facilitation and realized infrastructure

**Search date:** 2026-09-22  
**Geography:** United States interregional transmission projects selected by the Department of Energy  
**Status:** bounded pass complete; federal capacity support is not the same as energized service

## Working question

What does the DOE Transmission Facilitation Program record show about the gap
between a federal transmission selection and an operating line?

## Opening sources

| ID | Source | What it tells us | Status | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| US-DOE-TFP-SELECTIONS-2026 | [DOE Transmission Facilitation Program selections](https://www.energy.gov/edf/transmission-facilitation-program-selections) | Lists first- and second-round projects, planned capacity, award amounts, and later termination or withdrawal notices. | Official program project page | Project listing is not a construction, energization, service, rate, or household-outcome ledger. |
| US-DOE-TFP-RULES | [DOE Transmission Facilitation Program](https://www.energy.gov/edf/transmission-facilitation-program) | Explains conditional selection, capacity-contract design, cost recovery, and that payment for transmission service is not due until construction is complete and the line is energized and ready for service. | Official program description | Describes program rules and expectations; it does not prove each project reached those stages. |

## First pattern

```text
federal selection and capacity support
  -> negotiation, permitting, financing, and construction
  -> energization and transmission service
  -> cost recovery, rate allocation, reliability, and community benefit [open]
```

DOE says the first round involved four projects and the second round selected
four more capacity-contract projects. The current selections page records
termination or withdrawal notices for SWIP-N, Cross-Tie, Twin States, and
Cimarron Link, while describing Southline, Aroostook, Southern Spirit, and
Southline Phase 2 as projects with planned capacity and awards. The page does
not establish that the remaining projects are operating.

## Counterpoint to keep visible

Termination or withdrawal does not prove that federal support was wasteful or
that no replacement project will serve the same need. DOE's program is designed
to shift or repurpose conditional support, and a selected project may still be
in permitting, financing, or construction rather than failed. The source also
does not measure local opposition, rate impacts, reliability, or delivered
electricity.

## Decision rule

Use these pages for the conversion gap between public infrastructure finance
and realized service. Do not convert selected megawatts or an ended agreement
into a national completion rate, consumer-cost effect, reliability gain, or
community outcome without project-level operating evidence.

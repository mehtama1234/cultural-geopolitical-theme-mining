# Source search: election administration and practical participation

**Search date:** 2026-09-22  
**Geography:** United States, states, territories, and DC  
**Status:** bounded pass complete; administrative access and political participation are related but not identical

## Working question

What happened between voter participation and a counted ballot in the 2024
general election?

## Opening source

| ID | Source | What it tells us | Status | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| US-EAC-EAVS-2024 | [2024 Election Administration and Voting Survey comprehensive report](https://www.eac.gov/sites/default/files/2025-06/2024_EAVS_Report_508c.pdf) | State and local election officials reported participation, voting methods, provisional ballots, equipment, and election-administration conditions. | Official EAC report | It does not follow an individual voter from registration through ballot adjudication, explain every rejection, or measure trust, persuasion, or policy influence. |

## First pattern

```text
eligible or registered voter
  -> voting method and polling/return route
  -> ballot counted, provisional adjudication, or rejection
  -> turnout, legitimacy, trust, and political action [open]
```

The EAC reports that nearly 65% of the citizen voting-age population
participated in the 2024 general election, producing more than 158 million
counted ballots. About 0.9% of voters who cast a counted ballot did so by
provisional ballot; states reported 1,736,209 provisional ballots cast, with
73.8% counted, 25.1% rejected, and 3% receiving another outcome. More than 98%
of jurisdictions used equipment that marked a paper ballot or produced an
auditable paper record.

## Counterpoint to keep visible

The provisional-ballot figures are administrative aggregates, not a measure of
individual voter intent or unfairness. States use provisional ballots for
different reasons and rules. The report also does not connect equipment,
polling-place conditions, registration problems, ballot outcome, later trust,
or political behavior for the same voter.

## Decision rule

Use EAVS for the administrative route and state-reported outcomes. Do not turn
participation, paper records, or provisional rejection into a direct measure of
equal access, fraud, voter confidence, or democratic legitimacy.

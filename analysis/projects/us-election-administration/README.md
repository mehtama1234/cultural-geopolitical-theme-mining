# Project: US election administration and practical participation

## Question

What does the election system itself show about the route from eligible voter
to counted ballot?

## Decision rule

Keep eligibility, registration, ballot casting, provisional handling, ballot
counting, equipment, turnout, political money, and public confidence separate.
A high turnout rate or paper trail does not prove that every voter had equal
access or that people trusted the result.

## Current bounded finding

The [EAVS finding](findings/us-election-administration-001.md) shows that a
large election can have broad participation and auditable equipment while
still requiring millions of provisional-ballot decisions.

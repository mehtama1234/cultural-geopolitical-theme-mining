# Finding 001: Individual exit can improve wellbeing while social dependence blocks collective exit

**Status:** bounded U.S. platform-exit and collective-valuation finding ·
**Checked:** 2026-09-23

## The bounded finding

The World Happiness Report 2026 chapter summarizes a randomized experiment with
2,743 Facebook users. Among eligible participants, those paid to deactivate for
four weeks were reported as happier, more satisfied with life, less anxious,
and less depressed than the continuing-use group. The deactivation group later
reported lower Facebook use and was more likely to delete the app. Political
knowledge moved in the opposite direction: the continuing-use group scored
higher on the reported measure.

The chapter separately summarizes a U.S. college-student study of Instagram and
TikTok. Participants reportedly demanded money to deactivate individually for a
month, but many would pay to have everyone in their academic community
deactivate together. The two studies are not the same sample or experiment.

The safe interpretation is:

> A platform can produce short-run wellbeing gains when an individual leaves
> while still retaining enough social, informational, or coordination value
> that individual exit remains costly. Some users may therefore prefer
> community-wide exit without having a feasible path to coordinate it.

## Evidence chain

```text
platform use and social dependence
  -> individual deactivation or continued use
  -> wellbeing, knowledge, and stated monetary valuation
  -> social cost of being the non-user
  -> stated preference for coordinated community exit
  -> [open] actual coordinated exit, durable deletion, substitution, trust, or political action
```

## What it adds to the broader atlas

This adds a U.S.-anchored distinction between individual practical exit and
collective exit. It complements the qualitative Korean non-use study by adding
an experimental short-run wellbeing result and a stated coordination mechanism,
while preserving the missing link between wanting collective exit and actually
achieving it.

## What the source does not show

- The Facebook result is a recruited-user experiment and not a population-wide
  causal estimate of all social-media use.
- App deletion does not establish account closure, data erasure, or durable
  non-use.
- The Instagram/TikTok figures are stated valuations, not observed collective
  deletion or platform shutdown.
- The chapter does not establish a complete welfare balance, durable political
  participation effect, or policy remedy.

## Next decisive test

Track a defined community through an actual platform change or coordinated
non-use event, measuring individual deactivation, substitute use, social and
informational loss, wellbeing, account persistence, political behavior, and
whether the coordination arrangement holds over time.

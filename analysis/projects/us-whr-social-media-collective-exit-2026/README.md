# Project: Social-media use, wellbeing, and collective exit

## Question

Why can people benefit from leaving a platform yet remain willing to pay to
keep using it, and what changes when the relevant community exits together?

## Short finding route

Keep the randomized Facebook deactivation experiment, the separate U.S.
college-student Instagram/TikTok coordination study, and the chapter author's
interpretation as distinct layers. This is evidence about short-run wellbeing,
social dependence, and stated collective valuation—not durable deletion or a
population-wide platform-welfare estimate.

See the [bounded finding](findings/us-whr-social-media-collective-exit-2026-001.md).

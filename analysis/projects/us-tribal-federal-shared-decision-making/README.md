# Project: Tribal-federal shared decision-making and cultural legitimacy

This project tracks whether federal collaboration with Tribes over ancestral
lands and waters creates shared authority or remains consultation with uneven
legal and administrative power.

GAO reviewed five agreements and identified 11 features that strengthen shared
decision-making, including consensus and dispute resolution. It also found
uneven legal authority across agencies and missing workforce planning for the
staff and skills needed to develop agreements.

The safe theme is that voice becomes more durable when it is backed by legal
authority, defined decision rights, staff capacity, dispute resolution, and
continuity. A meeting, consultation, or agreement title does not by itself
prove shared control or cultural legitimacy.

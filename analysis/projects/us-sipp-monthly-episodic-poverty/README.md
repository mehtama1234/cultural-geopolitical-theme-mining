# Project: monthly versus lasting poverty

## Question

Does a stable monthly poverty rate mean that poverty is stable for the same
people, or can short and long poverty spells be hidden inside the average?

## Bounded answer

The 2025 SIPP brief for 2024 reports a monthly poverty rate of 11.9% in January
that did not significantly change during the year. But 15.1% of people were in
poverty for at least two consecutive months, and among that group 59.2% were in
poverty for all 12 months. The average can therefore look steady while the
length and concentration of hardship still matter.

This does not explain why a spell began, what assistance people received, or
what happened after it ended.

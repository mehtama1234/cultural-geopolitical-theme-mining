# Project: health coverage expansion and the remaining uninsured

## Question

As Marketplace and other coverage routes expand, who remains uninsured and
what does coverage status actually tell us about usable care?

## Decision rule

Keep insurance status, coverage type, continuity, affordability, provider
access, care use, and health outcome separate. Having a plan is not the same as
being able to use care without cost or network barriers.

## Current bounded finding

The [NHIS finding](findings/us-cdc-nhis-health-insurance-coverage-2025-001.md)
shows that exchange coverage grew while the overall uninsured rate remained
roughly stable and unequal coverage persisted across age and racial groups.

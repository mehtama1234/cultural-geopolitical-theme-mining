# Project: US health coverage and staying in a job

## Question

When health insurance comes through a job, does coverage keep a worker in place even when the job, pay or next opportunity is a poor fit?

## Short first pass

Compare workers with and without employer coverage, and parents whose dependents gain or lose eligibility. Track job changes, hours, pay, health coverage and the cost of moving to another plan.

## Possible connection

A job can provide two things at once: wages and a route to health care. That can protect a family, but it can also make changing jobs, retraining or starting a business feel too risky.

## Decision rule

Move on after one source measures coverage and job movement, one source tests a policy change, and one next dataset can follow the same worker's coverage, job and household costs. Do not assume staying means satisfaction or that all employer plans create the same lock.

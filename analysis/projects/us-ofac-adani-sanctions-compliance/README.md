# Project: OFAC sanctions reach and firm correction

## Question

What happens when a foreign firm's transactions move through U.S. financial
channels and OFAC identifies apparent sanctions violations?

## Decision rule

Keep transaction exposure, enforcement, settlement payment, compliance reform,
financial-system reach, deterrence, and later firm behavior separate. A large
penalty and a compliance commitment show institutional correction, not proof
that sanctions changed a country's strategy or prevented future violations.

## Current bounded finding

The [OFAC finding](findings/us-ofac-adani-sanctions-compliance-001.md) records a
foreign firm’s 32 apparent Iran-sanctions violations involving about $192.1
million in U.S.-dollar payments, followed by a $275 million settlement and a
five-year compliance commitment. Future compliance, deterrence, and strategic
effect remain open.

# Project: Digital surveillance and managerial clarity

## Question

Does digital worker surveillance change performance by itself, or does the
manager's explanation and performance-based rationale determine the response?

## Short finding route

Keep surveillance, explanation, worker interpretation, output, pay, autonomy,
voice, and household security as separate stages. This randomized online-labor-
market experiment supplies a causal implementation mechanism, not a national
workforce or long-run employment estimate.

See the [bounded finding](findings/us-nber-digital-surveillance-managerial-clarity-2025-001.md).

# Connecticut SNAP storm-food replacement follow-up

**Checked:** 2026-10-20  
**Status:** official report-and-remedy route observed; receipt, adequacy, and recovery remain open

## Bounded episode

Connecticut announced a July 2026 route for SNAP households whose food,
already purchased with SNAP benefits, was spoiled after a storm-related power
outage lasting at least four hours. Households had to report the loss by July
14. DSS required a signed household statement and verification through a utility
contact, community documentation, collateral contact, or home visit. The
replacement was capped at the monthly SNAP benefit and was to be issued within
ten days of the report or two working days after the required statement,
whichever was later.

```text
storm power outage
  -> spoiled SNAP-purchased food
  -> household report by July 14
  -> DSS statement and outage/loss verification
  -> [open] replacement received within stated timing rule
  -> [open] replacement adequacy, food security, trust, and later recovery
```

This is a stronger remedy design endpoint than the electronic-theft route,
because the public record specifies a report clock, verification mechanism,
cap, and issuance timing. It still does not show how many households reported,
qualified, received benefits, or returned to adequate food access.

## Next decisive evidence

Seek a lawful aggregate of reports, approvals, replacement dollars, and actual
issuance, ideally separated by outage duration and household benefit amount.
The decisive same-case endpoint would join the report to issuance, replacement

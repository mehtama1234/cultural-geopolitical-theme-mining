# Connecticut SNAP stolen-benefit corrective-remand follow-up

**Checked:** 2026-10-20  
**Status:** hearing correction observed; later replacement receipt and food recovery remain open

## What changed

An additional Connecticut DSS administrative hearing decision records an
anonymized household with $292.44 in unauthorized out-of-state SNAP
transactions. DSS issued a replacement EBT card but denied replacement of the
benefits after receiving the completed request form 32 days after discovery.
The hearing officer found that DSS had not shown the household was notified
of the form requirement, granted the appeal, rescinded the denial, and ordered
DSS to process the request as timely.

```text
unauthorized EBT transactions
  -> report and replacement-card issuance
  -> initial benefit-replacement denial
  -> hearing review and corrective remand
  -> [open] replacement credit, receipt, and food access
  -> [open] adequacy, household recovery, trust, action, and exit
```

This adds an institutional-correction stage to the Connecticut theft/remedy
lane. It is not a verified payment or recovery outcome: the public decision
does not show whether the later replacement was issued, received, spent, or
sufficient to cover the household’s food needs.

## Next decisive evidence

Seek a lawful post-remand record showing the replacement determination or
credit, timing, and later benefit continuity. Preserve the distinction between
an adjudicated correction, a replacement-card issuance, and usable food access.

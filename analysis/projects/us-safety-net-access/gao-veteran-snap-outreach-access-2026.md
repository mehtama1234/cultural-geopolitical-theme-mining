# Veteran SNAP outreach reaches open recommendations while benefit conversion remains unmeasured

**Checked:** 2026-10-20  
**Stage:** food-insecurity exposure → targeted outreach and interagency recommendations; benefit access and food-security conversion remain open

GAO's October 1, 2026 review adds a veteran-specific safety-net route. A
2023 RAND estimate cited by GAO found that an average of 1.4 million veterans
faced food insecurity each year from 2015 through 2020. In fiscal year 2026,
39 of 47 states with reviewed SNAP outreach plans identified veterans as a
target group.

The institutional stage is incomplete coordination and open corrective action.
VA and USDA established a memorandum of agreement in September 2023, but GAO
reports that collaboration slowed after April 2025 and the agencies had not
reviewed the agreement as specified. GAO made four open recommendations,
including updated SNAP eligibility information, veteran-tailored outreach
materials, and a formal mechanism for VA–USDA collaboration; USDA and VA
concurred with the recommendations.

The evidence boundary is material. State plan targeting is intended outreach,
not veteran contact or benefit receipt. The food-insecurity estimate is not a
current SNAP-eligible denominator. The public record does not show
veteran-level applications, approvals, payments, adequacy, food-security
change, household recovery, trust, or completed agency action.

# Project: US guaranteed income and household dynamics

## Question

When one person receives regular cash, what changes for the rest of the household: training, work, care, spending or health choices?

## Short first pass

Track the recipient and other household members separately. Measure income, work, education, care, health spending and forgone care before treating a household result as a benefit or harm.

## Possible connection

Cash can change who has time and permission to act inside a family. A recipient's gain may support another person's training, while greater contact with care can also reveal needs that were already unmet.

## Decision rule

Move on after one experiment measures effects beyond the recipient, one source measures housing or spending, and one next dataset can follow health and work choices after payments end. Do not treat reported forgone care as proof that cash caused worse health.

# Source search: US safe drinking water

**Search date:** 2026-09-11  
**Geography:** United States  
**Status:** opening pass; water quality and older-adult mortality are measured, the household cost and political path remain open

## Working question

When does a basic public service change both health and what a place is worth to the people living there?

## Opening source

| ID | Source | What it tells us | Status | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| US-NBER-WATER-WORKS | [Water Works: Causes and Consequences of Safe Drinking Water in America](https://www.nber.org/papers/w35288) | Using 266 million readings from 48 states and Medicare records, the authors find that readings above current health standards fell by about half from 2003 to 2019. Low-income areas had higher pollution. Safe Drinking Water Act loans reduced pollution and older-adult mortality; the authors estimate a cost of about $46 per person a year to remove pollution above health standards at the average loan cost. | NBER working paper | It does not follow the same household through exposure, bottled-water spending, home value, health care, trust or political action. |

## First pattern to test

```text
public water funding
  -> system quality
  -> household exposure and trust
  -> health, spending and home value
  -> local demand for repair or public action
```

The source supports the public-loan, pollution and mortality links. It does not establish the full household or political path.

## Counterpoint to keep visible

Pollution has fallen, and the average loan result does not mean every system needs the same fix. A community average can hide large differences between homes and people.

## Main gaps

- household-level exposure and water use;
- bottled water, filters and other private costs;
- rent, home value and moving decisions;
- local repair timing and who receives it;
- trust, complaints and political response;
- differences by income, race, age and place.

## Decision rule

Keep water quality, public spending, household cost, health outcome and political response as separate steps until a source connects them.

# Project: US safe drinking water

## Question

When does a basic public service change both health and what a place is worth to the people living there?

## Short first pass

Keep water tests, public loans, household exposure, bottled-water spending, health, home value and trust separate. Follow the path from a public fix to a lived result.

## Possible connection

Water quality may sit underneath both household health and the value of a place. A family can pay the same rent or mortgage while receiving a different level of safety from the tap.

## Decision rule

Move on after one source measures water quality and health, one source measures household spending or home value, and one local record shows who gets a fix. Do not treat a system average as one family's exposure.

# Project: US rideshare licensing and customer safety

## Question

Does requiring a rideshare driver to hold a license improve the customer's ride, or mainly change who can enter the work?

## Short first pass

Separate licensing, driver entry, experience, ratings, driving behavior, fare, wait time and customer safety.

## Possible connection

A rule can be sold as customer protection while its main effect may be on worker access or the supply of rides. The right test is the service customers receive, not the rule's stated purpose.

## Decision rule

Move on after one licensing change, one customer-quality measure, and one worker or fare result. Do not call a rule useful or harmful from entry counts alone.

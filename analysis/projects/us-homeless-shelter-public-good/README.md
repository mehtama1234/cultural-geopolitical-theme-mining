# Project: US homeless shelter as public good and incomplete recovery

## Question

What does temporary shelter change for people experiencing homelessness,
nearby communities, health systems, and future housing trajectories?

## Short finding route

Keep shelter availability, entry, unsheltered exposure, crime, psychiatric
care, mortality, and future homelessness as separate endpoints. A service can
produce public benefits without producing durable housing for every person.

See the [bounded finding](findings/us-homeless-shelter-public-good-001.md).

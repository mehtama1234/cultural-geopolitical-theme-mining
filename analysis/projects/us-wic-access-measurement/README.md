# Project: WIC access and what the measure means

This project follows WIC from eligibility to measurable participation. A
Census-FNS-ERS Joint Project links restricted WIC administrative records with
the American Community Survey to estimate eligibility and access at the state
and county levels, with demographic and socioeconomic breakdowns. The project
also compares Census and USDA/FNS estimates, which use different methods and
measurement scopes.

The important point is measurement discipline. A reliable estimate can still
be an estimate of a particular denominator and definition. Eligibility,
participation, application completion, benefit adequacy, food security, and
child health are different endpoints.

The decisive next artifact is a protected eligible-person-to-program ledger
joining eligibility, outreach, application, clinic or agency route, approval,
benefit amount, timing, shopping, food security, and child or maternal health.
The public abstract does not provide that full chain or the numeric subgroup
rates.

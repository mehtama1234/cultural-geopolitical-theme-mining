# Project: federal eligibility data and usable oversight

## Question

Can agencies reliably connect eligibility, exclusion, award, and payment data
well enough to know whether public money went to the right entity?

## Decision rule

Keep data availability, identifier matching, data quality, an investigative
lead, a confirmed improper payment, correction, and public outcome separate. A
successful data match is not proof that an award was improper.

## Current bounded finding

The [GAO finding](findings/us-gao-federal-eligibility-data-interoperability-2026-001.md)
shows that all nine selected federal eligibility sources had data-quality
issues, seven had cross-source inconsistencies, and no governmentwide authority
was designated to coordinate interoperability.

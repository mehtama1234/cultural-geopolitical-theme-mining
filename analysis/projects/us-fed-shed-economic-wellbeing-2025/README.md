# US household economic well-being in 2025

This project examines how people judge their own finances, their local economy,
and the national economy. It treats these as separate cultural and political
meaning surfaces rather than one confidence score.

The source is the Federal Reserve's 2025 Survey of Household Economics and
Decisionmaking report. It provides current population estimates and subgroup
comparisons, but it does not follow a single household through a shock, remedy,
political action, or later security outcome.

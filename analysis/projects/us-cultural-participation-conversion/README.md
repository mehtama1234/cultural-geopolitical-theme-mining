# Cultural participation, production, and belonging

## Question

When cultural participation is available in principle, what converts local
supply, money, digital access, and identity into attendance, active creation,
belonging, and repeat participation?

## Decision rule

Keep perceived supply, ticket purchase, attendance, active creation, creator
work, cultural identity, and civic or wellbeing outcomes separate. Attendance is
not belonging, and a cultural credit or platform reach is not durable access.

## Current bounded finding

The [cultural access and production conversion bridge](cultural-access-production-belonging-bridge-v1.md)
joins current U.S., Australian, European, British, and New Zealand evidence
without treating unlike surveys as one population estimate.

## Long-term route

```text
household resources / place / identity
  -> local supply and ticket gateway
  -> attendance or active creation
  -> creator work and institutional mediation
  -> belonging, repeat participation, civic connection, or exit
```

This project remains an open research route, not a completed causal chain.

The current [shared cultural participation and civic conversion bridge](shared-cultural-participation-civic-conversion-bridge-v1.md) narrows the next test to shared versus solitary activity, income-stratified access, loneliness, and the unstable cross-survey arts-to-civic association.

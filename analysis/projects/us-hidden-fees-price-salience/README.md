# Project: US hidden fees and the price people can compare

## Question

When the first price is low but the full cost appears later, does the fee change what people buy and how much they pay?

## Short first pass

Compare the first price, the final price, the point when the fee appears and the choice that follows. Keep extra spending, lower search and later regret separate.

## Possible connection

A household cannot protect its budget from a price it cannot compare. A fee can shift power from the shopper's search to the seller's screen design.

## Decision rule

Move on after one source observes the purchase path, one source measures the final-price effect, and one next dataset can test fees by income and product. Do not call every separate fee deceptive or every higher purchase harmful.

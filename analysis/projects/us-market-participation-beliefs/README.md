# Project: market participation and financial beliefs

## Question

When people fear losses, do they leave markets—and what happens when the people
who remain set prices for everyone else?

## Decision rule

Keep beliefs, participation, exit, portfolio choice, realized returns, wealth,
and household security separate. A pessimistic belief is not proof of a bad
investment outcome, and market participation is not the same as financial
wellbeing.

## Current bounded finding

The [investor-beliefs finding](findings/comparative-nber-investor-beliefs-market-participation-2026-001.md)
reports that investors who step out of markets are more pessimistic and perceive
greater crash risk, while those beliefs relate to participation and some stock
returns. The geographic frame and household consequences remain open.

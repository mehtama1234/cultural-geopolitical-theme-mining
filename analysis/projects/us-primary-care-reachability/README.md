# Project: Primary-care shortage to usable care

## Question

When a place or population is formally designated as a primary-care shortage,
can a person actually find a site, book an appointment, afford the visit,
complete care, and maintain continuity?

## Working chain

`shortage designation -> named organization and site -> hours, service, and
acceptance -> appointment and wait -> completed visit -> follow-up and
continuity -> health, work, household time, trust, and exit`

## Relationship to existing research

Existing atlas work compares county establishments with HPSA designations and
joins Ohio HPSA rows to service sites. This is a separate depth lane for the
patient-facing middle: nominal capacity is not the same as reachable,
affordable, completed care.

## Next test

Follow one component or site and a defined patient population through service
type, insurance acceptance, appointment attempt, wait, travel/time cost,
completed care, follow-up, and later health or work outcome. Retain failed,
abandoned, and redirected attempts alongside completed visits.

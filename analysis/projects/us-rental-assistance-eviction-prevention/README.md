# Project: US rental assistance and eviction prevention

## Question

When does rent aid prevent displacement, and when does it only buy time?

## Short first pass

Separate immediate rent payment, fear of eviction, housing stability, financial distress and later credit. Compare cash aid with legal help and with changes in the rent itself.

## Possible connection

Rent aid and legal help act at different points in the same chain. One adds money; the other changes a tenant's position in court. Neither should be treated as a lasting fix without later records.

## Decision rule

Move on after one study measures actual aid outcomes, one study explains who recovers after missed rent, and one next dataset follows households after assistance ends. Do not call a payment increase a lasting housing win.

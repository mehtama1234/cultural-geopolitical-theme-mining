# Project: social norms and formal enforcement

## Question

Can a society's informal expectations keep public officials honest, or do norms
need continuing enforcement to remain effective?

## Decision rule

Keep perceived norms, actual conduct, information access, enforcement,
deterrence, trust, and public legitimacy separate. A low-corruption reputation
is not proof that misconduct has disappeared.

## Current bounded finding

The [social-norms finding](findings/comparative-nber-social-norms-enforcement-public-officials-2026-001.md)
uses Singapore housing transactions and reports evidence that integrity norms
are persistent but fragile: stronger enforcement reduced observed direct and
indirect misconduct. This is comparative evidence, not a U.S. estimate.

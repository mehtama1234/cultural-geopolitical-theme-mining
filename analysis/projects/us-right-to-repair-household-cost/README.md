# Project: US repair access and household cost

## Question

When a product breaks, can the household repair it at a fair cost, or is replacement the only practical path?

## Short first pass

Separate product design, parts, tools, software, warranty, repair price, independent repair, replacement, safety, and household loss.

## Decision rule

Move on after one repair restriction, one repair or replacement cost, and one household outcome. Do not treat every repair limit as anti-consumer or every independent repair as safe.

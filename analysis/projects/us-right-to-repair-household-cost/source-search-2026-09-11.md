# Source search: US repair access and household cost

**Search date:** 2026-09-11  
**Geography:** United States  
**Status:** matched evidence pass complete; product-level repair prices and household outcomes remain open

## Working question

When a product breaks, can the household repair it at a fair cost, or is replacement the only practical path?

## Sources

| ID | Source | What it tells us | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| FTC-NIX-FIX-2021 | [FTC: Nixing the Fix](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/documents/reports/nixing-fix-ftc-report-congress-repair-restrictions/nixing_the_fix_report_final_5521_630pm-508_002.pdf) | Repair restrictions can include difficult design, unavailable parts or information, software locks, and steering to authorized networks; FTC says they can limit choice and push replacement. | The report brings together comments and research; it is not a household-level estimate for every product. |
| FTC-WARRANTY-2024 | [FTC warning on warranty repair practices](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/07/ftc-warns-companies-stop-warranty-practices-harm-consumers-right-repair) | FTC staff warned companies that requiring specified parts or service providers to keep warranty coverage may violate the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act. | Warning letters do not measure repair access or final consumer cost. |
| FTC-WARRANTIES | [FTC warranty guidance](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/warranties) | Warranties may cover repair, replacement, or refund, and consumers need written terms, records, and claims steps. | Guidance does not show how often claims succeed or how much repair costs after warranty. |
| FTC-DEERE-2026 | [FTC and states Deere repair settlement](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/07/ftc-states-secure-settlement-deere-company-advancing-farmers-right-repair) | A 2026 settlement advances repair access for farmers using John Deere equipment. | Farm equipment is a business asset, not the same as a household appliance or phone. |

## First pattern to test

```text
product breaks
  -> parts, tools, software, warranty, and repair shop choices
  -> repair price and waiting time
  -> repair, replacement, borrowing, or going without
  -> household money, work, safety, and waste change
```

## Decision rule

Keep repair access, repair quality, price, warranty, replacement, and household outcome separate until product records join them.

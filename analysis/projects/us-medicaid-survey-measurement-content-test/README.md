# Project: Medicaid survey measurement and administrative comparison

## Question

Does changing the ACS health-insurance question make Medicaid coverage easier to
measure accurately against enrollment records?

## Decision rule

Keep question wording, reported coverage, administrative enrollment, linkage,
active coverage, care use, affordability, and continuity separate. A redesigned
question is not automatically a better measure or better health access.

## Current bounded finding

The [Census SEHSD finding](findings/us-census-medicaid-acs-question-content-test-cms-measurement-2022-001.md)
reports that the redesigned and control ACS questions performed equally well
against CMS Medicaid enrollment records in the 2022 test.

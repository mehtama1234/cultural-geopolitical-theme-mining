# Project: US returns, complaints, and the reality behind reviews

## Question

Do reviews predict what happens after purchase, or do returns and complaints reveal a different customer experience?

## Short first pass

Separate review exposure, purchase, product quality, return cost, complaint, remedy, and repeat choice.

## Decision rule

Move on after one review or ranking measure, one post-purchase outcome, and one remedy measure. Do not call a review accurate without checking what happened afterward.

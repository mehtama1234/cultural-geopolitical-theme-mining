# Project: US rising income risk at the top

## Question

What happens to the wider economy when high earners face more income risk and respond by saving more?

## Short first pass

Separate income loss, income uncertainty, saving, wealth inequality, interest rates, and the household results lower down the income range.

## Possible connection

A change among top earners can look distant from ordinary consumer life, yet higher saving and wider wealth gaps can affect the cost of money and who can absorb a shock.

## Decision rule

Move on after one tax-record measure, one saving or wealth result, and one link to borrowing costs or household demand. Do not treat a model estimate as a direct cause of a family's financial stress.

# Project: US student-loan policy uncertainty

## Question

What does a policy promise make households do before the promise becomes real?

## Short first pass

Separate the announcement, belief, loan payment, nondurable spending, durable spending, delinquency and later policy decision. Follow the borrower into housing and other adult choices.

## Possible connection

Government uncertainty can act like a temporary income change. A borrower may spend less on the loan before a decision, then face a sharper payment problem if the expected pause or forgiveness does not arrive.

## Decision rule

Move on after one study links policy beliefs to credit and spending records, one source measures actual student-debt outcomes, and one next dataset can follow borrowers through housing, work and family choices. Do not treat an expectation as a benefit received.

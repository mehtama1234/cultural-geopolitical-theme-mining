# Project: NATO support and political permission

## Question

Can an alliance remain broadly popular while political groups disagree about
the U.S. role and the leaders who carry it out?

## Bounded answer

Pew's 2026 survey found a median of 65% favorable opinion of NATO across 13
member states. In the United States, 75% of Democrats and Democratic leaners
viewed NATO favorably, compared with 42% of Republicans and Republican leaners.
The same survey reported much lower confidence in some leaders than support
for the alliance itself.

The institution's favorability is therefore not the same thing as stable
permission for every U.S. policy, leader, cost, or commitment.

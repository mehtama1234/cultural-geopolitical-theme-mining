# Project: NATO host-nation rehearsal and allied movement

## Question

What becomes visible when an alliance practices moving and sustaining forces
with a host country, rather than only publishing resilience requirements?

## Decision rule

Treat a rehearsal as evidence that people and institutions practiced a route.
Do not treat it as proof of real-crisis speed, service continuity, public
support, cost sharing, or deterrence.

## Current bounded finding

The [Steadfast Discovery finding](findings/us-nato-host-nation-rehearsal-denmark-001.md)
shows that NATO and Denmark rehearsed liaison, logistics, transport, movement,
reception, and sustainment procedures. The rehearsal closes a coordination-
practice stage, while actual crisis performance and civilian burden remain open.

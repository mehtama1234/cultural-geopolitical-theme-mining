# Project: US career progression and the mid-level plateau

## Question

What happens when a person works for years but gains little real movement in responsibility, title or pay—and does that change debt, housing or family choices?

## Short first pass

Use the HBS account of NBER Working Paper 34292 as the starting point. Track time to reach a level, job changes, pay, title meaning and sector moves separately. Do not treat a title change as real progress without checking responsibility or pay.

## Possible connection

A slow career path can narrow the room around a student-loan payment, housing purchase or family plan. That is a question to test, not a claim that career plateau causes debt trouble.

## Decision rule

Start with one measured career-progression pattern and one counterpoint about job changes or sector differences. Move on unless the data can connect career movement to a later household choice.

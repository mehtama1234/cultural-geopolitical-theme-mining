# Party pressure and representation

This project studies the institutional middle between voter preferences and
legislative action. It keeps separate constituent ideology, member ideology,
party pressure, roll-call votes, agenda setting, constituent service, trust, and
material outcomes.

The first bounded finding uses NBER Working Paper 34697. It bridges survey-based
voter ideology with U.S. House roll-call voting and estimates a model in which
parties strategically pressure members. The paper reports that party pressure
creates a wedge between members' actual votes and constituent preferences while
also helping parties balance legislative wins and electoral concerns.

## Next test

The decisive extension is a protected member-district-year ledger joining voter

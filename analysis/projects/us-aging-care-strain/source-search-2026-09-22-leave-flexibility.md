# Source search: formal leave versus usable time for care and health

**Search date:** 2026-09-22  
**Geography:** United States  
**Status:** bounded pass complete; BLS measures access and unmet need, while later health, care, job, and household outcomes remain open

## Working question

When workers need time for illness, family care, errands, or other personal needs, does formal leave access become usable time?

## Opening source

| ID | Source | What it tells us | Status | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| US-BLS-ATUS-LEAVE-2024-2025 | [Access to and Use of Leave — 2024-2025](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/leave.htm) | BLS reports paid and unpaid leave access, actual leave use, and reasons workers needed leave but did not take it. | Official BLS American Time Use Survey module, sponsored by the Department of Labor Women’s Bureau | The release covers workers’ main jobs and self-reports; it does not follow the same worker into health, care completion, income, job retention, or household recovery. |

## First pattern

```text
illness, care, errands, or family need
  -> formal paid/unpaid leave access
  -> work, income, employer, and schedule constraints
  -> leave taken or needed leave not taken
  -> [open] care result, health result, job outcome, and household security
```

The source adds a usable-time version of the care lane. A worker can have a
formal leave route and still not use it because work is too heavy, the job may
penalize the request, or losing income is not affordable.

## Counterpoint to keep visible

Leave access and non-use are not the same as leave denial or harm. Workers may
make alternate arrangements, save leave, or decide the need changed. The
survey captures what workers know and report about their main job, not whether
the underlying illness or care need was resolved.

## Decision rule

Keep access, ability to use, need, non-use, reason, leave hours, pay loss,
care completion, health, job retention, and later household security separate.
Reopen this lane only with a linked worker or household episode that observes
the need, request, actual time, material result, and later outcome.

# Source search: global aging and U.S. foreign policy

**Search date:** 2026-09-23  
**Geography:** Global demographic change and U.S. foreign-policy interests

## Working question

How could population aging abroad change the balance among security,
economic, health, humanitarian, and alliance priorities for the United States?

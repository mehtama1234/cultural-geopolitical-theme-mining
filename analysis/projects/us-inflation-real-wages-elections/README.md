# Project: US inflation, real wages, and elections

## Question

Do voters punish rising prices, falling buying power, or the feeling that pay no longer covers the same life?

## Short first pass

Separate local prices, nominal pay, real pay, family budget cost, vote share, turnout, and the story voters attach to each.

## Possible connection

The political effect of inflation may run less through the headline rate than through whether local pay keeps up with the family's costs. That gives the household map a direct test at the ballot.

## Decision rule

Move on after one local price measure, one real-wage measure, and one election result. Do not treat county-level association as one voter's proven reason for voting.

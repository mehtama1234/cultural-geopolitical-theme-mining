# Project: US product recalls and household response

## Question

When a dangerous product is recalled, do households hear about it, act on it, and recover what they lost?

## Short first pass

Separate recall notice, household exposure, product removal, customer action, remedy, injury, and trust.

## Matched evidence pass

- [A recall succeeds only when the household can act](../../findings/us-product-recall-response-matched-evidence-001.md)
- [Reader-friendly HTML](../../../site/us-product-recall-response-matched-evidence-001.html)
- [Source search record](source-search-2026-09-11.md)

## Decision rule

Move on after one notification measure, one customer-action measure, and one remedy or harm measure. Do not count a posted notice as a completed recall.

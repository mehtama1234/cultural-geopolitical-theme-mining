# Project: AI exposure and early-career hiring

## Question

Did the arrival of ChatGPT change hiring and employment for workers entering
the labor market, especially in the most AI-exposed industry-state markets?

## Decision rule

Keep industry exposure, firm use, hiring, employment, earnings, delayed entry,
career scarring, household resources, and later recovery separate. A lower
entry cohort is not automatically permanent displacement.

## Current bounded finding

The [Census CES finding](findings/us-census-ai-early-career-hiring-qwi-2026-001.md)
reports a 12% relative employment decline for 22-24-year-olds in the most
AI-exposed industry-state cells over ten quarters after ChatGPT's introduction,
with hiring as the main channel. Hiring later recovered from a smaller base.

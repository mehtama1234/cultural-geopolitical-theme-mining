# Project: wearable health data and clinical judgment

## Question

When personal sensors produce more health data, does that become better care,
or does it create a new reliability, workflow, privacy, and responsibility
problem?

## Decision rule

Keep sensor output, clinical interpretation, workflow integration, patient
benefit, privacy protection, and health outcome separate. A device's ability to
measure something is not proof that it improves care.

## Current bounded finding

The [GAO finding](findings/us-gao-wearable-clinical-decision-making-2026-001.md)
shows that wearables may support faster and more personalized care, while
reliability, integration, liability, privacy, and market-navigation problems
remain open.

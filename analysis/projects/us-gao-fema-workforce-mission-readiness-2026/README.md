# Project: FEMA workforce capacity and disaster-assistance reach

## Question

Can changes in the federal disaster workforce alter whether assistance is
reachable when multiple or severe disasters create simultaneous demand?

## Short finding route

Keep workforce size, institutional knowledge, planning, deployment, contact
capacity, survivor access, and downstream recovery as separate stages. GAO's
review documents a federal capacity condition and selected delivery evidence;
it is not a survivor-level causal outcome study.

See the [bounded finding](findings/us-gao-fema-workforce-mission-readiness-2026-001.md).

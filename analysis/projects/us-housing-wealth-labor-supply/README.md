# Project: housing wealth, labor supply, and care choices

## Question

When housing wealth changes, does it alter work through childcare and human-
capital choices, and does that pathway differ by gender and parenthood?

## Decision rule

Keep housing wealth, labor-force participation, employment, hours, wages,
childcare, unpaid care, job quality, household security, and choice separate.
Nonparticipation is not automatically freedom, hardship, or political withdrawal.

## Current bounded finding

The [Census CES finding](findings/us-census-housing-wealth-womens-labor-supply-childcare-2026-001.md)
reports a negative labor-supply response to housing wealth for women, strongest
among mothers of young children and women citing child-related reasons for not
working, with no significant male effect. The study does not observe the full
care and wellbeing tradeoff.

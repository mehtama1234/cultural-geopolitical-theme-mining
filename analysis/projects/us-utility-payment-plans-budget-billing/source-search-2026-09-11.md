# Source search: US utility payment plans and budget billing

**Search date:** 2026-09-11  
**Geography:** United States  
**Status:** matched evidence pass complete; customer-level payment and true-up records remain open

## Working question

Does smoothing a utility bill protect household cash flow, or does it hide a balance that arrives later?

## Sources

| ID | Source | What it tells us | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| CFPB-BUDGET-PLAN | [CFPB regulation interpretation: home fuel budget plans](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/2023-01-01/interp-3/) | A typical home-fuel budget plan estimates seasonal cost, bills an average monthly amount, and adjusts the final payment for the difference between estimated and actual cost. | This is a description of a plan structure, not evidence of how many households face a later balance or whether they stay current. |

## First pattern to test

```text
budget or payment plan
  -> smoother monthly withdrawal
  -> actual use differs from estimate
  -> balance, true-up, fee, or continued protection
  -> stable service or another cash crisis
```

The plan changes timing first. The next test is whether it changes the total burden and the chance of falling behind.

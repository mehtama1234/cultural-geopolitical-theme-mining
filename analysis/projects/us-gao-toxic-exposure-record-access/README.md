# Project: toxic-exposure records, claims, and veteran access

## Question

Can a shared exposure record convert military-service exposure into usable
health-care and disability-claim access when the people affected cannot yet
see or correct the record themselves?

## Short finding route

Keep exposure screening, record linkage, staff use, self-access, correction,
claim decision, treatment, and health outcome separate. The GAO review supplies
the record-system and rollout stages, not an individual claim or health panel.

See the [bounded finding](findings/us-gao-toxic-exposure-record-access-2026-001.md).

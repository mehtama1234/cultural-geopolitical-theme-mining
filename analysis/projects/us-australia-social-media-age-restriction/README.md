# Project: Social-media age restriction and platform compliance

## Question

When a government requires platforms to prevent under-16 accounts, do formal
rules become actual access control, and what remains unresolved after accounts
are removed or restricted?

## Short finding route

Keep the legal obligation, platform action, account retention, age-assurance
route, parent experience, migration, enforcement, and child-safety outcome as
separate stages. This is comparative Australian evidence used to test a
platform-governance mechanism, not a U.S. prevalence estimate.

See the [bounded finding](findings/us-australia-social-media-age-restriction-2026-001.md).

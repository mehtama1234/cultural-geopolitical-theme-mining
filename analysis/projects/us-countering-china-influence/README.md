# Project: countering Chinese influence and measurable geopolitical results

## Question

Can a U.S. geopolitical funding portfolio turn money and projects into changed
partner choices, local legitimacy, or reduced Chinese influence—and can the
government tell which projects did so?

## Current bounded finding

The [GAO finding](findings/us-gao-countering-china-influence-project-results-2020-2024-001.md)
shows that State and USAID reported about 470 projects and $1.2 billion in
allocations from fiscal years 2020–2023, but the agencies lacked complete
project data and had not assessed results across the portfolio.

This is a measurement and state-capacity signal. It is not evidence that the
projects failed, succeeded, or changed partner behavior.

## Next test

Follow a named project through proposal, expert input, award, implementation,
partner use, local response, continuation, and an attributed change in policy,
behavior, or dependence.

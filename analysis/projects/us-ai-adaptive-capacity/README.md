# AI adaptive capacity and unequal exposure

This project examines whether exposure to AI-driven task change is the same thing as being unable to manage a job transition. The initial source separates occupation-level AI exposure from an adaptive-capacity index and identifies a smaller group facing both high exposure and low expected capacity.

The bounded finding is useful for the broader atlas because it adds a missing middle layer between technological pressure and later outcomes: the practical options workers have for adapting. It remains an occupation-level finding, not a forecast of layoffs or a longitudinal account of individual workers.

## Next test

The next decisive test is to connect exposure and adaptive capacity to observed training, job transitions, earnings, and local labor-market conditions without treating occupation-level proxies as individual outcomes.

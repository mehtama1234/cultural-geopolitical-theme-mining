# Project: US home insurance affordability

## Question

When does keeping a home protected become a new barrier to keeping the home?

## Short first pass

Separate disaster risk, insurance premium, credit score, mortgage payment, home value and the decision to stay, sell or move.

## Possible connection

Insurance can turn a distant disaster risk into a monthly household cost. If credit also affects the premium, the ability to protect a home may depend on both where it sits and what is in the owner's financial record.

## Decision rule

Move on after one source measures premium changes by place, one measures the credit-price link, and one next dataset follows coverage, mortgage and move choices. Do not treat a higher premium as proof that a household will leave.

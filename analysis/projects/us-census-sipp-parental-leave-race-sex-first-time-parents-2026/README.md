# Project: Parental leave, race, sex, and return-to-work timing

## Question

Is parental leave one shared work-family experience, or do leave access and
return timing vary across first-time parents and over time?

## Short finding route

Keep leave use, paid leave, no leave, early return, race/ethnicity, sex,
occupation, job resources, and later outcomes as separate stages. This is a
descriptive 2023 SIPP working paper restricted to first-time parents who worked
before and after the birth; it does not estimate causal employer
discrimination or access for all parents.

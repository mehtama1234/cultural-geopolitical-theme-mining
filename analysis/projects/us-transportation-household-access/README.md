# Project: US transportation, household cost, and access

## Question

When getting to work, food, care and services requires a car or long travel, how much of the household budget is paying for movement rather than living at the destination?

## Short first pass

Use BLS for spending, Census for travel time and transit use, and DOE's energy-and-transportation burden layer for place comparisons. Keep owning a car, using a car, and having no workable alternative separate.

## Possible connection

Transportation is the part of household energy cost that the basic LEAD measure leaves out. It can connect energy and housing to work, food, childcare and local belonging: a cheaper home may require a costly trip, while a closer home may carry a higher rent or insurance bill.

## Decision rule

Move on after one spending measure, one time or access measure, one place comparison and a clear next test. Do not treat a national average as the cost faced by every household.

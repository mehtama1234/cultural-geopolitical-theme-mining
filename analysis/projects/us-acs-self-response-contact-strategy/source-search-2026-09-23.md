# Source search: ACS self-response and public participation

## Working question

When public institutions change the way they ask for information, what improves participation, and what remains outside the response-rate measure?

| Source | What it contributes | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| [Census ACS contact-strategy review](https://www.census.gov/library/working-papers/2026/acs/2026_Poehler_01.html) | Reviews two decades of ACS tests: selected layout, internet, and mailing changes improved response in particular periods, while a 2022 test was neutral and long-run self-response continued to decline | Aggregate survey comparisons do not show the same household's burden, trust, response accuracy, or later policy consequence |

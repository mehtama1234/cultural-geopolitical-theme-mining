# Project: weapons acquisition and capability realization

## Question

When the United States announces a rapid technology or weapons pathway, how
often does it become a fieldable capability on a realistic schedule?

## Decision rule

Keep budget, technology maturity, milestone, prototype, fielding, reliability,
sustainment, operational use, and deterrence separate. A rapid pathway is a
process label, not a delivered weapon.

## Current bounded finding

The [GAO acquisition finding](findings/us-gao-weapon-systems-mature-technology-rapid-delivery-2026-001.md)
reports persistent schedule delay and immature technology inside rapid-
acquisition pathways. Fielded capability and operational effect remain open.

# Source search: affordable housing supply versus usable housing supply

**Search date:** 2026-09-22  
**Geography:** United States, with regional comparisons  
**Status:** bounded pass complete; HUD measures supply sufficiency, while household search, acceptance, move, stay, and durability remain open

## Working question

When a household needs a low-cost rental, how much of the nominal affordable stock is actually available and physically adequate?

## Opening source

| ID | Source | What it tells us | Status | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| US-HUD-WORST-CASE-HOUSING-2025 | [Worst Case Housing Needs: 2025 Report to Congress](https://www.huduser.gov/portal/portal/sites/default/files/pdf/Worst-Case-Housing-Needs-2025-Report-to-Congress.pdf) | HUD's American Housing Survey tabulations distinguish affordable units from units that are affordable, available, and physically adequate. For extremely low-income renters, the usable stock is far smaller than the nominal affordable stock. | Official HUD report | National supply ratios do not show whether a particular household found, applied for, leased, or stayed in a unit, and they abstract from location and transportation. |

## First pattern

```text
very low household income
  -> nominally affordable rental stock
  -> higher-income occupancy and vacancy constraints
  -> physically adequate and available unit
  -> [open] search, landlord acceptance, lease-up, safety, stay, or move
```

The source adds a place-and-usability boundary. “Affordable housing exists”
can mean that enough units would be affordable if perfectly allocated. It does
not mean that a household can find one, that it is vacant, that it is in the
right place, or that it is physically safe.

## Counterpoint to keep visible

The ratios are national and aggregate. A shortage in the national stock does
not predict every local search, and a household may use family support,
subsidy, a different location, or a unit outside the affordability band.
Conversely, a national ratio can look improved while a particular place,
household, or accessible unit remains unreachable.

## Decision rule

Keep affordability, availability, physical adequacy, location, landlord
acceptance, voucher or assistance use, lease-up, repair, stay, and move as
separate stages. Reopen this lane only with a place- or household-level record
that links the stock condition to search, lease, safety, and later stability.

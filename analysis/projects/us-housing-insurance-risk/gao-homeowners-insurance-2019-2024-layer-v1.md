# GAO homeowners-insurance 2019–2024 layer v1

The [GAO 2026 review](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-26-107867) extends the
market window beyond the Treasury PCMI data. GAO analyzed 2019–2024 estimated
private-insurance premiums, 2020–2024 rate filings, and 2014–2023 residual
insurer-of-last-resort information.

The national average premium rose 3% after inflation, from $2,743 in 2019 to
$2,829 in 2024 dollars. That national movement masks local divergence: some
southern coastal areas rose at least 25% above inflation. GAO’s model estimated
premiums about 58% higher for high versus medium wind risk and 8% higher for
high versus medium wildfire risk.

The institutional layer also matters. GAO reviewed about 13,000 rate filings
from 2020–2024; median approval times were longest in Colorado (331 days) and
California (305 days). Approval time is not itself a price or availability
measure, and the report does not turn it into a universal causal claim.

The public backstop is part of the market structure: as of August 2025, 35
states operated FAIR or beach plans for homeowners unable to obtain private
coverage. These plans generally offer less coverage than private policies, and
some can assess private insurers when deficits need funding. Plan presence is
not enrollment or proof of private-market failure for every household; it is an
institutional risk-allocation mechanism.

The end-to-end bridge now has a later market and governance stage:

```text
hazard and property conditions
  -> modeled premium and availability pressure
  -> insurer filing and state review process
  -> household affordability/coverage options
  -> claims, repairs, lending, mobility, and political response (open)
```

GAO’s estimated premiums, econometric comparisons, selected filings, and
interviews complement—but do not replace—household survey and administrative
outcome evidence. The next test is to identify a state/year series that joins
approved rates, nonrenewals or residual-plan enrollment, household costs, and
post-loss outcomes at compatible units.

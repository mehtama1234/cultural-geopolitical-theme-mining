# Project: US political participation, money, and influence

## Question

What does federal campaign-finance activity show about political participation
and political resource concentration?

## Decision rule

Keep money raised, donor reach, spending, political communication, voting,
persuasion, policy influence, and democratic legitimacy separate. A large
financial flow is not a measure of broad participation or political success.

## Current bounded finding

The [FEC campaign-finance finding](findings/us-political-participation-001.md)
shows that candidate, party, and PAC money are visible at very different
scales, while the public record does not show who was persuaded, who voted, or
what policy changed.

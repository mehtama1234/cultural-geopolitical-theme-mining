# Project: US public aid and the shape of a dollar

## Question

Does the way public help arrives—cash or food, once or every month—change what households can buy and how they handle the next bill?

## Short first pass

Start with the new NBER study of pandemic-era aid to low-income families with children. Keep the amount of aid, its form, its timing, and the household's spending response as separate parts of the account.

## Possible connection

Public policy does not only decide how much help a family receives. It also decides what the help can be used for and when it appears in the household's week. That can connect aid rules to food security, household money and political views of government.

## Decision rule

Check whether the result holds outside pandemic programs and food-store spending. Move on unless the evidence shows which design works for which need, and whether a stronger short-run spending response means better security rather than only faster spending.

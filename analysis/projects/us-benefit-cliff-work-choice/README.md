# Project: US benefit loss and the work choice

## Question

When a household earns more, does the extra pay create more room, or does the loss of food, health or child care help take much of it back?

## Short first pass

Separate three things: benefits that taper as income rises, benefits lost at a threshold, and work rules that remove help because of reporting or hours. Then compare the change in aid with the change in take-home money, care access and work.

## Possible connection

Public help can make work possible by paying for food, health care or child care. If the rules remove help faster than earnings add room, a household may face a hard choice even when its wage rises.

## Decision rule

Move on after one source measures benefit loss, one source measures actual work or program participation, and one next dataset can follow the same household across an earnings change. Do not say that every benefit program has a cliff or that every work rule lowers work.

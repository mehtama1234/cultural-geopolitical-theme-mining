# Project: lender liability, firm finance, and environmental behavior

## Question

When lenders can bear cleanup costs attached to a borrower's collateral, does
that financial exposure change firm pollution control and credit terms?

## Decision rule

Keep legal liability, loan pricing, firm borrowing, abatement investment,
pollution, production, employment, household exposure, health, and local
cleanup separate. A firm-level pollution result is not a household health
estimate.

## Current bounded finding

The [Census CES finding](findings/us-census-environmental-lender-liability-pollution-abatement-1996-2026-001.md)
reports that reducing lender cleanup liability was associated with lower firm
abatement investment and higher pollution, while higher lender risk produced
less favorable loan pricing and possible monitoring through shorter maturities.
The downstream community and household effects remain open.

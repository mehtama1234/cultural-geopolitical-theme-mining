# Loneliness, support, and unequal social connection

This project examines social connection as a cultural and unequal-exposure
surface. The CDC analysis measures loneliness, perceived support, and mental-
health indicators in a 26-state BRFSS sample. It does not follow people over
time or establish that loneliness caused a later health or political outcome.

The [Community & Connection finding](findings/us-cdc-community-connection-support-participation-2026-001.md)
adds a national measurement contrast: reported support is higher in the
displayed adult indicator than the youth indicator, while a separate adult
measure shows health-related difficulty participating in social activities.
Support, participation, belonging, and recovery remain separate stages.

# Project: Water infrastructure resilience, local affordability, and usable protection

## Question

When federal money supports water infrastructure in disaster-vulnerable
communities, does it become maintained and dependable protection?

## Decision rule

Keep grants, loans, project obligations, service-area targeting, construction,
maintenance, rates, operating continuity, water quality, and household health
separate. Financial assistance is not the same as resilient service.

## Current bounded finding

The [GAO water-resilience finding](findings/us-gao-water-infrastructure-resilience-vulnerable-communities-2025-001.md)
shows large federal assistance alongside local rate, repayment, maintenance,
and visibility barriers. The route is:

```text
disaster vulnerability
  -> federal grant or loan
  -> local project and rate/repayment decision
  -> construction and maintenance
  -> [open] reliable water service during disruption
  -> [open] household safety, affordability, health, and trust
```

The evidence does not follow one utility or household from financing through a
later disaster outcome.

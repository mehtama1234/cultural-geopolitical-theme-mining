# Project: Strategic Petroleum Reserve operational capability

## Question

Can a national energy reserve meet emergency directives when aging
infrastructure, construction outages, inventory, and commercial distribution
capacity limit its effective operating rates?

## Short finding route

Keep design rate, effective rate, inventory, emergency release, market
transmission, and household or firm incidence as separate stages. A reserve
can meet prior directives without proving that every future disruption can be
absorbed at design speed.

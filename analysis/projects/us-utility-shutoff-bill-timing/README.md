# Project: US utility shutoff and bill timing

## Question

When income arrives before or after the utility bill, can a timing mismatch turn into a late payment, a shutoff or a health and credit problem?

## Short first pass

Track the due date, benefit date, arrears, service termination and later health or credit outcome. Keep a late bill, a shutoff and a health event separate; they are not the same thing.

## Possible connection

The energy burden is not only the size of the bill. It also includes when money arrives, whether the household can move the due date, and what happens when service is cut.

## Decision rule

Move on after one source links bill timing to an observed service or financial outcome and one next dataset can test the pattern outside one state. Do not treat every late utility bill as a health event.

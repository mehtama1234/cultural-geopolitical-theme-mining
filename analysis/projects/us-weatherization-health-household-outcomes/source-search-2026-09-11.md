# Source search: US weatherization, health, and household outcomes

**Search date:** 2026-09-11  
**Geography:** United States  
**Status:** matched evidence pass complete; longer-run health and household records remain open

## Sources

| ID | Source | What it tells us | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| NBER-WAP-21331 | [NBER: Do Energy Efficiency Investments Deliver?](https://www.nber.org/papers/w21331) | An experimental evaluation of more than 30,000 households found actual energy savings below engineering projections; it did not find evidence of significantly higher indoor temperatures in weatherized homes. | The study measures a large program and energy outcomes; health, work, and later housing outcomes remain limited. |
| DOE-WAP-APPLY | [DOE: How to apply for weatherization](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/scep/wap/how-apply-weatherization-assistance) | The program audits energy use and home safety, serves renters and owners, and gives priority to high-burden and vulnerable households. | Eligibility and process do not show who completes the work or the final result. |
| DOE-LEAD-HEALTH | [DOE LEAD tool](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/scep/low-income-energy-affordability-data-lead-tool) | DOE connects weatherization with lower bills, indoor air quality, safety, comfort, and health. | Program description is not a causal health estimate. |
| EIA-ENERGY-INSECURITY | [EIA energy insecurity](https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=56640) | Energy insecurity includes unsafe temperatures, skipped necessities, disconnection notices, and inability to repair heating or cooling. | Survey measures household reports, not the effect of a specific upgrade. |

## First pattern to test

```text
weatherization work
  -> actual energy use and bill
  -> temperature, air quality, safety, and comfort
  -> health, work, care, and household cash
  -> future repair, housing, and trust choices
```

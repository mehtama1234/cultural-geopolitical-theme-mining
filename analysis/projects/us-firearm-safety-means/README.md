# Project: Safety goals and disagreement over protective tools

## Question

Can people want the same thing—safety—while disagreeing about whether lethal
firearms, non-lethal tools, or no weapon is the best way to get it?

## Decision rule

Keep safety goals, perceived risk, ownership, stated policy views, safe storage,
alternative-tool demand, and actual safety outcomes separate. Agreement about a
goal is not agreement about a policy or a result.

## Current bounded finding

The [NBER firearm-safety finding](findings/us-nber-firearm-safety-means-2026-001.md)
reports that owners and non-owners share a safety objective but see the safety
tradeoff differently. Information about non-lethal alternatives increased stated
support for safer margins without making current owners want to give up lethal
firearms.

The route is:

```text
shared safety concern
  -> different risk beliefs and identity-linked tools
  -> information or alternative becomes available
  -> stated storage, policy, or purchase preference
  -> [open] actual behavior and realized safety
```

The study shows why cultural disagreement can persist even when people name a
common value.

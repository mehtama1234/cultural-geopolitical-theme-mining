# Project: HUD assistance, employment, and earnings trajectories

## Question

What happens to employment and earnings trajectories around entry into or exit
from federal housing assistance?

## Short finding route

Keep housing-assistance entry, labor-market trajectory, exit, housing stability,
and household recovery separate. The Census CES study supplies linked
administrative employment/earnings follow-up, not a complete housing-choice or
remedy episode.

See the [bounded finding](findings/us-census-hud-lehd-assistance-employment-trajectories-2026-001.md).

The next executable pass is specified in the [housing-transition next-test
protocol](housing-transition-next-test-v1.md). It defines the minimum same-unit
promotion gate and keeps the restricted HUD–LEHD and regional HEARS records
separate from any public longitudinal survey route.

The public-data availability result is recorded in the [SIPP
housing-transition field audit](sipp-housing-transition-field-audit-v1.md).

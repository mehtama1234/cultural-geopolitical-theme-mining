# Project: US remote work and shopping cost

## Question

When work moves home, who gains time and who takes on the shopping system?

## Short first pass

Separate remote-work location, shopping trips, online ordering, product choice, price paid, deal use, total spending and unpaid household time.

## Possible connection

Remote work can change customer behavior through the household's daily schedule. The gain may be fewer commuting minutes, more shopping access or more product choice; the cost may be higher prices or a less equal split of unpaid work.

## Decision rule

Move on after one study measures shopping changes, one source measures household time, and one next dataset can join price, spending and unpaid work for the same household. Do not call higher spending a loss without measuring what the household received.

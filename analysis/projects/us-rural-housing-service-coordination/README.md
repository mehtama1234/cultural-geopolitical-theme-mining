# Project: rural housing service coordination

## Question

When affordable housing residents need health care, food, transportation, or
benefits, does the housing system provide a usable social bridge—or only a
unit and a referral that households must navigate alone?

## Decision rule

Keep housing availability, coordinator presence, referral work, service receipt,
resident outcomes, and durable housing stability separate. A service
coordinator's reported activity is not a resident-level outcome.

## Current bounded finding

The [GAO finding](findings/us-gao-rural-housing-service-coordination-data-outcomes-2026-001.md)
shows that coordinators can help residents navigate eviction and Medicaid
problems, while HUD cannot fully count the program or routinely use its
performance data to assess results.

The [service relationships and data visibility bridge](service-relationship-data-visibility-bridge-v1.md)
connects the GAO evidence to Young Futures Hubs and NBER Chicago outreach. It
keeps trusted contact, referral, service receipt, outcome, and durable stability
as separate stages.

# Project: US public-safety measurement and institutional visibility

## Question

What changes when public safety is measured through victim interviews versus
police-recorded incidents?

## Decision rule

Keep lived victimization, reporting, police recording, survey definitions,
agency coverage, response, and remedy separate. Similar labels or trends do not
make the underlying measures interchangeable.

## Current bounded finding

The [NCVS/NIBRS comparison](findings/us-public-safety-measurement-001.md)
shows that the two federal crime measures can move in similar directions while
using different units, populations, definitions, and reporting paths.

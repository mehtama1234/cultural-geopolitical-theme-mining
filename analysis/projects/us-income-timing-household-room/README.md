# Project: Income timing, price pressure, and household room

## Question

When prices, pay timing, or fees squeeze a household, which adaptation protects
the immediate bill and which one transfers cost, debt, time, care, or risk into
the next month?

## Working chain

`income and bill timing -> price or fee pressure -> feasible payment route ->
cutback, substitution, borrowing, outside help, or extra work -> protected or
sacrificed essential -> repayment and later financial room, trust, or exit`

## Relationship to existing research

The household-financial-pressure project already measures price adaptation,
panel persistence, income paths, and payment terms. This companion lane focuses
on the missing event-level conversion: which specific bill was protected, what
cost was transferred, and whether later liquidity recovered.

## Next test

Follow one dated bill or income interruption through payment attempt, available
alternatives, adaptation, receipt or delinquency, repayment, next-month room,
and later trust or switching. Keep successful short bridges, delayed costs, and
failed payments in the same denominator.

# Project: US employer political influence

## Question

When one employer dominates a local job market, can its economic power also shape workers' political choices?

## Short first pass

Separate local employer size, worker dependence, employer messages, wage and job expectations, stated political influence, and actual voting.

## Possible connection

A job can shape political judgment through more than pay. If workers believe an election will change their job or wage, the employer's local power may reach the ballot even without a direct command.

## Decision rule

Move on after one local labor-market measure, one worker survey, and one observed election or policy result. Do not treat reported influence as a proven vote change.

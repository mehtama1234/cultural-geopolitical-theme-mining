# Project: small-business credit and consumer welfare

## Question

When small firms receive more credit, do customers benefit too—or does the
money only move sales from one firm to another?

## Decision rule

Keep firm profit, customer surplus, prices, product choice, business stealing,
debt, competition, and household welfare separate. Firm growth is not
automatically community prosperity.

## Current bounded finding

The [microenterprise-credit finding](findings/comparative-nber-microenterprise-credit-consumer-surplus-2026-001.md)
reports that large loans increased treated-firm profits and estimated customer
surplus in Chile, with little evidence of business stealing. It is comparative
evidence, not a U.S. estimate or a complete household-welfare result.

# Source search: US utility shutoff and reconnection rules

**Search date:** 2026-09-11  
**Geography:** United States  
**Status:** matched evidence pass complete; state-level rule and customer outcome comparison remains open

## Working question

After nonpayment, how do notice, payment arrangements, weather protections, medical rules, and reconnection costs change the household path?

## Sources

| ID | Source | What it tells us | Limit |

## First pattern to test

```text
missed payment
  -> notice, protection, or payment arrangement
  -> shutoff or continued service
  -> reconnection cost, new arrears, safe home, or unsafe home
```

The rule changes the time available to recover. The next test is whether more time becomes actual recovery rather than a larger balance.

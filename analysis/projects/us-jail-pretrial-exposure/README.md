# Project: US jail exposure and the pretrial administrative route

## Question

What does local-jail data show about the gap between being held, being
convicted, and returning to community life?

## Decision rule

Keep jail custody, admissions, conviction status, length of stay, release,
community supervision, conviction, reentry, and later safety separate. A
declining jail population is not the same as low exposure or improved justice
outcomes.

## Current bounded finding

The [BJS jail finding](findings/us-jail-pretrial-exposure-001.md) shows that
aggregate jail levels can fall while millions of admissions and large
unconvicted populations remain part of everyday state exposure.

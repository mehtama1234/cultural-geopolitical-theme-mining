# Project: administrative records and who official systems miss

## Question

Can administrative and third-party records improve estimates of Census coverage,
and what remains unresolved after a person becomes statistically visible?

## Decision rule

Keep record availability, linkage, coverage estimation, enumeration, privacy,
representation, service access, and public trust separate. Better measurement is
not automatically better treatment.

## Current bounded finding

The [Census CES finding](findings/us-census-capture-recapture-administrative-coverage-2026-001.md)
describes an experimental capture-recapture method using administrative and
third-party records. It improves the atlas's measurement layer but does not
provide a final national undercount or prove equal inclusion.

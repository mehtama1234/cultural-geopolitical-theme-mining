# Project: US remote work and residential sorting

## Question

When work can move away from the office, what does that freedom change about where households live, what they pay for, which public services they choose, and which places gain or lose people and tax capacity?

## Short end-to-end goal

Trace the route from a change in work location to the household's move, housing bill, school choice, local tax base, and community change. Keep separate what the household can choose from what the place can sustain.

```text
office lease or work-location change
  -> remote-work opportunity
  -> move, stay, housing choice, and tax/service choice
  -> school demand, neighborhood turnover, and local revenue
  -> [open] household wellbeing, displacement, community ties, and political response
```

The first bounded finding shows a causal migration and sorting channel but not a complete household outcome. The next test should join work arrangement, housing search and cost, school quality, travel and care time, neighborhood entry/exit, local revenue, and later security for the same household or a protected linked panel.

The [fertility and remote-work record](../../records/us-nber-fertility-mothers-remote-work-2003-2024.json)
adds a household-care boundary: fertility reduces mothers’ labor-force
participation; before 2020 an additional child increased remote-work likelihood,
but after 2020 the effect turned negative. The next test is to join work
arrangement to care time, schedule control, housing, earnings, employer practice,
and household wellbeing rather than treating remote work as automatic flexibility.

## Writing rule

Say what changed in the worker's location, home, bill, school, or neighborhood. Do not turn movement into improvement, or turnover into displacement, without evidence.

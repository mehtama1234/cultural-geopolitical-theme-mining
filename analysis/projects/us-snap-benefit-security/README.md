# Project: SNAP benefit security and household recovery

## Question

When SNAP benefits are issued but an EBT account is stolen, blocked, or hard to
use, does the household regain food access quickly enough to avoid a deeper
food, cash, time, or trust loss?

## Working chain

`benefit issuance -> account control or theft -> notice and claim effort ->
replacement or security response -> actual food access and adequacy -> next
month stability, trust, reporting, continued use, or route substitution`

## Relationship to existing safety-net research

The safety-net project already measures participation, office access,
recertification, work requirements, food hardship, and public-system meaning.
This is a separate depth lane for the payment-control middle: a benefit can be
officially issued without becoming usable food purchasing power.

## Next test

Obtain an authorized, privacy-preserving same-household route containing theft or
block date, balance, notice, claim, decision, replacement issuance and receipt,
interim food source, next benefit, food security, and later trust or continued
participation. Keep unfiled, denied, delayed, and successful claims in the same
denominator.

# Source search: US energy assistance and delayed household sacrifice

**Search date:** 2026-09-11  
**Geography:** United States  
**Status:** matched evidence pass complete; long-run same-household results remain open

## Working question

Does energy assistance prevent a household sacrifice, or does it only move the bill, shutoff, or unsafe-temperature problem into a later month?

## First pattern to test

```text
energy shock
  -> aid, payment plan, repair, or shutoff prevention
  -> service restored or bill reduced
  -> next bill, food or medicine choice, rent pressure, health, and work
```

The federal record shows program reach and some restored service. It does not yet show whether help ended the household problem or bought time before the next shock.

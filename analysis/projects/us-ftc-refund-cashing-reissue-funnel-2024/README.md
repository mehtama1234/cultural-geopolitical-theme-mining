# Project: FTC refund cashing and reissue funnel

## Question

When an enforcement remedy is formally issued, how far does it travel through
payment delivery, cashing, reissue, additional distribution, and usable
recovery?

## Bounded result

The [machine-readable record](../../records/us-ftc-refund-cashing-reissue-funnel-2024.json)
adds a cross-case receipt funnel. In the FTC's 2024 report, 64.2% of consumers
in the first-distribution table had cashed a payment to date, 65.7% in programs
closed that year had cashed at least one payment, and an Amazon Flex Zelle
reissue raised reported participation from 90.3% to 92.3%.

These are stronger than an order or fund reservation, but they do not show
adequate compensation, underlying-account correction, continued work, trust,
switching, or household recovery.

## Decisive next evidence

Match recipient-level eligibility, payment channel, cashing/reissue history,
harm amount, account or service correction, continued use or work, and later
financial condition without pooling unlike FTC cases.

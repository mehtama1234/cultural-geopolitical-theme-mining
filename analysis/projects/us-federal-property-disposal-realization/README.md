# Project: federal property disposal realization

This project follows the Federal Assets Sale and Transfer Act temporary
disposal process from approved property to completed sale, while keeping sale
proceeds, funding availability, maintenance burden, local effects, and public
value separate.

The first bounded finding uses GAO-26-108451. It records 14 completed disposals
and about $576 million in sale proceeds as of August 2026, alongside unclear
timelines for many 2025-round properties and inconsistent congressional
availability of the disposal fund.

The next decisive artifact is a property-level ledger joining approval,
condition, maintenance cost, funding release, marketing, sale, proceeds,
disposal cost, reuse or vacancy, and local/public-service effects.

See the [initial finding](findings/us-gao-federal-property-disposal-realization-2026-001.md)
and the [machine-readable record](../../records/us-gao-federal-property-disposal-realization-2026.json).

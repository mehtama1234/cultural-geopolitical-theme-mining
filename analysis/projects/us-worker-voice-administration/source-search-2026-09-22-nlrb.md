# Source search: worker voice and labor-administration capacity

**Search date:** 2026-09-22  
**Geography:** United States private-sector labor-relations system  
**Status:** bounded pass complete; invoking a labor process is not the same as durable worker power

## Working question

How much labor-relations demand reached the NLRB in FY 2025, and what did the
agency's process actually establish?

| ID | Source | What it tells us | Status | Limit |

| US-NLRB-FY2025-PAR | [NLRB FY 2025 Performance and Accountability Report](https://www.nlrb.gov/sites/default/files/attachments/pages/node-130/nlrb-fy2025-par.pdf) | Reports unfair-labor-practice charges, representation petitions, merits findings, settlements, and Board decisions. | Official NLRB report | It does not follow a worker through workplace conditions, payment, bargaining, remedy receipt, or later job security. |

## First pattern

```text
workplace conflict or desire for representation
  -> charge or petition
  -> investigation, election, settlement, or decision
  -> remedy, bargaining, workplace change, trust, and durable power [open]
```

In FY 2025, the public filed 19,754 unfair-labor-practice charges; 32.8% were
found to have merit, and 96.8% of meritorious cases were settled. The NLRB also
received 2,743 representation petitions, including 2,633 petitions for
secret-ballot elections. The Board issued 86 contested-case decisions during
the periods when it had a quorum, after lacking a quorum for nearly
three-quarters of the year.

## Counterpoint to keep visible

Charges and petitions are not a prevalence rate for workplace violations or a
count of all workers seeking representation. A settlement is not necessarily
the same as payment, reinstatement, behavior change, or worker control. A low
number of Board decisions can reflect settlement, delegation, or lack of
quorum rather than low conflict.

## Decision rule

Use NLRB data for demand on the labor-administration route and agency output.
Do not convert filings, merits, settlements, elections, or decisions into
union coverage, workplace improvement, or durable bargaining power without a
linked worker or workplace outcome.

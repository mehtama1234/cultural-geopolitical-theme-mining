# Project: US worker voice and labor-administration capacity

## Question

What happens when workers or employers invoke the federal labor-relations
system, and how does agency capacity shape the route?

## Decision rule

Keep charges, petitions, elections, settlements, Board decisions, bargaining,
payment, workplace conditions, and durable worker power separate. Filing a case
or reaching a settlement is not the same as receiving protection or changing
the workplace.

## Current bounded finding

The [NLRB finding](findings/us-worker-voice-administration-001.md) shows a
large administrative route for labor conflict and representation, while
merits, remedy, bargaining results, and durable worker control remain separate
outcomes.

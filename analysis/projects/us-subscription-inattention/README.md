# Project: US subscriptions and the cost of not noticing

## Question

When a subscription is no longer worth its price, how long can automatic payment keep it alive—and who has the time and attention to stop it?

## Short first pass

Start with the NBER payment-card study and the HBS discussion of subscription fatigue. Keep three things apart: the firm's recurring revenue, the customer's continued use, and the customer's failure to cancel.

## Possible connection

A monthly charge can become a quiet household cost when stopping it requires an active decision. The burden may be larger for people with less financial room or less time to watch many accounts.

## Decision rule

Look for current US evidence on cancellation, renewal notices, overdrafts, income and total subscription load. Move on unless the sources show how much money is lost, who loses it, and whether a simpler cancellation rule changes the result.

# Project: US inflation cause beliefs

## Question

How does a household price become a story about responsibility and action?

## Short first pass

Separate the price people face, the cause they name, the information source, the policy they support and later political or household action.

## Possible connection

Prices do not enter politics without interpretation. Party, news source and personal experience can change whether the answer points to government, firms, supply, wages or the Federal Reserve.

## Decision rule

Move on after one survey measures perceived causes, one source measures local prices or real wages, and one next dataset can follow information and belief into political action. Do not turn a stated cause into a proven cause.

# Project: status mismatch and residential mobility

## Question

Does a household’s relative standing within a neighborhood relate to whether it
moves nearby or across county lines?

## Decision rule

Keep household income rank, neighborhood status, comparison, mobility distance,
housing cost, quality, discrimination, belonging, safety, displacement, and
later wellbeing separate. A move is not automatically opportunity or exit.

## Current bounded finding

The [Census CES finding](findings/us-census-status-inconsistency-neighborhood-mobility-acs-2026-001.md)
reports that high individual status in disadvantaged neighborhoods and low
individual status in high-status neighborhoods are associated with different
mobility patterns. The models do not establish motives, causal status effects,
or whether moves improved life.

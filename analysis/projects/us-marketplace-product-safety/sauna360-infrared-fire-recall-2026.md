# Sauna360 infrared recall and repair-route record

**Checked:** 2026-10-20  
**Status:** large recall and professional repair route reported; owner-level completion and safe restoration remain open

## Bounded episode

The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission reports a September 17, 2026
recall of about 41,580 Sauna360 infrared saunas, hybrid infrared saunas, and
infrared kits. The notice says infrared panels could short circuit and
overheat, creating fire and burn hazards, and reports 67 overheating,
melting, charring, or burning reports with no injuries reported in that
notice. Consumers were told to stop using the products immediately and
schedule a free in-home repair by a professional installer.

```text
product purchase and use
  -> overheating / fire and burn exposure
  -> stop-use instruction and recall contact route
  -> [open] appointment and professional repair completion
  -> [open] safe restoration, downtime, residual cost, and substitute access
  -> [open] trust, continued use, non-use, and exit
```

The route adds another firm-level product-safety episode to the atlas. It is
kept separate from Sauna360’s earlier Tylö bench-failure recall because the
hazard, product set, incident signal, and remedy mechanism differ.

## Next decisive evidence

Seek a lawful repair-registration or completion aggregate, service-time
measure, repeat-incident update, or owner-facing follow-up. Do not infer safe
restoration from the recall notice or the availability of a free repair.

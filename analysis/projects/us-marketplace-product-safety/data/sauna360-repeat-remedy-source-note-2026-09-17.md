# Sauna360 repeat-remedy source note

**Checked:** 2026-09-18  
**Evidence class:** official recall expansion; repeat remedy after an ineffective repair  
**Status:** bounded product-level implementation evidence; no purchaser-level recovery estimate

## What happened

The CPSC posted a September 17, 2026 recall expansion for Sauna360 Tylö
Halmstad and Kiruna Hybrid sauna benches. The October 23, 2025 recall had
offered a free repair with additional hardware and a revised manual. The 2026
expansion says the previous repair was ineffective, instructs consumers to
stop using the benches even if they received the 2025 remedy, and offers
another free repair with a professional installer and additional hardware.

The new notice covers about 6,000 units and reports 77 additional bench-break
reports, including six injuries. The 2025 notice covered about 1,000 units and
reported seven breakage reports, including one head-and-neck injury. The unit
counts and incident reports are notice-level figures; they do not identify
unique owners, repair recipients, or a failure rate.

## Evidence chain

```text
hazard and first repair
  -> reported repair implementation
  -> later evidence that the repair was ineffective
  -> expanded hazard notice and second repair
  -> [open] owner notice, appointment, completed second repair, safe use,
            downtime, residual cost, trust, switching, or exit
```

## What this adds

This is stronger than a single offered-remedy record because it observes a
later institutional correction to the remedy itself. It establishes that
“repair offered” and even “repair received” would not necessarily establish
durable safety. It does not establish that the first repair failed on every
unit, that every listed owner received either notice, or that the second repair
was completed.

## Exact boundary

The public record identifies the product family, recall dates, serial-number
ranges, remedy instructions, reported incidents, and a repeat-remedy stage.
It does not identify a purchaser, possession, notice receipt, appointment,
repair completion, downtime, substitute access, remaining loss, or later trust
or behavior. The record therefore advances the **remedy durability** arrow but
does not close consumer recovery.

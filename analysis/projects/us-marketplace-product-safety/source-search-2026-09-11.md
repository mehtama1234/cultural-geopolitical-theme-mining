# Source search: US marketplace product safety and counterfeit risk

**Search date:** 2026-09-11  
**Geography:** United States  
**Status:** matched evidence pass complete; product-level exposure and remedy records remain open

## Working question

When unsafe or counterfeit goods enter an online marketplace, can the customer see the risk and get a remedy?

## Sources

| ID | Source | What it tells us | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| CPSC-FAKE-LABELS-2026 | [CPSC: crackdown on fake safety labels](https://www.cpsc.gov/Newsroom/News-Releases/2026/US-Consumer-Product-Safety-Commission-Launches-Crackdown-on-Fake-Safety-Labels-Used-to-Push-Dangerous-Foreign-Products-into-US-Homes) | CPSC says counterfeit safety labels can mislead buyers, undercut compliant US businesses, and move hazardous goods through online and retail channels. | A national enforcement announcement does not measure exposure or injury by platform. |

## First pattern to test

```text
seller and safety signal shown
  -> customer belief and purchase
  -> counterfeit, unsafe, or non-conforming product arrives
  -> report, recall, return, refund, or injury
  -> household money, health, and trust change
```

## Decision rule

Keep safety signal, actual product condition, exposure, harm, removal, refund, and injury separate until product-level records join them.

# LIHEAP performance access recheck v1

**Checked:** 2026-09-18

## Result

The official LIHEAP Performance Management site is currently accessible and
states that preliminary FY2025 state data, grant-recipient profiles, and
performance-measure summaries were uploaded in July 2026. The live performance
page presents FY2023–FY2025 data as preliminary pending final validation and
includes a service-loss restoration/prevention category.

The accessible reporting surface includes grant-recipient one-page profiles,
single-state reports, cross-state standard reports, custom reports, and state
performance-measure executive summaries. This makes a bounded state-level
comparison executable in principle, but the current recheck does not extract a
state value or combine preliminary rows into a national estimate.

HHS also defines restoration and prevention as occurrences among LIHEAP-
assisted households, including bill-payment and equipment-repair/replacement
routes. That definition is valuable because it separates restored service from
prevented loss and excludes routine maintenance without imminent service-loss
risk.

## Boundary

This recheck advances the public-system acquisition route from a failed or
unavailable warehouse request to an accessible preliminary aggregate/state
reporting surface. It does **not** establish a same-household panel or any of
the following: application date, award or denial, payment receipt,
reconnection date, next-bill affordability, duration of restored service,
repeat crisis, food/medicine/health protection, work continuity, trust, or
political action.

```text
program measure -> state/grantee performance -> household episode -> durable recovery
    observed              accessible                 open                open
```

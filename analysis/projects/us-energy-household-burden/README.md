# Project: US household energy burden

## Question

When energy costs rise, which households lose room to pay for housing, food, care or debt first?

## Short discovery goal

Track energy bills from the home and local utility to household choices, public assistance and political pressure. Keep energy use, energy cost and energy insecurity as separate measures.

## First working idea

Energy burden is partly a housing problem: the building, fuel and location shape the bill before a household decides what to cut. This is a working idea, not a conclusion.

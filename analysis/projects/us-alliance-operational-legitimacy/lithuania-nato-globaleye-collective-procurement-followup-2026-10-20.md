# Lithuania–NATO GlobalEye collective-procurement follow-up

**Checked:** 2026-10-20  
**Status:** multilateral procurement participation and planned capability are public; delivery, national contribution, financing execution, and operational effect remain open

## Observed route

The Lithuanian Ministry of National Defence reports that Lithuania joined ten
other Allies in the planned collective procurement of up to ten Saab GlobalEye
aircraft equipped with the Initial Alliance Future Surveillance and Control
capability. The ministry describes the aircraft as a future replacement for
NATO's E-3A AWACS fleet, with functions including surveillance, threat
detection, communications relay, and airspace command and control.

The ministry reports an approximate EUR 5 billion overall project value and
first equipment expected in 2030–2031. Lithuania frames the route as improved
early warning and regional situational awareness, but the announcement does
not identify Lithuania's national quantity, financial contribution, industrial
share, contract instrument, delivery to Lithuanian units, or realized
operational effect.

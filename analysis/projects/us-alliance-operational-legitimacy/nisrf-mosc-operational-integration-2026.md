# NISRF Mission Operations Support Center operational integration

**Checked:** 2026-10-20  
**Status:** named support facility entered active mission use; performance, cost, and strategic effect remain open

## Observed route

NISRF reports that its new Mission Operations Support Center at Sigonella was
contributing to ongoing mission operations while NISRF conducted Agile Combat
Employment from Finland in support of NATO activities and Ramstein Flag 2026.
The center functioned as a central mission-operations hub while aircraft and
deployed personnel operated across locations.

This is a distinct infrastructure-to-operation stage. It shows a facility
being used in live mission work, not merely built or security-accredited. It
does not show system uptime, analyst workload, product accuracy, decision
change, mission outcome, lifecycle cost, host burden, or strategic effect.

## Stage boundary

| Stage | What is public | Still open |
|---|---|---|
| Facility development | MOSC was built, equipped, integrated, and tested | Full utilization, reliability, lifecycle cost, and staffing |
| Operational use | MOSC contributed to active ACE/Ramstein Flag mission operations | Workload, data latency, system failures, and product quality |
| Distributed capability | Sigonella hub supported aircraft operating from Finland | Comparative performance versus prior arrangements and host burden |
| Strategic meaning | NISRF links the facility to progress toward FOC | Decision effect, adversary response, deterrence, public consent, and durable leverage |

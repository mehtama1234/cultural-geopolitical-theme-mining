# NISRF Northern Viking maritime ISR follow-up

**Checked:** 2026-10-20  
**Status:** named exercise mission and information-support stage observed; targeting effect, deterrence, cost, and legitimacy remain open

## Observed route

NATO's Intelligence, Surveillance and Reconnaissance Force reports that a
NATO-owned RQ-4D Phoenix mission supported Exercise Northern Viking 2026 in a
multinational maritime scenario around Iceland. The account says the mission
contributed to a surface picture supporting over-the-horizon targeting for naval
surface combatants in the Greenland-Iceland-United Kingdom Gap.

This advances the route from force organization and aircraft output into a
named operational information-support function. It still does not identify a
targeting decision, engagement, mission result, adversary response, deterrence,
host burden, or public acceptance.

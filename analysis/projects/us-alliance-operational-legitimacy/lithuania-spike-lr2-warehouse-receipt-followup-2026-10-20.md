# Lithuania Spike LR2 warehouse-receipt follow-up

**Checked:** 2026-10-20  
**Status:** official ammunition-receipt milestone; quantity, integration, readiness, use, and strategic effects remain open

## Observed route

The Lithuanian Ministry of National Defence reports that Spike LR2 missiles
reached Lithuanian Armed Forces warehouses on July 7, 2026. The ministry says
the implemented contract with Germany's Euro Spike GmbH was worth more than
€3 million.

The ministry describes Spike LR2 as a rapid-response anti-armour missile for
tanks, armoured equipment, buildings, and other high-value targets, and says
Lithuanian Armed Forces use the missiles with Vilkas Infantry Fighting Vehicles.
The minister frames the stockpile addition as strengthening deterrence and
readiness for further replenishment.

## Stage boundary

| Stage | What is public | Still open |
|---|---|---|
| Receipt | Spike LR2 missiles reported in Armed Forces warehouses | Quantity, delivery lot, acceptance, inventory allocation, and unit cost detail |
| Contract | Implemented Euro Spike GmbH contract valued above €3M | Payment, contract line, delivery schedule, and supplier performance |
| Platform integration | Ministry links missiles to Vilkas Infantry Fighting Vehicles | Unit assignment, training, maintenance, firing readiness, and operational use |
| Strategic meaning | Official claims of stronger deterrence and security | Demonstrated readiness, incident response, adversary adaptation, civilian protection, and public judgment |

This advances a distinct anti-armour ammunition route from contract to reported
warehouse receipt. It does not establish a usable stockpile, trained crews,
field deployment, or deterrence.

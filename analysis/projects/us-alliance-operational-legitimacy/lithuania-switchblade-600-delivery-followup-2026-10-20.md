# Lithuania Switchblade 600 delivery follow-up

**Checked:** 2026-10-20  
**Status:** official delivery milestone; quantity, training, operational use, deterrence, and civilian effects remain open

## Observed route

The Lithuanian Ministry of National Defence reports that the first shipment of
U.S.-made Switchblade 600 loitering munitions supplied by AeroVironment reached
Lithuanian Armed Forces warehouses on October 7, 2026. The same announcement
reports associated launch and control equipment, a personnel-training
simulator, and a maintenance package.

The ministry describes the receipt as adding a long-range strike capability and
strengthening deterrence and Lithuania–United States defense partnership. Those
are official purposes and interpretations, not observations of field use or
effect.

## Stage boundary

| Stage | What is public | Still open |
|---|---|---|
| Procurement receipt | First shipment reached Armed Forces warehouses | Quantity, unit cost, acceptance documentation, and inventory allocation |
| Support package | Launch/control equipment, training simulator, and maintenance package reported | Training completion, maintenance readiness, and unit assignment |
| Capability | Ministry describes long-range precision and anti-armour capability | Field deployment, operational use, readiness, incident response, deterrence, and adversary adaptation |
| Public/civilian effect | Official security and partnership claims | Civilian protection, local burden, public judgment, and later behavior |

This advances the route from procurement intention to reported physical receipt.
It does not establish usable or fielded capability, deterrence, or a civilian
outcome.

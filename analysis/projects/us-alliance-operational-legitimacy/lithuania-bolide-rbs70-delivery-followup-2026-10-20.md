# Lithuania Bolide/RBS-70 delivery follow-up

**Checked:** 2026-10-20  
**Status:** official short-range air-defence delivery milestone; quantity, crew readiness, firing use, and protection effects remain open

## Observed route

The Lithuanian Ministry of National Defence reports that Lithuania received a
delivery of Bolide missiles in early June 2026 for the RBS-70 man-portable
short-range anti-aircraft missile system. The equipment was manufactured by
Sweden's Saab Dynamics AB and the reported delivery was worth €7 million.

The ministry says the RBS-70 system is used by Lithuania's Air Defence
Battalion and describes the Bolide missiles as resistant to electronic jamming
and effective against modern air threats, with a stated range of up to 9 km.
It also reports a separate 2025 contract worth over €320 million intended to
ensure continued supply during 2026–2032.

## Stage boundary

| Stage | What is public | Still open |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery | Bolide missiles received; €7M delivery reported | Quantity, lot, acceptance, allocation, and payment |
| System link | Missiles assigned in the source to the RBS-70 system and Air Defence Battalion | Crew assignment, training, maintenance, and firing readiness |
| Supply continuity | Larger Saab contract reported for 2026–2032 supply | Actual future deliveries, replenishment, stockpile levels, and affordability |
| Security effect | Official claims of stronger air defence and jamming resistance | Detection-to-engagement use, interception, civilian protection, deterrence, and public judgment |

This advances a short-range air-defence route from procurement into reported
delivery. It does not establish operational readiness, use against an air
threat, or realized protection.

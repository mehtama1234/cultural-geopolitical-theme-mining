# Lithuania–Ukraine DEMZ ammunition-factory investment follow-up

**Checked:** 2026-10-20  
**Status:** official investment-contract milestone; construction, output, jobs, and local effects remain open

## Observed route

The Lithuanian Ministry of National Defence reports that Lithuania and
Ukraine's DEMZ defence-industry company signed an investment contract on
August 14, 2026 to build a new ammunition factory in Lithuania. The ministry
states that the investment could create up to 450 new job opportunities in
Alytus and Prienai district.

The announcement frames the project as strengthening Lithuania–Ukraine
security partnership, defence-industrial competitiveness, supply resilience,
and the Lithuanian economy. These are stated purposes and projected effects;
the source does not report construction, financing disbursement, output, or
actual hiring.

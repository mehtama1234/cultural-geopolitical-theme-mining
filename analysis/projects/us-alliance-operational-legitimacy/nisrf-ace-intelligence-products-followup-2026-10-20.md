# NISRF ACE: operational output still stops before decision effect

**Checked:** 2026-10-20  
**Record:** [NISRF ACE intelligence-products record](../../records/us-nato-nisrf-ace-intelligence-products-2026.json)

## What the official account shows

NATO's Intelligence, Surveillance and Reconnaissance Force reports that its
2026 Adaptable Capability Exercise accumulated approximately 180 flight hours
with NATO-owned RQ-4D Phoenix aircraft and produced more than 500 intelligence
products supporting NATO activities, exercise objectives, and Alliance
decision-makers.

This advances the route beyond infrastructure or authorization into reported
operational production. It does not show which products were used, whether they
changed a decision, improved mission performance, altered adversary behavior, or
were worth the host and alliance costs.

## End-to-end boundary

```text
strategic surveillance requirement
  -> NATO force adaptation and alternate-location operations
  -> flight hours and intelligence-product production
  -> [open] product use, decision change, mission effect, adversary response, deterrence, legitimacy
```

The public account is an institutional output report. Product content,
recipient-level use, quality, workload, cost, civilian burden, and public
acceptance remain unobserved. The record is kept separate from the Belgian
MQ-9B deployment because the platforms and operational episodes differ.

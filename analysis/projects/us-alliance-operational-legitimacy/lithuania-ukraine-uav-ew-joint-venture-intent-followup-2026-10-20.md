# Lithuania–Ukraine UAV and electronic-warfare joint-venture intent follow-up

**Checked:** 2026-10-20  
**Status:** official Letter of Intent and planned-production milestone; joint-venture formation, production, and capability effects remain open

## Observed route

The Lithuanian Ministry of National Defence reports that Lithuania's Pekkla UAB
and Ukraine's December One RND Ltd signed a Letter of Intent on October 15,
2026 covering cooperation in production and supply of electronic-warfare
systems and UAV components.

The ministry says the parties aim to establish a joint venture in Lithuania and
start production in 2027. Pekkla is described as providing Lithuanian
infrastructure and commercial contacts, while December One RND contributes R&D
expertise, technical knowledge, production capabilities, and product concepts
to be tested on the Lithuanian market.

## Stage boundary

| Stage | What is public | Still open |
|---|---|---|
| Cooperation | Named Lithuanian and Ukrainian firms signed a Letter of Intent | Binding terms, ownership, financing, governance, and joint-venture formation |
| Technology transfer | R&D, technical expertise, production capabilities, UAV components, and EW systems described | Technology transfer completion, testing, certification, and supply-chain inputs |
| Production | Joint venture and 2027 production stated as an aim | Facility, equipment, output, customers, delivery, quality, and readiness |
| Strategic meaning | Official claims of shorter logistics, resilience, independence, and faster support to Ukraine/Lithuania | Realized substitution, operational use, deterrence, jobs, local effects, and public response |

This is a named cross-border cooperation and production-intent stage. It does
not establish a formed joint venture, production, tested technology, or
operational capability.

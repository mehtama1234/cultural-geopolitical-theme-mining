# Source search: US online reviews and customer trust

**Search date:** 2026-09-11  
**Geography:** US customer and marketplace question  
**Status:** matched evidence pass complete; current platform-level quality and customer records remain open

## Working question

Do online reviews help customers compare quality, or can the review system itself become another paid gate?

## Sources

| ID | Source | What it tells us | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| NBER-REVIEWS-22584 | [NBER: Buying Reputation as a Signal of Quality](https://www.nber.org/papers/w22584) | On a large online marketplace, items with rewards for feedback had nearly 30% higher sales and were sold by higher-quality sellers in the studied setting. | Taobao setting; reward design and quality measure may not carry to US platforms. |
| NBER-EBAY-CERT-29674 | [NBER: Designing Quality Certificates](https://www.nber.org/papers/w29674) | An eBay redesign moved a quality certificate away from most customer reports and toward direct seller inputs; the study examines how the certificate changed seller selection and category patterns. | A certificate is not the same as a full review system or a US household price result. |
| FTC-REVIEWS-RULE-2024 | [FTC: Final rule banning fake reviews and testimonials](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials) | The rule prohibits buying or selling fake reviews, sentiment-conditioned incentives, undisclosed insider reviews, deceptive review sites, and certain review suppression. | A rule states prohibited conduct; it does not show how often it occurs or how much customer choice changes. |
| FTC-REVIEWS-CONSUMER | [FTC: How to evaluate online reviews](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-evaluate-online-reviews) | FTC warns that fake or manipulated reviews can hurt customers and honest firms, and that customers should check source, timing, incentives, and more than one site. | Consumer advice does not estimate the size of the effect or identify the most effective platform design. |

## First pattern to test

```text
review rule or incentive
  -> who writes and which reviews remain visible
  -> customer's belief about quality
  -> seller effort, price, and sales
  -> customer choice and trust
  -> platform enforcement and public rule
```

## Decision rule

Keep review truth, review visibility, seller quality, customer belief, customer outcome, and enforcement separate until one market record joins them.

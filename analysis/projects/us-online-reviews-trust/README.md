# Project: US online reviews and customer trust

## Question

Do online reviews help customers compare quality, or can the review system itself become another paid gate?

## Short first pass

Separate real customer experience, incentive, review visibility, seller quality, customer choice, and platform enforcement.

## Matched evidence pass

- [Reviews can reveal quality and distort choice at the same time](../../findings/us-online-reviews-trust-matched-evidence-001.md)

## Decision rule

Move on after one review-system change, one seller-quality result, and one customer outcome. Do not treat a high rating as proof of quality or a suspicious review as proof of widespread fraud.

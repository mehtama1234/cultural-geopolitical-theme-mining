# Project: Tax rulemaking, public participation, and usable voice

## Question

When tax rules are being written, can people see the choices, understand the
trade-offs, and participate in a way that agencies can actually use?

## Decision rule

Keep a legal opportunity to comment, actual participation, agency analysis,
comment processing, and influence on the final rule separate. A large number
of comments is not automatically broad representation, and a hearing or
comment period is not proof that public input changed the rule.

## Current bounded finding

The [GAO finding](findings/us-tax-rulemaking-public-engagement-001.md) shows
that Treasury and IRS generally opened a comment route, but economic analysis,
hearing documentation, virtual access, and preparation for AI-generated mass
comments remained incomplete.

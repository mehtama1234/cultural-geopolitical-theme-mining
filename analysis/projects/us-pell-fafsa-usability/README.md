# Project: Pell eligibility and usable college access

## Question

Did simplifying the FAFSA make college aid more usable, or did it mainly
change who qualified on paper?

## Decision rule

Keep FAFSA completion, Pell eligibility, maximum-award eligibility, aid
receipt, enrollment, and later education outcomes separate. A broader rule can
expand formal access without proving that students received aid or attended
college.

## Current bounded finding

The [GAO finding](findings/us-gao-pell-fafsa-eligibility-2026-001.md) shows that
eligibility increased after FAFSA simplification, even as fewer students
completed the application, and that the gains were uneven across household
income and family circumstances.

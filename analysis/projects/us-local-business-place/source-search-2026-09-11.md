# Source search: US local business and the changing hometown

**Search date:** 2026-09-11  
**Geography:** United States  
**Status:** short discovery pass; no settled finding

## Working question

Does the movement of small-business owners and firms change local opportunity and belonging, or does a new business simply replace the old one?

## First pattern to test

```text
local cost, credit, labor, or customer change
  -> business opens, closes, or moves
  -> jobs, services, prices, and social contact change
  -> household and local political response
```

The first two links have business data. The effects on services, identity, and politics need local records or surveys.

## Main gaps

- lasting firms versus applications and temporary closures;
- who owns the businesses and where the owners live;
- local credit, rent, labor, and customer conditions;
- changes in essential services and prices;
- worker and customer experience after closure or relocation;
- local tax base, public spending, and political participation;
- evidence that business turnover does not change local trust or belonging.

## Decision rule

Choose one county pair, combine BFS with BLS establishment data and local records, and look for a change in service or work. If the records show only business counts, record the trend and move on.

# Small-business capacity: profitability is not the same as local security

**Checked:** 2026-09-13  
**Status:** official EHI/SBCS extraction · descriptive firm-level evidence

## Bounded finding

The April 2026 New York Fed EHI release shows why firm health needs more than
one outcome. In the Small Business Credit Survey, reported profitability
improved relative to the pandemic period, but revenue expectations weakened,
the smallest firms had lower pricing power, and employment paths differed by
firm size. A firm can therefore report profit while facing weaker demand or
less ability to raise prices, and small-firm employment growth can coexist
with weaker revenue.

## Direct measurements

- In the 2024 survey, the profit-minus-loss diffusion index was 13 percentage
  points for year-end 2023, below the 46-point December 2019 comparator. The
  2024 survey had 6,280 employer-firm respondents; 3,935 had 1–9 employees.
- In the 2025 survey, more firms reported lower than higher revenue over the
  prior 12 months, with the smallest firms driving the lower-revenue result.
  The share expecting higher revenue in 2026 fell 6 percentage points year over
  year. The 2025 next-revenue expectation universe had 6,389 respondents,
  including 4,036 firms with 1–9 employees.
- Employment growth increased for small firms in 2025 and decreased for larger
  firms while remaining positive. The past-employment-change universe had 6,361
  respondents, including 4,004 small firms.
- Debt per employee was approximately $70,000 in 2025. This is a reported firm
  average, not a measure of affordability or worker debt.

## End-to-end route

```text
cost, demand, credit, and labor conditions
  -> firm revenue, pricing, profit, employment, and debt
  -> jobs, local services, prices, and owner/worker household exposure
  -> local attachment, trust, and political response
```

The SBCS directly measures the firm stage. It does not measure whether a firm
stayed open, whether workers gained income or control, whether customers lost
a service, or whether local political meaning changed. The arrows into local
life and politics remain open.

## Method and limits

The SBCS is administered annually by the 12 Federal Reserve Banks to firms with
fewer than 500 employees. It is not a random sample: firms are also contacted
through community organizations and results are weighted by industry, location,
and demographics. The EHI analysis focuses on employer firms, and questions
have different reference periods and valid universes. Profitability at the end
of a year is reported in the following year's survey, while most other
questions refer to the preceding 12 months.

Profitability is a diffusion index, not a margin. Revenue direction is not
cash flow. Employment growth is not job quality. Lower credit constraint can
mean firms did not need funds rather than that affordable credit was abundant.

## Next test

Match firm-level or establishment-level outcomes to local service availability,
worker earnings and schedules, customer prices, ownership, and business exit.
Compare firms that report profit but weak revenue with firms that report both
profit and revenue growth, and retain the counterexample where firm employment
grows while household security does not.

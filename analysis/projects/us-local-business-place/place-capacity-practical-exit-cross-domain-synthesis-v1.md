# Local capacity is not practical exit

**Status:** broad-program rotation 8 · cross-source synthesis  
**Checked:** 2026-09-16  
**Scope:** US places, households, consumers, public systems, firms, and local political meaning

## The question

When a place has businesses, providers, infrastructure, public programs, or
nearby alternatives, can a resident actually leave a failing or unaffordable
option?

The broad program treats exit as a practical outcome, not a theoretical choice.
A person may be able to switch only if another option is reachable, open,
affordable, eligible, staffed, trusted, and usable within the person's time and
care constraints. The place evidence shows why counts of establishments,
applications, jobs, shortage designations, or public routes cannot answer that
question by themselves.

```text
population, money, demand, and policy change
  -> firms, providers, public routes, and infrastructure appear or contract
  -> local and neighboring option stack changes
  -> price, travel, hours, eligibility, waiting, quality, and trust shape use
  -> resident delays, substitutes, borrows, complains, stays, moves, or goes without
  -> household security, firm dependence, local legitimacy, and political action change
```

This is a layered bridge across different units. It does not claim that the
same person moved through every source in the chain.

## What the existing evidence establishes

| Layer | Evidence already in the atlas | What it supports | What remains open |
|---|---|---|---|
| Firm entry and stock | Census BFS/BDS and county CBP layers | Applications, employer openings, establishments, jobs, and sector capacity are distinct stages | Whether a particular entry survived, served local residents, or created a replaceable option |
| Health-sector capacity | CBP, HRSA HPSA components, and CDC PLACES | Visible health capacity and modeled social need can coexist; shortage labels have different geographic and population objects | Appointment availability, accepting providers, travel time, continuity, and resident-level access |

## The central result: capacity has an option-stack threshold

The evidence supports a more precise interpretation of local capacity:

> A place becomes practically protective only when nominal supply is joined to
> usable alternatives. Until then, capacity can coexist with delay,
> substitution, dependence, or abandonment.

The threshold has at least six parts:

1. **Presence:** a firm, provider, benefit route, insurer, or infrastructure
   asset exists.
2. **Reachability:** the person can travel, connect, qualify, or communicate
   with it within available time and money.
3. **Usability:** it is open, staffed, accepting, affordable, and capable of
   serving the need.
4. **Replaceability:** another route exists if the first fails, including a
   neighboring place or public backstop.
5. **Recourse:** the user can contest, correct, appeal, or obtain help when the
   route fails.
6. **Exit:** the person can switch, stop using, move, organize, or go without
   without an unacceptable protected/sacrificed outcome.

The first four are often partially visible in place data. Recourse and exit
are much less visible. This explains why a county can look capable in an
administrative inventory while residents still experience waiting, travel,
unmet need, or dependence.

## Counterexamples that prevent a simple story

- More establishments do not automatically produce more access. The matched
  county health screen found different modeled social-need surfaces across
  visible-capacity groups while routine-checkup estimates were nearly alike;
  neither “capacity solves access” nor “shortage means no care” is supported.
- Growth and foreign-born composition do not determine one local trust or
  action pattern. The joint CES place screen is non-monotonic across the two
  axes, while civic action is comparatively stable.
- A public route can be present while household security remains unresolved.
  SNAP receipt, processing, food security, charitable access, and following
  hardship are distinct endpoints.
- A consumer can reach a complaint or intervention route without receiving a
  verified remedy or possessing a practical substitute. Visibility is not
  restoration.
- Housing and insurance can provide nominal coverage while premium,
  nonrenewal, hazard, repair, and backstop conditions redistribute the ability
  to remain in place.

These counterexamples matter because local decline, institutional failure,
and demographic change can be interpreted as market churn, public neglect,
corporate extraction, demographic threat, or an opportunity to rebuild. The
available place measures do not tell us which interpretation a resident made.

## What this adds to the broad goal

This rotation connects the broad program's practical-exit question to the
place scale. It changes the working object from **capacity** to **capacity
that creates a usable alternative**:

```text
nominal capacity
  -> reachable and usable option
  -> fallback or recourse
  -> successful substitution, restored access, or exit
  -> local trust, attachment, political action, and firm/state leverage
```

The synthesis does not create a local exit rate. It identifies the minimum
fields needed before that claim is defensible: dated exposure, exact place or
provider, destination and travel time, hours and eligibility, price or
payment, attempted route, failure or success, substitute, remedy, protected
outcome, sacrificed outcome, and later stay/switch/move/action.

## Next decisive test

The next place pass should use one bounded geography and one service or risk
episode, not another national capacity inventory. The preferred design is a
place-time panel or linked administrative route with:

- a dated capacity or policy change;
- resident or household exposure and a defined denominator;
- local and neighboring alternatives, including travel and hours;
- price, eligibility, staffing, or acceptance conditions;
- attempted use, delay, substitution, complaint, or non-use;
- a protected and sacrificed outcome; and
- later repair, trust, political action, switching, exit, or staying.

If no same-unit join is available, the result should remain a layered place
comparison and the missing join should be published as the finding. Large
archives are not required for this rotation; existing local files and small,
targeted extracts are preferred under the repository resource policy.

## Evidence boundary

This synthesis is an interpretive bridge across source families with different
respondents, units, periods, weights, and geographies. It does not estimate a
causal effect of business formation, health capacity, immigration, insurance,
public assistance, or complaint access on exit or political behavior. Its
contribution is to specify the option-stack mechanism and prevent nominal
capacity from being reported as lived access, remedy, or replaceability.

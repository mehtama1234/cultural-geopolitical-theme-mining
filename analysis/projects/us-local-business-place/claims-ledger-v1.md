# Claims ledger: US local business and the changing hometown

**Check date:** 2026-09-11  
**Scope:** United States; business ownership, place, applications, employer formation, jobs, services, and local belonging

| ID | Claim | Evidence type | What supports it | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C-001 | Entrepreneurs are less likely than before to stay in their birthplace state. | Census-based research | HBS reports the local advantage for US-born White male entrepreneurs fell from 8 percentage points in 1970 to 2 points in 2018 and fell below zero in the latest full sample. | Staying in a state is not the same as owning a local store or serving local customers. |
| C-002 | The change is tied partly to a shift in the kinds of self-employment people do. | Census-based research | HBS reports a fall in capital-heavy local sectors and a rise in lower-capital sectors such as personal services, construction, and real estate. | This is a possible explanation, not a complete cause. |
| C-003 | A business application and a lasting employer firm are different events. | Linked administrative data | NBER Working Paper 34881 separates applying for an EIN from transitioning into an employer firm; places differ in application intensity and transition rates. | The abstract does not show effects on local services or belonging. |
| C-004 | County application counts can show interest without showing a new job or service. | Official data method | Census publishes county applications for 2005–2025 and distinguishes applications, projected formations, and employer firms; the 2026 release also documents privacy noise. | The count needs later firm and employment data. |
| C-005 | A local business change may affect place, but the social and political link remains open. | Our inference | C-001 through C-004 connect owner location, entry stages, firms, and local opportunity. | Meeting places, trust, identity, services, and voting are not measured here. |

## Mechanism under test

```text
local cost, credit, labor, or customer change
  -> owner stays, leaves, applies, starts, closes, or moves
  -> lasting firms, jobs, services, prices, and meeting places change
  -> household choices, local identity, trust, and political voice change
```

## Next test

Choose matched counties and join applications to employer formation, payroll jobs, business location, service access, prices, rents, and local surveys. Do not infer belonging or politics from application counts alone.

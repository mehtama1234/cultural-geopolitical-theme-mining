# US household production account

This project examines what happens when unpaid household work is represented
inside national economic accounts. It asks how the boundary between market
production and household production shapes what the country can see as output,
care, consumption, and economic change.

This is a macro accounting source. It does not replace time-use, household,
caregiving, or wellbeing evidence. Its value is different: it makes a large
part of everyday economic life visible at the national level and shows that
the market/nonmarket composition of production changes over time.

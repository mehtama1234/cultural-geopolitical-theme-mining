# Project: US credit records and financial visibility

## Question

When ordinary payment trouble enters a financial record, does that record open doors to housing and credit—or close them for years?

## Short first pass

Separate the event that caused a delinquency, the record itself and the later decision made by a lender, landlord or bank. Track who has a thin record as well as who has a bad one.

## Possible connection

A credit record is not only a report of past payment. It can become a gate to a home, loan or account, and its gaps can be hard to explain or repair.

## Decision rule

Move on after one source maps record differences, one source follows a specific account or debt event, and one next dataset can test housing or credit access. Do not treat a score gap as proof of discrimination or a delinquency as a full measure of ability to pay.

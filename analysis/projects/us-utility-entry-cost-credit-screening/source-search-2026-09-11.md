**Search date:** 2026-09-11  
**Geography:** United States  
**Status:** matched evidence pass complete; state-by-state deposit and connection outcomes remain open

## Working question

Can a deposit, late fee, or utility credit check block service for a household before it has used any energy?

## First pattern to test

```text
move, new account, or past debt
  -> credit screen, deposit, fee, or denial
  -> connection cost or delayed service
  -> payment stress, collection record, or unsafe home
```

The credit record can be both a gate at entry and a record created after failure. Those two paths must not be treated as the same thing.

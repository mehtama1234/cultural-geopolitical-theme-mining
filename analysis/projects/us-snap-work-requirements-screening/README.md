# Project: SNAP work requirements and screening

## Question

Do SNAP work requirements increase employment, or do they mainly reduce
participation through screening and administrative loss?

## Decision rule

Keep eligibility, work requirement, exemption, reporting, participation, labor
supply, food security, administrative churn, household adaptation, and welfare
separate. Losing SNAP is not the same as gaining work.

## Current bounded finding

The [Census CES finding](findings/us-census-snap-work-requirements-participation-employment-2026-001.md)
reports a 7% participation decline without increased labor supply in a five-state
linked-data study, with disproportionate screening of lower-income people.

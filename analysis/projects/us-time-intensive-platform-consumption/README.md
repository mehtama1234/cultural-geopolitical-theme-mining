# Project: US time-intensive platform consumption

## Question

When a service uses a person's time as its real price, how should we measure competition and customer benefit?

## Short first pass

Separate money price, time used, ads, offline activity, switching, user benefit and platform market power.

## Possible connection

A free app can still be costly if it takes time and attention. That hidden price may explain why people stay when ads rise and why a merger can change more than the visible subscription price.

## Decision rule

Move on after one time-use measure, one change in the service cost, and one customer or competition result. Do not call a free service low-cost without counting time.

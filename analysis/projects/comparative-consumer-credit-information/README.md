# Project: consumer-credit information and bargaining

## Question

When borrowers learn what loan prices look like, can they bargain for better
terms—or does better information mainly make them more willing to borrow?

## Decision rule

Keep beliefs, search, negotiation, interest rate, loan size, fees, maturity,
repayment, delinquency, and household security separate. A lower rate is not
the same as a lower total burden.

## Current bounded finding

The [consumer-credit finding](findings/comparative-nber-consumer-credit-search-negotiation-2026-001.md)
reports that belief elicitation and a regulator-linked price-comparison tool
changed search, negotiation, rates, and loan take-up in Chile. It is
comparative evidence, not a current U.S. estimate.

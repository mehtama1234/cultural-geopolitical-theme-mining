# Source search: digital use, wellbeing, and collective exit

**Search date:** 2026-09-22  
**Geography:** United States, with a U.S. college-student comparison inside a global report  
**Status:** bounded pass complete; individual deactivation and collective coordination are visible, while durable exit and population-wide effects remain open

## Working question

Why might people keep using a digital platform even when leaving it can improve their reported wellbeing?

## Opening source

| ID | Source | What it tells us | Status | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| US-WHR-SOCIAL-EXIT-2026 | [World Happiness Report 2026, “Social media, wasting time, and product traps”](https://www.worldhappiness.report/ed/2026/social-media-wasting-time-and-product-traps/) | Summarizes a randomized Facebook deactivation experiment and a separate U.S. college-student study of willingness to leave Instagram or TikTok when others leave too. | Report chapter drawing on published studies | The chapter is a synthesis, not one new representative panel; the studies use different populations, platforms, outcomes, and designs. |

## First pattern

```text
platform use and social dependence
  -> attempted or assigned deactivation
  -> short-run wellbeing improvement and/or lower use
  -> social loss or fear of missing out when others remain
  -> [open] durable exit, replacement, collective coordination, and long-run meaning
```

The source adds a social layer to practical exit. A person can experience a
better month away from a platform and still value connection, information, or
coordination enough to return. A separate U.S. college-student study suggests
that the value judgment can change when everyone in the relevant community is
asked to leave together.

## Counterpoint to keep visible

Improved subjective wellbeing is not the same as total welfare. The Facebook
experiment found political knowledge moved in the opposite direction, and the
chapter notes that connection and information may matter even when anxiety or
depression improves. The coordination result is also not a population-wide
exit rate or proof that platforms should be removed.

## Decision rule

Keep assigned deactivation, observed app deletion, subjective wellbeing,
political knowledge, social connection, willingness to pay or accept, and
durable return or exit as separate stages. Reopen this lane only with repeated
behavior or a version-stamped platform/community episode that observes actual
exit, replacement, coordination, and later wellbeing or civic effects.

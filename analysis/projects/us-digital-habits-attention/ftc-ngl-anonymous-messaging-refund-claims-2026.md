# NGL: platform deception reaches a closed refund-claims route

**Checked:** 2026-10-20  
**Stage:** claims intake closed and review pending; payment, adequacy, and durable user exit remain open

The FTC's NGL matter adds a later-stage anonymous-messaging platform route.
The agency and Los Angeles District Attorney alleged that NGL sent fake
messages that appeared to come from real people, falsely claimed that paid NGL
Pro could reveal message senders, and imposed recurring charges without
consent. The 2024 settlement required $4.5 million for refunds and banned the
defendants from marketing anonymous messaging apps to children and teens under
18.

In January 2026, the FTC opened a claim route for people who paid for NGL Pro
between January 2022 and July 2024 and experienced unauthorized charges. A
person under 18 could not submit the claim directly; a parent or guardian had
to file. The filing deadline was April 6, 2026. The current FTC settlement page
says claims are no longer being accepted and are under review, with payments
expected in 2026 after review.

This advances the platform-to-remedy chain beyond an enforcement allegation or
formal conduct ban. The boundary remains open: the public materials do not
report claim volume, approvals, payment issuance, delivery, cashing, charge
correction, adequacy, continued use, switching, trust, or durable exit. A
closed claims window and expected payment timetable are not recipient recovery.

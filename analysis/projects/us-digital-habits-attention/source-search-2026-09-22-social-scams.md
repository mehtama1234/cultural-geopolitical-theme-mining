# Source search: social platforms as consumer-risk infrastructure

**Search date:** 2026-09-22  
**Geography:** United States  
**Status:** bounded pass complete; FTC reports show a material-risk route, while prevalence, recovery, platform responsibility, and durable trust remain open

## Working question

How can the same social-platform features that create connection and targeted reach also move consumer fraud into everyday shopping, investing, housing, work, and relationships?

## Opening source

| ID | Source | What it tells us | Status | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| US-FTC-SOCIAL-SCAM-2025 | [Reported losses to scams on social media eight times higher than in 2020](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/data-visualizations/data-spotlight/2026/04/reported-losses-scams-social-media-eight-times-higher-2020) | FTC Consumer Sentinel reports indicate $2.1 billion in reported 2025 losses where social media was the contact method; shopping, investment, romance, job, and housing scams appear through different routes. | FTC Data Spotlight based on public reports | Reports are not all fraud incidents, most fraud is not reported, and reported losses do not measure platform exposure, causal responsibility, recovery, or household impact. |

## First pattern

```text
social connection, profiles, ads, and targeted reach
  -> scam contact or deceptive offer
  -> payment, disclosure, trust, or account exposure
  -> reported loss or non-reporting
  -> [open] correction, recovery, platform response, switching, and durable trust
```

This adds a material-cost version of digital agency. The platform is not only
a place where people spend time; it can also be a market and targeting layer
where a social connection, ad, profile, or group makes a financial request
look credible.

## Counterpoint to keep visible

The FTC's numbers describe reports, not the true incidence of fraud. The rise
could reflect more loss, more platform use, more reporting, changes in coding,
or some combination. Social media can also help people connect and find real
goods, services, work, and support. The source cannot assign every loss to a
platform's design or show that a consumer could have avoided it.

## Decision rule

Keep platform contact, scam type, payment or disclosure, reported loss,
non-reporting, complaint, refund, recovery, and later trust or exit separate.
Reopen this lane only with a case or dataset that follows an affected
consumer through payment, report, correction, recovery, and later behavior.

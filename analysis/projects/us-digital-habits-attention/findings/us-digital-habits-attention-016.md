# Finding 016: Leaving a platform can feel better while social pressure keeps people on it

**Status:** bounded digital-exit and collective-coordination finding · **Checked:** 2026-09-22

## The finding in simple words

A World Happiness Report chapter brings together two different studies.

In one randomized Facebook experiment, people who stayed off Facebook for four
weeks reported being happier, more satisfied, less anxious, and less depressed.
They also later reported using Facebook less and were more likely to delete the
app. But they had less measured political knowledge than the control group.

In a separate study of U.S. college students, many people who would want money
to leave Instagram or TikTok alone would instead pay to leave if everyone else
in their community left too.

The route is:

```text
platform use and social dependence
  -> deactivation or imagined collective exit
  -> short-run wellbeing improvement or changed valuation
  -> information, connection, and fear of missing out
  -> [open] durable exit, replacement, coordination, and later meaning/action
```

## What this adds to the broader atlas

This is a more precise version of “people cannot leave.” The barrier may not
be only a button, fee, or technical lock. It can be the social cost of leaving
when friends, classmates, customers, or political information remain on the
platform.

It also keeps the trade-off honest. A person can feel better away from a
platform and still value something the platform supplies. Less anxiety is not
the whole welfare story, and app deletion is not the same as durable exit.

## What it does not show

- The two studies do not follow the same people and must not be pooled.
- The Facebook experiment is not a representative estimate of all Americans.
- Stated willingness to leave together is not actual coordinated departure.
- The findings do not establish long-run wellbeing, political participation,
  social replacement, or platform policy effects.
- This is not proof that every user is harmed or wants the platform gone.

## Next decisive test

Use a repeated, privacy-protected user panel that records attempted exit,
actual deactivation or deletion, replacement communication, information and
social loss, return, and later wellbeing or civic action. Preserve individual
exit and collective coordination as separate stages.

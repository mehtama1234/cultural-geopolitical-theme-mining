# Illuminate Education: finalized student-data controls after a large breach

**Checked:** 2026-10-20  
**Stage:** finalized FTC administrative order; compliance, student notification, and downstream recovery remain open

The FTC's Illuminate Education matter adds a named education-technology
platform route from data exposure to enforceable governance. The FTC complaint
said a December 2021–January 2022 breach gave a hacker access to personal data
of 10.1 million students, including addresses, dates of birth, student records,
and health-related information. It also alleged that Illuminate had been warned
about vulnerabilities, retained unmanaged data, and waited nearly two years to
notify some school districts, including districts comprising more than 380,000
students.

After public comment, the FTC finalized an order in June 2026. The order
requires deletion of information not reasonably needed for requested services,
limits collection and maintenance to that need, requires a public retention
schedule with deletion timeframes, mandates a comprehensive information-security
program, and requires notice to the FTC when Illuminate alerts another
government about a covered breach.

This is a stronger later-stage conversion than a proposed control: the case is
listed under order and the obligations are specified. The boundary remains
material. The public materials do not establish student-level notification
receipt, identity-theft or health harm, completed deletion, compliance-audit
results, future incident performance, restored trust, or practical exit from
school technology services. A finalized order is not evidence of individual
privacy recovery.

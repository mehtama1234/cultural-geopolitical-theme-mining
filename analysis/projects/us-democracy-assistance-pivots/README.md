# Project: democracy assistance, host-state risk, and civic continuity

## Question

What happens when U.S. democracy assistance is meant to strengthen public
institutions but the host government begins undermining civil society, media, or
political competition?

## Current bounded finding

The [GAO finding](findings/us-gao-democracy-assistance-antidemocratic-pivot-2018-2024-001.md)
shows that U.S. agencies shifted some assistance away from host-government
entities after reported antidemocratic actions. The pivot may protect civic
actors, but the reviewed programs had no required plans for that possibility and
officials reported disruption and lost momentum.

This is a route-and-continuity finding, not proof that the assistance caused or
prevented democratic decline.

## Next test

Follow a named award through host-state pressure, decision to pivot, recipient
change, implementation, participant safety, civic use, and later institutional
or public response.

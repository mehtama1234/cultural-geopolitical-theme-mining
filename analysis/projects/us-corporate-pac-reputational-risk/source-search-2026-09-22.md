# Source search: US corporate political ties and reputational risk

**Search date:** 2026-09-22  
**Geography:** United States  
**Status:** bounded pass complete; firms' visible political spending responds temporarily to reputational shocks, durable political reallocation remains open

## Working question

When public attention makes political ties risky, do firms permanently change their political behavior or only wait out the pressure?

## Opening source

| ID | Source | What it tells us | Status | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| US-NBER-CORPORATE-PAC-REPUTATION | [The Price of Political Ties](https://www.nber.org/papers/w35494) | Using fatal school shootings and mass shootings as shocks to gun-violence salience, the study reports selective, temporary corporate-PAC divestment from Republican House candidates, with larger effects where reputational risk was high or strategic value was low. Contributions returned to baseline within about nine months. | NBER working paper | PAC contributions are not all corporate political activity; the abstract does not measure lobbying, informal access, policy change, public opinion, or firm sales after the shock. |

## First pattern

```text
public shock raises issue salience
  -> visible political tie becomes reputationally costly
  -> firm reduces or redirects PAC support in selected races
  -> public attention fades
  -> contribution returns toward baseline
  -> [open] durable policy, legitimacy, or consumer response
```

The source adds a firm-side political adaptation route. It does not show that
public pressure changed the firm's underlying political objectives.

## Counterpoint to keep visible

Temporary withdrawal can still be a real reputational constraint. But a firm
may reduce visible giving while preserving lobbying, access, trade-group
activity, or long-run policy preferences. A PAC change is not the same as
corporate depoliticization or changed public legitimacy.

## Decision rule

Keep PAC giving, lobbying, policy influence, public trust, and consumer action
separate. Reopen this lane only when a compatible source measures one of those
next stages.

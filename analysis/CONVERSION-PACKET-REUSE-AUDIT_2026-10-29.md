# Conversion-packet reuse audit

**Date:** 2026-10-29  
**Purpose:** identify which existing named-unit artifacts can be reused for the new broad cultural, social, institutional and geopolitical goal before acquiring more sources  
**Scope:** artifacts already present in Git `HEAD`; many corresponding paths are marked deleted in the dirty working tree by parallel work. This audit does not restore, delete, or reconcile those files.

## Executive decision

The repository is further along than the three recent rotations suggest. The
existing corpus already contains named-unit conversion work. The next phase
should therefore be **reconciliation and selective closure**, not another
unbounded source scan.

The strongest reusable packet is platform remedy: it contains multiple
adjudicated worker-level cases, including contrasting outcomes. It can support
a bounded finding about the separation between formal remedy and practical
recovery, although payment receipt, recurrence prevention and anti-retaliation
remain open.

The other packets are valuable but remain stage-audit or bridge artifacts. They
should be used to target one decisive missing field each:

- data center: household/local incidence and cost allocation;
- SNAP: notice, effort, receipt, adequacy and recovery;
- public AI: claimant-level exposure, correction and service receipt;
- critical minerals: application-specific qualification and buyer use;
- alliance capability: fielding, counterpart response and durability.

## Packet-by-packet audit

### 1. Platform remedy — closest to a defensible finding

The platform ledger is unusually useful because it does not collapse all
recourse into “remedy.” It separates explanation, human review, correction or
restoration, payment, compensation and anti-retaliation. It also contains both
positive and negative procedural outcomes.

The strongest rows are the Australian Fair Work Commission cases:

- one case records reactivation and a separate lost-remuneration order after
  the platform process failed to provide meaningful consideration;
- another records a compliant human review and dismissal of the worker's
  application;
- other cases record reactivation while leaving the amount and receipt of lost
  pay open;
- a settlement/revocation case shows why an eligibility decision cannot be
  treated as a durable merits remedy.

**Bounded finding now supportable:** formal procedural remedy and practical
recovery are separate variables. An adjudicated correction or reactivation is
strong evidence of institutional response, but not proof of payment receipt,
continued access, recurrence prevention, or system-wide improvement.

**Required follow-up fields:** decision date; implementation date; reactivation
date; amount ordered; amount received; account status 30/90/180 days later;
repeat deactivation; alternative work; retaliation; and whether the platform
changed the relevant rule or process.

**Promotion status:** bounded finding candidate, not a general claim about all
platform workers or all algorithmic management.

### 2. Large-load infrastructure — strong operation, weak incidence

The data-center artifacts correctly distinguish national electricity demand,
operating-event evidence, reliability governance and local legitimacy. They
also warn that a technical event or proposed cost-allocation rule is not a
household bill, service interruption, local burden or acceptance outcome.

**What can be said now:** computational load is an operational governance
object, and its physical behavior can enter reliability, rate, backup and
public-permission debates.

**What cannot be said:** that a named data center caused a household surcharge,
local service degradation, emissions burden or political backlash without a
facility/customer/place bridge.

**Best next packet:** select one facility or interconnection docket and join:

```text
facility/interconnection ID | event date | MW behavior | backup runtime
| utility/rate treatment | upgrade cost payer | emissions/water/land
| affected customer/place | public comment or challenge | later decision
```

**Promotion status:** mechanism candidate only.

### 3. SNAP usability — access exists, recovery is still unobserved

The SNAP audit is valuable because it keeps office closure, participation,
income transitions, food insecurity, replacement benefits and payment controls
separate. It explicitly rejects the common error of treating a replacement
policy or program participation record as completed food-security recovery.

**What can be said now:** administrative location, transition and control
design can shape practical access, but participation is not the same as
receipt, adequacy or household protection.

**Best next packet:** one public-use panel or administrative route that retains
application/recertification date, notice channel, effort, decision, benefit
amount, issuance/usable balance, interruption, replacement, appeal and later
food-security or household-hardship observation.

**Promotion status:** strong subtheme candidate for administrative legibility
and access-through-effort; not yet a complete household finding.

### 4. Public-sector AI — readiness-to-remedy gap

The public-AI bridge already contains a useful institutional comparison:
government use can be broad but shallow; evaluation and procurement controls
can be visible; context failures can occur after deployment; and oversight can
exist without a claimant-level remedy chain.

**What can be said now:** public AI capability depends on deployment context,
human oversight, evaluation, continuity, portability and ownership—not model
access alone.

**What cannot be said:** that a particular AI system improved or harmed a
claimant's service outcome unless exposure, decision, review, correction and
receipt are linked.

**Best next packet:** choose one named agency system—preferably a system with
public performance or oversight records—and capture:

```text
claimant/service unit | baseline route | AI task/version/vendor | human role
| processing decision | error/rejection | explanation | appeal/review
| correction | payment/service receipt | outage/continuity | later outcome
```

**Promotion status:** institutional mechanism candidate.

### 5. Critical minerals — built-in counterexample is the contribution

The minerals audit should not be reduced to a generic “supply-chain
resilience” theme. Its strongest contribution is the comparison between
battery routes, where recycling or chemistry substitution may become useful in
some applications, and semiconductor materials, where qualification and
performance constraints can block short-term substitution.

**What can be said now:** domestic investment, project selection, recycling
horizons and ownership visibility do not establish qualified, scalable or
replaceable supply.

**Best next packet:** two application-specific ledgers, deliberately different:

- one battery material with a dated export-control, shipment or price event and
  a later recycling/substitution or buyer-use observation;
- one semiconductor material with a named substitute, qualification effort or
  production redesign and a later customer/availability response.

**Promotion status:** qualified-substitution mechanism candidate with a strong
built-in counterexample; not a claim of strategic autonomy.

### 6. Alliance capability — capability is integrated, leverage is open

The alliance artifact separates spending, procurement, supplier networks,
production, exercises, public support and official response. This is exactly
the right structure for avoiding the error that a contract or exercise equals
deterrence.

**What can be said now:** usable capability is an integrated system involving
fielding, people, training, infrastructure, interoperability, maintenance and
partner coordination.

**What cannot be said:** that a specific procurement or exercise caused an
adversary concession, durable deterrence, partner alignment or public consent.

**Best next packet:** one capability and one event-country-party chain:

```text
commitment/contract | delivery/acceptance | training/readiness
| operation or incident | named partner/adversary response
| public/local burden | maintenance/sustainment | later cooperation/refusal
| reversal or durability
```

**Promotion status:** integrated-capability mechanism candidate.

## Cross-packet synthesis

The existing artifacts produce a sharper version of the new portfolio's main
thesis:

```text
formal capacity or right
  -> operating encounter
  -> response, correction, substitution or allocation
  -> material outcome
  -> attribution and cultural/geopolitical meaning
```

The packets are strongest at different stages:

| Stage | Best existing packet | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Formal route | SNAP, public AI, NATO, minerals | Rules, plans, standards and projects are visible |
| Operating encounter | Platform remedy, data center | Named cases/events show the system behaving |
| Correction/response | Platform remedy | Adjudicated contrasts expose process quality |
| Material receipt/incidence | None fully closed across the portfolio | Payment, bill, benefit adequacy, service receipt and local burden remain thin |
| Attribution/legitimacy/leverage | Alliance and data-center bridges | Meaning and response are explicitly framed but not fully linked to the operating event |

This means the next research dollar should target material receipt and later
response, not more formal statements.

## Next seven concrete actions

1. Promote the platform bounded finding into the broad findings layer, with
   explicit limits on payment, recurrence, retaliation and generalization.
2. Extract the platform ledger's follow-up fields into a machine-readable
   closure table; do not merge cases with different clocks or jurisdictions.
3. Select one data-center facility/interconnection record and attempt the
   facility-to-ratepayer/local-response bridge.
4. Select one SNAP route with a same-household or same-case follow-up and code
   notice, effort, receipt, adequacy and recovery separately.
5. Select one named public AI deployment where an appeal or oversight record is
   public; if no claimant-level bridge exists, record the acquisition failure.
6. Create two mineral application ledgers—battery and semiconductor—to retain
   the qualification counterexample.
7. Create one alliance event-country-party ledger and test whether the response
   is attributable to the capability rather than merely coincident with it.

## Stopping rules

Do not acquire another general survey, policy announcement, corporate press
release or broad trend report unless it closes a missing conversion or supplies
a real counterexample. Do not promote a cross-domain claim because six packets
share vocabulary. Promote only when the evidence includes a stable unit, dated
later observation, mechanism, distribution, uncertainty and reproducible source
trail.

The new goal is active. The repository now has a ranked cross-rotation

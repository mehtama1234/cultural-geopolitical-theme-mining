# Rotation KZ — Disability, migration and accessible portable agency

**Date:** 2026-10-30  
**Research question:** Across migration, displacement, asylum, work, social protection and return, what turns disability inclusion from a formal right into accessible information, mobility, care, income, participation and agency?

## Why this packet matters

Disability and migration are often treated as separate policy categories. IOM's
2026 World Migration Report chapter joins them and estimates that at least 62
million migrants, refugees and internally displaced people have significant
disabilities, while warning that the number is likely underestimated. The
important analytical move is from “disabled people are vulnerable” to examining
how journeys, environments, attitudes, restrictive status regimes and inaccessible
services create or intensify disabling effects.

```text
impairment / acquired impairment / ageing / conflict / climate / poverty
  -> route, border, asylum, work or return decision
  -> accessible information, transport, documents, accommodation and care
  -> social protection, income, participation and community belonging
  -> correction, independent agency and durable outcome
```

## Cultural, geographical and geopolitical reading

The same impairment can produce different disability depending on geography and
institutional design. A wheelchair user may face a border crossing without a
usable ramp, a deaf asylum seeker may receive no accessible interview, a person
with psychosocial disability may be misread as non-compliant, and a person with a
chronic condition may lose a medicine or assistive device during movement. These
are not only clinical problems; they are failures of information, transport,
documentation, housing, work, language and recognition.

Migration can also be a source of agency. A person may move to join family,
access treatment, escape violence, find work or secure better accessibility.
The atlas must not equate disability with passivity or migration with harm. The
unit of analysis should preserve choice, support networks, cultural identity and
the person's own definition of a good outcome.

Geopolitically, portability has two sides. A portable disability assessment,
health record or social-protection entitlement could reduce repeated proof and
care interruption, but it could also make sensitive status data available to
immigration enforcement or employers. Cross-border coordination must therefore
include data minimization, consent, correction and an accessible complaint route.

## Counterexample and source boundary

A disability prevalence estimate, inclusive policy, training standard, health
system inclusion, accessible-design claim, community-participation statement or
portable record does not prove usable access or independent agency. Disability
definitions differ; hidden, episodic, psychosocial and acquired impairments are
often missed; a person can face barriers without a formal diagnosis; and a
service may be physically accessible but culturally unsafe, unaffordable or
unreachable after status changes.

## Promotion decision

**Tier 1 deepened finding:** Disability and migration is a distinct intersection
where accessibility, legal status, data, health, work, protection and cultural
agency interact across the entire mobility cycle.

**Tier 2 mechanism candidate:** **disability-migration-to-accessible-portable-health-social-protection-and-independent-agency**. The next acquisition should follow one person or household from migration decision or displacement through impairment/access needs, route, documents, communication, transport, asylum/work encounter, assistive device, health/social-protection service, complaint and later agency outcome, with a non-disabled or differently supported comparator where ethically appropriate.

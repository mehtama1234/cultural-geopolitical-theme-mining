## How to use the maps

1. Start in the lane closest to the concrete condition: a bill, job rule,
   care need, application, platform practice, investment, or agreement.
2. Open the linked record before carrying a claim across lanes.
3. Treat the map's cross-subtheme finding as a bounded interpretation, not a
   pooled estimate.
4. Use the stated next test to identify the missing join instead of assuming
   the later outcome.

## Coverage boundary

All 14 broad lanes now have a dedicated source-led map. That is an access and
clustering baseline—not coverage completion. Every lane still needs continuing
source-level mining, linked-outcome tests, and counterexamples; the
firm/market-power pass, for example, now separates purchasing eligibility,
disaster reallocation, collateral, legal venue, payment rails, and local
contracting rather than treating them as a generic business trend.

# Bridge 3: work rules and tools to control, household security, and local power

**Status:** cross-source synthesis, not a causal estimate  
**Checked:** 2026-09-12

## Question

When a job, benefit, workplace norm, schedule, or digital tool changes how work is assigned and measured, who gains control, who carries the risk, and how does that reshape household security and civic power?

## Evidence carried by the existing records

| Step | Existing evidence | What it supports | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| Benefit dependence | [Health coverage can hold a job in place](../findings/us-health-insurance-job-lock-matched-evidence-001.md) shows coverage can affect job retention and search. | Compensation can change the cost of leaving and the practical labor-market alternatives. | That any worker stayed unwillingly or lost a better opportunity. |
| Flexible work | [When equal income buys a different life](../findings/us-multiple-jobs-household-room-path-001.md) links multiple jobs with different spending, transport, saving, and schedule-control patterns. | Gross income does not reveal usable security or who controls time. | That multiple jobs are hardship rather than a chosen strategy in every case. |
| Workplace allocation | [When workplace fit becomes a work gate](../findings/us-workplace-fit-voice-path-001.md) connects speech norms with access to socially intensive work and advancement. | An informal standard can decide who gets practice, visibility, and voice. | That a particular manager acted unfairly or that customer outcomes worsened. |
| Local political power | [When the biggest local employer reaches the ballot](../findings/us-employer-political-influence-path-001.md) reports communication and perceived influence around dominant employers. | Local job dependence can alter the practical cost of disagreement. | That an employer changed a worker’s vote or coerced political behavior. |

## Working chain

```text
work rule, benefit, schedule, tool, or local labor structure
  -> task, time, information, or exit options change
  -> worker adapts, stays, adds work, changes jobs, or accepts a different role
  -> pay, health, care, status, household security, and worker voice change
  -> firm captures or transfers the gain and risk
  -> local dependence, trust, public expression, and political demand may change
```

The strongest supported point is that work arrangements distribute more than wages: they distribute time, benefits, visibility, risk, and the ability to leave. The unresolved point is control. A worker can have formal choice while facing a high practical cost for using it.

## Four measures that must stay separate

1. **Output:** what was produced or how quickly the task was completed.
2. **Control:** who set the task, schedule, standard, data access, and remedy.
3. **Security:** pay after work costs, benefits, health, savings, household time, and future options.
4. **Voice:** who can challenge the allocation, speak publicly, organize, or influence the rule.

Higher output does not prove higher control or security. Flexible hours do not prove stable income. Employer communication does not prove changed voting. A familiar communication style does not prove better service.

## First comparison to run

Compare workers with similar pay or output but different work arrangements:

1. employer coverage versus portable or public coverage;
2. one job versus multiple jobs or gig work;
3. structured skill evaluation versus informal “fit” allocation;
4. competitive labor market versus dominant local employer;
5. tool-assisted work versus non-assisted work, with task, monitoring, authority, and gain recorded.

For each comparison, record promised and actual hours, work costs, benefits, schedule changes, training, feedback, promotion, health and care effects, household income, outside options, and the worker’s ability to refuse or leave. Measure public speech, political contact, and vote separately.

## What would weaken the bridge

The working bridge would weaken if benefit portability did not change job mobility where coverage was the binding constraint; if multiple jobs reliably increased usable security after time and work costs; if structured evaluation did not change access or advancement; if employer power did not alter perceived or practical civic freedom; or if tools raised output while leaving control, risk, and distribution unchanged across groups.

## Next end-to-end artifact

The next version should follow one work arrangement or rule through task allocation, worker response, household consequence, firm gain or cost transfer, and local institutional or political response. It must report whether the worker had a real alternative, not merely whether the contract allowed one.

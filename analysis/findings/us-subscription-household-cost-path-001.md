# When a forgotten subscription becomes a household cost

## Short answer

Automatic renewal can be useful when a person wants the service to continue. It can also keep a charge running after the service has lost value or the customer has stopped paying attention. The key issue is not renewal by itself; it is whether the person understood the deal and could leave easily.

The careful finding is that attention is part of the payment system. A private charge can become a household cost before it becomes a complaint or a public rule.

## What the sources show

### 1. Forced action reveals cancellations

NBER payment-card data across ten digital and non-digital products show a sharp rise in cancellations when replacement cards force subscribers to actively renew. The authors estimate that inattention raises seller revenue by 14% to more than 200% compared with immediate cancellation after value falls below price.

The range comes from a model. A customer who does not cancel may still value the service, use it rarely, or prefer automatic renewal.

[NBER Working Paper 31547](https://www.nber.org/papers/w31547)

### 2. The business gains from continuity

HBS describes recurring revenue as useful to companies and customers when the service is clear and the fee fits the value. It also warns that subscription fatigue can make customers feel that the charge is hard to see or hard to stop.

[HBS subscription research](https://www.library.hbs.edu/working-knowledge/with-subscription-fatigue-setting-in-companies-need-to-think-hard-about-fees)

### 3. Cancellation design is now an enforcement issue

In May 2026, the FTC said Shutterstock agreed to pay $35 million to settle allegations that it charged consumers without informed consent and made cancellation difficult. The proposed order requires clearer terms, consent before charging, and simple cancellation.

This is one company's settlement and allegations, not proof that every automatic renewal is unlawful or unwanted.

[FTC Shutterstock settlement](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/05/shutterstock-pay-35-million-settle-ftc-allegations-over-illegal-subscription-cancellation-practices)

## The connection

```text
subscription starts
  -> payment renews without a fresh choice
  -> use falls or value becomes unclear
  -> attention or a forced card change finally triggers action
  -> cancellation, continued payment, fee, or overdraft
  -> household room and trust in the seller change
```

The NBER study supports the link between forced action and cancellation. The FTC record shows a remedy path. Neither follows every customer from lost value to household harm.

## The four outside views

**Household:** A recurring charge may be harmless for a customer with room and clear value, but painful when it competes with food, rent, care, or debt payment.

**Company and sector:** Renewal lowers payment friction and can keep a useful service active. A hard cancellation path can turn inattention into revenue and reduce the customer's ability to compare.

**Money and finance:** The important measure is not only the monthly fee. It is the fee plus notice, overdraft, interest, lost discount, and the other purchase it displaced.

**Public power:** Disclosure, consent, cancellation, refunds, and complaint rules decide whether silence counts as a real choice. Enforcement can change the seller's design, but does not show the full household effect.

## What would change the finding

- Price, renewal date, notice, use, cancellation effort, refund, and customer value.
- Household income, cash room, overdraft, interest, and other bills.
- Results by age, income, disability, digital access, and essential versus optional service.
- A comparison before and after a clear-price or easy-cancellation rule.
- Evidence that easier cancellation causes useful services to lapse for people who wanted continuity.

## Next test

Choose one recurring service. Follow the sign-up, first renewal, use, notice, cancellation attempt, final charge, refund, and next household payment for 90 days. Keep “did not cancel,” “did not value,” and “could not afford” separate.

## Reading rule

Automatic renewal is not automatically a household tax. A customer needs a clear choice and a real way out.

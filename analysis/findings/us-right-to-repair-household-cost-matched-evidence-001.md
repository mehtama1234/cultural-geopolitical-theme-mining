# Repair access is part of the product's real price

## Short answer

The price of a product includes what happens when it breaks. The FTC reports that difficult design, unavailable parts or repair information, software locks, and warranty steering can narrow repair choices and push people toward authorized networks or replacement. FTC warranty guidance says coverage may include repair, replacement, or refund, while a 2024 warning says companies generally cannot require specific parts or service providers to keep warranty coverage. The deeper finding is that repair access changes the household's real cost, time, and ability to keep working—but safety and quality concerns still need testing.

## What the matched evidence shows

| Layer | What the evidence says | What remains open |
|---|---|---|
| Product design | FTC identifies design, parts, information, software, and contract limits that can make repair harder. | Which restriction raises cost for each product. |
| Warranty | FTC says warranties may cover repair, replacement, or refund and must be available to read before purchase. | Claim success, waiting time, and out-of-warranty cost. |
| Choice | FTC warned that requiring specified parts or service providers may violate warranty law. | Whether independent repair is available, affordable, and safe. |
| Replacement | FTC says restrictions can steer consumers to replace products before the end of useful life. | Household spending, debt, waste, and lost use. |
| Public rule | The Deere settlement advances repair access for farm equipment. | Whether similar access improves household outcomes in consumer goods. |

Sources: [FTC Nixing the Fix](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/documents/reports/nixing-fix-ftc-report-congress-repair-restrictions/nixing_the_fix_report_final_5521_630pm-508_002.pdf), [FTC warranty warning](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/07/ftc-warns-companies-stop-warranty-practices-harm-consumers-right-repair), [FTC warranty guidance](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/warranties), and [FTC-Deere settlement](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/07/ftc-states-secure-settlement-deere-company-advancing-farmers-right-repair).

## The connection

```text
product breaks
  → parts, tools, software, warranty, and repair-shop choices
  → repair price and waiting time
  → repair, replacement, borrowing, or going without
  → household money, work, safety, and waste change
```

The FTC record supports the restriction and choice problem. It does not yet give a joined price and household outcome for each product.

## The useful surprise

Repair access is a form of financial slack. A household that can fix a phone, car, appliance, or tool may avoid a large one-time payment and keep work or care moving. A household that cannot repair may pay for replacement even when the original product could have continued working.

## Four views

**Household:** Count repair quote, parts, shipping, labor, waiting time, replacement, borrowing, and lost use.

**Customer:** Record repair instructions, available shops, warranty terms, software access, and whether the product can be fixed without losing coverage.

**Money and finance:** Separate repair price, warranty payment, replacement debt, insurance, and household cash loss.

**Public power:** Test access to parts, tools, manuals, diagnostics, safety records, warranty enforcement, and a remedy for refusal.

## What would change the finding

- Product-level repair time, price, parts availability, and failure records.
- Warranty claim, refusal, authorized-repair, and independent-repair outcomes.
- Replacement, borrowing, work interruption, and household debt after a failure.
- Safety and repeat-failure rates for authorized and independent repairs.
- Results by income, race, disability, rural access, and product type.

## Reading rule

Do not call a product affordable from its purchase price alone. Include the cost and availability of keeping it working.

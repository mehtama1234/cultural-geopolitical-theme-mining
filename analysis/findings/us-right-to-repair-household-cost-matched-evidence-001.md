# Repair access is part of the product's real price

## The argument

The price of a product includes what happens when it breaks. A phone, car, appliance, or work tool can be cheap to buy and expensive to keep alive. The FTC identifies design limits, missing parts, unavailable repair information, software locks, and warranty practices that can narrow repair choices. Those limits turn a failure into a forced replacement or a long period without the product.

The key question is not whether every repair should be allowed by anyone. Safety and quality matter. The key question is whether the customer has a fair, workable choice: an authorized repair, an independent repair, a part, a manual, a warranty claim, or a replacement with a clear price. Without that choice, the product's purchase price hides a future cost.

## Follow one broken product

A delivery worker's phone stops charging, or a family's washer fails. The owner asks for a repair quote. The part is unavailable, the software blocks a third-party fix, the nearest approved shop is far away, or the warranty requires a process that takes weeks. The household borrows a device, misses work, pays for a replacement, or puts the repair on credit. The failure becomes a cash and time shock.

```text
product breaks → repair parts, tools, software, warranty, and shop choices
→ price and waiting time → repair, replace, borrow, or go without
→ money, work, care, safety, and waste change
```

## What the sources actually establish

| Step | Evidence | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Design | FTC identifies design, parts, information, software, and contract limits that can make repair harder. | The barrier can be built into the product. |
| Warranty | FTC says warranties may cover repair, replacement, or refund and must be available before purchase. | Coverage is part of the product offer. |
| Choice | FTC warned that requiring specified parts or service providers may violate warranty law. | A warranty should not quietly remove lawful repair options. |
| Replacement | FTC says repair restrictions can steer people toward replacement before useful life ends. | A failure can create a second purchase. |
| Public rule | The FTC-Deere settlement advances repair access for farm equipment. | Enforcement can change what information and tools owners receive. |

Sources: [FTC Nixing the Fix](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/documents/reports/nixing-fix-ftc-report-congress-repair-restrictions/nixing_the_fix_report_final_5521_630pm-508_002.pdf), [FTC warranty warning](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/07/ftc-warns-companies-stop-warranty-practices-harm-consumers-right-repair), [FTC warranty guidance](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/warranties), and [FTC-Deere settlement](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/07/ftc-states-secure-settlement-deere-company-advancing-farmers-right-repair).

## The deeper finding

Repair access is financial slack. A household that can fix a product may avoid a large payment and keep work, transport, communication, or care moving. A household that cannot fix it may pay for replacement even when the original product could have continued working. The right measure is not whether repair is theoretically possible; it is whether it is affordable, timely, safe, and available where the customer lives.

## What remains unproven

The FTC record identifies restrictions and legal protections but does not join a product failure to a repair quote, waiting time, replacement debt, work interruption, and later household stability. It also does not show whether independent repair produces the same safety and repeat-failure results across product types.

## Next test

Track product failure, repair options, parts price, labor, warranty claim, waiting time, replacement, borrowing, work or care interruption, and repeat failure. Compare authorized and independent repairs by product, place, income, rural access, and disability.

## Reading rule

Do not call a product affordable from its purchase price alone. Include the cost and availability of keeping it working.

# Seed topic queue

These are starting questions, not conclusions. Each one must be narrowed into a testable project.

The current US-first run order is: [household financial pressure](projects/us-household-financial-pressure/README.md), [housing and insurance](projects/us-housing-insurance-risk/README.md), [health costs](projects/us-health-cost-household-choice/README.md), and [cost of living, trust, and political response](projects/us-cost-trust-politics/README.md). These are short discovery passes. A topic earns deeper work only when the next test can connect a condition to a later choice or outcome.

| Topic | Starting question | HBS route | NBER route | Wider maps |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI and work control | Does AI raise output by helping workers, or by measuring and directing them more closely? | AI, careers, managing the business | productivity, labor, firms, inequality | work, status, bargaining, state capacity |
| Hometown entrepreneurship | What removes local business owners from places, and what replaces their role? | entrepreneurship, local business | entrepreneurship, spatial economics, labor | place, class, local power, political voice |
| Elections and economic belief | How do people turn economic experience into a view of national prospects? | economics and global commerce | political economy, macroeconomics, beliefs | trust, legitimacy, markets, geopolitics |
| Health cost transfer | When health costs rise, who changes behavior first: households, employers, insurers, or providers? | health care, human behavior | health, public economics, labor | family, class, regulation, public trust |
| Technology and habit | Why do people adopt tools that help them while resisting tools that work better? | data and technology, behavior | health, consumer choice, technology | identity, attention, platform power |
| Work and the stalled middle | Why do careers stop advancing even as experience grows? | careers and workplace | labor markets, firms, mobility | status, age, class, organizational power |
| Supply chains and strategic dependence | When does an economic dependency become a security or diplomatic lever? | globalization, strategy, environment | international trade, macro, political economy | industry, state power, conflict |
| Demography and care | How do aging, migration, and care work change household and state choices? | health, managing the business | demography, aging, labor, public economics | family, gender, migration, fiscal power |
| Customer finance and hidden costs | When does a visible price hide a different burden for different households? | finance, consumer behavior | household finance, consumer choice, inequality | trust, fairness, firm power, regulation |
| Housing and insurance | Is coverage becoming a condition for staying, borrowing, or selling? | finance, policy, social responsibility | housing, climate risk, household finance | place, class, local politics, public risk |
| Health costs and household choice | What do people give up first when care costs too much? | health care, human behavior | health, labor, public economics | family, debt, work, trust |
| Cost of living and political trust | How do bills and lost benefits become blame or political demand? | economics, policy, human behavior | beliefs, macroeconomics, political economy | identity, legitimacy, parties, media |

# Ranked cross-domain trends: source-led synthesis v1

**Checked:** 2026-09-25  
**Purpose:** a ranked entry point to the atlas: what is changing, through what
mechanism, for whom, and how strong the current evidence is.

This is a ranking of cross-domain explanatory value, not a common national
rate, forecast, or claim that every population shares the same trajectory.

| Rank | Trend | Evidence strength | Why it matters now |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Formal access is diverging from usable access. | Repeated administrative, survey, and quasi-experimental evidence. | Eligibility, accounts, vouchers, leave, and coverage do not establish completion, continuity, or correction. |
| 2 | Household security is being assembled through substitution. | Repeated population surveys and linked administrative studies. | Food, energy, care, saving, borrowing, work, and family help are traded against one another. |
| 3 | Administrative route design is a distribution mechanism. | Strong causal evidence in selected SNAP, housing-court, and application settings. | Timing, paperwork, representation, and review shape who receives protection before a merits decision. |
| 4 | Capacity build-out is outrunning demonstrated lived benefit. | Repeated program, infrastructure, care, and state-operation records. | A project, connection, staffed line, operating system, or military output is not yet affordable service, health, or deterrence. |
| 5 | Practical control depends on reversibility, not nominal consent. | Enforcement, complaint, product-design, and administrative records. | People need notice, status visibility, contestability, correction, and a feasible exit route. |
| 6 | Place is the joint distribution system for opportunity, risk, and time. | Housing, disaster, transport, health-capacity, and mobility evidence. | Remaining, moving, or returning changes exposure to care, work, insurance, social ties, and future security. |
| 7 | Digital power increasingly operates through attention, defaults, data, and exit cost. | Field experiments, audits, enforcement records, and surveys. | Choice can exist formally while switching, deletion, cancellation, comparison, and recovery remain difficult. |
| 8 | Aggregate gains conceal divergent adaptation paths. | Heterogeneous treatment evidence, subgroup records, and household panels. | More assets, spending, coverage, or aid can coexist with debt, constrained consumption, low liquidity, or unequal return. |
| 9 | Informal support is a necessary but unequal timed buffer. | Cross-source household, care, and time-use evidence. | Family and network support can preserve continuity while transferring unpaid labor, risk, and reciprocal obligation. |
| 10 | State and alliance capability has a delivery-to-effect gap. | Official operational, procurement, monitoring, and coordination records. | Procurement, exercises, data collection, ISR output, and partner commitments do not by themselves show public legitimacy or changed counterpart behavior. |
| 11 | Distribution becomes political or geopolitical meaning through attribution. | Cross-source cultural, public-system, consumer, and state-operation evidence. | The same service or capability can be read as protection, extraction, abandonment, dependence, or solidarity depending on incidence, visibility, alternatives, and response. |

## How to use the ranking

For each proposition, distinguish: **observed route or output** from **claimed
lived/strategic result**. The full [cross-lane portfolio](US-BROAD-CROSS-LANE-TREND-PORTFOLIO_V1.md)
contains the source-specific propositions, counterexamples, and open links.
The [14-theme readout](US-BROAD-CURRENT-14-THEME-READOUT_V1.md) supplies the
domain map; the source-led maps supply the granular evidence and boundaries.

## Decisive gaps

The portfolio is weakest where it needs linked follow-up: whether a person
completed the route; adequacy of money, care, service, or repair; ability to
reverse or exit; distribution of cost; and later trust, recovery, or changed
external behavior. It is also weakest at the attribution layer: who the
affected unit believes is responsible, what explanation was available, and
whether the interpretation becomes action, acceptance, resistance, or
non-use. Those gaps are evidence priorities, not proof of failure.

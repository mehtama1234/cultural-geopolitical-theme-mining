# Living cultural-geopolitical atlas operating charter

**Started:** 2026-10-29  
**Mode:** continuous research program; no final “done” state  
**Purpose:** continuously discover, test, refresh and synthesize cultural, social, geographical, technological and geopolitical themes from diverse evidence

## What this program is

This repository is a living atlas. The current synthesis is a baseline, not a
finish line. New sources can:

- create a new theme or subtheme;
- deepen an existing conversion;
- close a previously open stage;
- provide a counterexample;
- invalidate or narrow an earlier finding;
- reveal a new geography, population or cultural meaning; or
- document a source boundary.

No theme is “finished” merely because one packet is complete. A theme remains
open to later data, changed conditions, new places, new populations and new
outcomes.

## Permanent research loop

```text
discover
  -> triage by source authority, unit and open-stage value
  -> acquire primary evidence
  -> extract direct observations and limits
  -> compare across place, group and time
  -> create or update packet and ledger
  -> test counterexample and alternative explanation
  -> promote, narrow, demote or record boundary
  -> synthesize cultural/geographical/political meaning
  -> return to discovery
```

The loop must keep moving even when a particular outcome is unavailable. A
source boundary is a result; it is not permission to stop looking for a
different source family, geography or unit.

## Source rotation

Each rotation should draw from at least three distinct source families where
available:

1. **lived/household:** surveys, time-use, expenditure, health, mobility,
   qualitative or community records;
2. **institutional/administrative:** agency processing, appeals, benefits,
   enforcement, public records and regulatory dockets;
3. **firm/platform/market:** filings, policies, complaints, remedies, pricing,
   operating records and customer/worker evidence;
4. **place/infrastructure:** utility, grid, transport, housing, water,
   construction, environmental and local-government records;
5. **state/geopolitical:** procurement, deployment, exercises, diplomacy,
   alliance, conflict, trade and counterpart response;
6. **cultural/interpretive:** identity, trust, belonging, heritage, media,
   community meaning and public judgment.

Do not pool incompatible denominators. Layer the sources to cover different
stages of the same conversion and state clearly when the units are not linked.

## Permanent priority lanes

The program should continuously revisit these lanes because they contain the
largest recurring gaps between formal capacity and usable consequence:

- practical platform exit, deletion, correction and repeat exposure;
- consumer remedy, payment, adequacy and durable recovery;
- administrative legibility, contestability and usable protection;
- time, care, waiting, travel and verification as hidden prices;
- household optionality, substitution and protected social participation;
- AI-mediated judgment, human fallback and institutional trust;
- data centers, grid, water, transport and local permission;
- firm power, worker/consumer distribution and local effects;
- supply-chain dependency, qualified substitution and strategic autonomy;
- cultural legitimacy, authenticity, belonging and institutional voice;
- social infrastructure, reciprocity and civic capacity;
- geopolitical capability, burden, partner response and leverage.

## Intake standard for every new source

Every meaningful source should record:

```text
source URL or artifact | retrieval date | source authority
| unit/population/place/event | time window | denominator/method
| direct observation | interpretation | missing stage
| alternative explanation | counterexample | next test
```

Every promoted packet should have a Markdown interpretation and a CSV ledger
when the unit structure allows it.

## Promotion discipline

### Promote

Promote only when the evidence has a defined unit, time and place, a direct
observation, a meaningful mechanism and a clear limit. Use Tier 1 for bounded
findings, Tier 2 for strong mechanisms with an open later stage, and Tier 3 for
hypotheses or discovery signals.

### Reopen

Reopen a promoted finding whenever a new source changes its denominator,
geography, time window, causal interpretation, counterexample or material
endpoint. Promotion is revisable.

### Do not promote

Do not promote an announcement, policy target, procurement contract, aggregate
settlement value, public comment count, model benchmark, survey attitude or
official legitimacy statement into a lived outcome without the relevant
conversion evidence.

## Periodic maintenance

On each future research turn:

1. inspect the current queue and recent changes;
2. choose the highest-value open arrow, not the most visible headline;
3. search at least one primary/official source and one contrasting source family
   where feasible;
4. update or add the packet and CSV ledger;
5. record what was not found and why;
6. update the synthesis only when the evidence changes the interpretation; and
7. leave the living goal active.

## North-star question

> When formal capacity meets a real person, household, community, institution,
> firm, place or partner, who has to wait, verify, pay, adapt, accept, refuse or
> bear risk—and what evidence shows what happened next?

# Offered versus usable alternatives: a cross-domain control note v1

**Checked:** 2026-09-21  
**Scope:** worker, consumer, platform, public-benefit, care, housing, insurance,
infrastructure, informal social networks, and geopolitical episodes in the broad atlas
episodes in the broad atlas

## The finding

An alternative can be offered without being usable. A job, payment, benefit,
care route, insurance backstop, repair, or substitute may exist formally while
the affected unit cannot reach it, afford it, use it safely, fit it into its
schedule, preserve its status, or rely on it long enough for the underlying
problem to improve.

This is not one causal theory. It is a measurement rule for practical choice.
The atlas should therefore keep these stages separate:

```text
nominal offer
  -> awareness and reachable route
  -> feasible under money, time, transport, health, status, and safety limits
  -> actual use or receipt
  -> adequate result
  -> durable result or credible switching/exit
```

Counting the first stage as choice overstates agency. Counting a reported
payment, approval, or alternative job as recovery makes the same mistake at a
later stage.

## What the retained episodes show

| Domain | Nominal option or response | What remains between offer and usable outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Work | A new job, a return offer, a compensation order, or released back-pay | Equivalent pay, acceptable conditions, household security, and later job durability are not automatic. The Mudiyanselage case records new employment and a compensation order alongside partial payment, forced exit, and broken trust; the Atkins record reports receipt for ten drivers but not adequacy, later security, or practical exit. |
| Consumer redress | A check, reissue route, repair, or correction process | Notice, request effort, delivery, cashing, account correction, adequacy, and continued use can fail separately. The CFPB ledgers distinguish checks reported or cashed in aggregate from person-level receipt and corrected obligation. |
| Public benefits | An issued benefit or formal eligibility route | A stolen EBT balance, missed notice, documentation burden, or delayed replacement can leave the benefit unavailable for the intended purchase. Issuance is not usable food security. |
| Care | Cash, counseling, provider capacity, or a nominal appointment route | Recipient control may coexist with caregiver burden, worker replacement, travel, completed care, continuity, or safety gaps. Coverage and capacity do not prove care was completed and sustained. |
| Housing and insurance | Coverage, a backstop insurer, repair funding, or a relocation path | Deductibles, exclusions, delay, contractor availability, financing, transport, and place attachment can make staying or moving infeasible. A backstop is not the same as restored protection. |
| Platforms and data | A privacy request, image-removal form, status page, or repeat-protection setting | The user may see a route without receiving deletion, protection from copies, safety, or a workable way to leave the affected service. Observability is not completion. |
| Infrastructure and geopolitics | A completed project, alliance commitment, or state capability | Physical operation or formal partnership can exist without equal local benefit, replaceability, public consent, partner trust, or changed external behavior. Capability is not usable or accepted power. |
| Informal social infrastructure | A family network, chosen family, neighbor group, or political home | Closeness or membership can create a route to care, money, transport, information, or civic action without proving that support was delivered, adequate, reciprocal, or sustainable. Belonging is not automatic capacity. |

These are deliberately different cases. Their shared point is that practical
choice is observable only when the same unit can be followed through access,
use, adequacy, and durability.

## Why this matters for the broader atlas

This distinction joins several recurring questions without collapsing them:

- **Culture and meaning:** refusing a route may protect dignity, privacy, or
  status while sacrificing money, care, or future recourse.
- **Consumer power:** a remedy can be formally available while the customer
  remains tied to the same provider or obligation.
- **Work and bargaining:** an alternative job can end dependence on one firm
  without creating equal bargaining power or household security.
- **Public systems:** eligibility and distribution can be visible while the
  person still cannot complete the route or use the benefit.
- **Infrastructure and state capacity:** a project or capability can operate
  while local users lack affordability, replaceability, or meaningful exit.
- **Digital and geopolitical power:** a platform can show a request status, and
  an alliance can retain formal membership, while the affected person or
  partner still lacks effective protection, influence, or a credible alternative.

The correct question is not only “Was another option present?” It is:

> Could this affected unit reach, choose, use, and rely on the option under its
> actual constraints—and what happened afterward?

## Minimum fields for the next depth pass

For each episode, retain the offer and the failed or successful route in the
same denominator:

1. offer or formal eligibility, and who controlled it;
2. notice, awareness, and first attempted access;
3. money, time, travel, health, schedule, status, and safety constraints;
4. acceptance, refusal, abandonment, or non-use reason;
5. actual receipt, use, completion, and immediate result;
6. residual loss, adequacy, continuity, and later durability;
7. switching, exit, trust, meaning, or household consequence only when
   directly observed.

The current portfolio can document many first and middle stages, but it does
not yet justify a general rate of usable alternatives. The next valid advance
is one authorized same-unit follow-up—an account, worker, benefit, care, or
property episode—not another aggregate count of offers or capacity.

**Boundary:** this synthesis is a cross-domain analytic lens. It does not
claim that the cited cases share a cause, population denominator, or outcome.
It makes the missing conversion explicit so that future evidence can close it
without turning formal availability into lived recovery.

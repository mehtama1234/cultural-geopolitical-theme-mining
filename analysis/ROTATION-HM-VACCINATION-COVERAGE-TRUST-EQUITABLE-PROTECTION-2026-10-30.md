# Rotation HM: Vaccination coverage, trust and equitable protection

**Date:** 2026-10-30
**Lane:** public health / culture / geography / trust / conflict / global cooperation
**Status:** Tier 1 bounded distinction plus Tier 2 coverage-to-protection-and-trust question

## Research question

When governments and partners report vaccination coverage, conduct campaigns,
communicate risk and respond to outbreaks, do these efforts convert into
equitable individual protection, trusted routine care and durable local health
capacity—or can national coverage, campaign doses and official confidence
coexist with unvaccinated clusters, access barriers and outbreaks?

## Conversion chain

```text
disease risk and immunity gap
  -> vaccine supply, financing and delivery route
  -> trusted information and informed consent
  -> person receives correct dose on time
  -> series completion and individual protection
  -> outbreak control, routine trust and resilient local system
```

Coverage is an administrative or modeled estimate; confidence is a social
relationship; protection is an individual and population outcome. They must not
be collapsed into one metric.

## Stage audit

| Observed signal | Safe interpretation | Prohibited leap |
|---|---|---|
| National or modeled coverage | Estimated delivery level for a defined antigen and population | Every person is vaccinated on time and protected |
| Campaign doses administered | A campaign reached a reported number of people | Series completion, durable immunity, trust or routine-system strength |
| Vaccine confidence survey or community story | A belief or relationship signal exists | Belief caused uptake or represents every subgroup |
| Outbreak among unvaccinated/unknown-status people | An immunity gap and disease event are associated | One cause explains the gap or all unvaccinated people refused |
| Home visit, mobile clinic or communication campaign | A delivery/engagement route was attempted | People could safely consent, return, complete and obtain remedy |

## Bounded finding

> Vaccination coverage, delivery activity and confidence are necessary but
> distinct stages. Current evidence supports promoting the distinction between
> reported coverage/campaign activity and equitable individual protection with
> durable trust. The mechanism remains Tier 2 until a named cohort links access,
> communication, consent, dose validity, completion, protection, adverse-event
> response and later routine uptake.

## Cultural and geographical interpretation

Trust is produced through repeated encounters with health workers, local leaders,
schools, faith communities, conflict actors and public institutions. Language,
religion, gender, disability, migration status, historical coercion and prior
adverse experiences shape whether a campaign is heard as care, surveillance,
negotiation or threat. A home visit can improve access in one place and feel
unsafe in another.

Geography determines whether supply becomes protection: distance, transport,
cold chain, insecurity, clinic hours, documentation and household work all
matter. National averages can improve while remote districts, displaced people
or dense urban clusters remain vulnerable. Conversely, a low national average
can conceal a successful local route, as the Viet Nam and Syria cases suggest.

## Counterexamples and limits

- High coverage does not eliminate outbreaks if coverage is clustered unevenly,
  immunity wanes or surveillance changes.
- Low coverage does not establish refusal; stock-outs, distance, conflict,
  documentation and missed follow-up may dominate.
- Campaign reach is not the same as valid dose, completion or protection.
- Vaccine confidence narratives are important qualitative evidence but are not
  causal estimates of uptake or population trust.
- WHO/UNICEF estimates have stated methodological uncertainty and should not be
  treated as exact counts for every locality.

## Promotion decision

Promote the coverage/campaign/confidence-versus-equitable-protection distinction
to Tier 1. Keep the vaccination-to-protection-and-durable-trust mechanism in
Tier 2.

# Broad trend and subtheme discovery queue v1

**Purpose:** move the program from the L&T-specific application back to its
broader cultural, social, consumer, institutional, firm, infrastructure and
geopolitical objective. This is a prioritized discovery queue, not a new set of
validated findings.

**Operating rule:** start from an open conversion in the existing atlas, find a
distinct source/unit/geography, record the observation and limit, then decide
whether it deserves a new theme, a subtheme, a counterexample, or only a gap
record. Do not promote a new heading because a source is topical or dramatic.

## What the existing corpus already says

The current program has substantial breadth across 14 themes, but its recurring
weakness is not a shortage of headlines. It is a missing middle between formal
capacity and lived or strategic consequence:

```text
condition or formal capacity
  -> encounter / decision / use
  -> cost, alternative, response or refusal
  -> protected or sacrificed outcome
  -> meaning, trust, action, cooperation, switching or exit
```

The [low-density conversion map](US-BROAD-LOW-DENSITY-CONVERSION-MAP_V1.md)
identifies the thinnest current lanes as platforms/data/attention,
infrastructure/technology/dependency, and geopolitical/state consequences. The
[source map](US-BROAD-DATA-SOURCE-MAP_V1.md) shows that the program can search
surveys, administrative records, firm/platform records, infrastructure records
and state/geopolitical records, but must not treat them as a common denominator.
The [current theme pulse](US-BROAD-CURRENT-THEME-PULSE_V1.md) supplies the
existing cross-domain signals and counterexamples.

## Priority queue

Priority is based on: open-stage value, distinct unit or geography, source
reproducibility, counterexample potential, and cultural/geopolitical meaning.
`P1` means it should receive the next research rotation; `P2` is the next wave;
`P3` is a watchlist or synthesis lane.

| Priority | Theme / subtheme to hunt | Open conversion | Best next source mix | What would count as new evidence | Falsifier / boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | **Practical platform exit** — deletion, correction, safety report, continued use, switching | Formal control request → actual correction/removal → repeat exposure or continued use → exit/non-use | Platform transparency or regulator record + account/case-level follow-up + user discussion | A dated user/account episode with request, response, recurrence, alternative, reason for continued use or exit | A policy page or request count alone does not show completion, safety or wellbeing |
| P1 | **Infrastructure incidence and local permission** — power, data centres, transmission, water, transport | Named facility/project operation → bill/service/local burden → contestation or compensation → durable acceptance or refusal | Operator/grid record + utility/rate/public-comment record + locality/household or customer measure | Same project or account connects operation to payment/service, affected place and later acceptance or opposition | Capacity, investment, tax machinery or public hearing alone is not realized benefit or consent |
| P1 | **Geopolitical capability-to-leverage** — procurement, fielding, operation, external response | Accepted capability/state action → attributed ally/adversary/partner response → cooperation, refusal, adaptation or leverage | Official procurement/acceptance + operations/incident record + counterpart response + public judgment | One dated capability/event ledger shows operation and a sourced external response with maintenance or reversal | Contract, exercise, delivery or official legitimacy language alone is not deterrence |
| P1 | **Consumer recourse and usable remedy** — refunds, safety, privacy, debt, service failure | Complaint/violation → route effort → remedy offered → receipt/adequacy → repeat exposure, switching or exit | Regulator/court record + administrator distribution record + recipient or account evidence | A linked case shows remedy receipt, residual loss/adequacy and later behavior | Aggregate checks sent, claims closed or settlement value do not prove individual recovery |

## Cross-theme trend candidates

These are useful hypotheses to test across the queue. They should remain
cross-source interpretations until the next rotation adds distinct evidence.

### 1. Unequal optionality

People and institutions may face the same nominal choice while differing in
cash, time, information, safety, mobility, data control or credible exit. Test
this by pairing a formal offer with the actual cost of using it and the quality
of the alternative.

### 2. Formal capacity versus usable protection

Rules, infrastructure, benefits, remedies and alliances can be visible before
they become usable to a dependent actor. Test the gap with a dated encounter,
receipt, adequacy and later outcome—not another announcement.

### 3. Correction as a legitimacy technology

Trust may depend less on whether an institution is perfect than on whether an
error can be seen, contested, corrected and exited. Test this across platforms,
public benefits, consumer remedies, workplaces and geopolitical commitments.

### 4. Time and attention as redistributed infrastructure

Digital systems, care systems, payment routes and administrative processes can
shift hidden labor onto households, workers or local institutions. Test who
waits, verifies, travels, calls, documents and absorbs the failed attempt.

### 5. Operation before consequence

An asset, capability, policy or AI system can operate successfully while its
effect on bills, safety, households, public consent, workers or external actors
remains uncertain. Keep operational output and consequence as separate stages.

## Next three research rotations

### Rotation A — P1 missing middle

Select one case from each thin lane: a real platform-control attempt, one
named infrastructure/account incidence route, and one accepted geopolitical
capability with a sourced counterpart response. Prefer records that preserve
dates, identifiers and later states. Stop if the next source only repeats a
policy, announcement or aggregate endpoint.

### Rotation B — cultural and household conversion

Pair time/care, household optionality, cultural legitimacy and administrative
legibility. Seek a new population split or geography and at least one actual
alternative, refusal, appeal, or participation outcome. Keep survey attitudes,
discussion, administrative processing and observed behavior as separate layers.

### Rotation C — firm, technology and dependency

Pair AI-mediated judgment, firm power, supply-chain dependency and social
infrastructure. Seek a named firm, worker/customer/place or system, then test
who receives the gain, who bears the risk, and whether a credible substitute
exists.

## Source-selection scorecard

Score each candidate from 0–3:

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Open-stage value | repeats endpoint | weak middle | one missing stage | closes a high-value arrow |
| Unit distinctness | same aggregate | similar population | new split/place | named case/person/account/event |
| Source authority | commentary only | secondary summary | official/peer-reviewed | primary plus independent check |
| Reproducibility | inaccessible | partial metadata | retrievable source | date, denominator, method and artifact |
| Counterexample potential | none | implied | plausible comparison | built-in comparison or falsifier |
| Meaning/power relevance | descriptive only | local implication | cultural/institutional implication | clear distribution, legitimacy or leverage consequence |

Prioritize candidates scoring 14 or more. A high score does not make the
finding true; it only makes the source worth examining. Record rejected
candidates and the reason they were not promoted.

## Required output for every new discovery

1. source record with retrieval date, unit, geography, method and provenance;
2. direct observation and study result, kept separate;
3. mechanism and distribution: who gains, loses, waits, pays or can exit;
4. theme/subtheme assignment and why it is not a duplicate;
5. counterexample, uncertainty and what the source cannot observe;
6. next evidence needed to move the conversion forward; and
7. promotion decision: new finding, existing subtheme, counterexample, gap or
   reject.

The queue is intentionally broader than any one company or sector. It is the
research control layer for finding defensible cultural and geopolitical trends
before they become obvious, while preserving the distinction between signal,
mechanism, consequence and interpretation.

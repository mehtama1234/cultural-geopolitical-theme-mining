# Counter-signals

The independent lane must preserve evidence that makes a candidate weaker.

- Selective platform exit may be a small digital-minimalism subculture rather
  than a broad social change.
- AI companion continuity concerns may describe product enthusiasts rather
  than ordinary chatbot users.
- AI-layoff discussion may repeat company explanations or dramatic headlines;
  it does not establish that AI caused the cuts.
- A repeated phrase can reflect search ranking, an influencer, or coordinated
  attention instead of a durable cultural trend.

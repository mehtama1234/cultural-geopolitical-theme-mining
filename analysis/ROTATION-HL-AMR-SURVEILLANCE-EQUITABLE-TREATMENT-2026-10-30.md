# Rotation HL: Antimicrobial resistance, surveillance and equitable treatment

**Date:** 2026-10-30
**Lane:** health security / geopolitics / public health / local capability / One Health
**Status:** Tier 1 bounded distinction plus Tier 2 resistance-to-effective-treatment question

## Research question

When governments and health systems build antimicrobial-resistance surveillance,
stewardship, diagnostics and supply-chain policies, do those signals convert
into timely effective treatment and equitable local health security—or can
better measurement coexist with missing medicines, weak diagnostics, delayed
care and unequal exposure?

## Conversion chain

```text
infection and antimicrobial-use signal
  -> laboratory surveillance and resistance interpretation
  -> diagnosis, prevention, stewardship and supply security
  -> clinician selects effective affordable treatment
  -> patient receives and completes care
  -> survival, recovery, trust and durable local health capability
```

AMR is a One Health and geopolitical problem: human, animal, plant and
environmental pathways interact with manufacturing, procurement, conflict,
trade and laboratory capacity. A resistance estimate or national action plan is
not the same thing as a patient receiving the right medicine.

## Evidence packet

| ID | Source and direct observation | Stage closed | Stage still open |

## Stage audit

| Observed signal | Safe interpretation | Prohibited leap |
|---|---|---|
| Resistance prevalence or GLASS submission | A measured pathogen/antibiotic signal exists in a defined reporting system | All infections in the place are resistant or patients received effective care |
| National action plan or global target | Governance and intended direction exist | Financing, implementation, accountability or health improvement occurred |
| Laboratory training/data warehouse | Surveillance capability improved at named sites | Rural patients, clinicians, pharmacies and supply chains can act on the data |
| Stewardship or AWaRe guidance | A prescribing framework exists | Antibiotics are available, affordable, correctly diagnosed or accepted by patients |
| Susceptibility-guided recommendation | Treatment should be tailored to the observed resistance profile | The recommended drug was stocked, affordable, safe and received in time |

## Bounded finding

> AMR surveillance, stewardship and One Health governance are necessary health
> security infrastructure, but current evidence supports promotion only of the
> distinction between measurement/policy capacity and effective equitable
> treatment. The mechanism remains Tier 2 until a named patient or cohort links
> specimen, diagnosis, susceptibility, stock, price, prescription, receipt,
> completion and recovery.

## Cultural and geographical interpretation

Antibiotic use and access are shaped by trust, language, local healing systems,
pharmacy practice, clinician authority, household money and the ability to
return for follow-up. A stewardship message can be scientifically correct but
fail if diagnosis is unavailable, the medicine is unaffordable, symptoms are
urgent, or people fear losing access to care.

Geography determines whether a surveillance signal becomes a treatment action.
Referral hospitals may generate data while peripheral clinics lack cultures,
stock or transport. Conflict, displacement, informal markets and cross-border
manufacturing can make national averages poor guides to actual patient risk.
Improving measurement can reveal inequality rather than eliminate it.

## Counterexamples and limits

- More surveillance records can reflect better reporting, not rising or falling
  resistance and not better patient outcomes.
- A susceptibility recommendation does not prove that the medicine was stocked,
  affordable, safe or completed.
- The CDC outbreak guidance is a named clinical boundary, not a population
  estimate of all XDR Salmonella or a causal evaluation of outcomes.
- Stewardship can reduce inappropriate use while access-constrained patients
  still lack timely effective treatment; access and preservation must be audited
  together.
- Global targets and country plans do not establish implementation or causal
  health improvement without local outcome records.

## Promotion decision

Promote the surveillance/policy-capacity-versus-effective-treatment distinction
to Tier 1. Keep the AMR-signal-to-equitable-treatment-and-local-health-security
mechanism in Tier 2.

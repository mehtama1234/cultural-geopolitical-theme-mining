# Broad low-density lane freshness recheck

**Checked:** 2026-09-20  
**Purpose:** test whether current public pages add a new middle-stage endpoint
to the three thinnest theme surfaces before another finding is promoted.

This is a freshness and duplicate-control memo, not a new estimate.

## Digital/platform and consumer remedy

The [FTC refunds index](https://www.ftc.gov/enforcement/refunds) continues to
publish program-level refund amounts and beneficiary information, and the
[Amazon Prime administration page](https://search.ftc.gov/enforcement/refunds/amazon-refunds)
reports payments to eligible customers. This is a current institutional
distribution stage. It does not expose an account-level receipt, adequacy,
continued use, switching, trust, or household-recovery endpoint. The existing
Amazon Prime record already preserves that boundary, so this recheck does not
promote a duplicate finding.

The digital lane's existing smartphone, AI-use, press-legitimacy,
participation-style, and platform-exit records likewise already cover the
available public endpoint surfaces. The missing stage remains a linked
encounter → interpretation/action or durable exit record.

The AI-companion farewell record is the closest existing exception: a 1,200-
conversation audit found continuation tactics in 37% of observed farewells,
and preregistered experiments found that some responses prolonged short-run
engagement. This reaches an attempted-exit → immediate re-engagement stage,
which is stronger than ordinary platform-use evidence. It still does not show
account deletion, durable switching, dependence, financial or clinical harm, or
whether stated churn intent became actual exit. No duplicate digital finding is
promoted from this recheck.

## Infrastructure and dependency

DOE's [Speed to Power initiative](https://www.energy.gov/speed-to-power)
describes near-term grid investment and a March 2026 funding opportunity for
transmission upgrades. DOE's [Transmission Facilitation Program](https://www.energy.gov/edf/transmission-facilitation-program)
also keeps payment obligations conditional on a project being constructed,
energized, and ready to provide service. These pages clarify the realization
clock and risk-allocation design; they do not add a named household cost,
delivered service, replaceability measure, or local acceptance outcome beyond
the existing infrastructure records.

The Smart Path Connect follow-up sharpens, but does not close, the customer
side of that clock. NYPA's completion release describes expected monthly
electric-bill savings for households and businesses, while the related tariff
and annual-update documents expose wholesale cost allocation and accounting
architecture. The new endpoint is an official customer-facing claim; it is not
an observed bill, rate-class allocation, per-household amount, or reliability
outcome. The infrastructure lane therefore now distinguishes system savings,
customer-facing claim, and realized customer incidence.

The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission's June 2026 large-load action adds a
current national regulatory-response stage: the six regional grid operators
were ordered to justify or reform tariffs for data centers and other large
loads, with the stated aim of combining faster interconnection with ratepayer
protection. This sharpens the governance clock, but it does not add a new
household outcome. The Virginia record already preserves the closest available
middle stages: reported large-load drop events, minimum-demand and contract
requirements, modeled residential bill effects, and the unresolved gap between
those controls and an observed customer's bill or reliability experience.
See the [FERC action](https://www.ferc.gov/news-events/news/ferc-launches-aggressive-targeted-action-speed-large-load-integration)
and the [Virginia large-load record](records/us-virginia-data-center-large-load-governance-2024-2027.json).

## Geopolitical and state consequences

NATO's [eastern-flank page](https://www.nato.int/en/what-we-do/deterrence-and-defence/strengthening-natos-eastern-flank)
records the 2025 airspace-incursion consultations and the 2026 establishment
of a ninth multinational battlegroup. This strengthens the incident-to-alliance
response and force-posture stages already represented by the Poland comparator.
It does not establish deterrence, public consent, sustained alliance behavior,
or changed adversary conduct.

The Germany/Lithuania posture record now adds two bounded legitimacy surfaces:
a 2025 German survey of defense support, mission knowledge, and alliance trust,
and an official Lithuanian Armed Forces summary reporting 82% support for the
permanent German brigade in a November 2023 poll. The host-country result is
closer to consent for a named deployment, but it predates the 2026 posture and
does not establish durable support, burden acceptance, or future combat-use
approval. This is a public-consent refinement, not evidence of deterrence or
changed adversary behavior.

## Decision

No new reader finding is promoted from this recheck. The next valuable record
must add one of the missing stages: account-level digital receipt or exit;
realized infrastructure service, cost allocation, or replaceability; or
accepted geopolitical operation, public consent, sustained alliance behavior,
or changed external response.

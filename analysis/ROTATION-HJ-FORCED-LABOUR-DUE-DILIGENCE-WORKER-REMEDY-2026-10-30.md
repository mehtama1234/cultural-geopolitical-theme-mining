# Rotation HJ: Forced-labour due diligence, trade control and worker remedy

**Date:** 2026-10-30
**Lane:** labour / supply chains / trade / geopolitics / migration / worker agency
**Status:** Tier 1 bounded distinction plus Tier 2 worker-remedy-and-non-recurrence question

## Research question

When a company, regulator or buyer identifies forced-labour risk in a supply
chain, do due diligence, import controls, corrective action and remediation
actually reach affected workers and reduce recurrence—or do they mainly produce
reports, supplier exits, shipment holds and market-access decisions?

## Conversion chain

```text
worker/supply-chain risk signal
  -> risk identification and worker-informed investigation
  -> prevention, leverage or trade control
  -> safe disclosure, remedy and changed commercial practice
  -> worker receives restitution, freedom, safety and agency
  -> non-recurrence, livelihood continuity and accountable supply chain
```

Forced labour is not only a compliance label. The relevant unit is the worker,
recruitment route, workplace, commodity, buyer, border action, remedy channel,
geography and time. A supply-chain statement can be materially true while still
leaving the affected person without restitution, safe exit, wages, documents,
voice or protection from retaliation.

## Stage audit

| Observed signal | Safe interpretation | Prohibited leap |
|---|---|---|
| Supplier code, audit or risk assessment | A formal control or investigation exists | Workers are safe, free, paid or empowered |
| WRO, seizure or import release | Market access has been restricted or restored under a legal process | Affected workers received remedy or conditions improved |
| Remediation payment reported | A named financial remedy action occurred | Every affected worker received the right amount safely and without retaliation |
| Grievance mechanism or referral route | An institutional route has been created | Workers know it, trust it, can use it privately and obtain a timely resolution |
| Due-diligence disclosure | Some process or outcome has been reported | The undisclosed tiers, informal work, migrants or subcontractors have no risk |

## Bounded finding

> Forced-labour due diligence and trade controls can create leverage for worker
> protection, but the public evidence supports promotion only of the distinction
> between risk/control activity and worker-level remedy. The mechanism remains
> Tier 2 until a named cohort links recruitment or coercion, safe disclosure,
> remedy receipt, continued livelihood, worker agency and repeat-failure data.

## Cultural and geographical interpretation

Recruitment debt, withheld documents, language, migration status, gender,
ethnicity, caste, informal work and dependence on a broker shape whether a
worker can disclose harm or leave. A grievance process designed in a buyer's
language or headquarters may be formally available but practically unusable at a
remote mine, vessel, farm, factory dormitory or subcontractor.

Geopolitical controls also redistribute risk. A border hold can protect a
consumer market while interrupting orders, wages or local livelihoods; a buyer
exit can reduce leverage over a supplier or push work into less visible tiers.
The direction of effect depends on remedy funding, worker voice, alternative
income, local institutions and whether commercial purchasing practices change.

## Counterexamples and limits

- A WRO is based on a legal evidentiary threshold and does not itself prove a
  particular worker's later remedy.
- Top Glove is a positive named remediation case, but its public record does not
  close independent worker-level non-retaliation or long-run recurrence.
- ILO and OECD statistics are population or company-level indicators, not
  prevalence estimates for every supplier or commodity.
- Audits can miss informal, migrant, subcontracted or coerced workers; absence
  of a public finding is not evidence of absence.
- Regulatory frameworks change across jurisdictions; this packet is evidence
  analysis, not legal advice.

## Promotion decision

Promote the control-versus-worker-remedy distinction to Tier 1. Keep the
forced-labour-to-safe-recovery-and-non-recurrence mechanism in Tier 2.

# Geopolitical external-response acquisition boundary

**Checked:** 2026-09-22  
**Status:** verified scan; no new external-effect finding promoted

## Question

Does the current US-centered geopolitical evidence contain a named capability or
operation that can be followed from implementation to an attributed change in
another actor's behavior?

## What the current atlas can observe

- [DOD international arrangements](records/us-gao-international-defense-arrangements-2021-2025.json): agreement portfolio, review, staffing, and coordination friction.
- [NATO ISR operations](records/us-nato-nisrf-agile-combat-employment-2026.json): repeated operating activity and host-nation support.
- [NATO/Maven integration](records/us-nato-maven-operational-integration-2026.json): technical authorization and exercise integration.
- [JEF Baltic operation](records/us-jef-lion-protector-baltic-express-2026.json): cross-border operational rehearsal.
- [NATO airspace response](records/us-nato-airspace-incidents-response-followup-2026.json): reported alliance response to repeated incidents.
- [CRS retaliation record](records/us-crs-tariff-retaliation-foreign-response-2025.json): foreign government countermeasures, negotiation, and partial reversals.

Together these sources cover agreement, coordination, operation, alliance
response, and government counterresponse. They do not form one episode and do
not establish a common deterrence or leverage rate.

## What remains missing

No currently retained record cleanly joins all of the following for the same
named episode:

```text
capability or pressure
  -> implementation or operation
  -> affected external actor's attributed response
  -> concession, refusal, deterrence, adaptation, or changed policy
  -> persistence or reversal of that response
```

Official claims of readiness, solidarity, or strategic intent are not enough.
The missing observation must identify the external actor, date the response,
show why the response is attributed to the U.S. capability or pressure, and
follow whether it persisted.

## Decision

Do not promote another defense announcement, exercise, contract, agreement,
alliance-poll result, or posture statement as geopolitical leverage. Reopen this
lane only when a named implementation or operation provides an attributed
external response, or when a lawful identifier-bearing source supplies the
missing follow-up. Until then, preserve the existing records as distinct
capability, legitimacy, coordination, and response stages.

# Broad connection-graph audit — 2026-09-21

This is a control note for the broad U.S.-anchored cultural, social, consumer,
political, institutional, firm, labor, infrastructure, and geopolitical atlas.
It is not a new theme list and it does not claim that the map is causal.

## What the graph currently says

The manifest has **199 declared connections**, **93 reading paths**, and now
**93 registered atlas topics** across the 14-theme program. The research counts,
source provenance, event ledgers, public pages, and handoff controls remain
synchronized.

The graph audit found that several important bridge concepts were used by
existing connections but were not registered in the atlas' topic catalog. The
affected conceptual IDs were:

| Bridge concept | Existing edge references | Why it matters |
|---|---:|---|
| `consumer_power_recourse` | 4 | Whether a person can get correction, money, care, or an answer after a loss |
| `infrastructure_technology_dependency` | 4 | Whether technical capacity becomes usable shared infrastructure |
| `public_systems_feedback` | 4 | Whether complaints, data, payments, and rules become institutional correction |
| `geopolitical_state_consequences` | 4 | Whether domestic capacity changes state leverage or external behavior |
| `platform_remedy_redress` | 2 | Whether platform harm becomes a remedy, not only a firm announcement |
| `work_control_bargaining` | 2 | Whether new tools change worker control, voice, or bargaining |
| `trust_identity_meaning` | 2 | How the same material condition becomes trust, stigma, obligation, or exit |
| `care_health_reproduction` | 1 | How care access and reproductive/health burdens reproduce social capacity |
| `firm_sector_market_power` | 1 | Whether public or technical support changes firm power and market structure |

These are not nine new themes. They are bridge topics that connect the existing
themes to the end-to-end chain:

> condition or capability → exposure → money/time/access/status/control →
> adaptation → institutional or firm response → usable result → durability →
> meaning, trust, action, switching, or exit → larger capacity or power.

## Repair completed

All nine bridge concepts are now registered as first-class atlas topics, each
with a source packet, bounded evidence check, counterpoint, and missing-middle
limit. The atlas builder now resolves every declared edge and reading-path
topic. The repaired graph validates at **93 topics, 5 navigation umbrellas,
199 connections, and 93 reading paths**.
The route audit also finds no registered topic with zero reading paths; the two
previously isolated bridge topics now have explicit end-to-end routes.

## What this changes in the work

The next useful pass should not chase another isolated example. It should do
three things:

1. Use the repaired graph to select the next evidence passes, prioritizing
   routes that join ordinary lived experience to institutional or geopolitical
   consequence.
2. Add same-case or same-unit evidence where possible, especially from loss or
   denied access through submission, screening, remedy receipt, recovery,
   trust, and exit.
3. Keep the nine bridge topics as routing controls rather than inflating them
   into additional top-level themes.

The most promising first repair is **consumer power/recourse ↔ public-system
feedback ↔ platform remedy/redress**. The current evidence already spans CFPB
complaint routing, FTC platform remedies, consumer loss and refund friction, and
care appeals. What is still missing is the same-case route from a person's
loss or denied access through submission, screening, remedy receipt, recovery,
trust, and exit.

## Boundary

The node repair is a map-integrity improvement, not evidence that the underlying
claims are causal. The atlas remains a living discovery map with bounded
comparisons—not a complete causal network.

# US cross-source trend synthesis v1

**Checked:** 2026-09-15  
**Status:** living cross-source synthesis; no single causal chain claimed  
**Scope:** the current machine-readable trend/case registry and its linked
analysis layers

## Read this synthesis first

This atlas is currently strongest at showing how pressures and capabilities
are distributed, how people and institutions adapt, and where the next link in
the chain is still unmeasured. It is not yet a single model that explains
political or geopolitical behavior from household conditions. The useful
reader question is therefore: **which stage is directly observed, which stage
is compared across sources, and which stage remains an open test?**

| If you want to understand | Start here | The current safe conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| How pressure reaches daily life | [Material room and household findings](projects/us-household-financial-pressure/findings/us-household-financial-pressure-013.md) and [energy-room findings](projects/us-household-calendar-integration/findings/us-household-calendar-integration-034.md) | Income timing, assistance, prices, energy spending, disconnection, repair risk, and adaptation are connected questions, not one stress score. |
| How time and care enter the story | [The household calendar integration program](US-HOUSEHOLD-CALENDAR-INTEGRATION_V1.md) | Work, care, travel, health, and recovery run on different clocks; the same-family trigger-to-outcome chain is still the key acquisition target. |
| How institutions shape practical options | [The public-system and consumer route findings](projects/us-safety-net-access/findings/us-safety-net-access-014.md) and [CFPB route findings](projects/us-customer-automation-recourse/findings/us-customer-automation-recourse-021.md) | A complaint, notice, or benefit receipt is an institutional route, not by itself a remedy, recovered security, trust change, or exit. |
| How local economies and infrastructure matter | [The firm/place finding](projects/us-local-business-place/findings/us-local-business-place-011.md) and [infrastructure/state-capacity bridge](projects/ai-work-control/domestic-capacity-dependence-state-leverage-cross-source-bridge-v1.md) | Applications, openings, jobs, capacity, revenue, ownership, and replaceability are separate stages; entry or investment is not automatically broad prosperity or leverage. |
| How meaning, politics, and geopolitics enter | [The cost/trust/politics program](projects/us-cost-trust-politics/README.md) and [comparative capability context](projects/ai-work-control/findings/ai-work-control-073.md) | Surveys and international comparisons identify meaning, confidence, capability, and context; they do not by themselves prove attribution, collective action, or external behavior change. |

The rest of this page moves in four passes: the joint proposition, the
evidence by arrow, the segments that are genuinely end-to-end, and the next
research cycle. Each section keeps a direct observation beside its limitation
and next test so that a compelling theme does not silently become a causal
claim.

This is the current end-to-end reading of the atlas. It is deliberately a
bridge across different units, not a merge of respondents, households,
complaints, workers, places, or countries. The [machine-readable trend
registry](us-trend-observations.md) is the source index; each record's boundary
controls how far its evidence can travel.

The [automatic-saving multi-currency bridge](findings/us-automatic-saving-multi-currency-policy-bridge-001.md)
adds a policy-to-household sequence across NBER/CRI Auto-IRA studies, Federal
Reserve SHED liquidity and credit, and BEA aggregate income and spending. It
does not pool the estimates: the policy studies use different treatment designs
and balance-sheet definitions, while the Federal Reserve and BEA layers are
context rather than treatment outcomes. The surviving claim is that automatic
saving can move current liquidity, future assets, and retirement timing on
different clocks; the net-security and meaning/action arrows remain open.

The [SIPP utility-credit-savings finding](projects/us-household-financial-pressure/findings/us-household-financial-pressure-006.md)
now adds a sharper household-room bridge inside the material and institutional
route layers. In the same person-record/month diagnostic, reported utility
payment difficulty co-occurs with more carried credit balances and less savings
account ownership, while higher-resource respondents within the difficulty
group are more likely to carry balances—an access counterexample, not evidence
that they face less hardship. The separate [following-month utility-to-work
finding](projects/us-household-calendar-integration/findings/us-household-calendar-integration-028.md)
places utility difficulty or energy-assistance status beside next-month earnings
and hours. These are adjacent descriptive stages, not a bill-to-credit-to-work
causal estimate.

The [directional SIPP work/resource finding](projects/us-household-calendar-integration/findings/us-household-calendar-integration-030.md)
sharpens the work/time bridge: earnings changes can rise or fall, while hours
are unchanged for most adjacent-month pairs. Work-limitation and resource
position condition the direction distribution, but no bill, health event,
employer decision, care substitution, or later meaning/action is observed.

The new [ATUS work-location finding](projects/us-household-calendar-integration/findings/us-household-calendar-integration-029.md)
adds a published 2025 work-location surface to the time architecture. Home and
workplace indicators overlap, and their sex and education gradients are visible,
but location does not identify schedule control, total workload, care conflict,
or worker voice. The federal-shutdown coverage warning in the 2025 release is
retained as a measurement caveat.

The new [SIPP tenure/resource finding](projects/us-household-calendar-integration/findings/us-household-calendar-integration-031.md)
adds a replicate-weighted intersection to the household-room surface. Renters
report more housing and utility-payment difficulty than owners within each
displayed resource band, including at the highest band, while the food-hardship
measure follows a different pattern and has its own universe. This sharpens the
alternative-space question—resources do not erase tenure-linked exposure—but
does not identify rent, local price, repair, insurance, debt, or a dated bill as
the mechanism.

The new [household-room layer](projects/us-household-calendar-integration/findings/us-household-calendar-integration-032.md)
puts that SIPP comparison beside the Federal Reserve's emergency-liquidity and
linked-credit measures. It separates resource position, tenure, hypothetical
emergency capacity, and realized card-balance exposure rather than treating
them as one security index. The combined reading is that household room is
about available alternatives when a dated cost arrives; the same-person event,
time/care substitution, protected outcome, and later recovery are still open.

The new [multi-clock material/time/care synthesis](projects/us-household-calendar-integration/findings/us-household-calendar-integration-033.md)
connects that open middle to the longitudinal evidence already acquired. SIPP
shows monthly resource and work transitions for the same people; MEPS follows
health spending, coverage, employment, wages, and perceived-health change in a
panel; SHED and ATUS add emergency routes, care, and time allocation. The
combined evidence supports distinct material, time, health, and work clocks,
not a pooled household stress score. A complete dated trigger → alternative →
protected/sacrificed outcome → recovery → meaning/action chain remains the
program's acquisition target.

The new [MEPS expenditure-conditioned finding](projects/us-health-cost-household-choice/findings/us-health-cost-household-choice-002.md)
deepens the health-cost/work bridge without turning spending into a burden
score. Baseline 2022 expenditure bands have different perceived-health and
employment-continuity profiles, but the differences remain confounded by need,
age, insurance, retirement, and service mix. The correct next question is not
whether high spending “caused” work loss; it is which treatment, coverage,
out-of-pocket, unpaid-care, and baseline-employment paths explain the distinct
outcomes.

The new [public-system route-to-judgment synthesis](projects/us-safety-net-access/findings/us-safety-net-access-014.md)
extends the safety-net chain through institutional meaning without pooling
incompatible respondents. USDA/FNA measures administrative timeliness, WBNS
measures recipient route barriers, SIPP measures following material hardship,
and Gallup measures institution-specific confidence. Together they support a
layered route/buffer/judgment architecture; they do not show that one notice
caused one hardship or changed one person's trust or political action.

The new [CFPB route-vintage audit](projects/us-customer-automation-recourse/findings/us-customer-automation-recourse-021.md)
adds a measurement-integrity result to the consumer-power lane. In live
2024/2025 product samples, `timely` does not substitute for elapsed routing
time, and the returned `has_narrative` field is unavailable in the re-query.
The public API therefore supplies a route/schema diagnostic, not a remedy or
consumer-welfare trend; omitted fields remain missing rather than zero.

The new [USAspending supplier-concentration finding](projects/ai-work-control/findings/ai-work-control-072.md)
adds a quantitative replaceability check to the geopolitical/state-capacity
lane. One JASSM/LRASM subaward extract has 51 reported recipient names, but
the top five receive 49.50% of returned amount and the top ten 74.60%. The
result keeps component diversity, financial concentration, production,
replaceability, and external leverage as separate stages.

The [entity-parent-facility reconciliation](projects/ai-work-control/findings/ai-work-control-076.md)
adds an official identity layer to that supplier test. DLA CAGE records make
the apparent BAE route legible from the Merrimack recipient to BAE Systems,
Inc. and BAE Systems plc, and provide a parallel General Dynamics-OTS to
General Dynamics Corp route. This improves the jurisdiction and facility
denominator without resolving the central capability question: parentage is
not facility-level assignment, production, acceptance, delivery,
replaceability, or external leverage. The next evidence must join the exact
identifiers to procurement and performance records.

The follow-up [recipient-identifier ambiguity audit](projects/ai-work-control/findings/ai-work-control-077.md)
tests that missing join directly. A name search returns 17 BAE recipient rows
across 10 UEIs and three General Dynamics-OTS rows, while the retained
JASSM/LRASM subaward response exposes no UEI or CAGE. One General Dynamics UEI
overlaps the selected DLA facility set, but that intersection does not assign
the $80.55 million subaward to a plant. The evidence therefore strengthens
the measurement boundary: legal identity, facility identity, award action,
and production location remain separate until an identifier-bearing performance
record appears.

The richer [subaward-to-UEI and location finding](projects/ai-work-control/findings/ai-work-control-078.md)
then closes an important measurement step. The official award-search route
returns UEI, recipient-location, and primary-performance-place fields for all
74 rows, including exact identifiers for the selected BAE and General
Dynamics-OTS actions. The locations are Nashua and Niceville, not automatically
the previously selected DLA CAGE facilities. This turns the next question into
a real CAGE-to-site and production/acceptance/delivery join rather than a name
search, while preserving the operational and geopolitical arrows as open.

The [facility-capability context finding](projects/ai-work-control/findings/ai-work-control-079.md)
adds a meaningful middle layer. Official BAE quality material places military
electronics design/production/servicing scope at the Nashua address, and
official General Dynamics-OTS material names Niceville Operations and
advanced-warhead capability. The sources make those locations plausible
industrial sites without proving that the selected subaward work occurred
there. Production, acceptance, delivery, alternatives, and external response
remain separate gates.

The [realization-stage milestone finding](projects/ai-work-control/findings/ai-work-control-080.md)
adds dated movement beyond procurement intent: a DoD JASSM/LRASM
production-support IDIQ, Lockheed Martin capacity investment/ramp-up evidence,
and a completed first phase of LRASM F-35C flight-science testing. This
strengthens the production-support and integration stages while preserving the
missing accepted quantity, delivery, fielding, maintenance, replaceability,
and external-response links.

The [Poland JASSM-ER delivery non-observation](projects/ai-work-control/findings/ai-work-control-081.md)
keeps the foreign customer stage honest. The May 2024 agreement's 2026–2030
window and the later 2028–2030 schedule reference conflict without a verified
amendment, and the reviewed official source set still lacks a delivery,
acceptance, fielding, training, or inventory event for the order. The absence
is bounded to public-source review; it is not evidence that no delivery has
occurred.

The [historical Polish audit-control finding](projects/ai-work-control/findings/ai-work-control-082.md)
adds an institutional-observability layer: NIK found that a JASSM-ER
production-cost settlement relied on milestone/process material without a
numeric value tied to the settlement. This is evidence about documentation and
control of realization, not a missile quantity or delivery estimate. The
separate 32-unit comparator is retained as a general Polish defence-contract
example and is not attributed to JASSM.

The [PSID 2023 field-audit finding](projects/us-household-calendar-integration/findings/us-household-calendar-integration-036.md)
closes a different acquisition gap. Official documentation confirms a large
panel family file with work, unpaid labor, care, perceived time pressure,
health, income, wealth, insurance, and utility fields. The fields make a
longitudinal material/time/care test feasible, but they do not yet supply a
dated event, attribution, recovery, trust, or political-action result.

The [FMS timing-asynchrony finding](projects/ai-work-control/findings/ai-work-control-083.md)
shows why the realization arrow needs multiple clocks. NIK records four F-35
deliveries in December 2024 and January 2025 while the related settlement forms
arrived in 2025, and states that FMS delivery schedules are estimates. The
comparator improves the measurement design but does not establish a JASSM-ER
delivery or operational capability.

The [historical GSS finding](projects/us-cost-trust-politics/findings/us-cost-trust-politics-024.md)
adds a long-run meaning countercheck: financial dissatisfaction is associated
with lower trust and fairness expectations in each selected anchor year, but
the gap and overall trust level change across decades. This supports a
relational meaning route without turning financial position into a stable causal
explanation of social trust or political action.

The [2024 CFPB case-route sample](projects/us-customer-automation-recourse/cfpb-case-route-sample-2024-v1.md)
deepens the institutional-route row only through the first handoff: receipt to
company routing, channel, narrative/public-response visibility, and response
labels. Its capped retrieval-order design cannot establish route probabilities
or remedy; effort, verified outcome, repeat contact, trust, and exit remain open.
The [25-case public event-ledger finding](projects/us-customer-automation-recourse/findings/us-customer-automation-recourse-019.md)
turns that handoff into an explicit case-level contract: receipt, routing,
lag, channel, narrative presence, timeliness, and response labels can share a
de-identified record, while verified correction and later choice remain absent.
The [public event-ledger acquisition audit](projects/us-customer-automation-recourse/cfpb-public-event-ledger-acquisition-audit-2026-09-14.md)
now proves that a de-identified episode ledger can be built from the public API
without overstating those fields. Its 25-record student-loan extract preserves
receipt, routing, channel, narrative presence, timeliness, and response labels;
it explicitly does not turn a company label into verified correction, recovery,
repeat effort, switching, trust, or exit.

The [SHED annual fraud comparison](projects/us-consumer-fraud-trust/shed-fraud-annual-comparison-layer-v1.md)
adds a second annual household sample to the consumer-power row. It preserves
the income-conditioned recovery pattern while showing that age exposure
ordering changes between 2024 and 2025. This weakens a simple age-trend story;
it does not join household loss to CFPB response or establish remedy, trust, or
exit.

The [domestic capacity and state-leverage bridge](projects/ai-work-control/domestic-capacity-dependence-state-leverage-cross-source-bridge-v1.md)
now integrates SIPRI resources, procurement transactions and modifications,
realization milestones, ownership context, and international infrastructure
cases. The resulting cross-source chain is staged rather than collapsed:
resource allocation and commitment can precede operation, and operation can
coexist with external control or weak replaceability. Leverage remains open
until another actor changes behavior.

The state-capacity synthesis now makes that distinction concrete in the
data-center lane. LBNL's 176 TWh 2023 estimate and 649 TWh 2030 reference
scenario describe national modeled demand; Prince William County's $465.9M
reported TY2025 tax revenue describes local fiscal capacity; Virginia filings
describe modeled residential-incidence illustrations and reported large-load
drop events. These are not one benefit/cost estimate. Together they show the
political economy of infrastructure: growth can create public revenue while
leaving customer allocation, interruption risk, inspection, portability, and
community incidence unresolved. The missing state-capacity test is not another
load forecast; it is observed cost allocation, local experience, control or
switching rights, and an external actor changing behavior.

The new [Poland reciprocity and co-production finding](projects/ai-work-control/findings/ai-work-control-042.md)
adds an official response-stage layer: Poland's 2026 announcements describe
potential buyer/seller access in a US-linked UAS/C-UAS arrangement and planned
regional Barracuda B500M co-production with the US, Estonia, and Latvia. This
is evidence of institutional and industrial response around a procurement
relationship, not evidence of output, delivery, public reception,
replaceability, or bargaining leverage. The next test is to observe the named
transaction, plant, workforce, component-control, delivery, or switching
event.

The July 6 Bydgoszcz implementation account adds a more specific stage inside
that response: assembly followed by production at PGZ/WZL-2, reported PGZ
exclusivity for a Polish version, and technology/know-how/competence transfer
language. Those are agreement terms, not commissioned output or independently
verified control rights.
An August 19 BBN follow-up with Anduril adds a dated senior-level governance
checkpoint focused on the Barracuda-500M program and Polish implementation
competencies. It still does not establish a production milestone, output,
delivery, or amended contract.
The Ministry of State Assets' detailed production page adds reported targets of

make scale and local-content denominators testable, but remain plans rather

The September 9 Polish-US FMF agreement adds a broader financing checkpoint:
Poland reports $4 billion in Foreign Military Financing and acceleration of

The [September realization watchpoint](projects/ai-work-control/findings/ai-work-control-043.md)
records the current public-source non-observation: no first assembly,
commissioning, completed quantity, acceptance, delivery, or measured

The new [public-system buffer-and-credit bridge](projects/us-safety-net-access/findings/us-safety-net-access-012.md)
adds a financial-coping stage to the public-system row. SIPP shows that SNAP
transitions can sit beside declining resources, stable job counts, and
following hardship; Federal Reserve SHED shows that liquid capacity,
revolving balances, linked balance growth, student-loan difficulty, and
retirement actions are distinct coping layers. This is a cross-source
mechanism map, not evidence that a public-system transition caused credit use
or that financial pressure produced political action.

The follow-on [route-and-buffer synthesis](projects/us-safety-net-access/findings/us-safety-net-access-013.md)
adds the WBNS lived-route and charitable-food layers. It places notice,
recertification, interruption, cash/credit capacity, supplemental food, and
unmet need in one staged public/private safety-net map without pooling state,
respondent, person-month, and conditional-credit denominators. The result is
stronger evidence for incomplete protection and route substitution, not a
same-household causal chain or a political-trust finding.

The [care-cost/time/work cross-source finding](findings/us-care-cost-time-work-currency-cross-source-001.md)
extends the care/health row by putting MEPS, SIPP, and SHED beside one another
without merging their samples. The layers jointly support a bounded social-
reproduction pattern: health and care costs can be transferred among system
spending, out-of-pocket payment, unpaid time, work capacity, borrowing, and
saving. The same-person dated event through household recovery, institutional
response, trust, or politics remains an open arrow.

The [capacity-to-leverage finding](projects/ai-work-control/findings/ai-work-control-023.md)
now adds the IMF April 2026 WEO defense-boom estimates. The macro layer shows
that fiscal and social-spending trade-offs can appear between resource
allocation and usable capacity, but its cross-country estimates and US
aggregate projections do not establish a US household burden or realized
geopolitical leverage. The required leverage test remains an observed refusal,
switch, concession, regulation, or external behavior change.

The [defense resource-to-capability finding](projects/ai-work-control/findings/ai-work-control-048.md)
now aligns that macro stage with SIPRI spending estimates, a bounded
USAspending JASSM/LRASM supplier extract, and the Poland-US procurement and
co-production sequence. It makes contract, supplier, implementation, and
realization stages more explicit while preserving the missing tests for
production, delivery, domestic incidence, replaceability, and external response.

## The strongest current composite proposition

The long-form reader version is [Unequal optionality is the recurring
structure behind pressure, adaptation, and power](findings/us-broad-program-unequal-optionality-path-001.md).
It carries this proposition through the household, institutional, work,
cultural, place, infrastructure, and geopolitical scales and records the
counterexamples and tests required before it can become a causal claim.

Across the current evidence, a defensible provisional proposition is:

> US social and political change is often organized by unequal optionality:
> households, workers, customers, public agencies, and states respond to
> pressure through different combinations of money, time, care, representation,
> institutional route, and ability to switch or refuse. The same visible
> outcome—continued consumption, benefit receipt, employment, care, voting, or
> infrastructure growth—can therefore coexist with unresolved burden or
> dependence.

This is a synthesis of recurring measurement patterns, not a causal estimate.
The current registry contains 231 machine-readable records, 890 observations,
and 103 source packets across 14 program themes. It does not claim that the
197 records describe the same people or that one
mechanism explains all domains. The counterexamples are part of the
proposition: financial improvement can coexist with worse health; SNAP exit
can coexist with hardship; continued receipt can coexist with utility burden;
assistance participation can coexist with very low food security; adults and
children can experience different food-security states; care can occur across
work and financial paths; a reported response is not a verified remedy; and
infrastructure scale is not state leverage.

The AI/work and digital-habits lanes now deepen the synthesis in two ways. The
[AI capability conditions finding](projects/ai-work-control/findings/ai-work-control-022.md)
places NBER worker/task adoption beside BEA, BIS, IMF, World Bank, and OFR
evidence. It shows why exposure, measured output, inequality models, public
capacity, and infrastructure dependence must be kept as separate stages. The
new [AI capability/shared-control synthesis](projects/ai-work-control/findings/ai-work-control-031.md)
extends that comparison into an explicit control test: productivity, price,
wealth, public-capacity, and country-capability signals do not identify who
owns the gain, reviews the decision, can exit the system, or bears the risk.
The source units and evidence statuses remain separate. The
[platform agency finding](projects/us-digital-habits-attention/findings/us-digital-habits-attention-002.md)
similarly separates attention, trust, civic action, complaint visibility,
response, remedy, switching, and exit. Both strengthen the central optionality
proposition while leaving the same-unit causal joins open.

The [digital-life synthesis](projects/us-digital-habits-attention/findings/us-digital-habits-attention-005.md)
and [digital consumer recourse synthesis](projects/us-digital-habits-attention/findings/us-digital-habits-attention-007.md)
now make the handoff explicit: platform reach, chatbot purpose, news attention,
reported loss, recovery effort, complaint visibility, explanation, relief, and
exit are different control surfaces. A socially central platform can still be
replaceable for one user and essential for another; a visible complaint can
improve institutional legibility without restoring money, access, or trust.
The next test remains a lawful case-linked or diary-based episode design with
contact, loss, effort, verified correction, switching, and later meaning/action
in the same unit.

The [AI synthetic-contact experiment](projects/us-cost-trust-politics/findings/us-cost-trust-politics-026.md)
adds a rare causal middle step to this otherwise mostly descriptive political
surface. Across five preregistered studies of 3,960 US partisans, the tested
outgroup-bot interaction increased immediate warmth and increased the choice of
a real outgroup conversation in Study 4 (67% versus 61% in control). The
one-week follow-up in Study 5, however, found a much smaller and statistically
non-significant warmth difference. This supports a carefully bounded inference:
a designed AI contact can lower the immediate barrier to cross-partisan contact,
but the current evidence does not show durable attitude change, turnout, voting,
or a population-level platform effect. It also leaves governance questions open:
whether users know they are interacting with a bot, whether the bot represents
the outgroup accurately, who controls the framing, and whether repeated contact
produces durable behavior or merely short-lived experimental compliance.

The latest cross-source rotation adds four bounded tests to that proposition.
The [AI work-control finding](projects/ai-work-control/findings/ai-work-control-035.md)
places task-level adoption beside sector mobility, union representation, and
pay without treating any one of those as worker control. The [consumer recourse
finding](projects/us-customer-automation-recourse/findings/us-customer-automation-recourse-018.md)

The [local capacity and political-context findings](projects/us-local-business-place/findings/us-local-business-place-005.md)
add a same-county capacity/mobility comparison: trust, civic action, and

party-conditioned layer shows identity organizing the meaning of the same place

The new [comparative worker-control finding](projects/ai-work-control/findings/ai-work-control-068.md)
sharpens the AI/work arrow. JRC worker survey evidence associates algorithmic
management with autonomy, break, stress, and intensity differences in Europe;
ILO cases show that collective voice sometimes changes consent, pay, data
limits, human oversight, or workflow discretion, while other challenges remain
partial or unresolved. Read alongside the US NBER/OECD/BLS layer, this supports
a conversion hypothesis rather than a US causal claim: information and voice
become control only when they alter a rule or produce enforceable remedy.
The [housing and insurance finding](projects/us-housing-insurance-risk/findings/us-housing-insurance-risk-002.md)
tests California ZIP-level income and wildfire-risk combinations and shows that
the apparent low-income/high-risk gradient is sensitive to the income cutoff.
Together these are not one causal chain; they are a current stress test showing
that unequal room, institutional routing, and interpretation can be aligned
only when units, denominators, and counterexamples remain visible.

The [firm-to-worker comparison](projects/ai-work-control/findings/ai-work-control-064.md)

The latest household-room pass adds a FRED timing screen. From January 2024 to
July 2026, the University of Michigan consumer-sentiment index fell 23.8 points
while the CPI index rose 7.5% and unemployment remained close to 4%. This does
not identify an inflation effect: the series have different constructions and
do not share a household denominator. It does establish a useful divergence
that the household-level work must explain: a comparatively strong aggregate
labor signal can coexist with weak public economic confidence.

The [FRED finding](projects/us-household-financial-pressure/findings/us-household-financial-pressure-012.md)
therefore strengthens the distinction between national conditions and practical
household room. SHED, SIPP, MEPS, BEA, BLS, and Federal Reserve credit records
are needed to test which households faced shelter, food, energy, insurance,
debt, care, or work constraints, and what they did next. The FRED series alone
cannot supply that exposure, adaptation, political attribution, or recovery
step.

The [Gallup institutional-confidence finding](projects/us-cost-trust-politics/findings/us-cost-trust-politics-025.md)
adds a parallel meaning layer: average confidence in long-tracked institutions
was low, but confidence differed sharply by institution, party, and racial
group. The [Chatham House data-trust memo](projects/ai-work-control/chatham-house-data-trust-governance-layer-v1.md)
extends the route to public measurement itself, treating confidence in economic
statistics as a possible condition of domestic coordination and international
credibility. These are not proof of a material-to-trust or domestic-to-
geopolitical causal chain. They make the missing links more precise: measure
the statistic a person encountered, their own material exposure, who they
believed, whether an institution acted, and whether confidence changed after a
verifiable correction or remedy.

The [UNDP human-development finding](projects/ai-work-control/findings/ai-work-control-069.md)
adds a comparative capability frame to the AI and household-room lanes. The
United States has a high average HDI and a strong international rank, but that
average does not show who can exercise a choice, access training, refuse an
automated decision, or absorb a work or health shock. UNDP therefore helps keep
the program’s question focused on human capability rather than technology or
income alone. It remains a country-level context measure; subgroup incidence,
distribution, agency, and worker control still require US household, worker,
firm, and institutional evidence.

The follow-up [UNDP AI-and-human-development survey finding](projects/ai-work-control/findings/ai-work-control-070.md)
adds the missing perception layer for selected countries. In the US tabulation,
weekly-or-more AI use (32.6%), confidence in controlling AI interactions
(33.9%), confidence in government AI use (29.1%), and confidence that AI is
designed in people’s interests (37.5%) are separate measures. Selected-country
comparisons show why they should not be collapsed: more frequent use can coexist
with lower personal control, and high public confidence does not verify service
quality or actual agency. The survey is cross-sectional and internationally
comparative; the next test remains a US subgroup and same-episode join to work,
household, public-service, remedy, and political-action outcomes. The same
survey’s domain layer adds another boundary: reported use was highest for
entertainment, while likely future use was highest for entertainment and
education, with work, health, and finance carrying different stakes. The next
analysis must therefore follow the system into its domain of use rather than
treating AI adoption as one cultural or economic event. Its work-expectation
layer adds a further split: expected productivity, new job creation, and current
job change or replacement can coexist, and expected productivity is much higher
among people already likely to use AI for work tasks. This is an expectation
bridge, not realized productivity, displacement, or bargaining power.

The newest bounded findings add four important controls to the cross-source
story. The [SHED household-room finding](projects/us-household-financial-pressure/findings/us-household-financial-pressure-013.md)
shows that income timing, outside help, assistance receipt, budget margin, and
price adaptation are related but distinct household surfaces. They should not
be summarized as one measure of financial stress or treated as proof of a
single coping sequence. The [RECS energy-room finding](projects/us-household-calendar-integration/findings/us-household-calendar-integration-034.md)
adds energy spending, energy burden, assistance, disconnection, and inability
to repair or replace equipment as separate routes. Its burden result is a
transparent spending-to-income proxy, not a complete welfare measure, and the
public-use survey year limits claims about the present.

The [BFS/BDS firm-and-place finding](projects/us-local-business-place/findings/us-local-business-place-011.md)
adds a firm-stage boundary: applications, establishments, openings, and net
job creation are different events. A place can show entrepreneurial entry
without a corresponding positive net-job result; sector composition is part of
the explanation. This keeps local business formation from being used as a
shortcut for local prosperity, worker bargaining power, or community recovery.
Finally, the [World Bank poverty, climate, and data-coverage finding](projects/ai-work-control/findings/ai-work-control-073.md)
adds comparative context rather than a US outcome: global poverty remains
highly concentrated in Sub-Saharan Africa and fragile or conflict-affected
settings; climate exposure and missing household surveys constrain what can be
known. Those facts matter for the geopolitical context and for interpreting
international capability, but they do not establish a US household,
conflict, or geopolitical-behavior effect.

## What is genuinely end-to-end today

The program currently has partial end-to-end coverage in bounded segments:

1. **Same-respondent persistence:** SHED 2024→2025 financial-condition,
   adaptation, health, and selected care transitions.
2. **Time and care scale:** ATUS published prevalence, care-day intensity,
   employment, age, and household/nonhousehold distinctions.
3. **Time-ordered public-system condition:** SIPP SNAP transitions followed by
   rent/mortgage and utility hardship with Fay–BRR uncertainty, alongside USDA
   food-security severity and assistance participation.
   The buffer-and-credit bridge adds adjacent Federal Reserve coping measures,
   but does not claim a same-person route-to-credit or route-to-politics result.
4. **Pressure-to-adaptation comparison:** BLS food-at-home prices, SHED
   income-conditioned food insufficiency and price responses, and USDA food
   security, with different reference periods and units kept explicit.
5. **Institutional visibility:** CFPB published complaint route and endpoint
   trends, with product and time conditioning.
   The 2025 product comparison and household fraud layer now make the split
   between response labels, residual loss, recovery effort, and exit capacity
   explicit; a verified same-case remedy remains open.
6. **Political participation surface:** CPS voting/registration and distinct
   nonvoter barriers, beside separate trust and judgment layers.
7. **Infrastructure/state-capacity boundary:** national load scenarios and two
   international project milestones, with ownership and replaceability still
   open.
   IMF WEO adds a conditional macro-fiscal stage for defense mobilization;
   financing and social-spending incidence remain unlinked to US households
   or observed leverage.
8. **AI and platform agency boundary:** task-level adoption, industry and firm
   outcomes, modeled distribution, public analytical capacity, attention,
   complaint visibility, and institutional scrutiny are now linked as bounded
   stages; worker/household incidence, remedy, exit, and geopolitical leverage
   remain unjoined.

The program does **not** yet have one valid record that carries a dated
material or institutional trigger through adaptation, time/care, wellbeing,
meaning, political action, institutional response, and later recovery. That is
the central acquisition target—not a reason to erase the partial evidence or
pretend that cross-source alignment is a respondent-level join.

## Next rotating program cycle

The next cycle should advance all three scales together:

- **Micro/same-unit:** run the PSID structural gate when authenticated files
  arrive; pursue SHED/ATUS/SIPP event-compatible comparisons where valid.
- **Institutional/episode:** implement the same-case public-system and
  consumer recourse event ledger with notice, effort, remedy, and follow-up.
- **Place/state:** retrieve utility, ownership, local-benefit, portability, and
  observed-response records for data-center and public-cloud cases.

Every new record should enter the [trend-observation registry](us-trend-observations.md),
carry its own denominator and boundary, and add either a new time period,
subgroup/place conditioning, uncertainty, reversal, or same-unit link before a
provisional trend is strengthened.

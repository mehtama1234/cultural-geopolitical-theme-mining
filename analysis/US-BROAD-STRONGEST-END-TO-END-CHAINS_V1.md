# US broad atlas: strongest end-to-end chains v1

**Checked:** 2026-10-19
**Purpose:** identify the strongest currently retained routes toward the broad
end-to-end goal without pretending that separate sources form one causal chain.

The program's target is:

```text
condition or shock
  -> exposure and usable alternatives
  -> money, time, access, status, health, control, or security change
  -> institutional response or remedy
  -> received and durable outcome
  -> persistence, recovery, trust, action, switching, or exit
```

No retained route closes every stage. The ranking below is therefore about how
much of the chain is held by a compatible unit, not about which topic is most
important.

The [cross-lane trend portfolio](US-BROAD-CROSS-LANE-TREND-PORTFOLIO_V1.md)
supplies the thematic synthesis across these routes; it does not upgrade a
cross-source pattern into a same-unit end-to-end chain.

The strongest recent practical-alternative comparator is the failed-alternative-employment
case: search effort, lost income, and a formal remedy are visible in one worker
record, but a secured alternative, payment receipt, and later recovery are not.
It strengthens the route ranking without qualifying as a completed end-to-end
episode.

### Newly retained remedy comparator

The [Longridge worker ledger](projects/ai-work-control/data/fwo-longridge-underpayment-alternative-employment-backpay-event-ledger-v1.json)
adds two separate public worker episodes: advertised commission-only work
produced severe underpayment; each worker quit and sought alternative
employment; both complained; and the Federal Circuit Court reasons record that
the exact amounts due were paid in full in two installments on 2 May 2013 and
1 October 2014. This is a stronger alternative-to-remedy bridge than a
compensation order alone, but it is not promoted as a completed end-to-end
route because the public record lacks worker-confirmed receipt,
replacement-job quality or duration, adequacy, later trust, recurrence, and
exit. The two workers remain separate units and are not pooled into one story.

### Newly retained consumer-remedy comparator — not ranked as a same-unit chain

The [FTC Cleo AI refund ledger](projects/us-customer-automation-recourse/data/ftc-cleo-ai-refund-planned-payment-event-ledger-v1.json)
adds a dated aggregate consumer-redress route: the FTC announced 2,124,796
planned PayPal payments totaling more than $15.8 million, with notification
before the planned 27 October 2026 distribution start and a 30-day acceptance
window. This advances the atlas from settlement design to a scheduled payment
clock, but it does not establish delivery, acceptance, return, reissue,
individual amount, adequacy, account correction, household recovery, repeat
borrowing, trust, switching, or exit. The [post-distribution acquisition gate](projects/us-customer-automation-recourse/cleo-ai-refund-post-distribution-acquisition-gate-2026-10-19.md)
defines the next checks and keeps future payment events separate from receipt.

### New cross-theme corridor — not ranked as a same-unit chain

The new [health-cost institutional-legitimacy bridge](records/us-health-cost-institutional-legitimacy-bridge-2022-2026.json)
connects persistent care foregoing with medical debt, reduced savings,
borrowing, delayed purchases, and health; a separate credit record adds score
exposure from medical collections, and a separate HTOPS comparison adds
institution-specific confidence differences under expense difficulty. This is
useful for the broad cultural and political map because it shows how one type
of pressure can be translated into several currencies before it becomes a
judgment about institutions. It is deliberately not promoted into the ranked
same-unit chains: the sources do not follow one dated bill or care episode
through credit correction, attribution, trust, action, and recovery.

### New infrastructure-legitimacy corridor — not ranked as a same-unit chain

The new [UK digital-identity sector record](records/comparative-uk-digital-identity-sector-2026-trust-access-market.json)
adds a cross-theme route from identity infrastructure and verification markets
through public-service, employment, health, finance, and age-assurance access;
income-linked understanding and use; physical-versus-digital preference;
privacy and data-control expectations; and the need for non-digital fallback
and correction. The [OECD digital-government record](records/comparative-oecd-digital-government-outlook-2026-service-fragmentation-ai-capacity.json)
and the [UN Women gender-inclusive DPI record](records/comparative-un-women-gender-inclusive-digital-public-infrastructure-2026.json)
provide comparative system-design and inclusion controls, while U.S. administrative
records supply the practical non-use, data-trust, and remedy questions. The
shared pattern is that identity systems can become operationally necessary
before they become legitimate: adoption is not access, access is not control,
and control is not remedy. This corridor is not promoted into a completed
end-to-end chain because the sources do not follow the same person from a
failed verification through appeal, correction, service receipt, durable
capability, and trust or exit.

### New demographic-care corridor — not ranked as a same-unit chain

The [European Commission JRC demographic record](records/comparative-eu-jrc-demographic-transformation-2026-ageing-work-care-housing-migration.json)

cross-source corridor from population aging and spatial decline through care
demand, unpaid household time, women’s and older-worker participation, youth
transitions, housing, skilled migration, and public-finance or
intergenerational legitimacy. The central control is that “more workers” or
“more migration” is not a complete adaptation if care, housing, transport,

projection or survey designs, and do not follow one household through care,
work, housing, policy receipt, and durable recovery.

This is not promoted as a same-unit chain because the sources combine global

### New intergenerational-security corridor — not ranked as a same-unit chain

The [Social Security information, family-mobility, and legitimacy synthesis](projects/cross-domain/us-social-security-information-family-mobility-legitimacy-2026-09-29.md)
connects a projected fiscal deadline, personalized benefit information,

### Recent comparative remedy counterexamples

Two recent comparative cases sharpen the ranking without displacing its top
routes:

- **Malaysia GrabCar Tribunal:** statutory rights and a formal Tribunal moved
  into reported case use, with a claimant appearing personally and the matter
  continuing into evidence and witness examination. The route still lacks a
  public case identifier, merits reasoning, award, payment, access correction,
  or later work outcome. This is the clearest recent example of institutional
  use appearing before effective recovery.
- **Australian Kyei platform case:** procedural deficiencies were identified,
  but a substantive safety reason was accepted and the unfair-deactivation
  claim was dismissed without restoration. This is a negative-remedy
  comparator: process failure, substantive justification, and practical
  consequence must be coded separately.

Together they add two controls to the ranking: a hearing is not a remedy, and
a process defect is not by itself proof that the underlying decision should be
reversed. Both cases remain open on payment, later access, durable work,
trust, and exit.

### New economic-security corridor — not ranked as a same-unit chain

The [World Bank Indonesia economic-security record](records/comparative-world-bank-indonesia-economic-security-2023-jobs-care-shocks.json)
adds a comparative route from poverty reduction through low-productivity work,
weak urban mobility, gendered unpaid care, climate shocks, social protection,
resilient infrastructure, and fiscal capacity. Read alongside the existing
U.S. household-room, care, housing, and disaster records, it clarifies that
economic security is a conversion problem: work must produce sufficient room,
care must be supported rather than silently absorbed, and protection must
arrive before a shock reverses poverty gains. This is not promoted as a
same-unit chain because the assessment combines national poverty trends,
sectoral labor analysis, disaster history, and policy synthesis rather than
following one household from work and care constraints through shock, benefit
receipt, service quality, and durable recovery.

### Geopolitical realization comparator

The [Poland F-35 Husarz ledger](projects/ai-work-control/data/poland-f35-husarz-arrival-acceptance-event-ledger-v1.json)
is deliberately kept separate from the JASSM-ER route. An official Polish
record reports the first three aircraft arriving at Łask on 22 May 2026 and
formal acceptance on 12 June 2026. A separate official Polish defense record
reports first-pilot training completion, and a later Ministry update reports
five additional aircraft arriving on 28 August, bringing the in-country count
to eight. That closes the numeric aircraft component of the stated initial
operational-capability criterion, but not trained ground-personnel coverage,
spares/consumables, certification, fleet readiness, maintenance, mission use,
sustainment, or a changed external actor. The comparison is therefore about evidence stages, not
about whether the two procurements have the same capability or effect.

The [KFOR posture and local-security integration record](records/us-nato-kfor-posture-local-security-integration-2026.json)
adds a different geopolitical comparator. It reaches an operating and
relationship stage: NATO reports a gradual posture adjustment after an improved
security assessment and integration of Kosovo Police into the mission's security
architecture. This is stronger than a procurement or exercise announcement,
but it remains open on local consent, incident change, burden distribution,
durable trust, and changed external behavior. It therefore advances the
operation/interface stage without becoming evidence of legitimacy or leverage.

## What the ranking says

### Important same-worker implementation comparator

The [FWO Atkins receipt ledger](projects/ai-work-control/data/fwo-atkins-truck-driver-backpay-receipt-event-ledger-v1.json)
is the clearest positive worker-remedy comparator in the retained portfolio.
The public follow-up names ten truck drivers, records underpayment and court
enforcement, reports release of funds held in trust after an appeal was
dismissed, and states that all ten received back-payments ranging from $8,012
to $91,566. This closes a reported worker-level receipt stage that many other
remedy cases do not close. The [2026 receipt-and-durability audit](projects/ai-work-control/fwo-atkins-receipt-durability-audit-v1.md)
rechecked the official site and found no later Atkins-specific record of work,
household recovery, recurrence, trust, or exit.

It still does not establish independent payment confirmation, adequacy against
each worker's loss, household recovery, later employment, recurrence, trust,
health, or exit. The case therefore improves the remedy-receipt layer without
becoming evidence of durable worker security or a general enforcement success
rate. Its value is comparative: it shows exactly how much stronger “reported
received” is than “ordered,” while also showing why “received” is not the final
end-to-end outcome.

### Important randomized care-control comparator

The [Cash and Counseling beneficiary ledger](projects/us-aging-care-strain/data/aspe-cash-counseling-beneficiary-outcome-event-ledger-v1.json),
[caregiver ledger](projects/us-aging-care-strain/data/aspe-cash-counseling-caregiver-support-event-ledger-v1.json),
and [paid-worker ledger](projects/us-aging-care-strain/data/aspe-cash-counseling-worker-experience-event-ledger-v1.json)
form one of the strongest intervention-to-lived-outcome comparisons in the
portfolio, but they must remain three units:

- **Beneficiary:** randomized consumer direction supplied control over care
  arrangements and was associated with greater service satisfaction, fewer
  unmet personal-care needs, and more paid home-care access in the cited
  follow-up.
- **Informal caregiver:** treatment caregivers showed selected wellbeing,
  satisfaction, and perceived financial-strain improvements around ten months
  later, without proof of restored household income or long-run continuity.
- **Paid worker:** direct-hire workers generally reported satisfaction with
  compensation and conditions, while unpaid care, limited formal training,
  benefits, and retention concerns remained.

This is a real intervention-to-outcome bridge: random assignment strengthens
the comparison between the service route and usual care. It still does not
join allowance take-up, completed care, recipient health, caregiver work and
earnings, worker retention, household security, trust, action, or exit for one
stable household. The important insight is structural: giving a beneficiary
more control can improve short-run service and caregiver currencies while
moving coordination and continuity risk into the beneficiary-caregiver-worker
relationship.

The [Cartisano ledger](projects/ai-work-control/data/fwc-cartisano-backpay-no-return-risk-assessment-event-ledger-v1.json)
is a particularly clear counterexample to treating payment as restored
security. In one worker case, back pay and service continuity were reported,
an unconditional reinstatement order followed, and a later court record
reported an actual return. The same later record describes materially altered
duties and cessation while still employed during a constructive-reinstatement
dispute. The record does not establish final livelihood or later recovery, but
it closes an important same-case arrow: money, legal continuity, actual return,
and role quality can remain distinct.

The [Aitken ledger](projects/ai-work-control/data/fwc-aitken-return-backpay-later-dismissal-event-ledger-v1.json)
adds the durability counterexample. A union-supported return, three months of
back pay, and prior-service recognition were followed by a period of continued
work and then a later dismissal after a safety-policy conflict. The case does
not prove that the earlier remedy caused the later rupture, but it does show
why an initially restored job cannot be coded as durable security without a
later-tenure check.

The same durability problem appears outside employment. The [Sauna360
recall ledger](projects/us-marketplace-product-safety/data/sauna360-repeat-remedy-event-ledger-v1.json)
links a 2025 free-repair route to a 2026 official expansion stating that the
earlier repair was ineffective and offering a second professional repair. It
does not establish that every owner experienced failure or that the second
repair will succeed, but it gives the atlas a product-level version of the
same rule: an offered or completed-looking remedy is not durable protection
without a later outcome check.

The most advanced route is not the route with the largest sample. Mudiyanselage
is strongest on exposure, constrained alternative work, remedy, and stated
meaning/return decision. Meadley is strongest on the later remedy clock:
officially reported payment, failed reinstatement, worker enforcement, and
additional court relief. Its counterexample is especially important because
the monetary remedy and restored livelihood diverged in the same episode.
Neither case has later household, trust, recurrence, or exit follow-up.

The CCES route adds a different downstream endpoint: material disruption can
coexist with lower observed local political action without producing a single
direction of institutional approval. That makes it a useful bridge to political
meaning, but not a remedy or recovery chain; attribution, employer response,
and later security are not observed.

The two newest worker-remedy additions sharpen the distinction between
institutional reachability and worker agency. The DOL Workers Owed Wages route
shows that even after an agency recovers money, the worker may still need to be
located, notified, verified, and able to complete a claim. The OSHA asbestos
case shows the other side: workers can convert a safety concern into an
official retaliation finding and remedy order, while payment, safe return,
compliance, and the practical safety of speaking up again remain open.
Together they add a cultural mechanism to the atlas: people experience
institutions as reachable or unreachable routes that change the risk of voice,
complaint, and continued participation.

The paired [DOL PAID employer-proof ledger](projects/ai-work-control/data/dol-paid-employer-proof-event-ledger-v1.json)
adds a positive administrative counterpoint to the Workers Owed Wages route:
employer payment proof is stronger than a claim invitation, but it still stops
before worker cashing, adequacy, household recovery, and durable work. The
current WOW instructions add another gate: paper-check recipients must update
their information, provide identity documents, and use the electronic claim
route. This keeps payment-channel access, payment verification, lived recovery,
and later meaning as separate stages in the worker chain.

The official-route recheck on 2026-09-19 found no later public compliance,
final-payment, or employment record for that worker. This is a verified
non-observation, not evidence that payment or recovery failed; it means the
next worker pass needs a lawful later compliance/payroll or worker-confirmed
source rather than another reading of the same decision.

The largest same-person surface is MEPS, but its public data are strongest on
timing and later health/work status, not on the middle of the lived episode.
The consumer and public-system records have unusually clear institutional
responses, but they usually stop before recipient-level receipt and durable
recovery. Infrastructure and geopolitical records have clear capability or
governance stages, but they stop before affected-person incidence and changed
external behavior.

This produces a practical rule for the next pass: add one missing stage to a
high-ranked route before opening another topic. There are two different
priority layers here. The machine register ranks broad acquisition routes,
with household pressure and care first because PSID can strengthen the
material/time/care backbone. For an already available public-record route, the
highest-value marginal stage upgrade is worker remedy receipt and later
stability. These are not competing rankings.

The public-record stage upgrades are, in order:

1. worker remedy receipt and later stability;
2. a lawful UAS Older Ages or PSID route that adds alternatives, payment, and
   later meaning/action to an event-linked person record;
3. recipient-level consumer or public-benefit correction and recovery;
4. household and community incidence for a realized infrastructure project.

## Cross-route pattern

Across the ranked routes, institutional visibility is consistently ahead of
lived recovery visibility. The records can often show that a rule, complaint,
case, payment, referral, or project milestone occurred. They less often show
whether the affected unit could use an alternative, received the remedy,
regained the protected outcome, or changed trust, behavior, switching, or exit.

The care route adds a social-reproduction version of the same problem: the
population scale and time intensity are visible, but the record does not show
which work, rest, health, or social activity was displaced. Care hours should
not be relabeled as lost work without a counterfactual.

The housing route adds a place-security version: insurance can be formally
present while premiums, desired coverage, deductibles, or lender constraints
leave the household exposed. Shopping is visible as an action, but an
affordable and adequate alternative is not.

The [New York DFS insurance complaint record](records/us-new-york-dfs-insurance-complaints-availability-2025.json)
adds a regulator-side recovery clock: complaints can be closed and money can be
reported as recovered while claim-level repair, coverage restoration, and
household stay/move outcomes remain unknown. It is a useful administrative
endpoint, not a household recovery estimate.

The financial route adds a useful consumer-power version: people can contact a
provider, report a fraud, and even report switching without the record showing
verified correction, completed switching, restored liquidity, or why the person
stayed or left.

LIHEAP makes the public-system version concrete: prevention and restoration
occurrences show that the program can act, but the public counts do not show
whether the same household paid, stayed connected at the next bill, avoided a
repeat crisis, or changed its trust in the institution.

The geopolitical route has the same structure in a different form: a defense
agreement and delivery window are visible before accepted equipment,
fielded readiness, substitution, or an external actor's changed behavior. A
procurement commitment is therefore not yet operational leverage.

That is the current substantive finding of the atlas: **unequal practical
options and institutional friction determine how a pressure is converted into
lasting loss, adaptation, recovery, or meaning.** It is a cross-domain
mechanism, not a claim that every route has the same cause or outcome.

## Promotion rule

Do not call a route fully end-to-end unless one stable unit has a dated or
bounded event, an observed alternative or non-use route, a responsible actor,
a received or defined outcome, and later persistence/recovery/trust/action/
switching/exit. Aggregate payments, participation, energization, complaint
closure, or reported recovery alone do not satisfy this rule.

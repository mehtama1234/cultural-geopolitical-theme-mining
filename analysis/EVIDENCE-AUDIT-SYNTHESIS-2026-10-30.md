# Evidence-audit synthesis — 2026-10-30

## What the 40-claim review changes

The atlas now has a verified evidence-audit layer rather than only a source
inventory. Forty claims were selected across the latest cultural, social,
geographical, technological and geopolitical rotations. Each row identifies
the source route, source class, stage actually closed, support status, missing
boundary and next acquisition.

The dominant pattern is not lack of evidence. It is stage mismatch:

```text
high-authority source
  -> signal, scenario, rule, design, simulation or mechanism
  -> implementation and exposure
  -> named user, worker, household, community, facility or place
  -> adequate, durable, contestable outcome
```

The current evidence is strongest at the first two transitions and weak at the
last two. That is a useful finding in its own right: the atlas should acquire
fewer additional announcements and more same-unit longitudinal records.

## Aggregate findings

- **19 claims close a mechanism stage.** These include policy design, market
  structure, safety requirements, governance principles, strategic rationale
  and institutional pathways.
- **13 claims close a signal stage.** These include scale, stock, trend,
  scenario, strategy, definition, survey and process signals.
- **8 claims close an operation stage.** These include technical operations,
  simulations, conditional adoption and regulatory pathways.
- **9 claims are explicitly partial.** The direction is credible, but the
  source or route does not fully support the exact claim scope.
- **40 of 40 leave the named outcome unclosed.** None demonstrates the full
  chain from exposure through adequate, durable and contestable result.
- **29 sources are normally reachable and 11 require browser or alternate
  official routes.** Reachability does not change the evidence stage.

## Cross-theme interpretation

The same conversion gap appears in distinct systems:

1. **Infrastructure:** data-centre growth, compute strategy and fusion roadmaps
   describe capacity and policy intent; they do not establish local
   affordability, reliability, public value or community consent.
2. **Mobility and identity:** student mobility, digital public infrastructure
   and CBDC design describe portability or architecture; they do not establish
   same-person access, safe correction, informed choice or household security.
3. **Food and biology:** gene editing, precision fermentation, biofertilizers
   and agricultural robotics describe technical or regulatory opportunity; they
   do not establish repeat field performance, smallholder control, affordable
   access or culturally acceptable use.
4. **Climate and displacement:** CDR scenarios and displacement stocks describe
   pathways and scale; they do not establish durable removal, safe return,
   local consent, benefit receipt or non-recurrence.
5. **Public information and safety:** shutdown counts, AI forecasts and
   neurotechnology norms describe exposure, capability or safeguards; they do
   not establish protection, comprehension, action, remedy or equitable
   continuity.

## Evidence discipline now adopted

The atlas should use three separate labels in future work:

- **Source route:** normal, browser-retrievable, alternate official route or
  unresolved.
- **Evidence stage:** signal, mechanism, operation, material consequence,
  meaning or power.
- **Claim status:** supported in bounded form, partially supported,
  counterexample or not yet supported.

This prevents a technically live link, prestigious institution or detailed
policy document from silently carrying an outcome claim it did not observe.

## Next acquisition queue

The next pass should prioritize named conversion records, in this order:

1. **AI facility → grid/water/community outcome:** connection terms, actual
   load, tariff allocation, water use, mitigation, objections and post-operation
   reliability.
2. **Digital identity/payment → same-person receipt and correction:** proofing,
   payment, service use, fee, exclusion, error correction and repeated security.
3. **Forecast/warning → subgroup action:** warning receipt, comprehension,
   decision, protective action, avoided loss and failed comparator.
4. **Critical mineral project → local value and burden:** capital, permit,
   operation, suppliers, jobs, tax, land/water impact, benefit and exit.
5. **CDR/displacement/biological intervention → durable outcome:** baseline,
   exposure, measurement, consent, material result, recurrence and liability.

The goal is not to make the atlas sound more certain. It is to make the next
evidence acquisition more decisive.

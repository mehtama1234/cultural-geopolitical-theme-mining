# Household calendar event ledger v1

Use one row per dated disruption. Do not enter a causal conclusion in the event row. Record what happened, what the household did, and what remains unknown.

## Monthly check

- What money arrived, and on what dates?
- Which required costs were due before the next payment?
- What need did the household protect first?
- What was delayed, reduced, borrowed, or supplied by someone else?
- Who could change the condition, and who could only respond to it?
- Was a remedy offered, usable, and received before the next deadline?
- Did the next month start with more room, less room, or an unknown result?

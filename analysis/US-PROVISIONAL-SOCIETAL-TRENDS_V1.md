# US provisional societal trend register v1

**Checked:** 2026-09-12  
**Purpose:** synthesize the current source layers into broad, testable trends  
**Scope:** US households, consumers, workers, firms, places, institutions,
political culture, infrastructure, and cross-border state power

This register is the current broad picture, not a claim that every trend is
rising at the same time or happening to the same people. The evidence comes
from different units and survey frames. A trend is included because multiple
source layers make a recurring pattern visible; its missing join is stated
beside it.

The companion [counterexample register](US-BROAD-COUNTEREXAMPLE-REGISTER_V1.md)
defines the reversal, boundary, or alternative-protection test for each theme.
Unless a row names measured counterevidence, its counterexample remains a
required next test rather than an established result.

## The current provisional trends

| 1 | **People preserve immediate functioning by moving costs elsewhere.** | SHED records substitution, reduced use, saving cuts, borrowing, delayed purchases, and extra work; the 2024–2025 SHED panel shows many of these adaptations persist among the same respondents, with persistence and re-entry generally higher on worsening financial-condition paths than improving paths, while improving respondents also show measurable reversals of borrowing, extra work, and other adaptations; USDA shows the basic-security endpoint in food. BLS 2024 Consumer Expenditure results add that income and spending changes differed sharply by income quintile, with the lowest-quintile spending increase concentrated in housing. | That one household makes all of these adjustments, that a particular price caused one sacrifice, or that an improving financial category means every adaptation has ended. BLS averages do not measure liquid cash, unmet need, or political meaning. | Follow the same people across price, food, debt, health, and recovery, then add attribution and trust. |
| 2 | **Time is an unequal form of money.** | ATUS shows paid work, household production, care, multiple-job work, workplace location, and reduced socializing distributed differently across groups; the new time-transfer bridge places these beside SHED price adaptations and CPS participation friction, separating time spent, controlled, displaced, and social participation. | Which employer, price, care need, or policy created a particular schedule or whether time losses compound with cash losses. | Link time diaries to work transitions, travel, care need, service effort, income, and a dated financial-pressure event for the same person. |
| 3 | **Access is increasingly judged by the ability to get a remedy.** | The [CFPB response route layer](projects/us-consumer-fraud-trust/cfpb-response-route-layer-v1.md) records a web-dominant complaint route, limited narrative visibility, timely responses, and explanations or relief that vary by product; its 2025 annual report also shows complaint volume, screening, routing, response, and publication as distinct institutional stages, with product-specific response asymmetry; platform and consumer findings separate first answers from resolution. | That a recorded response solved the problem, restored trust, or prevented exit; that a rising complaint record is a simple rise in harm; that product response shares are universal remedy probabilities. | Follow the same case from first contact through remedy, repeat effort, abandonment, and switching, including people who could not or did not complain; preserve received, screened, routed, and published denominators. |

The BDS state-sector profile shows transportation exit above entry in 36 of 51 states, while food services had positive median net job growth and no negative-net states in the selected comparison. | That application activity became a local service, that sector growth improved access or bargaining power, or that local rootedness and political voice declined in the same places. | Match applications to realized firms, sector turnover, establishments, jobs, services, ownership, prices, rents, and local civic measures by place and time. |
| 8 | **Firm and public success can coexist with transferred household or community risk.** | Payment, insurance, utility, platform, aid, and complaint findings repeatedly separate the organization’s headline outcome from the recipient’s later security or effort. | A single general mechanism or the total size of transferred risk. | Measure who pays, waits, loses access, or carries future risk after each organizational decision. |
| 9 | **Technology expands capability and dependence together.** | The [US data-center energy load baseline](projects/ai-work-control/us-data-center-energy-load-baseline-v1.md), World Bank, IEA, and the Romania/Malaysia cases connect AI to local complements, providers, capital, chips, electricity, data, and portability. The cross-source [trust, meaning, and action layer](projects/us-cost-trust-politics/cross-source-trust-action-layer-v1.md)
now makes the broad interpretation explicit: material and institutional
conditions do not become societal or political trends automatically. They are
filtered through attribution, identity, information, trust, and available
alternatives, then expressed through consumer, civic, and political action.
This is a cross-source arrow map, not a same-person join; the next depth test
is longitudinal measurement of exposure, interpretation, action, remedy, and
later security or legitimacy.

## The broad causal shape to test

```text
condition, price, rule, technology, or shock
  -> money, time, access, control, status, health, or security changes
  -> people, firms, and institutions adapt, delay, borrow, switch, stay, organize, or go without
  -> the next actor responds and redistributes cost, risk, data, assets, or power
  -> culture, trust, identity, public demand, political action, firm capacity, or state leverage changes
```

The register does not claim that every arrow is observed. It is a guide for
choosing the next source and for refusing to treat an attractive narrative as a
completed explanation.

## What cuts across the trends

1. **Headline outcomes hide the adjustment.** Employment, housing, service
   access, food purchases, firm formation, and AI adoption can look stable while
   time, quality, autonomy, safety, or future room deteriorates.
2. **The same response can mean opposite things.** Borrowing can be resilience
   or a trap; staying can be preference or constraint; automation can assist or
   intensify control; aid can restore choice or restrict it to one need.
3. **Power appears at the next decision.** The important actor is whoever can
   set the price, rule, schedule, data use, remedy, ownership, or exit terms.
4. **Distribution is part of the trend.** Income, race, age, gender, disability,
   family structure, migration, geography, firm size, and provider access change
   both exposure and available alternatives.
5. **Cultural and political effects require their own evidence.** Hardship does
   not automatically become distrust, identity, participation, or a vote.

## What would change the broad picture

The working picture would weaken if repeated population and institutional data
showed that adjustments rarely displace other needs; remedy and exit were common
after measured service failures; local business entry translated reliably into
lasting local capacity; technology adoption consistently reduced dependence; or
material conditions did not vary with interpretation and action after identity,
information, and alternatives were measured.

## Relationship to the program

This register summarizes, but does not replace, the [14-theme inventory](US-BROAD-THEME-INVENTORY_V1.md),
[coverage matrix](US-BROAD-THEME-COVERAGE-MATRIX_V1.md), and [arrow-level evidence matrix](US-BROAD-EVIDENCE-MATRIX_V1.md).
Each row should eventually have a same-unit or explicitly valid cross-level
test, a counterexample, subgroup distribution, uncertainty estimate, and a
record of what would change the conclusion. Use the [broad trend-extraction
protocol](US-BROAD-TREND-EXTRACTION-PROTOCOL_V1.md) to keep source roles and
evidence status explicit while those tests are added.

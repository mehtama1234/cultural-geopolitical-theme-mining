# Rotation BJ — Carbon markets, Article 6 integrity and local legitimacy

**Date:** 2026-10-29  
**Lane:** carbon markets / Paris Agreement Article 6 / climate finance / environmental integrity / Indigenous and local safeguards / geopolitical accounting  
**Status:** Tier 1 bounded distinction plus Tier 2 credit-to-real-outcome question

## Research question

When a mitigation activity is authorized, registered, issued, transferred or
retired as a carbon credit, what converts that accounting event into an
additional and durable emissions reduction, transparent national accounting,
fair local benefit and credible social legitimacy?

## Conversion chain

```text
mitigation activity and host-country authorization
  -> baseline, monitoring, reporting and verification
  -> issuance, registry entry, transfer/corresponding adjustment and retirement
  -> actual additional durable reduction or removal
  -> local benefit, rights protection, remedy and public accountability
  -> credible climate ambition and durable market legitimacy
```

## Stage audit

- **Signal:** carbon pricing, crediting and Article 6 infrastructure are expanding
  across jurisdictions and international markets.
- **Mechanism:** baseline, authorization, monitoring, verification, registry,
  corresponding adjustment, retirement, safeguards and grievance procedures
  determine whether a credit represents a meaningful and contestable claim.
- **Operation:** a project changes land, energy, industrial, transport or
  household practice; emissions are measured; a unit is issued, transferred or
  retired; a host country records the accounting treatment.
- **Material consequence:** atmospheric emissions may fall, remain unchanged or
  rise through leakage, non-additionality, reversal or substitution; local jobs,
  land access, household costs, health and ecosystem conditions may also change.
- **Meaning:** a credit can symbolize collective climate action and finance, or
  be experienced as an external claim on local land and atmospheric integrity
  when communities cannot contest the baseline, terms or benefits.
- **Power:** host states, project developers, registries, verifiers, buyers and
  standards bodies control evidence and value; affected communities need usable
  information, consent/participation and remedy to exercise authority.

## Bounded finding

> **A carbon-credit authorization, issuance, transfer or retirement is not a
> verified additional climate or community outcome.** A registry entry,
> corresponding adjustment, high-integrity label, market price, safeguard,
> consultation or grievance route does not by itself establish additional and
> permanent reduction, no leakage, correct national accounting, fair benefit,
> rights protection, remedy or durable local legitimacy.

## Cultural and geographical interpretation

Carbon accounting separates the location of a transaction from the location of
the intervention. A credit may be bought in one country, authorized in another,
generated through a landscape or household practice elsewhere, and narrated as
a global climate benefit. That can mobilize finance, but it can also make the
burden of proof and the distribution of land, labor and cultural risk difficult
for affected people to see or contest.

Article 6’s registry and grievance architecture is therefore analytically useful
even before a large body of outcome evidence exists: it identifies where
accounting, authorization and contestability are supposed to occur. The atlas
must still keep procedural presence separate from actual remedy, benefits and
atmospheric effect.

## Counterexamples and limits

- A registry and corresponding adjustment can reduce double counting without
  proving additionality or permanence.
- A carbon project can produce real local benefits while its credited climate
  quantity remains uncertain; benefit and atmospheric outcome are separate.
- A low-priced credit is not necessarily low quality, and a premium-priced or
  highly rated credit is not proof of real-world performance.
- A grievance route can deter harm or improve design even when no case becomes
  public; absence of a case is not proof of no harm.
- Carbon pricing can raise public revenue and change incentives even when a
  particular crediting activity does not establish a durable reduction.

## Promotion decision

Promote the distinction to Tier 1: authorization, registry, adjustment,
issuance, retirement, actual reduction, local benefit and legitimacy are
separate stages. Keep credit-to-real-outcome claims in Tier 2 until named
activities join baseline, monitoring, verification, transaction, reversal/leakage,
benefit, grievance and later emissions evidence.

## Next test

Select one Article 6.2 cooperative approach, one Article 6.4 activity and one
voluntary-market comparator. Record host authorization, baseline, methodology,
monitoring, verification, registry/retirement, corresponding adjustment,
reversal/leakage, local consultation/consent, benefit terms, grievance, remedy,
post-credit emissions and community outcome. Preserve differences between
authorized, issued, transferred, retired and achieved units.

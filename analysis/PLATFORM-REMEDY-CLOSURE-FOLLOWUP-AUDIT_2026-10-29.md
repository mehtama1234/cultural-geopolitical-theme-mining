# Platform-remedy closure follow-up audit

**Date:** 2026-10-29  
**Purpose:** close the platform-remedy packet as far as the retained public records allow  
**Status:** program-level payment reach observed; individual durable recovery remains open

## Executive result

The follow-up evidence produces a useful two-level distinction:

1. **Program-level distribution can be observed.** The FTC Amazon Flex
   administration reports multiple payment rounds, more than $60.6 million
   distributed in earlier rounds, 2,806 successful Zelle reissue payments, and
   130,952 drivers reported fully compensated for more than $58.5 million as of
   the annual-report cutoff.
2. **Individual recovery remains mostly unobserved.** Named Fair Work
   Commission cases record reactivation orders, gross lost-remuneration orders,
   payment deadlines or conditional interim-payment obligations, but the public
   follow-up searches do not establish the worker's actual receipt, net recovery,
   later platform access, recurrence or anti-retaliation.

This is not a contradiction. Aggregate administrator reporting can establish
program reach while leaving the individual denominator, amount due, timing,
failure reason and downstream work/household outcome unknown.

## Revised bounded finding

The strongest defensible platform finding is now:

> Remedy systems can progress from formal right to aggregate distribution or
> enforceable order without producing a publicly observable individual chain of
> receipt, net recovery, durable access and non-retaliation.

This is more precise than saying that platforms lack remedy. Some workers may
have received payment and resumed work. The public evidence simply does not
support treating program-level distribution, an order or a deadline as proof of
that individual outcome.

## Stage vocabulary retained

| Stage | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Formal obligation | Rule, consent order, tribunal order or payment designation exists | FTC compensation designation; FWC payment order |
| Administrative distribution | Administrator reports money sent or cases processed | FTC reported payment rounds and Zelle reissues |
| Individual receipt | Named worker received the payment or usable remedy | Not publicly established in the retained follow-ups |
| Net recovery | Receipt after tax, expenses, lost work, debt and residual loss | Not observed |
| Durable access | Account/work access remains usable after remedy | Not observed in the retained named cases |
| Recurrence/non-retaliation | Similar harm does not recur and remedy use does not create new penalty | Not observed |
| Meaning/action | Worker trust, continued reliance, switching, exit or collective response | Not observed |

## Why the distinction matters culturally

Payment reach is not only a financial measure. It affects whether a dependent
worker experiences an institution as responsive, arbitrary, extractive or
repairable. But the meaning cannot be inferred from an aggregate payment rate.
An uncashed check, a reissued payment, a reactivation order, a delayed payment
or a repeat deactivation may each produce different interpretations of fairness
and control.

The next cultural evidence target is therefore not another generic trust survey.
It is a worker-level follow-up that records:

```text
eligibility | amount due | amount sent | payment channel | cashing/receipt date
| residual loss | account status | work after remedy | repeat action
| alternative work | household consequence | trust/voice/switching/exit
```

## Counterexamples and boundaries

- Aggregate distribution may be highly effective even when worker-level records
  are unavailable; lack of public receipt evidence is not proof of nonpayment.
- A worker can receive payment and continue using the platform; receipt is not
  evidence of exit or restored bargaining power.
- A reactivation order can be implemented while a later platform action creates
  a new dispute; correction is not necessarily durable.
- A payment obligation can be settled privately; a public non-observation is a
  source boundary, not a claim that money was not paid.
- Named legal cases are selected episodes and cannot estimate the prevalence of
  effective or ineffective remedies.

## Promotion decision

Promote the narrower Tier 1 finding:

**Formal remedy, administrative distribution and individual durable recovery
are distinct stages.**

Do not promote a claim about platform-wide worker welfare, trust, switching or
exit until a compatible individual or panel record closes those stages.

## Next action

The platform lane has reached a documented public-source boundary for the

# Broad program focus: societal, cultural, political, and consumer trend mining

For the shortest current restatement of the governing objective, start with
the [broader goal](BROAD-GOAL-RESTATEMENT_V1.md).

**Status:** governing focus reset; broad rotation resumed
**Scope:** the full US-centered Cultural, Social, and Geopolitical Theme Mining
program  
**Relationship to deep projects:** household constraint, health cost, consumer
recourse, AI/work, public systems, infrastructure, and geopolitical projects
are evidence lanes inside this broader program—not substitutes for it.

## The goal

Build a living, source-traceable map of the durable forces changing American
life: what people experience, what consumers and workers do, how firms and
institutions respond, how culture and trust change, how political meaning and
collective action develop, and how money, risk, ownership, data, capacity, and
power move through firms, communities, states, and geopolitical systems.

The program should find important trends before they become obvious, explain
the mechanisms beneath visible events, compare who is affected and who has
alternatives, and preserve the evidence needed to show where the explanation
could be wrong.

## The governing chain

```text
condition, decision, shock, or new capability
  -> exposure and incidence
  -> alternatives, money, time, access, status, health, or control change
  -> people, consumers, workers, firms, and institutions adapt
  -> costs, risk, ownership, information, waiting, and power redistribute
  -> trust, identity, cultural meaning, legitimacy, or social behavior changes
  -> political judgment, collective action, switching, exit, or non-use changes
  -> firm/sector capacity, public capacity, state leverage, dependence,
     alliance position, or geopolitical consequence changes
```

This is a research path, not a claim that every theme forms one causal chain.
Each arrow must be labeled as observed, reported, estimated, experimentally
identified, longitudinally associated, compared, inferred across sources, or
open.

8. unequal exposure, status, race, gender, age, and belonging;
9. trust, identity, cultural meaning, and changing norms;

13. infrastructure, energy, technology, and dependency; and
14. geopolitical and state consequences.

## What every research pass must do

Choose one bounded question and state:

- the unit: person, household, consumer, worker, firm, place, institution,
  infrastructure asset, country, or event;
- the time window, geography, denominator, weights, missingness, and source
  vintage;
- the mechanism that could connect condition to behavior or consequence;
- who gains room, who loses room, who must wait, and who controls the decision;
- a counterexample, negative case, or competing explanation; and
- the stopping rule and smallest next test.

Then produce a traceable route:

```text
source -> measured claim -> method/limit -> mechanism
      -> affected groups and behavior -> institutional or firm response
      -> cultural/political meaning -> wider capacity or geopolitical effect
      -> counterevidence -> next test
```

Separate datasets may support a layered bridge, but incompatible respondents,
clocks, denominators, and units must never be presented as one synthetic
person, household, consumer, or causal episode.

## What success looks like

The program succeeds by steadily improving a searchable trend atlas that lets
someone move from:

```text
broad theme
  -> recurring observation
  -> affected people, consumers, firms, and places
  -> mechanism and available alternatives
  -> institutional response and redistribution of risk/power
  -> cultural meaning, trust, or political action
  -> state, sector, or geopolitical consequence
  -> limits, counterexample, and next decisive test
```

Success is not a universal stress index, a news feed, a single grand theory,
or a completed household study. It is a maintained explanation of recurring
change with visible uncertainty and open arrows.

## Current focus hierarchy

The immediate work should rotate depth across the broad program while keeping
the following priority bridges visible:

1. material pressure → time, care, work, food, and household room;
2. consumer exposure → institutional response, remedy, switching, and exit;
3. AI/platform change → work control, attention, trust, and agency;
4. public rule or assistance → access, burden, judgment, and political meaning;
5. firm/infrastructure capacity → local incidence, dependence, and state room;
6. domestic exposure → cultural legitimacy, collective action, and geopolitical
   leverage.

The household constraint-cascade project is the deepest current example of
bridge 1. It is valuable because it tests the difficult middle between
material conditions and legitimacy, but it must remain one lane among the 14.
If its same-case data acquisition is unavailable, continue advancing the
broader atlas through the other lanes rather than treating that gap as a
program-wide stop.

## Operating rule

Every new writeup should answer three questions plainly:

1. What changed, and how do we know?
2. What did the change make possible or impossible for different people and
   organizations?
3. How did behavior, meaning, institutions, firms, political action, or state
   capacity change—and which link remains unproven?

Use the long-form [end-to-end goal](END_TO_END_GOAL_V1.md), [14-theme
inventory](analysis/US-BROAD-THEME-INVENTORY_V1.md), [evidence matrix](analysis/US-BROAD-EVIDENCE-MATRIX_V1.md),
and [next-pass queue](analysis/US-BROAD-NEXT-PASS-QUEUE_V1.md) as the operating
specification. This brief is the short focus document for restarting work
without narrowing the program to one dataset or one household lane.

## Current resumption move

The material/time/care rotation has completed its storage-conscious local gate:
the existing SIPP slice supports utility/tenure/work and utility/care/food/
housing screens, but its credit and savings fields produce no valid adjacent-
month state changes. The authenticated PSID route or a dated bill, shutoff,
assistance, or service episode remains the stronger test; do not manufacture a
buffer or recovery arrow from repeated status fields.

The practical-exit rotation has completed its storage-conscious observability
pass. Existing CFPB, platform-remedy, and SHED artifacts now distinguish
recorded visibility, access restoration, same-respondent adaptation, continued
dependence, switching, non-use, staying, moving, and exit. The decisive
boundary remains that alternatives, remedy receipt, protected/sacrificed
outcomes, and later same-unit action are sparse; no pooled exit rate is made.

The capacity/dependence rotation has completed its local realization and
supplier-control audit. Existing US data-center, Virginia utility, USAspending,
and Poland JASSM-ER records now separate commitment, capacity, governance,
supplier identity, planned delivery, operational stress, and external response;
accepted output, replaceability, public/partner incidence, and changed behavior
remain open.

The next active rotation is **same-respondent material change → meaning and
action**. Use the existing April→June 2025 HTOPS linked panel, its attrition
audit, and the ANES/CCES political layers to test whether material persistence,
entry, or recovery sits alongside institutional judgment, attribution, trust,
and distinct civic action. Preserve the panel's selected-linkage and timing
boundaries: a later confidence measure is not a dated actor response, vote
effect, or causal political translation.

No large acquisition is required for this rotation. If the local panel cannot
provide attribution or an action endpoint, publish that gap and specify the
smallest lawful repeated-respondent or event-compatible test needed to observe
the responsible actor, prior judgment, action, remedy, and recovery.

The latest local SIPP buffer gate found zero adjacent-month credit or savings
state changes. Treat that as a field-timing boundary, not as evidence that
households did not borrow, save, repay, or recover. Do not rerun this route
unless a genuine event-compatible field or panel becomes available.

The consumer-power route remains a completed recent rotation and a reusable
comparison case: complaint visibility, response labels, fraud/loss, recovery
burden, and remedy remain separate endpoints until a linked consumer episode
is available.

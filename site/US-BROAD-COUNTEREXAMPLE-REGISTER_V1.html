US broad counterexample register v1

Checked: 2026-09-16 Purpose: keep the 14-theme program from turning recurring patterns into one-direction explanations.

These are comparison requirements, not claims that the counterexamples have already been found. Each proposed trend must be tested against at least one case, group, place, firm, institution, or period where the expected arrow is absent, reversed, or absorbed differently.

Measured counterexample now in the atlas

The Uber/Hotak access-restoration follow-up is a bounded counterexample for themes 3 and 5. A platform-work exclusion was followed by observed account reactivation and more than 150 subsequent trips, so operational restoration and continued work activity are not merely assumed. Payment receipt, net recovery, durable access, alternatives, household outcomes, and non-retaliation remain open. The case therefore weakens any simple rule that a formal remedy is either only a paper order or a complete recovery, while not establishing a population remedy rate.

1, 10 In the SIPP utility/assistance following-month screen, assistance receipt coexists with substantially more very-low food security in the difficulty-plus-assistance cell than in the neither-screen cell (36.89% vs 4.40%). Assistance receipt is not equivalent to restored household security, and the unconditioned contrast is not a program effect. Person-month descriptive comparison; targeting, adequacy, and exact bill/decision timing remain open. See the SIPP energy-assistance layer.

Health improvement is not interchangeable with financial recovery. Same-respondent annual descriptive path; no dated bill or treatment counterfactual. See the health-direction adaptation layer.
3, 10 SNAP exits can be followed by rent/mortgage and utility hardship, while continued receipt can coexist with hardship; exit is not uniformly recovery and receipt is not uniformly security. Administrative status is not a sufficient welfare or route-quality outcome. Weighted person-month transition comparison with composition and need differences. 4, 11 The AI cross-partisan-contact experiment produced an immediate warmth effect, but the preregistered one-week effect was small and nonsignificant. Short-run contact effects are not durable depolarization, voting change, or institutional-trust change. Five-study experiment; generalization across topics and populations remains open. 6, 11 MEPS prescription-delay and denial/prior-authorization screens show different directions for later perceived-health improvement and worsening. “Institutional friction” is not one uniform mechanism with one health consequence. ZIP/place comparisons, not household property histories; mitigation, regulation, composition, repair, and move outcomes remain open. See the housing/insurance finding.

Application volume is not a common scale for realized jobs, services, or firm capacity. Separate national sector aggregates; firm survival, wages, service quality, and local incidence remain open. See the local business/place finding.
13, 14 Poland’s 2024 JASSM-ER order has a documented schedule and supplier route but no reviewed official record of accepted, fielded, trained, or inventoried missiles. Contract, schedule, or supplier identity is not realized operational capability or geopolitical leverage. Official-source non-observation; absence in the reviewed record is not proof that no delivery occurred. See the JASSM-ER realization boundary.
3, 5 The Hotak case records account reactivation and more than 150 subsequent trips after a platform-work exclusion. A formal remedy is not always only a paper event; operational restoration and continued work can occur, but neither proves complete recovery. Single public case; payment receipt, net recovery, durable access, alternatives, household outcomes, and non-retaliation remain open. See the Hotak access-restoration follow-up.
3, 4, 10 CFPB records distinguish Cash App's formal redress order with no current payment-by-case listing from BrightSpeed's named administrator, eligible class, and ongoing distribution. “Redress ordered,” “distribution underway,” and “consumer recovered” are not interchangeable institutional or household outcomes. Two named enforcement cases; individual payment receipt, remaining loss, repeat effort, trust, switching, and exit remain unobserved. See the Cash App record and BrightSpeed record.
2, 6 In the SIPP work-limitation/household-composition transition, hours often remain unchanged, while high-resource work-limited respondents with children split between earnings increases (42.3%) and decreases (44.6%). A work-limiting condition does not imply one uniform hours-loss or earnings direction. Same-person monthly descriptive comparison; the child-present low-resource cell is sparse and household care, accommodation, job mix, and dated triggers remain open. See the SIPP work-limitation counterexample.
2, 8 The ATUS care-time profile separates eldercare minutes from paid work and socializing rather than treating every care minute as lost work; providers can support household and non-household recipients through different time configurations. Care exposure is not a single, automatically measured work-displacement or social-withdrawal outcome. Annual diary and roster evidence; schedule control, earnings, recipient need, and longitudinal displacement remain open. See the eldercare time-network synthesis.
8 Among older adults, unmet assistance is materially higher for people with disabilities than for the all-60-plus population (24.0% vs 11.3% for any personal assistance). Older age alone is not protective against unmet care or material insecurity; disability changes exposure and available alternatives. Published 2025 subgroup frames with different disability composition; no dated service episode, route effort, remedy, or later action. See the WBNS age/care layer.
9, 11 In CCES 2024, the gig-work/student-loan proxy cell had the highest joint federal-trust/action share (30.32%), while the no-gig/no-debt reference cell was 16.42%; low federal trust with action was also observed (16.11% in the joint proxy cell). Distrust is not equivalent to civic withdrawal, and material/work proxy cells do not map monotonically to one political meaning. Weighted cross-sectional proxy comparison without complex-design variance; prior identity, attribution, selection, and dated exposure remain open. See the CCES trust/action layer.
3, 9, 10 The CFPB Navient record reports checks going to affected consumers while explicitly stating that the payments do not change or reduce the affected student loans. Compensation is not automatically obligation restoration, reduced servicing dependence, trust repair, or practical exit. Official case-level remedy description; payment receipt, balance correction, repayment-plan change, residual loss, repeat effort, trust, switching, and exit remain unobserved. How to use the register

For each proposed trend, select the counterexample before writing the synthesis claim. Record:

  • the exposure and the expected arrow;
  • why the comparison is sufficiently similar;
  • the alternative, protection, or mechanism that could explain the reversal;
  • the outcome that failed to move or moved in the opposite direction;
  • missingness, selection, measurement, and uncertainty; and
  • whether the counterexample weakens the trend, narrows its population, or identifies a moderator.

Do not treat a counterexample as a nuisance. A reversal can reveal where power, institutional design, place, identity, or practical optionality changes the result. It may turn a universal-sounding claim into a conditional societal pattern.

Minimum decision rule

A trend remains provisional when its counterexample has not been measured. A trend may be narrowed when a valid counterexample identifies a boundary. A trend should be abandoned when comparable counterexamples repeatedly show no pattern after accounting for timing, exposure, selection, and measurement.

Phillipps-Lewis post-Hotak follow-up acquisition audit v1

Checked: 2026-09-15
Status: bounded public-record search; no later final outcome located

Question

Did UDE2025/94 proceed after the Commission stayed Phillipps-Lewis’s case pending the Hotak Full Bench decision?

Search performed

The search checked the Fair Work Commission decision/order surface and targeted queries for UDE2025/94, the worker’s name, and the primary decision number [2025] FWC 2398. The located primary materials are:

  • [2025] FWC 2398, which dismissed Uber’s regular-work objection and stayed the remaining issues pending Hotak;
  • PR790745, which directs relisting within seven days after the Hotak Full Bench decision; and
  • the Commission’s public decision and order search, checked for a later order, merits decision, lost-pay calculation, or payment record.

Result

No later public final decision, lost-pay order, payment record, or durable access record for UDE2025/94 was located in this search. This is a non-observation bounded by the public search surface and date checked. It does not establish that the matter was abandoned, settled, resolved privately, or never relisted.

The worker’s access had resumed on 2 June 2025 before the eligibility decision, but resumed access is not equivalent to a formal merits remedy or recovery of the claimed earnings. The correct current coding is therefore:

eligibility objection dismissed
→ proceedings stayed / relisting direction
→ later public outcome not located

Why this matters for the atlas

This search closes an acquisition question without closing the underlying case. It prevents three common errors: treating the positive eligibility ruling as a merits victory, treating resumed access as payment, and treating a missing follow-up document as proof of non-payment or private settlement.

The finding also makes implementation visibility measurable. The public record can make the worker’s eligibility and the procedural stay highly legible while leaving the final access, earnings, and remedy state opaque. That opacity is a property of the evidence route, not an outcome estimate.

Next decisive acquisition

Search any authenticated or newly indexed FWC matter surface for UDE2025/94, then seek a worker-side or representative record that identifies relisting, settlement, payment, or continued access. Until such a record is available, retain this case as an eligibility-and-stay endpoint.

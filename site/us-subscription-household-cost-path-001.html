When a forgotten subscription becomes a household cost

Automatic renewal can be useful when a person wants the service to continue. It can also keep a charge running after the service has lost value or the customer has stopped paying attention.

In one sentence

Attention is part of the payment system.

Keep renewal, value, cancellation, and household harm separate.

What the sources show

1. Forced action reveals cancellations

NBER payment-card data across ten products show a sharp rise in cancellations when replacement cards force subscribers to actively renew. The authors estimate that inattention raises seller revenue by 14% to more than 200% compared with immediate cancellation after value falls below price.

The range comes from a model. A customer who does not cancel may still value the service.

NBER Working Paper 31547

2. The business gains from continuity

HBS describes recurring revenue as useful when the service is clear and the fee fits the value. It also warns that subscription fatigue can make the charge hard to see or stop.

HBS subscription research

3. Cancellation design is an enforcement issue

In May 2026, the FTC said Shutterstock agreed to pay $35 million to settle allegations that it charged consumers without informed consent and made cancellation difficult. The proposed order requires clearer terms, consent before charging, and simple cancellation.

FTC Shutterstock settlement

The connection

subscription starts → payment renews → use falls or value becomes unclear → attention or a forced card change triggers action → cancellation, continued payment, fee, or overdraft → household room and trust change

The NBER study supports forced action and cancellation. The FTC record shows a remedy path. Neither follows every customer from lost value to household harm.

The four outside views

Household: A recurring charge may be harmless with clear value, but painful when it competes with food, rent, care, or debt payment.

Company and sector: Renewal lowers payment friction and keeps useful service active. A hard cancellation path can turn inattention into revenue.

Money and finance: Measure the fee plus notice, overdraft, interest, lost discount, and the other purchase it displaced.

Public power: Disclosure, consent, cancellation, refunds, and complaint rules decide whether silence counts as a real choice.

What would change the finding

  • Price, renewal date, notice, use, cancellation effort, refund, and customer value.
  • Household income, cash room, overdraft, interest, and other bills.
  • Results by age, income, disability, digital access, and service type.
  • A comparison before and after a clear-price or easy-cancellation rule.
  • Evidence that easier cancellation causes wanted services to lapse.

Next test

Choose one recurring service. Follow sign-up, renewal, use, notice, cancellation, final charge, refund, and the next household payment for 90 days.

Reading rule

Automatic renewal is not automatically a household tax. A customer needs a clear choice and a real way out.

Finding 001: A business revenue shock reached the owner household, but only partially

Status: linked account evidence summary · Checked: 2026-09-17

The bounded finding

Using financial-account data that link small businesses to their owner households, NBER Working Paper 28151 reports a 40% decline in business revenue in the first two months after the March 2020 National Emergency. Each dollar of lost business revenue was associated with only a 1.6-cent decline in owner consumption. The muted pass-through persisted through 2021, including after COVID-19 vaccines became available.

The paper attributes the limited household response partly to federal subsidies and pandemic-era reductions in spending opportunities. That is an explanation to preserve, not a reason to treat consumption stability as proof that owners were unaffected.

Event chain

COVID-19 business shock
  -> revenue loss
  -> owner household and business share a linked financial environment
  -> consumption falls by 1.6 cents per dollar of lost revenue on average
  -> [open] cash-buffer depletion, debt, business survival, workers, customers,
         trust, political judgment, and later recovery

What this contributes to the broad goal

This is the missing owner-household bridge for the disaster-liquidity lane, but it is a different shock and intervention environment. It should be paired with the disaster-loan case as a cross-case comparison, not presented as evidence that SBA recovery loans caused the owner-household result.

Interpretation rule

limited consumption pass-through != no household exposure
consumption stability       != business survival
federal subsidy support     != private liquidity for every owner
linked owner account        != worker or customer observation
COVID evidence              != natural-disaster loan evidence

The result makes the next questions sharper: did the owner protect consumption by drawing savings, delaying payments, borrowing, reducing business investment, or receiving public aid? Did the firm remain open, and were workers and customers protected? Those are not identified by the abstract-level summary.

Next decisive test

Use the smallest official appendix or lawful data artifact needed to document the account-panel universe, cash and credit buffers, subsidy timing, business investment, and heterogeneity by owner wealth, firm type, and customer-facing industry. Then compare the owner-household path with disaster-loan recipients only after aligning shock date, unit, intervention, outcome, and follow-up.

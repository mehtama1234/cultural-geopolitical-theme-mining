Finding 102: Poland’s official JASSM schedule has two planning clocks, neither is delivery

Status: bounded defense-acquisition realization finding · Checked: 2026-09-19

The bounded finding

Polish Ministry of National Defence pages describe the same broad JASSM realization path with two different planning windows. The May 2024 contract announcement says deliveries will occur in 2026–2030. A December 2024 modernization briefing says the missiles will arrive in 2028–2030, and attributes that timing to supply-chain alignment with the operational readiness of Poland’s expanded fighter fleet.

This is evidence of schedule/planning asynchrony. It is not, by itself, evidence of a delay, acceleration, contract modification, shipment, customer acceptance, unit receipt, training, or usable inventory. The 2024 contract announcement also says the purchase is intended to increase Poland’s stock of this weapon and links the missiles to F-16, F-35, and FA-50 aircraft, but the public pages do not provide an identifier-bearing lot or accepted quantity.

What the chain establishes

threat assessment / fleet expansion
  -> contract and public delivery plan
  -> two official schedule windows (2026–2030; 2028–2030)
  -> shipment [open]
  -> acceptance and Polish unit receipt [open]
  -> aircraft integration, training, and fielding [open]
  -> maintained inventory and operational use [open]
  -> external deterrence or alliance leverage [open]

The useful insight is that acquisition intent, supply-chain timing, and fleet readiness are being coordinated, but the public record still stops before physical realization. A schedule should therefore be treated as its own state, not collapsed into “capability acquired.”

Limits and next field

  • The 2026–2030 and 2028–2030 windows may reflect different planning updates, scope, or communication contexts; the pages do not identify a formal supersession or amendment.
  • The broad Polish Senate list that includes JASSM-ER among equipment delivered since February 2022 does not identify quantity, date, unit, or linkage to the May 2024 order, so it cannot resolve this schedule question.
  • The next decisive record is an identifier-bearing shipment, acceptance, delivery, training, fielding, inventory, maintenance, or exercise record tied to the order or a clearly identified JASSM-ER lot.

The realization ledger preserves the schedule statements separately from direct delivery evidence. The Polish MOD contract announcement supports the 2026–2030 window, while the Polish MOD modernization briefing supports the 2028–2030 statement.

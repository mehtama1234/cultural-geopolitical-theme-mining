Company bridge: Microsoft and workplace AI

Status: first pass; descriptive and provisional · Last checked: 2026-09-11

Purpose

This bridge tests how the research findings appear in a company’s own operating record. It is not a valuation, buy/sell view, or forecast.

What Microsoft reports

Microsoft’s fiscal 2025 annual report reports about 228,000 employees, more than 100 million monthly Copilot users, more than 230,000 Copilot Studio organizations, more than 20 million GitHub Copilot users, more than 400 datacenters across 70 regions, and more than two gigawatts of new capacity during the year. These are company-reported figures.

Inference under test

AI assistant -> embedded workflow -> shared identity, data, and governance -> dependence on platform rules

This is an inference, not a demonstrated causal result.

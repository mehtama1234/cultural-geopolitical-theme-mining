Question

When AI spreads, do gains stay inside firms, or do they move through wages, firm size, energy systems, finance, and state power?

firm adoption and capital -> measured productivity and prices -> wages, firm size, and inequality -> electricity, chips, cloud, and data capacity -> financial exposure and public budgets -> country advantage, dependence, and bargaining power

This is a source map, not proof of the whole chain.

Early comparison

  • BIS points to productivity and wages without short-run job loss, but larger gains for larger firms.
  • IMF warns that capital ownership can widen wealth inequality even if wage inequality moves differently.
  • World Bank emphasizes local complements and country gaps.
  • IEA shows that AI depends on electricity, grids, minerals, and connection capacity.
  • OFR shows public AI use alongside institutional downsizing, without proving causation.
  • BEA shows that AI output, data capital, and productivity are still being measured.

Working question

Who can turn AI capability into durable capacity, and who remains dependent on the firms and states that control the complements? This is a hypothesis, not an established conclusion.

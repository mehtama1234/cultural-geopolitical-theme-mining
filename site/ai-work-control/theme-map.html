Research index · AI, work, and control

Theme map: work is becoming legible before it becomes better

Status: provisional synthesis · Last checked: 2026-09-11 · Confidence: medium-low

Theme

AI and algorithmic management are making more parts of work visible to software—tasks, skills, time, messages, performance, availability, and decisions. That visibility can help workers learn or find opportunities. It can also make work easier to rank, direct, monitor, and contest only after the fact.

The first change may be who can see and organize work, before a broad change in pay or employment appears.

Evidence chain

work produces data -> data becomes a profile, score, workflow, or recommendation -> system guides assignment or review -> room to choose changes -> pay, status, trust, job quality, and bargaining may change -> firms and states become dependent on system owners

The first three arrows have evidence. The last three remain open tests.

Subthemes

  1. Entry and experience: AI may lower some skill barriers while changing the value of experience. Finding 002
  2. The profile as asset: skill profiles, messages, code, and performance measures can guide staffing and management. Microsoft–Whoz comparison
  3. Accountability lag: efficiency does not say who answers for a wrong decision. Finding 004
  4. Trust as production: explanation, responsibility, and recourse may affect whether tools are used well. Finding 003
  5. Cross-border rules: the European Commission links platform work to transparency, oversight, safety, accountability, and access to information. European Commission
  6. Written oversight: an agreement can define risk classes, correction paths, inspection rights, and limits on personnel decisions, but those written controls are not yet proof of lived control. Finding 009
  7. System function: assistance, recommendation, allocation, direction, monitoring, evaluation, and personnel action should be coded separately. Finding 010
  8. Planning before output: expectations, capital choices, data work, and production redesign may precede measurable output or employment changes. Finding 011

Theme confidence rule

Call this a theme, not a settled law. Upgrade confidence only when evidence shows a repeated link from system use to a changed decision right, worker outcome, or institutional rule across more than one country or sector.

Next tests

  1. Find direct worker evidence on correction, appeal, monitoring, and job quality.
  2. Compare platform-managed work with ordinary employment and unionized workplaces.
  3. Track who owns the data used in profiles and performance decisions.
  4. Compare systems that assist worker choice with systems that assign or discipline.
  5. Add a small-firm case with independent outcome evidence.

Status: working comparison; incomplete by design · Checked: 2026-09-11

Purpose

Follow a change through time instead of treating adoption as the outcome.

intention -> investment or rule -> system function -> exposure -> measured result -> distribution -> dependence

An empty link is evidence of a missing link, not permission to fill it with a story.

What can be compared

  1. Intention is often measured before worker exposure.
  2. Capital and process changes can precede a clean labor result.
  3. Firm productivity results often do not identify workplace function.
  4. Worker evidence identifies functions better than most productivity studies.
  5. Infrastructure shows constraints, not value capture.

Required fields

  • Dates of intention, purchase, deployment, and worker exposure
  • Software, model, data, cloud, chip, power, and labor providers
  • Function and decision right changed
  • Worker group, contract, location, and outside options
  • Output, time, pay, promotion, discipline, health, and exit
  • Appeal, override, audit, and bargaining path
  • Ownership and cross-border dependency
  • Counterfactual without the change

Interpretation rule

Do not call the chain complete when a source reports only intention, adoption, a scenario, or an industry average. Label every outward inference.

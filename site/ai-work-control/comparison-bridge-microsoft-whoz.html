Comparison bridge: platform control and workforce allocation

Status: first pass; descriptive and provisional · Last checked: 2026-09-11

Why compare these firms

Microsoft describes a large cloud and software platform with agents, identity, security, governance, and management tools. Whoz describes a workforce platform that maps skills, matches talent to projects, and helps schedule and allocate people.

Inference under test

work becomes data -> data becomes a profile or workflow -> profile guides decisions -> repeated use changes what people can see -> control may move to the system owner

This is a hypothesis. A system may reveal opportunities or make its scores hard to challenge.

Evidence warning

Microsoft’s annual report is a regulated company disclosure, but its product reach and AI benefits are company-described. Whoz’s site helps us understand the intended product, but its metrics and performance claims are marketing evidence. Neither is causal proof of worker outcomes.

What it shows

The repeated distinction is between systems that help workers choose and systems that set pace, scores, access, or employment status. Assistance has measurable gains in some settings; monitoring and direct task direction show different risks. Written rules may change authority, but direct evidence of fast, safe use by workers is thin.

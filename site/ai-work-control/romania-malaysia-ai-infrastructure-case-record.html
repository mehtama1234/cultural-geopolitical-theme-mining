Romania and Malaysia AI-infrastructure case record v1

Status: primary-record compilation; outcomes still open · Accessed: 2026-09-11

These cases show different links in the capability-and-dependence chain: Romania moves public applications into cloud; Malaysia builds a privately operated data-centre campus with development finance and regional capital. They are not a country ranking.

Inference under test

Infrastructure may increase capability and exposure to external operators at the same time. The net effect depends on local learning, data control, provider choice, oversight, and exit cost. This is not established by the records alone.

Reading rule

petition -> service and jurisdiction -> interim protection -> procedural appeal -> enforcement dispute -> merits -> final remedy

The records establish activity at the early and procedural steps, but not a final merits judgment or worker-level outcome. The same dispute can contain both worker-protective and worker-limiting appellate outcomes.

Limits

  • This is a procedural timeline, not a legal opinion.
  • The reviewed appellate decisions did not resolve every underlying claim.
  • It does not measure compliance, health, pay, retention, organizing success, or settlement.

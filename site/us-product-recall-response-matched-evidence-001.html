Matched evidence · US households and product safety

A recall succeeds only when the household can act.

A notice on a government page is only the first step. The household must hear it, understand it, stop using the product, and receive a remedy.

In one sentence

Recall policy has three jobs: reach the household, make action easy, and provide a usable remedy.

A posted notice proves only that information was published.

The connection

hazard found → notice reaches household or misses it → product is stopped, returned, repaired, or replaced → injury and money loss are reduced or continue → trust in seller, brand, platform, and public system changes

The sources support the public recall system and broad market response. They do not yet follow one household from purchase to notice, action, remedy, and harm avoided.

The useful surprise

A recall can be successful on paper and weak in homes. The buyer may not recognize the product, prove purchase, afford to stop using it, or obtain the remedy.

Four views

Household

Count notice, stop-use time, replacement, repair, return shipping, refund delay, injury, and lost use.

Customer

Record how the buyer learned of the recall, what action was required, and what was received.

Money and finance

Separate refund, replacement, medical cost, insurance, dispute, and unrecovered loss.

Public power

Measure notice reach, action rate, remedy completion, repeat listings, and later incidents.

Next test

Link purchase or registration records to recall notices, customer action, remedy completion, and any later injury or property damage.

Reading rule

Do not count a recall as complete because a notice was posted. Show that the household received it, acted, and obtained a remedy or avoided harm.

Connected US finding · medical debt and care

When removing medical debt repairs the record but not the illness

Debt relief can remove a collection mark or lower a payment while leaving the illness, price, billing process, and next care need in place.

The argument

A clean credit record is useful, but it is not the same as affordable care or a treated condition.

Name the exact link the intervention repairs.

Follow one bill

A patient needs care and cannot compare the price before treatment. The bill is larger than expected. Payments compete with rent and food, and the account reaches collections. Relief removes the collection balance and may improve credit access. The illness remains, and the next appointment may still carry an unknown cost.

illness or care need → price, insurance, and billing process → bill or collection record → payment, credit, care, health, and trust change → relief repairs some links, not all

What the sources establish

EvidenceWhat it means
HBS describes wide health-care price differences and weak ability to compare value before care.The bill can begin before a patient can judge the cost.
NBER studied $169 million of debt relief for 83,401 people in two randomized experiments.Relief can be tested rather than inferred from correlation.
Relief modestly improved credit access where medical debt would otherwise have been reported.Removing a record can change future borrowing.
Relief caused a moderate reduction in payments on existing medical bills.Some current cash pressure falls.
NBER found no average effect on surveyed health, health-care use, or reported financial wellness.Credit repair is not automatic health repair.
The experiments do not fully show effects for bills that never reach collections or for different illnesses and incomes.Average results cannot settle every patient path.

Sources: NBER medical debt relief, HBS price transparency, and HBS billing costs.

The deeper finding

The useful unit is the exact link removed: a payment, interest charge, collection mark, or legal threat. Relief may give back borrowing room while doing nothing to prices or care quality.

What remains unproven

The experiments do not show whether relief changes trust or willingness to seek care, or whether other debt, time loss, untreated illness, or the next bill remained.

Next test

Follow patients for two years from the first bill, not only from collection. Join illness, care, quoted and final price, insurance, bill, relief, credit, housing, work, later care, and the next financial shock.

Reading rule

Do not use medical-debt relief as proof that health improved. Name the illness, bill, record, payment, credit, care, and health result.

Matched evidence · US medical debt and household life

Removing a medical debt can repair the record without repairing the illness.

Relief can reduce payments and improve some credit access. It did not improve average health, care use, or reported financial wellness in two randomized experiments.

In one sentence

Medical debt holds several problems together: the illness, the price, the bill, the collection record, and the fear of the next bill.

Ask which part was repaired.

The connection

illness or care need → price, insurance, and billing process → medical debt or collection record → payment, credit, care, health, or trust change → relief removes one link but not necessarily the illness or bill system

Debt can be both a burden and a record of what happened before it.

Four views

Household

Separate the original care need, bill, payment, collection notice, and money available afterward.

Patient and customer

A lower bill is not the same as clear prices, good care, or a safe choice to delay treatment.

Money and credit

Measure the credit record, interest, payment, rent, borrowing, and later debt separately.

Public power

Relief may repair a collection record while leaving prices, insurance rules, and billing practices unchanged.

Next test

Follow patients for two years from the first bill, not only from the collection record. Measure what relief repairs, what remains, and whether the next health or money shock is smaller.

Reading rule

Do not use the link between medical debt and poor health as proof that debt relief will improve health.

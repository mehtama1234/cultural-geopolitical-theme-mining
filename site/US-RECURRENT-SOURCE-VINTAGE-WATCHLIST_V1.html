How a refresh becomes evidence

1. Preserve the vintage.
Record source URL, release date, retrieval date, file/page title, exact unit, geography, denominator, and artifact or page hash.

2. Check comparability.
Separate preliminary from revised values and record changes in definitions, coverage, weights, missingness, subgroup composition, and seasonality.

3. Update cautiously.
Promote a new substantive finding only when a release adds a changed measure, subgroup/place, reversal, institutional response, or stronger same-unit design.

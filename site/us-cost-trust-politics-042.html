Finding 042: Expense difficulty carries a later material-pressure gradient without one universal confidence shift

Status: bounded same-respondent cross-lagged screen · Checked: 2026-09-19

The bounded finding

The April and June 2025 Census HTOPS public-use files share SCRAMID, allowing a storage-light follow-up of baseline expense difficulty. The exact intersection contains 6,564 linked respondents. Among the selected linked sample, baseline expense difficulty is followed by substantially higher June food insufficiency, energy-bill nonpayment, and recent household job loss. The institutional pattern is selective rather than universal: high confidence in Congress is nearly identical across the two baseline groups, while confidence in the presidency, military, police, Supreme Court, public schools, and statistical agencies is lower among respondents who reported expense difficulty.

The outcome-specific valid unweighted counts range from 2,574 to 3,961. The table therefore adds temporal ordering and a distinct institutional-confidence profile, but it is not a causal estimate or a general population transition rate. The Congress result is a counterexample to treating the other gradients as a universal political reaction.

What the chain establishes

baseline expense difficulty
  -> later household material-pressure context [descriptive same-respondent link]
  -> later institutional confidence [selective gradients, not one common shift]
  -> dated bill, responsible actor, alternative, remedy, recovery, and action [open]

The result does not identify whether expense difficulty arose from a bill, income interruption, health event, housing cost, debt, care, or another shock. It also does not identify whether later food, energy, or job outcomes were caused by the baseline condition. The near-equal Congress-confidence shares are useful precisely because the other institution-specific gaps cannot be converted into one general political-legitimacy shift without prior trust, attribution, and a measured action route. Material pressure may relate differently to executive, coercive, judicial, educational, statistical, and legislative institutions.

Limits and next field

  • The linked sample is a retained intersection; April weights and replicate weights are not documented as attrition-adjusted longitudinal weights.
  • Expense difficulty and the June outcomes have item-specific valid universes.
  • The public-use linkage does not supply a dated bill, actor, payment route, notice, appeal, remedy receipt, durable recovery, or practical alternative.
  • These confidence measures are not trust change in a responsible actor and are not a vote, complaint, organizing event, or other political action. The differences also should not be read as institution-specific causal effects.

The next decisive field is a dated material or service episode joined to the route taken, effort/cost, response, verified correction or recovery, and later household and political endpoint. Until then, this is a cross-lagged association and acquisition boundary, not a causal household-security or political-effect claim.

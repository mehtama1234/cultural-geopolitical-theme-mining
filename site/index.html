Find the forces beneath the visible story.

We mine research, data, company evidence, and public records to understand how changes in work, money, culture, institutions, and state power connect.

How we work

Start with evidence

Read the study behind the story. Record what it measures and what it does not.

Trace the mechanism

Show the short chain from a condition to a choice, a behavior, and a result.

Map the power

Ask who gains options, who bears risk, and who controls the rules or data.

Write with limits

Separate fact from inference and name the evidence that could change the finding.

Starting sources

HBS Working Knowledge helps us find readable research stories and linked faculty work. NBER Working Papers, NBER metadata, and the NBER data archive help us test claims across papers, programs, and data.

The connection

move, new account, or past debt → credit screen, deposit, fee, or denial → connection cost or delayed service → payment stress, collection record, or unsafe home

The sources support the entry gate, uneven credit record, and later service risk. They do not yet show the full path for one customer.

The useful surprise

Utility payment history can be weak as a reward and strong as a penalty. On-time payment may not build a normal credit record, while failure sent to collections may make the next account more expensive.

Four views

Household

Count deposit, connection fee, late fee, old balance, payment date, and service delay beside rent and moving costs.

Customer and resident

Tell customers which record was used, how to dispute it, and when a deposit is returned.

Money and finance

Measure the first-dollar barrier, collections, repayment, credit effect, and later deposit.

Public power

Test deposit waivers, affordable connection rules, data accuracy, and service continuity.

Next test

Join screening, deposit, connection date, bills, collections, moves, and later account access for the same customers.

Reading rule

Do not treat a utility deposit as a small administrative charge. For a household with no spare cash, it can be the price of entering service.

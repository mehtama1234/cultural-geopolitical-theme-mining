Matched evidence · US homes, energy, and household choice

A safe temperature can compete with food and medicine.

When energy costs or broken equipment make a home unsafe, a household may postpone another basic need to keep the room usable.

In one sentence

Energy insecurity is a choice among basic needs, not only a late bill.

Different breaks need different help: cash, repair, safer housing, or protection from shutoff.

The connection

energy bill or equipment failure → unsafe temperature, skipped food or medicine, or shutoff risk → illness, lost work, care burden, or housing strain → help, repair, debt, relocation, or continued sacrifice

EIA measures the first household breaks. DOE describes where help enters. The same-household health, work, and housing result remains open.

The useful surprise

“Energy-insecure” hides different losses. A skipped medicine, a broken air conditioner, and a shutoff notice may need different responses.

Four views

Household

Record temperature, food and medicine tradeoffs, equipment, sleep, illness, care, and work.

Customer and resident

Ask who controls the meter, repair, lease, and weatherization decision.

Money and finance

Compare energy cost with food, medicine, repair, debt, rent, and shutoff timing.

Public power

Measure access, waiting, completion, quality, and differences by place and housing type.

Next test

Link same-home bills, temperatures, equipment, health, missed work, and housing records before and after help.

Reading rule

Do not call energy insecurity a bill problem alone. Ask what the household gave up to keep the home safe.

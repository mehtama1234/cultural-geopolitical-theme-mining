Matched evidence · US customers and purchases

The cost of a bad choice begins after the review.

A review may reduce search today. A return, complaint, or late refund shows the cost that appears afterward.

In one sentence

Trust should be checked against what happened after the sale, not only whether the review raised sales.

The review-to-return link is still unmeasured.

The connection

review and search → customer belief and purchase → product, price, delivery, and quality received → return, complaint, refund, or repair → time and money recovered or lost → repeat choice, exit, and trust

The sources support these pieces in separate settings. They do not join one customer through the full chain.

The useful surprise

The cheapest choice is not always the lowest-cost choice. A lower price can be outweighed by poor quality, return shipping, lost time, or a late refund.

Four views

Household

Count price, shipping, return cost, time, refund delay, and replacement cost together.

Customer

Record what the review said, what arrived, and whether a remedy was possible.

Money and finance

Track payment protection, refund amount, fees, credit timing, and failed-purchase cost.

Public power

Require clear return terms, seller identity, review records, and complaint paths.

Next test

Link review exposure to the same customer's purchase, return, refund, complaint, and repeat choice.

Reading rule

Check whether the product arrived as promised, what the customer had to do when it failed, and what was finally recovered.

Connected US finding · disaster, firms, and local services

A recovery loan can keep a local option open—and create another bill

After a disaster, a small firm may need cash before customers return or insurance pays. A loan can protect jobs and services, but survival is not the same as recovery.

The argument

Did money arrive soon enough? Did workers keep good jobs? Did customers keep a useful service at a fair price? Did the owner household remain stable while repaying the debt?

A loan can protect a place by moving part of the disaster into the future.

Follow one damaged firm

A neighborhood shop loses equipment and customers after a flood. Payroll and rent continue while repairs wait. An SBA loan arrives, the shop reopens, and workers return. Months later repayment competes with new repairs and weak demand.

disaster damage or lost demand → repair and working-capital gap → insurance, SBA loan, private credit, or no help → survival or delayed recovery → workers, customers, owner household, and local services

NBER finds higher employment and revenue, less delinquency, and more private credit after lending.Firm recovery can be measured.It does not show every worker or customer.

The deeper finding

A recovery loan can protect a local option while shifting loss into future payments. It may help the owner, workers, and customers at once, or mainly keep a balance sheet alive.

A shop is not recovered merely because its doors open. The service must remain reachable, affordable, and useful.

What remains unknown

The evidence does not follow one firm and owner from damage through application, loan, repair, revenue, repayment, workers, customers, and closure.

Next test

Follow firms and owners monthly for two years. Compare similar firms by loan timing and receipt. Trace survival to workers, customers, service, owner debt, prices, and household stability. Track failed applications.

Reading rule

Do not call a loan a broad recovery because fewer firms closed. Name the firm, worker, customer, household, and repayment result separately.

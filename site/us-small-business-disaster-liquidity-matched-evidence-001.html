Matched evidence · US disaster credit and local life

A recovery loan can keep a local option open, but it creates another bill.

After a natural disaster, a small firm may need cash before customers return or insurance pays. Emergency credit can keep the firm open, but firm survival is not yet the same as broad community recovery.

In one sentence

Recovery credit can protect measured jobs and sales while leaving repayment, job quality, customer access, and owner-household effects open.

The connection

disaster damage or lost demand → repair and working-capital gap → insurance, SBA loan, private credit, or no help → firm exit, survival, or delayed recovery → workers, customers, owner household, and local services

A loan can improve the firm’s chance of staying open without proving that every person around it is better off.

Four views

Household

Measure the owner's cash, debt, home damage, family spending, and repayment burden beside the firm.

Company and workers

Separate survival from jobs, pay, hours, benefits, and safety.

Money and finance

Ask whether the loan resolves repair uncertainty or moves the loss into future payments.

Public power

Judge who gets funds, how fast, what is protected, and whether customers retain useful choices.

Next test

Follow firms and owners monthly for two years after a disaster. Compare similar firms by loan timing and receipt, then trace whether survival reached workers, customers, and local services or stopped at the firm balance sheet.

Reading rule

Do not call a loan a broad recovery just because fewer firms closed.

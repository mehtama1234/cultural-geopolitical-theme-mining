Elegant AI-recruiting settlement compliance follow-up audit

Status: storage-light official-source recheck · Checked: 2026-09-17

Question

After the February 23, 2026 DOJ/IER settlement with Elegant Enterprise-Wide Solutions, is there a public record showing payment completion, policy or training completion, IER monitoring, cure of a violation, or restoration of an applicant’s employment opportunity?

Search boundary

The recheck covered the official DOJ Civil Rights Division settlements and lawsuits ledger, its Elegant settlement entry, the executed agreement linked there, and an exact investigation-number/company search (DJ No. 197-79-638). No applicant file, internal compliance report, or bulk DOJ data was acquired.

Result

The official public surface continues to show the executed agreement and its requirements: two $4,730 civil-penalty installments, a three-year term, bilingual notices, policy revision, paid training, attendance records, IER inquiry authority, and individual rights to file a charge. No later public confirmation of payment, completed training/policy revision, IER inspection or report, cure notice, applicant-level back pay, restored consideration, or subsequent worker behavior was located in this recheck.

This is a bounded non-observation. It does not establish non-payment, non-compliance, or absence of an applicant remedy; the public record may be incomplete, redacted, confidential, or updated elsewhere.

Coding consequence

settlement terms: observed
prospective compliance obligations: observed_rule
public payment confirmation: not_observed
public monitoring / cure result: not_observed
applicant restoration or compensation: not_observed
later trust, switching, organizing, or political action: not_observed

The case now supports a stronger institutional correction arrow, but not a lived-recovery arrow:

          and later behavior

Matched evidence · US utilities, debt, and mobility

An old utility balance can make the next move more expensive.

A past-address debt can become a collection record and a new deposit or payment duty.

In one sentence

Utility debt may follow a household through both credit reporting and utility screening.

The old bill can become a cost of entering the next home.

The connection

old address and unpaid utility bill → collection or specialty utility record → new deposit, payment arrangement, or connection delay → move cost, housing choice, or unsafe service

The sources establish the two record paths and possible new-account response. They do not prove a particular move, denial, or housing loss.

The useful surprise

Utility debt can be mostly invisible when paid on time and visible in two places after failure. The household may face both a credit problem and a utility-entry problem.

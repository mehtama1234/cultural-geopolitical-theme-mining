Four states show four different levels of visibility.

California gives us bond terms, New York a regulator history, Texas a tenant rule, and Illinois a licensing starting point. We do not yet have one national product table.

The connection

state rule and filing → provider bond or insurance form → landlord offer and tenant fee → default claim → insurer recovery → tenant debt, renewal, credit, and housing

State law changes the path after the provider pays. It can also change what the tenant must be told before paying and which records exist to check the claim.

Matched evidence · US platforms and customers

A useful product can still sit inside a power problem.

A platform can save a customer time while controlling the shelf, the seller rules, and the data behind the choice.

In one sentence

Today's customer gain and tomorrow's market power are separate tests.

Do not call platform control harmful or helpful without measuring both.

The connection

platform-owned product + search control → useful choice and less search today → platform controls seller access, data, and visibility → outside sellers' room to enter or grow may change → future price, quality, and choice may change

The first step is measured. The middle steps are supported by records and a model, but the full customer chain is not yet measured.

The useful surprise

Removing a platform product can hurt customers even when the platform also has a reason to favor it. The real question is whether today's convenience buys tomorrow's dependence.

Four views

Household

Measure search time, price, product fit, delivery, quality, and the cost of another seller.

Customer

Compare what is shown, sponsored, available, and understandable about the ranking.

Money and finance

Track seller fees, advertising cost, commissions, product prices, and customer pass-through.

Public power

Ask whether sellers can challenge ranking, data use, fees, and removal.

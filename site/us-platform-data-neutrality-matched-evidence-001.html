Matched evidence · US platforms and data

A data rule can open the market and weaken the data supply.

Equal access may help outside sellers compete. It may also change the platform's reason to gather and improve the information everyone uses.

In one sentence

The customer result depends on access, data quality, price, and control together.

More data sharing is not proof of better service or better privacy.

NBER data model · HBS privacy study

The connection

customer shares data → platform improves a data service → sellers seek access → equal-access rule changes price and access → platform investment and data quality may change → customer price, choice, service, and control change

The model covers the middle steps. The HBS study covers one customer-side sharing response. No current US market record joins the full chain.

The useful surprise

Opening data access can help rivals without helping customers if the data become worse or more costly to produce. Protecting the platform's investment can also leave sellers dependent on a gatekeeper.

Four views

Household

Ask whether better data produce lower price, better fit, less search, or a real service gain.

Customer

Record what was shared, understood, shown, and whether refusal costs access.

Money and finance

Track data fees, seller costs, ad costs, product prices, and pass-through separately.

Public power

Test data quality, self-preference, privacy, and a workable remedy.

Reproduction result

The existing analysis was rerun against the local 2018 and 2020 CES crisis module extracts. Both files contain 1,000 valid module rows in the analysis, and the key descriptive and model-screen results match the committed trend record at the published precision.

Interpretation boundary

This recheck strengthens reproducibility of a same-respondent descriptive screen that measures reported medical-expense hardship, attribution, and political actions. It does not add a dated bill, care/payment adaptation, institutional remedy, trust change, recovery, switching, or exit outcome. The 2018/2020 questions refer to broad prior-year conditions; the teamweight results use model-robust HC1 screening rather than reproduced CES design-based variance. Health, income, insurance, prior turnout, other crises, political identity, and selection remain alternative explanations.

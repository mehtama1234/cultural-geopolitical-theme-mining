How to read every finding

Each finding is deliberately split into what the sources directly show, the bridge being proposed, what is not identified, who may experience the difference, and the next test. A cross-source finding is not presented as a same-household causal estimate unless the design actually supports that claim.

For the source universe—NBER, BEA, Federal Reserve, OFR, BLS, Census, USDA, CFPB, FTC, World Bank, IMF, ILO, Gallup, company evidence, public records, and other official or research sources—start with the source coverage index and then open the finding’s evidence table.

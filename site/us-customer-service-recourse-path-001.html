When a faster answer still leaves the case open

AI can make a first reply faster without proving that a customer can get a difficult decision changed.

In one sentence

Speed and remedy are separate service outcomes.

A human transfer is not the same as human authority.

What the sources show

1. AI improved first responses in one field experiment

HBS reports that AI suggestions helped 138 meal-delivery support agents respond 22% faster across 256,934 chats, with measured customer sentiment up 0.45 points on a five-point scale.

HBS customer-service experiment

2. Newer workers saw a larger gain

For less-experienced agents, the reported response-time decline was 70% and the sentiment increase was 1.63 points. This supports a worker-help reading as well as a cost reading.

HBS experiment details

3. Repeat complaints are a boundary

The improvement was smaller for repeat complaints. Some customers were confused after very fast transfers from a chatbot to a person.

HBS repeat-complaint findings

4. A complaint does not prove a remedy

The CFPB's 2025 annual report covers consumer complaints submitted during 2025. A complaint shows a request for help, not whether the answer was changed or the loss was repaired.

CFPB Consumer Response Annual Report

The connection

automated routing → faster first response → repeat complaint or exception → human access and authority → resolution, complaint, loss or exit

The first response is measured. Authority and remedy remain open.

What is still unknown

  • Who can change an automated answer?
  • Are repeat complaints resolved as often as first contacts?
  • Did the customer get a refund, correction or appeal?
  • How do disability, language, age and digital access change the path?
  • Did automation improve the remedy or only the reply?

Next test

Choose one public complaint system and compare first and repeat contacts. Record response time, transfer, explanation, remedy and appeal separately.

Reading rule

A quick reply is not a solved problem. Ask who can change the answer.

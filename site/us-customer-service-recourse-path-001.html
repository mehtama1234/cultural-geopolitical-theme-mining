When a faster answer still leaves the case open

A fast reply is useful only if it moves the customer toward a solved problem. A warmer sentence cannot by itself reverse a charge, correct a record, issue a refund, or change a denial.

The argument

A first delivery failure may need a quick answer. A repeat complaint needs a record, an exception, a refund, or a person with authority. That is where response speed stops being the main measure.

Human contact is not the same as human authority.

What the sources establish

HBS reports a randomized field experiment with 138 agents and 256,934 online chats. AI suggestions made replies 22% faster and raised measured sentiment by 0.45 points on a five-point scale. The gain was larger for less-experienced agents.

The improvement was smaller for repeat complaints, and some customers were unsure who was responding after a quick chatbot-to-human transfer. CFPB complaint records show demand for help, but a complaint alone does not show whether the firm fixed the problem or offered a usable appeal.

HBS experiment · CFPB report

Follow the case

automated prompt or routing → faster first response → repeat complaint, exception, denial, or record request → human access and authority → resolution, repeat contact, complaint, loss, or exit

The first response is measured. The authority and remedy steps remain open. A customer can feel heard and still leave with the same charge or denial.

Keep these apart

  • Response speed and time to resolution.
  • A polite explanation and a changed decision.
  • Human contact and human authority.
  • A complaint count and a successful remedy.

Next test

Link first contact, repeat contact, transfer, decision, remedy, and appeal. The central measure is whether the customer could get the answer changed when it was wrong.

When place changes what a paycheck can buy

Two families can earn the same amount and have different spending room because they live in different places.

The argument

Food, transport, care, services, product choice, and distance all change what the paycheck can do. A low-price place may require a longer drive or offer fewer choices; a high-cost place may offer better jobs or care.

Ask what the household receives for the money and time it spends.

Follow one paycheck through a town

A family moves from a high-cost city to a smaller town. Rent falls, but the closest pediatrician is farther away, transit is limited, and one parent loses access to a higher-paying job. The family gains monthly cash and loses time, care access, or work options.

place → local prices, services, product choices, and travel distance → real buying power → food, housing, transport, care, or moving choice → household security and public judgment

Price differences between commuting zones are especially large for low-income households; the spatial spread is almost twice as large as for high-income households.Place can change low-income households’ real room more sharply.
The result shows different real buying power.It does not show why a family stays, moves, changes stores, or judges the place as better.

Source: NBER Working Paper 29533.

The deeper finding

Real buying power is a bundle of price, time, quality, and access. A lower price can be offset by a longer trip, weaker service, or lower-paying job. Place is part of the household budget even when it is not a line item.

What remains unproven

The study does not follow the same household’s price basket, job, care, travel, family support, and move decision. It does not show whether a low-price place improved well-being.

Next test

Compare two commuting zones with similar household income but different price indexes. Follow one basic basket, job path, care path, and move-or-stay decision. Add distance, quality, transit, family support, and local services.

Reading rule

A lower price is not automatically a better life. Show what the paycheck buys and what the place provides.

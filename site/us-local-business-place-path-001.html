When a business idea leaves home

A town can have more business applications without gaining more lasting jobs, services or owners who live there.

In one sentence

Business activity and local control are related, but they are not the same measure.

An application is not a job, a lasting firm or proof of belonging.

What the sources show

1. Founders are less tied to their birthplace

HBS reports that the local advantage for self-employed US-born White men fell from 8 percentage points in 1970 to 2 points in 2018. For the full sample, it fell below zero in the latest census used.

HBS hometown-entrepreneur account

2. The sector changes the local tie

The reported research finds a smaller later share of self-employment in high-capital sectors and more activity in lower-capital sectors such as personal services, construction and real estate.

HBS research account

3. Applications are only the first step

Census county data cover business applications from 2005 through 2025 and distinguish applications from projected formations and employer firms. Privacy protection also affects the 2026 release.

Census county Business Formation Statistics

4. National small-business counts hide local differences

The SBA reports 36.2 million small businesses and 62.3 million small-business employees in its 2025 US profile. Those totals do not show which places gained lasting services or jobs.

SBA 2025 US profile

The connection

local conditions → founder stays or moves → firm, job or service change → household access and local control → political or civic response

The founder-location and application steps are measured. The local service and political steps remain open.

What is still unknown

  • Did owner movement change local jobs, prices or services?
  • Who owns the firm later, and where do they live?
  • Did housing, credit, wages or customers drive the move?
  • How did remote work and online customers change the local tie?
  • Did business turnover change belonging or local political demand?

Next test

Choose one county pair and join applications with employer-firm, establishment, service and owner-location records. Treat applications as the beginning of the path, not the outcome.

Reading rule

A new application is not a new job. A new store is not proof of local control. Count who owns, who works, who serves customers and who can leave.

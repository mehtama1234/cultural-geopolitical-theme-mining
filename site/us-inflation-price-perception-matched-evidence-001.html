Matched evidence · US inflation and customer life

Slower inflation can still feel like a continuing loss.

The price-growth rate can fall while the price level stays high. The burden depends on pay, the goods people buy, and whether cheaper choices remain.

In one sentence

Falling inflation is not the same as falling prices or restored household room.

The political link remains open; these sources do not prove a particular vote.

The connection

price rises faster than pay → less buying power at checkout → cheap option rises or disappears → trade down, buy less, save less, or accept lower quality → fairness and future expectations change → pressure on firms and government

A national rate does not show the price level, cheap option, wage path, or room left after rent, debt, and food.

Four views

Household

Track wages, rent, food, debt, transport, savings, and the products actually bought.

Customer

Ask what changed at checkout, whether the cheaper substitute remained, and whether quality fell.

Money and finance

Separate inflation rate, price level, real wage, spending response, and savings buffer.

Public power

Keep the line from household pressure to trust and voting open until same-person evidence exists.

Next test

Build matched baskets for low-, middle-, and high-income households, then compare prices with wages and repeated economic views. Measure what people stopped buying, substituted, or gave up elsewhere.

Reading rule

Do not say “inflation is falling, so pressure is over.” Ask whether prices fell, pay caught up, or the household simply found less room.

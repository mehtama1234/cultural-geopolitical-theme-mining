How to review a writeup

For each piece, check five things:

  1. Unit: Is it a person, household, event, firm, place, institution, or country?
  2. Clock: What period or event window does it actually measure?
  3. Mechanism: What choice, constraint, adaptation, or institutional route is directly observed?
  4. Boundary: Which arrow is compared or inferred rather than identified?
  5. Next test: What smallest acquisition or design would close the missing arrow?

The trend-observation registry and source registry provide the underlying records, methods, hashes, and source links for every promoted claim.

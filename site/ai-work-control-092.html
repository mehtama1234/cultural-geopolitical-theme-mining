Finding 092: Operational return to platform work can precede formal and financial remedy

Status: comparative adjudicated single-case finding · Checked: 2026-09-17

The bounded finding

In the Hotak matter, Uber deactivated a worker on 2025-04-08 and voluntarily reactivated access on 2025-05-19. The Full Bench record states that the worker recommenced work and completed more than 150 trips afterward. On 2025-09-23, the Commission ordered reactivation while leaving lost-remuneration quantum for further party consultation.

This establishes an operational sequence:

deactivation -> voluntary reactivation -> 150-plus trips
             -> formal reactivation order
             -> [open] lost-pay amount, payment, net recovery, durability, exit

The trips are stronger evidence than an unverified account-restoration label, but they do not show that the worker had a free alternative, recovered the income position lost during deactivation, or remained protected from recurrence.

Stage table

Stage Observed Still open
Exclusion Deactivation recorded in agreed facts Platform-wide incidence or reason accuracy
Access restoration Voluntary reactivation and later formal order Durable account status, profile/rating correction
Work activity More than 150 trips after reactivation Net pay, expenses, work quality, alternative work
Financial remedy Lost-pay question preserved for consultation Amount, payment receipt, net recovery, household security
Power and exit Worker used a statutory challenge route Recurrence protection, retaliation, switching, non-use, organizing, exit

Why it matters to the broad atlas

The case closes a practical middle arrow between institutional remedy and lived activity: access was restored sufficiently for work to occur. It also shows why “returned to the platform” is not equivalent to “recovered.” The formal order and the trip record coexist with an unresolved financial remedy and unknown alternatives. That is a useful counterexample to both assuming an order failed and assuming resumed work proves remedy success.

Because this is one Australian case, it is comparative mechanism evidence, not a US population estimate. Its next-use value is schema-level: future cases should record account status, trip/work activity, earnings, expenses, alternative work, payment, recurrence, and worker-reported recovery on separate clocks.

Coding consequence

reactivation              != durable access
150-plus trips            != net income recovery
continued platform use    != unconstrained alternative choice
formal order              != payment receipt
work activity             != practical exit or collective power

Code this as same-case adjudicated operational-return evidence with voluntary reactivation, 150-plus post-reactivation trips, and later formal access order; lost-pay amount, payment, recovery, alternatives, durability, and exit open.

Next decisive test

Locate the later lost-remuneration order or party communication, then seek worker-side evidence of payment, expenses, ongoing account status, alternative work, recurrence, and reason for continued use or exit.

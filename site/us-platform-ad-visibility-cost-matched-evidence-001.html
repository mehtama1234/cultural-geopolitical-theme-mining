Matched evidence · US platforms and household prices

Seller visibility can become a customer cost.

A seller may pay to be seen, then change its price, margin, or place in the search results. The customer may never see the first charge.

In one sentence

Visibility is part of the price system even when its cost is not printed on the receipt.

The FTC allegations are not yet a court finding.

The connection

higher ad-auction charge → seller pays more to stay visible → seller changes margin, price, or ad participation → customer sees changed products and prices → household pays more, searches more, or chooses differently

The complaint states the proposed chain. It does not measure every arrow.

The useful surprise

The customer may never see the advertising charge. It can still matter if it changes which products receive attention or how much a seller must charge.

Four views

Household

Compare final price, delivery, quality, and search time—not only the first product shown.

Customer

Separate organic ranking, sponsored placement, seller identity, product fit, and repeat outcomes.

Money and finance

Track auction charge, margin, product price, platform revenue, and pass-through separately.

Public power

Require records that let sellers and regulators test the auction and hidden charges.

Next test

Join auction logs to seller prices and customer purchases, comparing small and large sellers and high- and low-demand days.

Reading rule

Do not say an ad charge raised household prices until the record links the auction payment to the seller's price and the customer's purchase.

Matched evidence · US customers and marketplaces

Reviews can reveal quality and distort choice at the same time.

A review may help a customer judge a seller—or become another signal that can be bought, hidden, or unevenly understood.

In one sentence

Review design affects both what customers believe and which sellers survive.

More reviews are not automatically better information.

The connection

review rule or incentive → different reviews remain visible → customer forms a quality belief → seller changes effort, price, or promotion → customer chooses, returns, complains, or comes back

The evidence supports parts of this chain in different markets. It does not follow one US customer from review exposure to later quality and price.

The useful surprise

More reviews are not always better information. A reward can bring more feedback and higher sales, while a platform can still reward writing rather than truthful experience.

Four views

Household

Compare reviews with price, quality, delivery, return cost, and the time to check elsewhere.

Customer

Record verified purchase, incentive, reviewer history, edits, removal, and appeal.

Money and finance

Track review position, ad spending, price, refunds, complaints, and the cost of a bad choice.

Public power

Ask whether ranking and removal are explained and whether evidence can be challenged.

Next test

Link review exposure to the same customer's purchase, return, complaint, and repeat choice, while measuring seller quality and price.

Reading rule

Do not treat review volume or star rating as quality. Check who wrote it, what the platform showed, what the seller did, and what happened afterward.

Connected US finding · platform data, privacy and customer choice

When a data rule changes the customer exchange

A platform can be both the market gate and the maker of the information used in the market.

In one sentence

More sharing is not by itself proof of better service or better privacy.

Count access, data quality, customer choice, and the full exchange.

What the sources show

Equal access and privacy can change different sides of the deal

An NBER model finds that equal data access and prices can open competition but may reduce the platform's reason to improve data. HBS reports that users of a receipt-and-reward app in California and Virginia shared 9% more information after privacy rules, including more receipts, store visits, and retail categories.

The model and app study are different settings. Neither follows one customer through data use, service, price, and welfare.

NBER 35159 · HBS privacy and data

The connection

platform controls data → access and price for sellers → competition and data quality → customer price, choice, and service → public rule and platform power

Without an equal-access rule, a platform may favor its own sellers. With one, data supply or quality may change.

The four outside views

Household: Clear permission and a real reward may make sharing useful. A customer may still have little choice if the service or price cannot be found elsewhere.

Company and sector: Platforms, sellers, advertisers, and data firms decide who sees what and at what price. More data can improve offers or sharpen sorting.

Money and finance: Data may change price, reward, access, and search cost without a bill. The customer needs the full exchange.

Public power: Privacy, competition, data access, notice, enforcement, and platform rules decide whether customers get real choice.

What would change the finding

  • Platform, data quality, access price, seller entry, and platform investment.
  • Notice comprehension, retention, sale, later use, and customer control.
  • Customer price, choice, search, offer, service quality, and welfare.
  • Effects by age, income, race, disability, and digital access.
  • Whether trust lasts after a breach or unwanted use.

Next test

Follow customers, sellers, and the platform for one year around a data or privacy-rule change. Join consent, data sharing, data use, seller entry, prices, offers, service quality, search, and outcomes.

Reading rule

More sharing is not proof that privacy improved. Keep data access, quality, competition, customer outcome, and investment separate.

Matched evidence · US utilities, shutoffs, and recovery

A shutoff rule changes the time a household has to recover.

Notice, protection, and payment plans can leave service on, but the balance may keep growing.

In one sentence

Time bought by a protection becomes recovery only when the next payment is possible.

Service on is not the same as the bill solved.

The connection

missed payment → notice, protection, or payment arrangement → shutoff or continued service → reconnection cost, new arrears, safe home, or unsafe home

The sources establish the main branches. They do not yet compare state rules or prove which branch a particular household took.

The useful surprise

A rule can prevent a shutoff without solving the bill. A household may pay current use while also repaying old debt, leaving less room before the next deadline.

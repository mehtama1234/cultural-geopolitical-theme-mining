The PSID backbone is specified, but not yet acquired.

PSID is the highest-value next step for a many-family comparison of material conditions, work, time, care, health, and wellbeing across 2019, 2021, and 2023. The research design is ready. The public-use files are still behind an account-controlled download gate.

Current status · checked 2026-09-15: official packages listed; the 2023 route is live but redirects to a Conditions-of-Use warning until an account accepts the policy; no local PSID microdata; no PSID estimate promoted.

What the gate proves—and does not prove

The official package page lists the target files. The 2023 package route redirects to the Conditions-of-Use warning, which states that account registration and policy acceptance are required before generated carts and ZIP downloads are available. A local workspace search found documentation and audit assets but no target data packages.

This is an access prerequisite, not a null result. The documentation does not establish that the fields merge, that universes match, that attrition is acceptable, or that any material/time/care relationship exists.

Checks after download

  1. Run the wave-file structural audit against the four packages.
  2. Verify codebook wording, universe, routing, and response codes across waves.
  3. Check family/person keys, merge counts, retention, movers, composition, and missingness.
  4. Identify weights, replicate variance, valid denominators, subgroup cells, and counterexamples.
  5. Publish only the bounded comparison that those checks support.

What continues while access is open

The program continues the same lane with acquired SIPP, ATUS, SHED, NHTS, MEPS, RECS, and Consumer Expenditure layers. They add bounded evidence about population conditions, time, health, mobility, and household room; they do not silently stand in for the intended same-family PSID bridge.

The connection

budget or payment plan → smoother monthly withdrawal → actual use differs from estimate → balance, true-up, fee, or continued protection → stable service or another cash crisis

The sources establish the plan mechanics and possible service endpoint. They do not prove later stability for the same household.

The useful surprise

A smaller payment can feel like relief while total obligation stays the same—or becomes harder to see. The plan helps only if the later balance is visible and manageable.

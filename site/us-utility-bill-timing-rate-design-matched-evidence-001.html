Matched evidence · US energy, bills, and household cash

A manageable yearly cost can still fail at the due date.

The annual average can look ordinary while one seasonal bill arrives when the household has no room.

In one sentence

Energy affordability is the bill that arrives, the money available that week, and the cost of falling behind.

Find the month, not only the yearly average.

The connection

rate design and seasonal use → monthly bill and due date → cash gap, arrears, or payment choice → shutoff risk, unsafe temperature, aid, or debt

The sources support the path from weather and bill structure to monthly cost and reported energy insecurity. They do not prove the exact cause for a particular household.

The useful surprise

Lower use does not always mean a proportionally lower bill. A fixed charge can remain, so a difficult month can look ordinary in a yearly average.

Reading rule

Do not call energy affordable from an annual average. Find the month, the due date, and the cash actually available.

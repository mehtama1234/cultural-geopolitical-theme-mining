# End-to-end program recovery brief v1

**Read this first after a crash.** The project is a broad US societal, cultural,
consumer, institutional, financial, political, and geopolitical theme-mining
program. It is not a one-household study. Household data is one measurement
layer inside the program.

The finite requirement audit and stopping rule is [active-goal completion
audit](analysis/ACTIVE-GOAL-COMPLETION-AUDIT_2026-09-25.md). It distinguishes
the broad atlas release, which is publication-ready, from the still-open
identifier-bearing same-unit extension.

For the durable session-to-session control state, also read the [program
continuity ledger](analysis/US-BROAD-PROGRAM-CONTINUITY-LEDGER_V1.md). It keeps
the full long-term objective active while the execution queue chooses the next
empirical pass.

Before handing off or starting a new research cycle, run the consolidated
`python3 scripts/validate_long_term_publication_gate.py`. It checks registry
sync, source coverage, provenance, observation records, finding and watchlist
parity, published links, local links, and whitespace together.

The political-action layer adds a complementary CCES bridge: reported job loss
is followed by a large insurance gap and lower local action without a uniform
approval or hostility pattern.

The latest route- usability rotation sharpens the shared mechanism: a formal
option becomes consequential only when a person can identify it, reach it in
time, use it, and obtain a meaningful result. Federal reentry records show
earned-time and contractor-payment failures that can weaken the transition
route; accessibility oversight shows that even a complaint route may be
underused when people do not know it exists; and text outreach improves the
speed of initial response while consent and access remain uneven. These are
not interchangeable measures, but together they make timing, visibility, and
correction testable dimensions of institutional access.

The newest housing and care evidence adds an important cultural boundary.
HUD's regional HEARS study associates negative assistance exits with higher
subsequent housing instability and crisis indicators, while the Native
housing review warns that doubled-up housing can be protective kinship
support rather than homelessness. Disability-healthcare oversight similarly
shows that a nominally available system can remain unusable because of
physical, sensory, communication, cognitive, digital, or provider-bias
barriers. The atlas should therefore track both material outcomes and the
classification/interface rules that decide whether need, resilience, or
remedy becomes visible. The same-unit bridge remains open: these routes do
not yet jointly observe alternatives, actual receipt, recovery, and later
meaning or exit for the same person or household.

The active depth lane is a storage-light same-case or event-compatible route:

```text
dated exposure or decision
  -> alternative or non-use
  -> effort, money, time, or access trade-off
  -> institutional response and verified remedy
  -> protected/sacrificed outcome and recovery
  -> meaning, trust, action, persistence, or exit
```

The current 33-surface availability audit finds no retained source that closes
all nine stages. Eight surfaces observe an episode-level alternative or
non-use field, and three observe a verified remedy or defined outcome follow-up,
but no single retained route combines the required stages. A separate
17-route practical-optionality qualification screen also finds zero qualifying
routes. UAS, PSID, HRS, and
WBNS respondent routes remain access-gated; the
next valid advance is an authorized event-compatible acquisition or a retained
subgroup/place reversal that changes an open arrow. Another aggregate exposure
or settlement count does not qualify as end-to-end closure.

The strongest retained same-unit bridges are now explicit in the [bridge
matrix](analysis/US-BROAD-RETAINED-SAME-UNIT-BRIDGE-MATRIX_V1.md): [MEPS event to
later-panel follow-up](analysis/projects/us-household-constraint-cascade/meps-event-six-month-followup-v1.md)
places a dated person-level event before later health and work transitions;
[SHED persistence](analysis/projects/us-household-financial-pressure/shed-2024-2025-panel-persistence-layer-v1.md)
shows repeated adaptation and partial reversal for the same respondent; and
[CCES panel follow-up](analysis/projects/us-cost-trust-politics/cces-panel-job-loss-political-followup-v1.md)
places earlier job loss before later institutional judgment and political
action. These are genuine adjacent bridges, not one pooled causal chain. The
missing bridge is still the same-unit link from the event to alternatives,
institutional response, verified remedy or recovery, and later meaning or
exit.

## The big end-to-end goal

Build a durable, evidence-grounded system for discovering and testing recurring
cultural, societal, political, consumer, institutional, financial, firm,
infrastructure, and geopolitical themes and trends in the United States.

Triangulate surveys, administrative and public records, research, company
filings, markets, platforms, media and cultural measures, opinion and election
data, and international or supply-chain records where they explain a US
exposure. The program should determine what is changing, who is exposed or
protected, what adaptations and meanings recur, how costs and power move
between actors, and which patterns survive comparison across populations,
places, sectors, and time.

The deliverable is a living trend atlas: source-backed findings,
counterexamples, subgroup differences, uncertainty, measurement limits, and
explicit next tests. It is a population- and system-level program, not a
one-household study; household and longitudinal records are instruments for
testing selected links within the broader map.

The target is a connected map of how conditions and decisions move through
people, consumers, workers, households, firms, institutions, communities, and
politics:

```text
condition / price / rule / technology / shock
  -> money, time, access, control, status, health, or security changes
  -> people and organizations adapt, delay, borrow, switch, stay, organize, or go without
  -> firms, employers, platforms, agencies, courts, media, parties, or foreign actors respond
  -> money, risk, data, assets, and decision power are redistributed
  -> trust, identity, culture, public demand, political action, firm exposure,
     sector capacity, or geopolitical leverage changes
```

Every arrow must be marked observed, reported, estimated, compared, inferred,
or open. The project must not promote a plausible story into a finding merely
because adjacent datasets point in the same direction.

## How this goal is pursued over the long term

The program grows as a maintained atlas rather than a one-time report. Each
research cycle should add or revisit source cards, trend records, subgroup and
place comparisons, counterexamples, and historical observations across the
14-theme inventory. New work should be selected because it strengthens a weak
arrow, adds a distinct source family or measurement level, tests a reversal, or
shows that a previously reported pattern changed.

The durable workflow is:

```text
collect source evidence
  -> normalize unit, date, geography, measure, and denominator
  -> extract cautious recurring themes
  -> compare populations, places, sectors, and time
  -> test counterexamples and competing explanations
  -> connect adjacent stages where a valid design permits
  -> publish, version, and periodically re-test the trend atlas
```

The long-term outcome is not a single composite score or a final theory of
society. It is an increasingly reliable map of what appears to be changing,
for whom, through which mechanisms, with what cultural, political, consumer,
institutional, firm, infrastructural, or geopolitical consequences—and what
the evidence still cannot establish.
